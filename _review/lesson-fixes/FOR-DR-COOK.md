# Lesson questions for Dr. Cook

Everything that was plainly a writing error has been fixed, and so have the Spanish passages that said something different from the English, the untranslated passages, the factual slips and the tú/usted mixing. What is below needs your judgement: which Bible wording to use, doctrine, the unfinished sermon sections in Matthew, and a few points only the author can settle. Each item names the course, unit and the place in the lesson.

Spanish passages rewritten or newly translated to match the English are marked "Translated automatically" in the editor, so you can find and review them there.
