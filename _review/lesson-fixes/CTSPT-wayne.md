# CTSPT — items for Dr. Cook

- **Unit 4, block b018 ("What Prayer Is"):** the English has a passage in brackets about the King James "thee, thy, and thou" once being everyday words and "you" the formal one. The Spanish leaves this out and just says "Muchas veces usamos con Dios palabras rígidas y 'religiosas'…". (The point is about English, so leaving it out may be deliberate, but please confirm.)
- **Unit 9, block b017:** the unit's key term in Spanish is "dominio propio", but the quoted Galatians 5:22–23 (RVG) says "templanza". Students may not see that it is the same fruit. A short note ("templanza, es decir, dominio propio") might help.
