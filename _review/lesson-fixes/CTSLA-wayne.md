# Biblical Languages: questions for Dr. Cook

- **When John wrote his Gospel (unit 7 b010 and unit 8 b018):** the two units disagree. Unit 7: "Sixty-five years earlier [than Patmos, AD 95] the same John, then a young fisherman from Galilee, had opened his Gospel" (so about AD 30). Unit 8: "around AD 90, an old fisherman from Galilee picked up his pen on the island of Patmos and opened his Gospel". Spanish says the same in each place. Which do you want? (Most date the Gospel around AD 85–95, and Revelation, not the Gospel, to Patmos.)
- **Unit 9, b044, Eudokia:** both languages now give the proper name "Eudokia" and say it means "good righteousness" («buena justicia»). Eudokia actually means "good pleasure", and it does not come from dikaios. (The Spanish used to say "Eudoxia", which means roughly "good repute".) What word did you intend here?
- **Units 9 and 10, "Wayne and Andi":** a few places still name two teachers: unit 9 b072 ("as Wayne and Andi point them out to you"), unit 10 b044 ("Wayne and Andi's seminary"), unit 10 b070 ("another word approved by Wayne and Andi"); the Spanish says "Wayne y Andi" in each. Unit 11 names only Wayne. Should these name only you?
- **Unit 4, b024, who chose "created":** the Spanish used to credit the Reina-Valera translators with choosing «creó»; it now says what the English says (the King James translators of 1611 chose the English word "created"). If you would rather tell Spanish readers about the Reina-Valera, say so and we will change both languages.
