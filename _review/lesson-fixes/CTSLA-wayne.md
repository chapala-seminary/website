# Biblical Languages: questions for Dr. Cook

- **Unit 9, b044, Eudokia:** the meaning is now "good pleasure" («beneplácito»), as you asked, but the sentence still lists Eudokia among words that come from dikaios ("The Greek dikaios gives us … the proper name Eudokia"). Eudokia comes from *eu* + *dokeō* ("to seem good"), not from dikaios. Drop Eudokia from this sentence, or give it its own line?
