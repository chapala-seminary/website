# CTSDP (Doctrinal Preaching) — items for Dr. Cook

- **Unit 1, block b026 — "carry in unseen".** EN: "the griefs they carry in unseen, the fears they will not name aloud". It may mean "carry in [to church] unseen", or the "in" may be a slip for "carry unseen". ES: "los duelos que cargan sin que se vean" (fine as it is). Please confirm the English.
- **Units 2 to 5 — Spanish word for "Subject".** Unit 2 (b039–b050) calls the sermon's subject "Sujeto" ("Día 4 — Sujeto, objetivo, y las dos direcciones"; "El sujeto de un sermón es su brújula"), but Units 3, 4 and 5 label it "Tema" ("Tema: la Palabra de Dios", "Tema: Dios", "Tema: el hombre"). Which word should the course use? ("Tema" is the usual Spanish homiletics term.)
- **Unit 4, block b090, and Unit 5, block b034 — the unit summaries.** Both say "one developed by E-A-I with two left to the preacher" (ES "uno desarrollado por E-A-I con dos dejados al predicador"). In the units themselves one point is developed in full, one is worked "together" with only the illustration left to the student, and one is left wholly to the student (Unit 4: Point 4 full, Point 1 together, Point 5 yours; Unit 5: Point 1 full, Point 2 together, Point 4 yours). Please check whether the summaries say what you mean.
- **Unit 6, block b032 — "five-point parallel".** EN: "Unit 6 preached the central doctrine in the five-point parallel." ES: "en el paralelo de cinco puntos". But Unit 6's sermon is built on four movements ("the four movements end facing forward", b023, b100). Should it say four?
