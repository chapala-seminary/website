# The Holy Spirit: questions for Dr. Cook

- **Unit 2, block b011 (Spanish), Isaiah 63:10:** the paragraph argues that the Spirit can be *grieved* (a love word, not anger). The English quotes "they rebelled and grieved His Holy Spirit", but the Spanish quotes "hicieron enojar su santo Espíritu" (made Him angry), which works against the point. Use a Spanish wording with "contristaron" (as in unit 7 for Ephesians 4:30)?
- **Unit 7, block b011, the seal of Ephesians 4:30:** the two languages say different things about perseverance. English: "Paul's immediate point is not to settle every Christian disagreement about final perseverance, but to show how serious it is for those marked as God's own to grieve the Holy Spirit." Spanish: "El sello significa que aun cuando contristamos al Espíritu Santo, seguimos siendo suyos; no somos desechados ni perecemos" (the seal means that even when we grieve the Spirit, we remain His; we are not cast off and do not perish). Which one do you want both languages to say?
- **Unit 10, blocks b021 and b022 (Spanish), Ephesians 4:11:** the heading says the key word is "<i>algunos</i>" (English "some"), but the verse as quoted in the next paragraph says "a unos… a otros", and that paragraph points to "a unos… a otros". Change the heading to match the verse?
