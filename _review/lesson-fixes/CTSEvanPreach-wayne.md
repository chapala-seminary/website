# CTSEvanPreach (Evangelistic Preaching) — items for Dr. Cook

- **Units 6 to 10 — repetition.** The later units reuse the same examples and explanations many times (for instance the keyword method's "three short parallel points, each five words or less"). Would you like these units tightened, or is the repetition intended for drill?
- **Unit 2, blocks b039–b044 — the Spanish keyword "aseguranzas".** EN keyword "assurances" (of salvation) is rendered "aseguranzas" throughout ("Tres Aseguranzas de la Salvación", "Una aseguranza es..."). In much of Latin America "aseguranza" means an insurance policy. Would you prefer "seguridades" or "certezas" (e.g. "Tres Certezas de la Salvación")? Since it is the sermon's keyword, all six blocks would change together.
- **Unit 4, block b024 — Acts 16:31 wording.** EN: "Believe in the Lord Jesus." ES: "Cree en el Señor Jesús". The RVG, used elsewhere in the course, reads "Cree en el Señor Jesucristo". Which do you want?
