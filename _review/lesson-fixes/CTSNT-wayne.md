# CTSNT — items for Dr. Cook

- **Unit 4, block b010:** the Spanish quotes Luke 3:38 as "hijo de Adam" (Reina-Valera spelling), but the section heading (b008) and the rest of the course say "Adán". Keep the Bible's spelling in the quotation, or make it "Adán"?
- **Unit 4, block b013:** "Blind Bartimaeus shouts above the crowd until Jesus stops (Luke 18:35–43)" / "El ciego Bartimeo … (Lucas 18:35…)". Luke does not give the blind man's name; only Mark 10:46 calls him Bartimaeus. Cite Mark, or drop the name?
- **Unit 10, block b024 (§VI):** "Three little words" / "Tres palabritas", followed by a long quotation ("Demas has forsaken me, having loved this present world…"). The count does not fit the quote.
- **Unit 12, blocks b011–b015:** the Spanish spells the same word two ways: "Postmilenialismo" (b011, b012, b014) and "posmilenialismo" (b015). Both are acceptable Spanish; which one should the course use?
- **Unit 12, block b025:** "the 'Thief on the Cross' hardening his heart" / "el 'Ladrón en la Cruz' endureciendo su corazón". Unit 9 teaches that the thief on the cross was saved; this seems to mean the other thief, who mocked. Say "the other thief" / "el otro ladrón"?
- **Unit 12, blocks b006, b010, b013, b022, b023, b025:** terms used without being explained: "Practice Report" / "Informe de Práctica", "Red Letter" / "Letras Rojas", "Whole Life" policy. Students may not know what these mean here.
