# CTSPsalms — items for Dr. Cook

- **Unit 5, block b009 (§1):** "There are two psalms that are devoted entirely to the Word of God, Psalm 19 and Psalm 119" / "Hay dos salmos dedicados enteramente…". Psalm 19 is only half about the Word (verses 1–6 are about creation), and Psalm 1 is sometimes named too. Perhaps "two great psalms of the Word"?
- **Unit 7, block b018 (§4):** Job 12:23 means different things in each language. EN "He enlarges nations, and guides them"; ES "Él esparce las naciones, y las vuelve a reunir" ("He scatters the nations and gathers them again"). The Spanish is how the Reina-Valera reads the verse, so this is a question of which wording you want the Spanish page to use.
- **Unit 7, block b022 (§6):** Job 42:2 differs. EN "no purpose of Yours can be withheld"; ES "no hay pensamiento que se esconda de ti" ("no thought is hidden from You"). Again the Spanish is the Reina-Valera reading; the English fits the unit's point about God's power better.
