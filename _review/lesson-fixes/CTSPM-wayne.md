# CTSPM — items for Dr. Cook

- **Unit 7, block b018 (§III):** "Be diligent" is quoted as 2 Timothy 4:5. The NKJV there reads "be watchful in all things"; the Spanish already has "Sé sobrio" (as the RVG does).
- **Unit 8, block b010 (§I):** "Parainos means 'to encourage'" / "Parainos significa 'alentar'". This Greek word is doubtful; the usual New Testament words for "encourage" are parakaleō or paramytheomai. Please check.
- **Unit 8, block b045 (§VI):** "maintaining a male presence — wife, deacon, secretary nearby" / "una presencia masculina — esposa, diácono, secretaria cerca". The examples (wife, secretary) are not male, so the sentence contradicts itself in both languages. Perhaps "another adult present"?
