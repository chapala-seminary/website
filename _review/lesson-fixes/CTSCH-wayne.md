# CTSCH (Church History) — items for Dr. Cook

- **Unit 9, block b046 — the Uganda martyrs called "bakopi".** Both languages say the young men of the royal court, "pages, often teenagers", were "called the *bakopi*". In Luganda *bakopi* usually means commoners or peasants, not royal pages; the pages are usually called something else (e.g. *abasiige*). Please check the word. EN: "The young men of the royal court, called the *bakopi* — pages, often teenagers". ES: "Los jóvenes de la corte real, llamados los *bakopi* — pajes, a menudo adolescentes".
- **Unit 9, block b032 — Spurgeon "by twenty ... by twenty".** The same age is given twice for two different milestones. EN: "Spurgeon was preaching to thousands by twenty and pastoring London's New Park Street Chapel by twenty." ES: "Spurgeon predicaba a miles para los veinte y pastoreaba la New Park Street Chapel de Londres a los veinte." Spurgeon began preaching at about 16 and was called to New Park Street at 19 (1854); please give the ages you want.
