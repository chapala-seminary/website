# Hermeneutics: questions for Dr. Cook

- **Unit 2, block b025, definition of eisegesis:** both languages describe eisegesis as "pulling our own meaning out of the text instead of letting the text put meaning into us" (Spanish: "sacar nuestro propio significado del texto en lugar de dejar que el texto ponga significado en nosotros"). Unit 3 (b013) defines it the usual way: "leading in, reading our own ideas into the page" ("meter dentro, leer nuestras propias ideas en la página"). Should unit 2 say "reading our own meaning into the text"?
- **Unit 4, block b017 (Spanish):** "The hardest word ... is one syllable long: *wait*." The Spanish keeps "tiene una sola sílaba: *espera*", but "espera" has three syllables. Perhaps "es una sola palabra: *espera*"?
- **Unit 7, block b035, Dr. Maurice Rawlings:** both languages say he "is a heart specialist in Chattanooga" ("es un especialista del corazón"). Dr. Rawlings died in 2010. Change to "was"?
