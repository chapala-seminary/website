# CTSBibleCharacters (Bible Characters) — items for Dr. Cook

- **Unit 1, block b018** — The Spanish adds a sentence the English does not have, at the end: "Por eso Enoc no fue un esclavo temeroso sino un amigo cercano, y por eso su largo caminar nunca se volvió una carga." Keep it (and add it to the English), or remove it?
- **Unit 1, block b038** — The Spanish of this paragraph is missing. In its place is a copy of the next paragraph's Spanish (b039, "Y aquí está el evangelio que hace posible semejante caminar…"), so Spanish readers get b039 twice and never read b038. EN b038 begins: "What hope this gives to everyone who walks with God. The world looks at the long parade of human lives…" It needs a Spanish translation.
- **Unit 4, block b011** — This paragraph may be out of place. It sits between "We will trace five movements in the life of Rebekah" (b010) and the first movement's heading "I. The Bride Sought at the Well" (b012), and it talks about Abraham's care in choosing a bride — which belongs inside the first movement. Move it after the heading?
- **Unit 4, blocks b020 and b035** — The Spanish mixes "usted" and "tú". b020: "Piense en lo que le costó" (usted), then "comprometer toda tu vida … porque confías" (tú). b035: "Oye ambas. Dile al Novio invisible, con todo tu corazón… confía en Él… sin tu engaño" (tú). Which form should the unit use?
- **Unit 7, blocks b038 and b039** — Your car story changes state. b038: "some men from the Gideons in Louisiana insisted on helping" (ES "en Luisiana"). b039: "as I once did with that wrecked car in South Dakota" (ES "en Dakota del Sur"). Which is right?
- **Unit 10, block b007** — The Spanish stops short. It ends with "Habla, Señor, que tu siervo oye." The English goes on: "Samuel's own calling that night was a unique prophetic commission, given at a time when the word of the Lord was rare — but the humility behind his answer … is exactly the posture every believer needs toward the Word God has already given us." The earlier sentence also differs: EN "when a child answered God's call with a posture every believer must learn" / ES "cuando un niño aprendió a decir las palabras que todo creyente debe aprender a decir".
- **Unit 11, block b027** — The Spanish adds a closing sentence the English does not have: "A Él podemos correr en toda batalla, en toda necesidad y en todo pecado, sabiendo que su gracia siempre es mayor que nuestra mayor falta." Keep it (and add it to the English), or remove it?
