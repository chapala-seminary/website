# CTSBible (How We Got the Bible) — items for Dr. Cook

- **Unit 10, block b013** — Luke 1:4 in Spanish is marked as a paraphrase: "para que conozcas la certeza de las cosas en las cuales has sido instruido" (Lucas 1:4, según el sentido). EN gives it as a plain quotation: "that you may know the certainty of those things in which you were instructed" (Luke 1:4). Quote the RVG wording ("para que conozcas bien la verdad de las cosas en las cuales has sido instruido"), or keep the paraphrase? Note the lesson's whole point rests on the word "certeza", which the RVG does not use.
