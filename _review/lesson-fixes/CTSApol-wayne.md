# CTSApol (Apologetics) — items for Dr. Cook

- **Unit 1, block b033 (last heading)** — The Spanish heading switches from "usted" to "tú": "¿Estás Listo para Dar una Respuesta?" The rest of the lesson speaks to the student as "usted" (e.g. "Déjeme contarle una historia"). Suggested: "¿Está Listo para Dar una Respuesta?"
- **Unit 3, block b027** — The closing verse differs. EN: "Now He who has sealed us and given us the Spirit in our hearts as a guarantee is God." — 2 Corinthians 1:22 "(paraphrased from the apostle)". ES quotes the Reina-Valera text: "Y el que nos ungió... y nos selló…" — 2 Corintios 1:21-22 (RVG). Which reference and wording do you want in both?
- **Unit 10, block b008** — "He added five small words … 'with meekness and fear.'" That phrase is four words. ES says the same: "cinco palabras pequeñas … 'con mansedumbre y reverencia'" (also four).
