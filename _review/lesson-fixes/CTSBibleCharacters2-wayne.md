# CTSBibleCharacters2 (Bible Characters II) — items for Dr. Cook

- **Unit 4, block b012 (Esther)** — English and Spanish say different things about how Esther came to the palace. EN: Esther was "taken like the others" by the king's decree; "Neither Esther nor Mordecai appears to have had much real choice … this was less an ordinary marriage than an imperial conscription", and God works through "hard, morally complicated circumstances His people did not choose." ES: "Mardoqueo … presentó a su hermosa joven prima Ester"; "al hacerlo, Mardoqueo quebrantaba el mandamiento de Dios contra el matrimonio con los paganos … estaba desobedeciendo" (Mordecai put her forward and so broke God's command). Which reading do you want both languages to teach?
- **Unit 5, block b035** — The Spanish mixes "tú" and "usted" in one paragraph. "Tú mismo, creyente, eres…", "oye el mensaje", "Si has caído… Vuelve al altar" (tú); but "Y vea cómo…", "Recuerde, también…" (usted). Which form should it use?
