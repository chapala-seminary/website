# CTSPentecostal — items for Dr. Cook

- **Unit 4, block b041 ("Four Scenes, One Author's Purpose"):** the Spanish leaves out two English sentences: that Luke reports tongues only at Pentecost, Cornelius's house and Ephesus, not in Samaria; and that this "should make us cautious about turning any one scene into a rule that binds every believer for all time."
- **Unit 5, block b054 ("unknown tongue"):** the Spanish is shorter. The English says the New King James "drops it, reading simply that a person 'speaks in a tongue'"; the Spanish only says "La New King James Version la omite."
- **Unit 5, block b036:** EN "The whole question rests on four words: what is 'that which is perfect'?"; ES "descansa en unas palabras" ("a few words"). In Spanish "lo que es perfecto" is also four words, so the count could be kept.
- **Unit 6, block b006 (heading):** EN "The Rise of Pentecostalism, 1901–1910s"; ES "1901–1910". The English means the whole decade; the Spanish means up to one year. Perhaps "1901–década de 1910"?
- **Unit 8, blocks b008 and b022:** Acts 1:5 is quoted two different ways, both marked RVG: "Porque Juan a la verdad bautizó con agua…" (b008) and "…porque Juan ciertamente bautizó con agua…" (b022).
