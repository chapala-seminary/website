# The Life of Christ: questions for Dr. Cook

- **Unit 3, block b014, the new birth:** the Spanish is an older, shorter version of this paragraph and says something different. The English has: "it is God's gift, worked by His Spirit ... Yet Scripture also calls us to respond to that gift" and quotes John 1:12 ("as many as received Him...") and "received the moment genuine faith reaches out and takes hold of Christ". The Spanish has none of that; instead it says the new birth "es algo hecho <i>a</i> nosotros y <i>por</i> nosotros, nunca <i>de</i> nosotros" (something done to us and for us, never by us). The Spanish should be brought up to date with the English, or the English changed; which wording do you want?
- **Unit 7, block b011, Judas and the washed hands:** English: "The hands that had been washed in the basin were already taking the traitor's price" (the foot-washing already past). Spanish: "Las manos que serían lavadas en la vasija aquella noche ya tomaban el precio del traidor" (hands that *would be* washed that night). Matthew 26:14–16 places the bargain before the Last Supper, which fits the Spanish. Which should both languages say?
- **Unit 10, block b025 (Spanish), Titus 2:13:** "Esta es la esperanza bienaventurada, que aguardamos «la esperanza bienaventurada y la manifestación gloriosa...»" says "la esperanza bienaventurada" twice in a row. The English quotes only "for the glorious appearing of our great God and Savior Jesus Christ", so it does not repeat. Perhaps shorten the Spanish quote to «la manifestación gloriosa de nuestro gran Dios y Salvador Jesucristo»?
