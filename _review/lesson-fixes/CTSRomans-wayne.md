# Romans: questions for Dr. Cook

These are small wording questions; none is a mistake as such.

- **Unit 2, block b038 (Spanish):** "Encadena una catena de citas del Antiguo Testamento" repeats itself ("chains a chain"), since "catena" means chain. The English is "He strings together a catena of Old Testament quotations." Perhaps "Reúne una cadena (catena) de citas..."?
- **Unit 9, block b026:** Romans 12:9-10 is quoted from different Bibles, so the two languages read differently. English (NKJV): "Abhor what is evil. Cling to what is good. Be kindly affectionate to one another with brotherly love." Spanish (Reina-Valera): "Aborreciendo lo malo, seguid lo bueno. Amaos los unos a los otros con amor fraternal." Is that acceptable, or should the Spanish follow the English sentence by sentence?
- **Unit 10, block b015 (Spanish):** Romans 15:1 is quoted from the Reina-Valera Gómez as "sobrellevar las flaquezas de los flacos", which is the Bible's own wording but sounds odd to modern readers ("flacos" today usually means "skinny"). Keep it, or add a short note such as "(los débiles)"?
