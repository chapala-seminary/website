# Joshua: questions for Dr. Cook

- **Units 1, 2 and 5 (Spanish shorter than English):** in several paragraphs the Spanish leaves out sentences the English has. Examples: unit 2, b044 ends after "...la separó de todo lo que había conocido." and has nothing for "The girl who had lived as a Canaanite prostitute became the woman who confessed... The confession cost her everything. The confession saved her family."; unit 2, b035 stops after "...la fe genuina siempre se muestra en acción." and leaves out "She did not just say she believed — she hid the spies... Her works were the visible evidence of her invisible faith." Other paragraphs that look shortened: unit 1 b013, b039, b042; unit 2 b012, b014, b021, b026; unit 5 b038. Should the Spanish be completed?
- **Distance to Jericho (unit 3 b045 and unit 4 b023):** unit 3 says the walls stood "six miles away" ("a seis millas"); unit 4 says Jericho was "two miles away" (Spanish "a tres kilómetros"). Which distance is right?
- **Unit 5, Joshua 7:19 in Spanish:** the Course Scripture (b007) reads «Hijo mío, te ruego que des gloria a Jehová Dios de Israel, y dale alabanza; declárame ahora lo que has hecho.», but b030 quotes «Hijo mío, da gloria a Jehová Dios de Israel, y dale alabanza, y declárame ahora lo que has hecho; no me lo encubras». Use one wording in both places?
- **Unit 8, b044:** "The remaining six chapters of allotment in 18:11–19:48" ("Los seis capítulos restantes") — 18:11 to 19:48 is less than two chapters. Change the wording?
- **Unit 9, b036 and the hymn credits (b013, b053, b075, and unit 10 b076):** "Jesus, Lover of My Soul" and the bird-at-the-window story are credited to John Wesley. The hymn was written by his brother Charles Wesley. Change to Charles Wesley?
- **Unit 9, b013 (overview):** it says "Section IV listens to John Wesley's hymn-bird, A.B. Simpson's London waif, and the Confederate soldier", but Section IV in the lesson (b048) is "Corrie ten Boom — Strength Just in Time". The overview needs to match the sections.
- **Unit 9, b012:** "thirty-five verses (chapter 20 plus the levitical-cities list of chapter 21)" ("treinta y cinco versículos"). Chapter 20 has 9 verses and chapter 21 has 45. Please check the count.
- **Unit 10, b007 (Course Scripture):** Hebrews 11:30 ("By faith the walls of Jericho fell down...") belongs to the Jericho story, while unit 10 is Joshua 23–24, "Choose This Day". Keep it, or choose a verse from Joshua 24?
- **Unit 10, the word "capstone" in Spanish (b001, b004, b012, b048, b079):** the Spanish keeps the English word ("(Capstone)", "el capstone del Intensivo"). Other courses do the same. Keep it as a school term, or say "culminación" in Spanish?
