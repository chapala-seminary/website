# Reading rooms — Spanish drafted for the language switch (5 Oct 2026)

For Dr. Cook. When the reading rooms were given the unit design and the English / Español / Both switch, a few short labels had no Spanish: in Spanish view they would have stayed in English. Claude drafted the Spanish below. These are **machine drafts**; correct any of them in `tools/data/room-topics-es.json` (the labels) or directly in `src/body/rooms/<room>.html`.

Nothing else in the rooms was translated: every other Spanish passage is the seminary's own, only marked so the switch can show or hide it.
