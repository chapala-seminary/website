Each unit has the full lesson, 20 multiple-choice questions, 10 fill-ins and 10 short answers (essays for WiseSpeak), all in French. With Old Testament Survey (done earlier), all seven foundation courses now have a French draft.

**How each unit was checked**
- Every Bible quotation uses Louis Segond 1910 wording, looked up online. Where Segond reads differently from the NKJV in a way a question depends on, the French follows Segond and the item is flagged (⚠ in the review file).
- Every unit passed a test that copies the website's own grading rules: each fill-in has one blank, multiple-choice answers are in the same position as the English, and each French model answer is graded correct by its own keywords.
- A second, independent pass compared every unit with the English and fixed about 135 small problems (wording, a few meaning slips, keywords that would have marked good answers wrong).

**Still needed before publishing:** a French-speaking reader with theological training (Pastor Kalo's contact, perhaps) and Robert's import into the French pages. The drafts are marked as machine work until a person reviews them.

2. **NT Survey Units 5–11: multiple-choice explanations are attached to the wrong questions** (shifted by about one from question 2–4 onward). The keyed answers are right, but the "why" text explains a different question.
3. **Exam items that test things the lesson never teaches**: Systematic Theology Units 4, 5, 6, 8, 11; Pastoral Ministries 2, 12; Evangelism 7; WiseSpeak 1, 6, 8; Church History 2 (Valerian).
4. **Short-answer model answers that disagree with the lesson**: Evangelism 2 (SA 4 lists the wrong propositions), 5, 6, 8; Pastoral Ministries 3, 4, 5, 8; Church History 10 (SA 10 asks for a "divorce parallel" the lesson doesn't contain).
5. **Church History has no English model answers** for any short answer. The French ones were written from the lesson and need your check.
6. **Factual slips worth a look:** NT 2 — Ahaz's alliance was with Assyria, not Egypt; the "voice in the wilderness" is Isaiah 40:3, not Malachi 3:1. NT 12 — MC 10's answer contradicts the lesson. Evangelism 5 — "times and bounds" is Acts 17:26. Church History 5 — Bernard preached the Second Crusade; Charlemagne founded schools, not universities. Church History 8 — "Bonnie Dundee" was John (not James) Graham. Church History 9 — MC 19 says 1845, answer says 1868. WiseSpeak 9 — "thirteen men" in the upper room; Hippo was far from Rome.

## Decisions that apply to the whole French edition

1. **Pronouns for God:** lowercase « il / lui » as in Louis Segond (used now), or capitalised?
2. **Great Commission:** « la Grande Commission » (most common in the drafts) vs « le Grand Mandat » / « la Grande Mission ». Currently mixed.
3. **Track names:** « Certificat », « Associé », and keep "Master of Divinity" in English?
4. **John 1 "the Word":** Segond's « la Parole » (used) or the traditional « le Verbe »?

6. **Segond's older words:** « sacrificateur » (priest), « centenier », « charité » (1 Cor 13), « païens » (Gentiles) — keep, or explain in a short note?
7. **Bilingual diagrams:** French/English (used), French only, or French/Spanish?
