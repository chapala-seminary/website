# CTSPent Unit 5 (Isaac, Jacob, and Joseph): French draft review

Checker: every Scripture quotation (lesson blocks, MC stems/options/why, fill prompts, SA models) was re-checked against the offline LSG 1910 text (tools/lsg.py): all verbatim, 0 corrections needed. Only change from LSG is the modern capital accents (l'Éternel, Ésaü, Égypte).

## Questions for Wayne

1. **Gen 25:23** NKJV "the older shall serve the younger" / LSG "le plus grand sera assujetti au plus petit". I quoted LSG in b011 and added "c'est-à-dire que l'aîné servirait le cadet". MC4 uses "L'aîné servirait le cadet".
2. **Gen 49:10** "until Shiloh comes" / LSG "Jusqu'à ce que vienne le Schilo" (with article). Fill 9's answer is "Schilo". I also accept Shiloh, Schiloh, Shilo and Silo.
3. **Gen 32:30** "my life is preserved" / LSG "mon âme a été sauvée". I kept LSG verbatim (b020). A Congolese reader could take it as salvation language. Is that OK, or should we add a gloss?
4. **Gen 41:38** "a man in whom is the Spirit of God" / LSG "ayant en lui l'esprit de Dieu" (lowercase). I kept LSG's lowercase.
5. **Gen 41:16** "an answer of peace" / LSG "une réponse favorable". **Gen 39:2** "he was a successful man" / "la prospérité l'accompagna". **Gen 39:21** "showed him mercy" / "il étendit sur lui sa bonté". I quoted LSG in each case.
6. **Gen 50:24** "God will surely visit you" / LSG "Dieu vous visitera" (no "surely"). This affects MC20 and SA10. I used LSG.
7. **Gen 50:20 and section 11 title.** The English title uses KJV wording ("Ye thought evil…"). I used LSG instead: "Vous aviez médité de me faire du mal : Dieu l'a changé en bien". Fill 10's answer is "bien".
8. **Gen 25:30** "Feed me, please" / LSG "Laisse-moi, je te prie, manger de ce roux". **Gen 37:19** "this dreamer" / "le faiseur de songes". **Gen 28:17** "How awesome" / "Que ce lieu est redoutable!" I quoted LSG in each case.
9. **Meaning of "Jacob".** I chose "supplanteur" for "supplanter". It is correct French but uncommon. Fill 2 also accepts trompeur, usurpateur and celui qui supplante.
10. **Gen 45:5** "to preserve life" / LSG "c'est pour vous sauver la vie". I quoted LSG (b028, MC19).

## Problems in the English source

1. **MC9 `why`:** it says "Seven for Leah, seven more for Rachel". The lesson (b017) says the first seven years were worked *for Rachel*, and Leah was substituted. I translated it as written.
2. **SA10 prompt:** it says Joseph "made the next generation swear", and the model answer says the same. The lesson never mentions the oath (Gen 50:25). The English keyword "will" is also trivial and matches almost any answer. In French I replaced it with serment/jurer/remonter.
3. **SA9 keywords** "romans", "harm" and "intent" are not taught in the lesson. Romans 8:28 appears only in the model answer.
4. **SA7 keywords** "purity" and "fear" are not taught in the lesson. The French model answer reaches 6 of 8 keyword groups without them.
5. **SA8 keywords** "without" and "two" are near-trivial. The prompt itself contains "two".
6. **MC13 `why`:** it says "couldn't even speak peaceably to him". This is true to Gen 37:4, but the lesson does not teach it.
7. **b008:** the servant is named Eliezer. Genesis 24 does not name him; the name is a traditional inference from Gen 15:2. Consider noting this.
8. **b013:** "getting something the right way by the wrong means" probably means "the right thing by the wrong means". I translated it as "une bonne chose par de mauvais moyens".
9. **b022:** says "Ishmaelite traders". Gen 37:28 also names Midianite merchants. This is minor, and I left it.
