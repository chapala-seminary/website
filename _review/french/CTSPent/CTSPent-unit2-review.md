# CTSPent Unit 2 (Adam, Eve, and the Fall): French draft review

## Questions for Wayne

1. **Genesis 2:7, "living soul" vs "living being".** The lesson (b006) and MC3 say Adam became "a living soul". The NKJV quotation (b007) and LSG both say "un être vivant". I kept "âme vivante" in the prose and added "(Louis Segond traduit : « un être vivant »)". MC3 and SA1 accept either wording.
2. **Psalm 70:5, *ezer*.** LSG renders *ezer* there as "mon aide" (Psaumes 70:5, same numbering as NKJV in LSG) and in Psaumes 33:20 as "notre secours", so the link to "aide" in Genèse 2:18 is less visible in French. The argument still stands.
3. **"Helper suitable" = "une aide semblable à lui" (LSG 2:18).** "Semblable" means "like", not "corresponding". I kept the lesson's explanation as "« Semblable » signifie ici correspondant et égal — face à face."
4. **"You will be like God" (3:5) = LSG "comme des dieux".** I quoted LSG verbatim in b016. Please confirm this is acceptable.
5. **"Sin is crouching at the door… you must master it" (4:7).** LSG reads "le péché se couche à la porte, et ses désirs se portent vers toi: mais toi, domine sur lui." I quoted it verbatim in b028 and MC16. The fill-in and short-answer items do not depend on the wording.
6. **"Bruise his head" vs "écrasera la tête" (3:15).** LSG says "crush" (écraser) for the head and "bruise" (blesser) for the heel. That fits the lesson's point better than the NKJV does, so I used "écraser" in the lesson, MC11 and SA5.
7. **Eve "deceived" = "séduite".** This follows LSG 3:13 ("Le serpent m'a séduite"). I used it in b016 and Fill 6, with "Adam ne fut pas séduit".
8. **"Federal head" = "chef représentatif de l'humanité (ce que les théologiens appellent le « chef fédéral »)".** I added the explanation because "chef fédéral" alone would be opaque to most Kinshasa readers.
9. **"To be celebrated within covenant" (b012) = "dans le cadre de l'alliance du mariage".** I added "du mariage" because "dans l'alliance" alone is unclear in French. Please confirm.
10. **Lamech's boast (b030).** I used the LSG wording of 4:24 ("Caïn sera vengé sept fois, Et Lémec soixante-dix-sept fois"). It matches the NKJV. Side note: the Spanish edition has "setenta veces siete", which is not RVG 4:24.
11. **Name forms.** I followed LSG spelling for names: Lémec, Énosch, Seth, Nod, Schinear (MC17). For Seth's meaning I wrote "« désigné » (ou « établi »)".

## Problems in the English source

1. **MC14: confusing stem.** The stem asks what Cain's *name* meant according to Eve. "Like God I have made a man" is not a meaning of the name Cain (which plays on *qanah*, "acquire"). It is the lesson's alternative reading of Eve's statement. I translated it faithfully; the stem should be reworded.
2. **SA3: the prompt alone passes the grader.** "Explain the three strategies of evil: subvert, pervert, invert" already hits 3 keyword groups. I reworded the French prompt so it does not name the three verbs.
3. **MC11 and SA5 vs the lesson.** These items say the Seed will "crush" the serpent's head, but b021 and b024 (NKJV) say "bruise". The French (LSG "écraser") removes the inconsistency.
4. **MC10 `why` vs the lesson.** The `why` says they "hid from each other and from God". The lesson says they were ill at ease with each other and hid from God. This is minor; I translated it as is.
5. **MC4 `why`.** It names the Hebrew word *da'at*, which the lesson never mentions. This is minor.
6. **SA4 keyword "legalism".** The lesson never uses this concept by name. This is harmless.
