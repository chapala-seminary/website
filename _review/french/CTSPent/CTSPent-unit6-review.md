# CTSPent Unit 6 (Exodus: Redemption and Deliverance), French draft review

## Questions for Wayne

1. **Exodus 3:5, "sandals" vs. LSG "souliers".** The quote uses LSG ("ôte tes souliers de tes pieds… une terre sainte"). The lesson (b016), MC6 option C and SA4 say "souliers (sandales)" so the link to "sandals" stays clear. Fill 4 asks for "sainte" (LSG), and accepts saint/sacrée/sacré.
2. **Exodus 3:14, "I AM WHO I AM" vs. LSG "Je suis celui qui suis".** I used LSG and kept *Ehyeh Asher Ehyeh* and YHWH (Yahweh). "Self-existence" has no natural single French word, so Fill 5 now reads "Dieu existe par ____ et qu'il est éternellement présent". The answer is "lui-même" (also accepted: soi-même, soi, lui seul). In MC7's `why`, "I will be who I will be" is rendered "Je serai qui je serai".
3. **1 Corinthians 5:7, "sacrificed" vs. LSG "immolé".** I quote LSG "Christ, notre Pâque, a été immolé" in b029, MC20 and Fill 10. The Fill 10 answer is "Pâque" (also accepted: agneau pascal, agneau de la Pâque).
4. **Exodus 2:10, "water" vs. LSG "eaux" (plural).** The Fill 2 answer is "eaux", and "eau" is also accepted.
5. **Exodus 12:6, "at twilight".** I used the LSG phrase "entre les deux soirs". It is accurate, but it may puzzle some readers.
6. **Third plague, "gnats".** LSG has "poux" (lice) in Exodus 8. In b024 I wrote "les poux, ou moustiques" so both readings show. Is that acceptable?
7. **"The first gospel" (b026).** I rendered it "le premier Évangile". In French theology, *protévangile* usually means Genesis 3:15, so I avoided that word. Should the French say "une première image de l'Évangile" instead?
8. **"God doesn't call the qualified; He qualifies the called."** This became "Dieu n'appelle pas ceux qui sont capables ; il rend capables ceux qu'il appelle". The Fill 6 answer is "rend capables" (also accepted: rend capable, qualifie, équipe, forme).
9. **Names follow LSG:** Schiphra and Pua, Séphora, Pithom and Ramsès, Madian, Jéthro, Horeb. The Egyptian gods use standard French forms: Hapi, Héqet, Seth, Rê, Nout, Hathor, Sekhmet. Note that the lesson writes "Re" but MC11's `why` and SA7's model say "Ra". I used "Rê" everywhere.

## Problems in the English source

1. **The order of Moses' objections contradicts itself.** b019 and MC8 make "Who am I?" the first objection. b021 lists five objections ("They won't believe me" first) and leaves out "Who am I?". The SA6 model lists yet another set: it includes "Who am I?" and drops "The people won't listen". b021's order also doesn't follow the text: the question about the name (3:13) comes before "they won't believe" (4:1). And "The people won't listen" is really part of 4:1, not a separate fifth objection. I translated each part as written.
2. **MC11 `why`** mentions "three days" of darkness, which the lesson never teaches.
3. **MC15 `why`** says "a male", which the lesson doesn't mention. The lesson says only "without blemish".
4. **SA1** keywords and model rely on "dynasty", which the lesson never mentions. **SA9** keyword "provisi" isn't taught either. I kept groups of the same size but chose French stems the lesson does support.
5. **b006 dating.** It gives ~1290 BC as the conservative date and 1450 BC as the alternative. Most conservative evangelical scholars hold the early date (~1446 BC, from 1 Kings 6:1), so the two may be the wrong way round.
6. **b024 plague-deity pairings.** These are popular reconstructions and contested; Isis appears three times, for example. A note such as "according to some scholars" might help. I translated as written.
7. **b029 "400 years of slavery".** Exodus 12:40 gives 430 years of sojourning. This is minor (it echoes Genesis 15:13).
