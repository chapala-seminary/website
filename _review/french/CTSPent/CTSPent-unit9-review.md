# CTSPent Unit 9 (Numbers) — French draft review

## Questions for Wayne

1. **LSG checked.** All quotations (Num 11:4-6, 11:14, 11:33; 12:2, 12:6-8, 12:13; 13:28, 13:30-31; 14:2-3, 14:7-9, 14:11-12, 14:16, 14:18, 14:22-23, 14:28-30; 16:3, 16:41; 21:5, 21:7-9; 24:17; Jacques 4:3; Jean 3:14-15; Ésaïe 7:14, 52:13; Malachie 3:1) were checked against the offline LSG 1910 text by the checker. Corrections are listed below.
2. **Num 12:1, "Cushite woman".** LSG says "femme éthiopienne". I used the LSG word and added "(c'est-à-dire de Cusch)".
3. **Num 12:8, "face to face".** LSG says "bouche à bouche … sans énigmes". I quoted LSG. The lesson's point (God spoke to Moses directly) still holds.
4. **Miriam.** LSG names her "Marie". I used "Marie (Miriam)" the first time and "Marie" after that.
5. **Num 24:17, "a Star shall come".** LSG says "Un astre sort de Jacob, Un sceptre s'élève d'Israël" (present tense, "astre" rather than "étoile"). Fill-in 9 uses the answer "astre" and also accepts "étoile". MC 18 option C uses the LSG wording.
6. **Jean 3:14-15.** LSG leaves out "should not perish" ("afin que quiconque croit en lui ait la vie éternelle"). I quoted LSG as it stands. MC 16 is not affected.
7. **Num 14:8-9.** LSG has "Si l'Éternel nous est favorable" for "delights in us" and "ils n'ont plus d'ombrage pour les couvrir" for "their protection has departed". I quoted LSG and marked omitted words with […].
8. **Num 16:3.** LSG has "C'en est assez !" where NKJV has "You take too much upon yourselves." I quoted LSG.
9. **Mixed multitude (Num 11:4).** LSG says "le ramassis de gens". I kept the lesson's explanation in parentheses after it.
10. **Censer and plague.** I used the LSG words "brasier" for censer and "plaie" for plague. These may sound old-fashioned in Kinshasa; "encensoir" and "fléau" are the alternatives.
11. **"Type of Christ".** I wrote "figure (type) de Christ" the first time and "figure" after that, which is the usual French evangelical term.
12. **MC 18 option A ("A virgin shall conceive").** LSG Ésaïe 7:14 reads "la jeune fille deviendra enceinte". I followed LSG, which makes the option less recognisable as a virgin-birth text. Should we use "la vierge" instead?
13. **"Tabernacle".** Where LSG has "tente d'assignation" (Num 12:4), I used it. Everywhere else I kept "tabernacle". Fill-in 2 accepts both.
14. **MC 18 options B and D** are now exact LSG wording: "Voici, mon serviteur prospérera" (Ésaïe 52:13; NKJV "will be exalted") and "Soudain entrera dans son temple le Seigneur" (Malachie 3:1). They are distractors, so the meaning shift does not affect the key.

## Problems in the English source

1. **MC 18** says "Balaam's **fourth** oracle", but the lesson never numbers the oracles and only says "three times Balaam tried to curse Israel". The item is correct (24:17 is in the fourth oracle), but the lesson doesn't teach this and the wording may confuse students.
2. **MC 6 `why`** says God "poured out His Spirit" on the seventy elders. The lesson doesn't mention the Spirit.
3. **MC 20 `why` and SA 6 model/keywords** ("honor God's holiness", "consequence"). The lesson only says Moses struck the rock "in anger … instead of speaking to it". It never mentions honouring God's holiness.
4. **b027** says "God told Balaam not to go … Balaam went anyway." This leaves out Num 22:20, where God allowed him to go if the men came to call him. It is an oversimplification.
5. **b011** gives the Num 11:4-6 complaint to the "mixed multitude" alone. In the text, the Israelites joined the weeping too (11:4b, 11:10).
6. **SA 2** keyword "even" is a generic word that matches almost any answer. SA 4 "did" and SA 6 "did" have the same problem. In French these groups now use content stems instead (SA 2 "esclav/servitude", SA 4 "veng/représaill", SA 6 "au lieu/désobé").
7. **MC 5** option C calls the vegetables "meat" ("Meat — fish, cucumbers, melons…"). I translated it as written.

## Adaptations

- "three feet high" (quail) became "près d'un mètre de hauteur", as in the Spanish edition.
- "programs, budgets, and buildings" became "programmes, argent et bâtiments".
- "logistical nightmare" became "un immense défi d'organisation".
- The US number format became the French one (603 550, 14 700).
