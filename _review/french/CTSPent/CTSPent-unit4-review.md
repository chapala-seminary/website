## Questions for Wayne

1. **LSG verified (checker).** Every quotation was checked against the offline LSG 1910 text (Gen 12:1-3, 12:5; 13:9, 14-15; 14:18-20, 23; 15:5-6; 16:9-10; 17:1, 19; 22:2, 5, 7-8, 14). Two were corrected: 12:1 "Va-t-en" (not "Va-t'en"), and 12:5, which the draft had paraphrased (see 4).
2. **"Be a blessing" (b006, MC2, SA1).** LSG Genèse 12:2 reads "tu seras une source de bénédiction". That is a future tense, which is the very reading the lesson argues against. I quoted LSG unchanged in b007. In b006 I give the command as « Sois une bénédiction » and say plainly that LSG renders it "tu seras une source de bénédiction". Please confirm that you are comfortable with the lesson correcting LSG openly.
3. **Genèse 15:6 (b022, MC15, Fill 5, SA5).** NKJV has "he believed in the Lord, and He accounted it to him for righteousness". LSG has "Abram eut confiance en l'Éternel, qui le lui imputa à justice", so the verb is "trusted" rather than "believed". The lesson still works, since the heading reads "la foi imputée à justice". Fill 5 now blanks "justice", and SA5 keywords accept both croire and confiance.
4. **"The people whom they had acquired" (b009, MC3, Fill 1).** LSG Genèse 12:5 reads "les serviteurs qu'ils avaient acquis à Charan". "Serviteurs" undercuts the lesson's point about proselytizing. The draft put a paraphrase in quotation marks; the checker now quotes LSG exactly and adds "(en hébreu, littéralement « les personnes »)". Please confirm.
5. **Other LSG wording.** Genèse 16:9 has "Retourne vers ta maîtresse, et humilie-toi sous sa main" for "Return and submit". Genèse 17:1 has "sois intègre" for "be blameless". Genèse 15:17 has "une fournaise fumante" for "a smoking oven", and I translated "flaming torch" as "des flammes" (LSG: "des flammes passèrent"). Genèse 22:13 has "un bélier retenu dans un buisson" for "a ram caught in a thicket".
6. **Divine names.** For El Roi, LSG Genèse 16:13 has "Atta-El-roï". I used *El Roï* and mentioned the LSG form (b025, Fill 7 also accepts it). For Jehovah Jireh, LSG 22:14 itself has "Abraham donna à ce lieu le nom de Jehova-Jiré" (the draft's note that LSG does not transliterate it was wrong). b029 now gives *Jehova-Jiré* as the name, with « L'Éternel pourvoira » as its meaning. Fill 9 accepts other spellings.
7. **Priest.** I used "sacrificateur", following LSG (Genèse 14:18, Hébreux 7), and not "prêtre".
8. **Melchisédek.** I used the LSG spelling.

## Problems in the English source

1. **MC17 "why".** It says Hagar was "the first person in Scripture to name God". The lesson never teaches this. I translated it as written.
2. **MC12 "why".** "Before any Levitical priesthood existed" is not in the lesson. This is minor.
3. **SA3 keywords.** "had" and "after" are filler words, not content. They are matched by almost any English answer. I replaced them with French content stems for "growth" (grandi/mûri) and "Égypte".
4. **SA6 keyword "years".** The SA6 model never mentions years, and the lesson does not tie them to "mistrust". I used "dix ans/attend" and added the ten-year detail to the French model.
5. **SA7 keywords "covenant" and "expanded".** The model does not mention either one. The French model mentions both.
6. **SA10 English model.** It omits "faith" and "pattern". The French model includes them.
7. **b027 Sarai/Sarah etymology.** The claim that "Sarai" means "my princess" and "Sarah" means a princess for all nations is a traditional explanation, but it is debated among Hebraists. I translated it faithfully.
8. **b029.** The lesson says "on that same mountain range" and gives "two thousand years" between Abraham and the cross. This is a common view, but the lesson presents it as fact. Not changed.
9. **b011.** NKJV 12:17 says "plagued Pharaoh and his house". The lesson says only "Pharaoh". This is fine.

No answer keys were found to be wrong.
