Checker verified every quotation (lesson, exam stems, options, `why`, prompts, models) against the offline LSG 1910 tool: Gen 5:24, 5:29, 6:2, 6:5, 6:8-9, 7:16, 7:24, 8:1, 8:21, 9:1, 9:6, 11:4, 12:2, Jean 10:9, Héb 11:6, 2 Pi 2:5. All match LSG exactly; none needed correction.

## Questions for Wayne

1. **"Translated" (Enoch; b008, fill 1, SA 1).** I followed LSG Heb 11:5 and used "enlevé / enlèvement". Fill 1's answer is "enlevé", and it also accepts transporté, emporté, pris. Note that in French evangelical usage "l'enlèvement" usually means the Rapture. The word is LSG's own, but please confirm it doesn't seem to tilt the course on eschatology.
2. **Nephilim (b011, fill 2).** LSG Gen 6:4 has "les géants". I kept "Néphilim, « ceux qui sont tombés »" and added "(Louis Segond traduit « les géants »)". Fill 2 also accepts "géants".
3. **Gen 6:5.** NKJV has "every intent of the thoughts of his heart was only evil continually". I quoted LSG: "toutes les pensées de leur cœur se portaient chaque jour uniquement vers le mal".
4. **Gen 6:6, "God grieved".** LSG has "l'Éternel se repentit… et il fut affligé en son cœur". I used "fut affligé" and left out "se repentit".
5. **Gen 6:9, "just man, perfect in his generations".** I quoted LSG "juste et intègre dans son temps".
6. **Gen 7:24, "prevailed 150 days".** LSG says "les eaux furent grosses". I used that wording in b017 and in the MC 9 stem.
7. **Gen 8:21, "soothing aroma".** I quoted LSG "odeur agréable".
8. **Gen 9:13, "bow in the cloud".** LSG has "mon arc dans la nue". French "arc" / "arc-en-ciel" keeps the warrior's-bow wordplay naturally (fill 7 answer: "arc").
9. **Gen 11:4, "whose top is in the heavens… lest we be scattered abroad".** I quoted LSG "dont le sommet touche au ciel… afin que nous ne soyons pas dispersés". MC 15 and MC 16 use this wording.
10. **Gen 12:2, "I will make your name great".** I used LSG "je rendrai ton nom grand" (MC 17, SA 9).
11. **2 Pet 2:5 and Heb 11:6.** I used "ce prédicateur de la justice" (MC 7) and "sans la foi il est impossible de lui être agréable" (b008).
12. **Righteousness.** I rendered it as "justice" throughout, as LSG does (e.g., "Noé était un homme juste").

## Problems in the English source

1. **b015.** It says Noah preached "for 120 years (2 Peter 2:5)". 2 Pet 2:5 calls Noah a preacher but gives no time span. The 120 years comes from Gen 6:3, which many read as a limit on human lifespan, not a preaching period. MC 7's `why` cites Gen 6:3 correctly. "Eight people believed him" is also odd, because the eight were Noah and his own family.
2. **SA 7 model.** It calls the Canaanites "Semitic peoples in Palestine". The lesson (b024) is more careful: they spoke a Semitic language, but Genesis 10 traces them to Ham. I translated the model faithfully.
3. **SA 4.** The model and keywords rely on "Sinai", "design" and "sacrifice", which the lesson never mentions. The lesson only says "Levitical law".
4. **SA 5 model.** "survival came from God, not from his own labor — gratitude" is not taught in b019, though it is a fair inference.
5. **MC 5 `why`.** "the flood reset that ground" is not in the lesson.
6. **MC 3.** It grades the angelic reading of "sons of God" as the only correct answer, and offers "The line of Seth only" as a distractor. The Sethite view is held by many evangelicals. The lesson does say "whatever the precise interpretation", but the exam does not allow for that.
7. **b028 and MC 19.** Babel/balal is a Hebrew wordplay in Gen 11:9. The name's actual etymology is Akkadian *bāb-ili*, "gate of god", which is close to MC 19's distractor "House of god".
8. **b028.** It says "God would later tell Abraham", but in Gen 12:2 he is still Abram. I translated it as written.
9. **b008.** It cites Hebrews 11:5 for "without faith it is impossible to please Him", but that quotation is Heb 11:6. I kept the English reference as written.
10. **SA 2, 7, 9, 10.** Each English prompt reaches 3 or more of its own keywords by itself, so a student could pass by copying the question. I worded the French prompts so that none reaches 3. SA 2 and SA 10 were reworded more than the others.
