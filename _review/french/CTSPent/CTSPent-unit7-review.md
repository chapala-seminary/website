## Questions for Wayne

1. **Exodus 14:13 (b009, MC3, Fill 2).** NKJV says "see the **salvation** of the Lord", but LSG says "regardez la **délivrance** que l'Éternel va vous accorder". I quoted LSG. The Fill 2 answer is "délivrance", and "salut" is accepted. LSG's "restez en place" stands where NKJV has "Stand still".
2. **Exodus 14:14 (b009).** NKJV has "you shall hold your peace" and LSG has "gardez le silence". I used LSG. Nothing in the exam depends on it.
3. **Exodus 14:25 (b009).** The lesson says God "caused their chariot wheels to swerve". LSG (like the NKJV) says "il ôta les roues de leurs chars". I followed LSG.
4. **Exodus 15:25 (b012, MC5, Fill 3).** NKJV has "a **tree**" and LSG has "un **bois**". I wrote "un bois (un arbre)" so the lesson matches both, and MC5 reads "Un bois (un arbre)".
5. **Exodus 32:6 (b029).** For "rose up to play", LSG has "se levèrent pour se divertir". I kept the lesson's remark that this is a euphemism for sexual immorality, attached to LSG's wording. Please confirm you want that interpretation stated as fact.
6. **Exodus 32:14 (b029).** NKJV has "The Lord relented" and LSG has "l'Éternel se repentit". I didn't quote it. I paraphrased it as "L'Éternel renonça au mal qu'il avait annoncé contre son peuple" to avoid suggesting that God repents of sin. OK?
7. **Hebrews 8:5 (b027).** NKJV has "shadow of heavenly things" and LSG has "image et ombre des choses célestes". I quoted only the part "ombre des choses célestes".
8. **Exodus 34:6-7 (b031).** I quoted LSG up to "qui ne tient point le coupable pour innocent", which is where the English quotation ends. LSG says "conserve son amour jusqu'à mille générations" where NKJV has "keeping mercy for thousands".
9. **Terms.** I used LSG's own words for the tabernacle and its service: "autel d'airain", "cuve", "pains de proposition", "chandelier", "autel des parfums", "propitiatoire", "souverain sacrificateur", "jour des expiations". I added "(de bronze)" once. The tablets are "les tables". The staff is "verge", with "(son bâton)" added once. I kept the divine names "Yahweh Rapha" and "Yahweh Nissi" as the lesson has them. Exodus 17:15 is quoted from LSG: "l'Éternel ma bannière".

## Problems in the English source

1. **MC9.** The correct answer ("It bred worms and stank") is never taught. The lesson says only that manna could not be stored overnight. I translated it with LSG's wording from Exodus 16:20.
2. **SA10.** The keywords and model answer depend on the Passover lamb and the blood on the doorposts. This unit mentions the Passover only once, in passing, and never mentions the lamb or the blood. The model also uses "propitiation", which the lesson never uses.
3. **SA4.** Two keywords ("type" and "life") are not taught in the lesson. The model answer is fine.
4. **b009.** The lesson says Pharaoh "hardened his heart". Exodus 14:4 and 14:8 say that **the Lord** hardened Pharaoh's heart. I translated it as written.
5. **b029.** The lesson says Aaron himself said "This is your god, O Israel". In Exodus 32:4, "they said" it. The French follows the English but uses LSG's words.
6. **b009.** The "swerve" wording does not match the NKJV that the lesson otherwise quotes (see Question 3).
7. **MC20 `why`.** It says Moses "offered his own salvation". The lesson says only "blot me out of Your book". This is a theological gloss, not something the lesson teaches. I translated it faithfully.
