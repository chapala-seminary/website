# CTSPent Unit 12 (Covenant Faithfulness — A Capstone): French review notes

Every quotation was checked with the offline LSG 1910 tool.

## Questions for Wayne

1. **Genesis 3:15 (b007, b006, b009, b040, b028, MC2, SA2).** NKJV has "her Seed… He shall bruise your head." LSG has "sa postérité: celle-ci t'écrasera la tête," with no capital letter and no masculine "He." The lesson's own verb "crush" matches LSG's "écraser" well. I used "postérité" for "Seed/seed" throughout, including "the Seed of the woman" in b028 and the "Seed" on the covenant chart.
2. **Genesis 15:6 (b011, MC4, Fill 3).** LSG reads "Abram eut confiance en l'Éternel, qui le lui imputa à justice." I kept MC4 option C as "En croyant Dieu" and quoted LSG in the `why`. The lesson prose keeps "Abraham a cru… le lui a imputé à justice."
3. **Deuteronomy 30:19-20 (b021).** In NKJV verse 20 ends with "for He is your life." LSG goes on: "car de cela dépendent ta vie et la prolongation de tes jours… Abraham, Isaac et Jacob." I quoted the full LSG verse because cutting it off mid-sentence would read badly.
4. **"The Lamb who was slain is the light of the city" (b026).** This alludes to Revelation 21:23, where LSG says "l'agneau est son flambeau," so I wrote "le flambeau de la ville."
5. **Romans 3:25 (MC16 `why`).** "Christ is our propitiation" became "Christ est notre victime propitiatoire," which follows LSG's wording.
6. **"in seed form" (b006, Fill 2).** This is rendered "l'Évangile en germe," which is the natural French idiom. The Fill 2 answer is "germe," and it also accepts semence, graine, germes and "forme de semence."
7. **"The Lord saves" (b020, MC9, MC18, Fill 8, SA7).** This is rendered "l'Éternel sauve" to stay consistent with LSG's divine name. I kept the transliteration "Yeshua" as it stands.
8. **Covenant chart (b117).** The three systems are translated as "théologie de l'alliance, dispensationalisme, théologie progressive des alliances." The French reader should confirm the third term, since no standard French equivalent exists.
9. **Title "A Capstone" (b003, b029).** I translated it as "Synthèse finale."
10. **SA7 prompt reworded.** A literal translation that named Yeshua, Josué and Jésus would score 3 keyword hits from the prompt alone. The new prompt is "Que signifie le nom de Josué, et en quoi ce nom annonce-t-il le Sauveur ?" The meaning is unchanged.
11. **The blessing in b028.** Its allusions are paraphrased, as they are in the English. Deuteronomy 6:5 is put into the plural "votre" so that it speaks to the student. It is not presented as a direct quotation.

## Problems in the English source

1. **SA6.** Nothing in this unit teaches why Moses did not enter the land (striking the rock twice, Meribah, holiness). The lesson only says that he died outside the land (b020, b026). The keyword "twice" does not appear in the English model answer either.
2. **MC11 and SA5 (Melchizedek).** The lesson only says that he "prefigures Jesus' eternal priesthood (Hebrews 7)." It never mentions King of Salem, God Most High, righteousness/peace, bread and wine, or tithes.
3. **MC13.** The lesson never teaches the tearing of the veil from top to bottom. It only says the veil "pointed to the barrier of sin that only Christ could tear down."
4. **SA3 and SA4.** "Unblemished," "firstborn," "applied," and "Jesus became a curse for us" (Galatians 3:13) are not in the lesson.
5. **MC9 and MC18** test the same fact (Yeshua means "The Lord saves").
6. **MC3.** The stem asks "What event…" but the answer is an object (the Passover lamb). The French says "Quel élément."
7. **Fill 4** cites Hebrews 6:13, which the lesson never mentions. The answer itself ("oath") is taught in b011.
8. **Factual detail (b020, MC9).** Joshua's Hebrew name is properly Yehoshua. Yeshua is the later short form (Nehemiah 8:17). This is minor, and I translated it as written.
