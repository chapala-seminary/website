# CTSPent Unit 10 (Deuteronomy — Covenant Renewal): French review notes

Checker verified every quotation against the offline LSG 1910 text (Deut 1:1-3; 6:4-7; 8:11-14, 18; 18:18; 28:1-2; 34:4, 10-12; Gal 3:24; Luke 22:19 / 1 Cor 11:24; John 6:14). All match exactly; one paraphrase presented as a quotation (MC9) was corrected.

## Questions for Wayne

1. **Deut 6:4 (b009, b010).** NKJV has "the Lord is one!" and LSG has "l'Éternel, notre Dieu, est le seul Éternel." The lesson's point about monotheism and exclusive loyalty still works, so I quoted LSG unchanged.
2. **"Yahweh" in b009** ("loyalty to Yahweh"). The lesson does not discuss the name itself, so I wrote "l'Éternel" to match LSG. I did the same in the SA1 model answer.
3. **"God's words" in b009** (the Shema demands that God's words be in the heart). LSG 6:6 says "ces commandements," so the French prose says "les commandements de Dieu" to match the quotation.
4. **Deut 6:8-9 (b009).** I used LSG's words: "fronteaux entre tes yeux" (frontlets, which the lesson calls phylacteries) and "poteaux de ta maison" (doorposts). For mezuzahs I used the normal French plural "mezouzot." Fill 4 accepts mezouza, mezouzas, mezuzah, mezuzot and mezuzahs.
5. **Deut 8:11-14 (b014).** The NKJV wording is replaced by the LSG clauses in the same order. "Your heart be lifted up" becomes "ton cœur ne s'enfle" (your heart swells). SA2 keyword group 7 therefore uses "enfl" / "élev" in place of "lifted."
6. **Deut 8:18 (b015, MC12 option D).** LSG reads "c'est lui qui te donnera de la force pour les acquérir." The word "les" points back to "richesses" in verse 17, so on its own the quote does not mention wealth. I added "[les richesses]" in brackets. Please confirm that is acceptable.
7. **Gal 3:24 (b027, b028, MC16, Fill 10, SA8).** NKJV has "tutor" and LSG has "comme un pédagogue." Fill 10's answer is "pédagogue," and it also accepts tuteur, précepteur, gardien and surveillant. The b027 heading now uses "pédagogue."
8. **Gerizim.** LSG spells it "Garizim" and spells Ebal "Ébal." I used the LSG spellings everywhere. SA9 accepts both "garizim" and "gerizim."
9. **Deut 28:1-2 (b018).** NKJV has "set you high above all nations" and "overtake you." LSG has "te donnera la supériorité" and "seront ton partage." I quoted LSG, and nothing in the exam depends on the NKJV wording.
10. **Meribah.** LSG spells it "Meriba" (Nombres 20:13), and I used that spelling.
11. **SA4 prompt reworded.** The literal French prompt ("prophète semblable à Moïse… Jésus") scored 3 keyword hits by itself, so a student could pass by copying the question. I rephrased it as "Deutéronome 18 annonce un prophète à venir. En quoi cette promesse désigne-t-elle le Christ ?" The meaning is unchanged.

12. **MC9 option A.** The English puts a paraphrase in quotation marks ("I will raise up a prophet like Moses"). The French now quotes LSG Deut 18:18 ("Je leur susciterai […] un prophète comme toi") and adds "(comme Moïse)" so the option stays clear. The correct answer is unchanged.

## Problems in the English source

1. **MC10.** Stephen (option D) is also a correct answer to "Who quoted Deuteronomy 18 in the NT to identify Jesus as that Prophet?" The lesson itself says "Stephen cited it in Acts 7:37." The key (Peter) is defensible because of "(Acts 3)," but the item is ambiguous. The `why` mentions "Solomon's Porch," which the lesson never teaches.
2. **MC14 / SA6.** The answer "struck the rock twice … instead of speaking to it" and the SA6 model ("in anger… dishonoring God's holiness") go beyond the lesson. The lesson only says "his sin at Meribah" (b025) and "struck the rock twice" (b020).
3. **b010 label.** The heading says "Deuteronomy 6:4-9," but the quotation stops at verse 7. In French I labelled it 6:4-7.
4. **b006 factual claim.** "The most quoted Old Testament book in the New Testament" is doubtful. Most counts put Psalms and Isaiah ahead of Deuteronomy. The claim that Jesus quoted it the most is more widely held. I translated it as written.
5. **MC17 `why`.** It cites Leviticus 19:18, which the lesson does not mention. This is minor.
