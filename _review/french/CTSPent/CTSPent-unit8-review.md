# CTSPent Unit 8 (Leviticus) — French draft review

## Questions for Wayne

1. **"If" vs "when" (b016).** The lesson says the Hebrew "if" in Lev 4:2 is better read "when". LSG already says "Lorsque quelqu'un péchera involontairement". So I reworded it to say that some versions have "if", that "when" is the better reading, and that Segond reads it that way. (LSG 4:27, quoted in b017, does say "Si".)
2. **"Salt of the covenant" (b011, MC6).** LSG Lev 2:13 says "signe de l'alliance de ton Dieu", not "sel de l'alliance". I quoted LSG and adjusted the MC6 stem to match. The answer (salt) is unchanged.
3. **"Soothing aroma" (b009).** I used the LSG wording "d'une agréable odeur à l'Éternel" and added the reference Lev 1:9.
4. **Matthew 5:23-24 (b019, MC13).** I quoted the LSG text in full: "laisse là ton offrande devant l'autel, et va d'abord te réconcilier avec ton frère". The MC13 option uses LSG 5:24.
5. **Hebrews 9:12 (b022).** LSG reads "il est entré une fois pour toutes dans le lieu très saint, non avec le sang des boucs et des veaux, mais avec son propre sang…". I quoted the whole verse, including the clause that the English leaves out.
6. **1 Cor 5:7 (b030).** The English has "was sacrificed for us" with no reference. LSG has "Christ, notre Pâque, a été immolé", without "for us". I quoted LSG and added the reference.
7. **Scapegoat (b021, fill 8).** LSG says "bouc vivant" and "pour Azazel", not "bouc émissaire". I used the common term "bouc émissaire", added a short note on the LSG terms, and accepted "bouc vivant" and "Azazel" in fill 8.
8. **Names for the offerings.** LSG names them differently from the lesson. LSG uses "sacrifice d'actions de grâces" for the peace offering, "sacrifice d'expiation" for the sin offering, and "offrande" for the grain offering. I used "sacrifice de paix", "sacrifice pour le péché" and "offrande de grain" because they follow the lesson's headings. In b014 I noted the LSG name for the peace offering. Is that acceptable?
9. **"Communion" (b030).** I rendered it "Sainte Cène", the usual Protestant term in Kinshasa.

## Problems in the English source

1. **MC19 vs b019, factual.** The lesson puts the doves and flour rules (Lev 5:1-13) under the guilt offering. MC19 and b011 call the flour a sin offering. The Hebrew text, and LSG 5:11 ("offrande d'expiation"), treat 5:1-13 mainly as a sin offering. The lesson's labelling is inconsistent.
2. **SA1.** The model answer brings in "High Priest", and the keywords include "hebrews" and "system". Section 1 of the lesson teaches neither in connection with this question. The lesson also says "around 40" references, where the model says "40".
3. **SA6.** The prompt asks for "two things beyond the sacrifice". The lesson's two things are confession and restitution. The model gives restitution and the added 20% instead. My French keywords allow for both readings.
4. **SA10.** The model and keywords rely on "veil", "direct access" and "never repeated". The lesson does not teach these. It quotes only Heb 9:12 and 10:4.
5. **SA8.** The keyword "presenc" (presence) is not taught in the lesson.
6. **MC8 `why`.** It calls the fat "the best portion", but the lesson never says this. The lesson also says "lobe of the liver", not "liver".
7. **The SA models are terse fragments,** not 2–5 sentences. I translated them as complete sentences without adding content.
8. **SA10 keywords are weak (English too).** "christ", "sacrifi", "blood" and "repetit" are generic: a wrong answer such as "Christ's sacrifice must always be repeated with blood" scores 4 hits in both languages. Consider replacing "christ"/"sacrifi" with stems the lesson teaches (e.g. "lieu très saint"/"Holy of Holies").

## Checker's changes

- Scripture: all 14 quotations/allusions (Lev 1:1-2, 1:9, 2:13, 4:2, 4:27-28, 16:21-22; Rom 5:20; Matt 5:23-24; Heb 9:12, 10:4; 1 Cor 5:7; MC13, MC6, MC20) checked against the offline LSG; all verbatim, no corrections needed.
- MC4 and MC6 stems: reworded into natural French questions (meaning unchanged).
- b006, b011, b027, b029: small wording fixes ("Moïse en est l'auteur"; clearer "tenir lieu de sacrifice pour le péché"; "avait été emporté" for "était parti"; "vous vous rendez compte" for anglicism "réaliser").
- SA1: dropped 4-letter "cité"/"cite" stems (matched "citer", "société"); now "référen"/"cité dans"/"mentionn".
- SA6: replaced "rendre/rendu" (matched "rendre gloire", "se rendre") with "restaur"/"rembours"/"ce qui avait été pris".
- SA7: replaced bare "peine" (matched "à peine") with "peine du"/"la peine".

## Adaptations

- "Communion" became "Sainte Cène". "Middle class" became "gens de condition moyenne". "Fine flour" became "fleur de farine (farine fine)", using the LSG term with a gloss on first use.
- Hebrew-word fill-ins (zebah, shalom): I added the French spelling variants "zevach" and "chalom".
- The Lev 1:9 and 1 Cor 5:7 references were added so readers can find the quotations.
