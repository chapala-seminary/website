# CTSPent Unit 11 (Christ in the Pentateuch — Typology): French review notes

All Scripture was checked with the offline LSG 1910 tool.

## Questions for Wayne

1. **Rom 5:14 (b009, MC3, Fill 3, SA2).** NKJV has "a type of Him who was to come" and LSG has "la figure de celui qui devait venir." The prose keeps "type (en grec *typos*)" and quotes LSG. The MC3 stem says "la « figure » (le type)." SA2 keywords accept both "type" and "figure."
2. **Col 2:17 (b006, b007, Fill 2).** NKJV has "the substance is of Christ" and LSG has "le corps est en Christ." "Shadow" is "ombre" in both, so Fill 2 (answer "ombre") still works.
3. **Isa 45:22 (b022).** NKJV has "Look to Me, and be saved" and LSG has "Tournez-vous vers moi." The quote no longer repeats the word "look" from b021. The idea (turn your eyes to him) still fits, but the verbal link is weaker.
4. **John 3:14-15 (b021, MC14).** LSG has no "should not perish but," so I quoted it as it stands. Num 21 vocabulary follows LSG: "serpents brûlants," "serpent d'airain," "perche." SA7 and SA8 keywords accept "perche" and "airain"/"bronze."
5. **Gen 22 (b014, MC6, Fill 5, SA4).** LSG has "pays de Morija," not "Mount Moriah," and "un bélier retenu dans un buisson par les cornes" for "a ram caught in a thicket." I used "le mont Morija." Fill 5 accepts Moriah and Moria.
6. **1 Cor 5:7 (b017, MC8, Fill 6).** LSG reads "Christ, notre Pâque, a été immolé" and has no "for us." Fill 6's answer is "Pâque," and it also accepts agneau pascal and Pâques.
7. **Ps 110:4 (b011).** LSG has "à la manière de Melchisédek" where NKJV has "according to the order of." I quoted LSG and kept "selon l'ordre d'Aaron" for the contrast. The name uses the LSG spelling "Melchisédek." In the MC4 `why`, "Zedek" became "tsédek."
8. **Heb 8:5 (b024).** NKJV has "copy and shadow" and LSG has "image et ombre des choses célestes." **Heb 1:3 (b026):** "radiance" is LSG's "reflet." **Rom 3:25 (MC16 why):** "propitiation" is LSG's "victime propitiatoire."
9. **1 Cor 10:4 (b019, MC11).** LSG uses "rocher" where NKJV has "Rock." I used "rocher" throughout, including the heading "Le rocher qui les suivait."
10. **Ex 33:11 (b026).** The English says "Moses spoke with God face to face." I followed LSG: "L'Éternel parlait avec Moïse face à face."
11. **Offering names (b028).** I used the terms from earlier French units: holocauste, offrande de grain, sacrifice de paix, sacrifice pour le péché, sacrifice de culpabilité, Jour des expiations, bouc émissaire. LSG itself has "sacrifice d'actions de grâces" for the peace offering and "bouc pour Azazel" for the scapegoat. Fill 10 also accepts "bouc pour Azazel."
12. **SA2 prompt reworded.** A literal translation ("Expliquez comment Adam est un type du Christ") scored 3 keyword hits by itself. The new prompt asks for "le parallèle que Paul établit en Romains 5 entre Adam et « celui qui devait venir »." The meaning is the same.
13. **Gen 3:15 (b029).** "Seed of the woman" became "la postérité de la femme," following LSG. "Lord's Supper/communion" is "la sainte cène."

## Problems in the English source

1. **MC19.** Option D ("One for the Lord, one for Azazel") is also biblically correct (Lev 16:8). It is a weak distractor that could confuse a careful student. The key (A) is fine.
2. **MC9.** The lesson never teaches "male, one year old." It says only "without blemish." Ex 12:5 supports the key, but students cannot learn it from the unit.
3. **MC15.** "Torn in two from top to bottom" is not in the lesson, which says only "the veil was torn." The other options make the answer easy, but the item is not fully taught.
4. **SA9 model.** "Performing signs" and "will be cut off" are not in the lesson. **SA10:** Hebrews 10:1 and "annual reminders" are not in the lesson either.
5. **b014 / MC6 factual claim.** The lesson says Moriah is "almost certainly the same hill where… Jesus was crucified." 2 Chr 3:1 links Moriah to the temple, but identifying Golgotha with the same hill is speculative. The MC6 `why` softens this to "the same mountain range." I translated both as written.
6. **b011 logic.** "Unlike Levitical priests who offered sacrifices for their own sins first, Melchizedek has no recorded beginning or end" sets two unrelated points against each other (compare Heb 7:3 and 7:27). I translated it as written.
7. **b028.** The lesson links the grain offering to "firstfruits," but Lev 2 is mainly the grain/gift offering, and firstfruits are only one kind of it. This is minor.
