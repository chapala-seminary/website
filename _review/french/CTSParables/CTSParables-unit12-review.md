## Questions for Wayne

1. **Luke 10:31-32, "passed by on the other side."** Segond has only "passa outre" (with no "other side"). The lesson (b016, b029, b030), Fill 3, MC17-18 and SA8 rely on the image. I quoted Segond and added one sentence in b016: "le texte grec dit littéralement qu'ils « passèrent du côté opposé »". Elsewhere I used "passer outre". Fill 3 answer: "outre". It also accepts "de l'autre côté", "son chemin", "à côté".
2. **Luke 10:32, "came and looked."** Segond: "l'ayant vu" (no "came"). The lesson says the Levite "went a little further". The same Greek note in b016 ("« vint et vit »") covers it.
3. **Luke 10:37, "Go and do likewise" / "showed mercy."** Segond: "Va, et toi, fais de même" and "C'est celui qui a exercé la miséricorde envers lui". Used throughout. Fill 8 answer "de même" (accepts "même", "pareillement", "ainsi"…). Fill 7 answer "miséricorde".
4. **Luke 10:25, "lawyer."** Segond: "docteur de la loi". Used everywhere. In b010 I translated "in typical lawyer fashion" as "En vrai juriste".
5. **1 John 3:17, "shuts up his heart."** Segond: "il lui ferme ses entrailles". In b027 I added one sentence explaining that "entrailles" means the seat of compassion (the heart). MC16 `why` uses "ferme ses entrailles".
6. **Malachi 3:8, "rob God."** Segond: "tromper Dieu". b013 quotes Segond's "dans les dîmes et les offrandes" and adds "le texte hébreu de Malachie dit littéralement « voler » Dieu."
7. **Luke 10:27 vs Deut 6:5.** b009 quotes the lawyer's answer from Luke 10:27 (Segond), not Deut 6:5, because only Luke has "pensée". The references cited are unchanged.
8. **Mount Gerizim** → Segond "Garizim" (Fill 4 answer; it also accepts "Gerizim").
9. **b017, "touch a dead body".** I added "se rendre impurs" (from Segond's Lév 21:1, "ne se rendra point impur") so that the SA3 keyword "unclean/impur" is taught in the lesson. Please confirm.

## Problems in the English source

1. **SA3 keyword "unclean":** the English lesson never uses the word (b017 says only "forbade priests to touch a dead body"). This is handled through Q9 above.
2. **Fill accept_en lists include wrong answers:** Fill 2 "conscious"; Fill 3 "way", "path"; Fill 5 "vinegar", "ointment"; Fill 10 "mission field". None of these were carried into French.
3. **b018, Martin of Tours:** in Sulpicius Severus's account, Jesus speaks to the angels around him ("Martin… clothed me with this garment"); no angel asks Him. I translated it as written.
4. **b035:** the "Christ has no body but yours" prayer is not found in Teresa of Ávila's writings. The lesson's "traditionally attributed" covers this, and I kept it.
