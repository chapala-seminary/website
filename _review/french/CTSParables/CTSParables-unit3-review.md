# CTSParables Unit 3 (The Potter's House): French draft review

## Questions for Wayne

1. **Jeremiah 18:3, "the wheel".** Segond has "un tour". In b012 I wrote that the Hebrew word for "tour" literally means "les deux pierres". MC5 and Fill 3 follow this.
2. **Jeremiah 18:4, "marred".** Segond has "Le vase qu'il faisait ne réussit pas". In b013 I added that the Hebrew literally says the vessel "fut gâté". Fill 4 quotes Segond, so the answer is **réussit** (it also accepts réussissait / réussi). MC6 and b036 use "ne réussit pas" / "gâté".
3. **Jeremiah 18:8, 10, "relent / reconsider".** Segond has "Je me repens du mal… / du bien". In b017 I added one sentence saying the Hebrew verb means God turns from the announced disaster, not that he had done wrong. MC7 and Fill 5 use Segond. Fill 5's answer is **repens**, and it also accepts "ravise". Please confirm this added sentence is acceptable.
4. **Jeremiah 18:12, "That is hopeless! So we will walk according to our own plans".** Segond has "C'est en vain! Car nous suivrons nos pensées". In b019 I added "c'est-à-dire nos propres projets". MC8 and Fill 6 use Segond. Fill 6's answer is **vain**.
5. **Isaiah 64:8, "we are the clay, and You our potter".** Segond has "c'est toi qui nous as formés". In b021 I added that the Hebrew literally says "toi, notre potier". This affects MC10 (option and why), Fill 7 (answer: argile) and b036.
6. **Isaiah 29:16, "He did not make me".** Segond has "Pour que l'ouvrage dise de l'ouvrier: Il ne m'a point fait". I rephrased the sentence in b021 ("comme si l'ouvrage pouvait dire de l'ouvrier").
7. **Romans 9:21–22.** For "dishonor" Segond has "un usage vil", and for "much longsuffering" it has "une grande patience". This affects b022, MC11 and SA6. SA6 accepts both "patience" and "longanimité".
8. **2 Timothy 2:21, "if anyone cleanses himself".** Segond has "se conserve pur". In b023 I added that the Greek literally says "se purifie".
9. **Luke 22:32, "when you have returned to Me".** Segond has "quand tu seras converti". In b025 I added that the Greek literally says "quand tu seras revenu".
10. **Daniel 5:27, "found wanting".** Segond has "trouvé léger". I used Segond's name, Belschatsar, in b026, MC16 and SA8. SA8 also accepts Balthazar.
11. **Numbers 20:10.** Segond has "Est-ce de ce rocher que <em>nous</em> vous ferons sortir de l'eau?". I kept your emphasis on "we" with the same `<em>` tag.
12. **Hebrews 12:6, "chastens".** Segond has "châtie". Fill 9's answer is **châtie**, and it also accepts discipline, corrige, reprend and éduque.
13. **"Enacted parable".** I translated it as "parabole en action", and Fill 2 also accepts "en actes", "mimée" and "jouée". The French Unit 1 must use the same term.
14. **Jeremiah 19:2.** Segond names the valley "Ben-Hinnom". I used this name in b030 and SA10. SA10 also accepts "Hinnom".

## Problems in the English source

1. **Fill 3, accept_en "wheels".** "The two wheels" is not a correct answer for the Hebrew "two stones". I did not carry it over. Fill 7's "dust" and Fill 1's "craftsman" are also loose alternates. I did not carry those over either.
2. **b021.** The quotation "He did not make me" is from Isaiah 29:16 only. Isaiah 45:9 makes the same point in different words, so citing both verses together is slightly misleading.
3. **MC9 and MC11 `why`.** Both explain the Reformed view, which is the opposite of the correct option. They do not explain why the keyed answer is right. I translated them faithfully.
4. **SA4.** The keywords "relent" and "repent" are one idea in Segond ("je me repens"). In French I made the first group "malheur/désastre/du mal" and the second "repen".
5. **Self-passing prompts.** The SA1 prompt contains several of its own keywords (enacted, potter, clay). I reworded the French prompt. No French prompt reaches more than 2 hits, and every model hits 8 of 8.
