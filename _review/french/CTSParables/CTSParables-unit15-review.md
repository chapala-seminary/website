# CTSParables Unit 15 — French draft review notes

## Questions for Wayne

1. **WiseSpeak terms in French.** No French WiseSpeak vocabulary exists yet, so I chose: probing question = "question d'approfondissement"; keyword = "mot clé"; subject = "sujet"; idea = "idée"; objective = "objectif"; arrangement = "disposition"; preaching analysis = "analyse en vue de la prédication". Because "sujet" is the technical term for *subject*, I rendered the idea's "general topic" as "thème général" so the two do not blur. The course title is "WiseSpeak : des outils simples pour prêcher". Please confirm or supply your preferred terms, since they will recur in the WiseSpeak course.
2. **The eight interrogatives.** French has no one-to-one set. I used: qui, quel, que (quoi), pourquoi, quand, où, comment, combien. "Which" = "quel"; "what" = "que (quoi)"; "how much" = "combien". The French sample questions use "quel(le)s" more often than the English uses "which" (e.g. Sower, "Quelles réponses…"), because that is natural French. Fill3's answer is "mots interrogatifs" (also accepts "interrogatifs").
3. **Keyword "oversights".** Rendered "oublis", which keeps the link to the four "Il a oublié de…" divisions. Fill6 accepts oubli, omissions, négligences, manquements.
4. **"People will…"** The objective formula is "Les gens vont…". Fill5's blank had to move to "gens" (as the Spanish did, "gente"); accepts personnes, auditeurs, fidèles.
5. **Luke 12:15, "covetousness".** Segond has "avarice". The idea is rendered "l'avarice" throughout; SA keywords accept "convoitise" too.
6. **Luke 12:21, "rich toward God".** Segond: "riche pour Dieu". Used throughout (b032 objective, Fill7).
7. **Luke 12:17–19, "crops", "barns".** Segond: "ma récolte" (singular), "greniers". b024 lists "ma récolte, mes greniers, mes biens, mon âme".
8. **Luke 12:20, "this night" / 12:19 "many years".** Segond: "cette nuit même", "plusieurs années". Quoted as such.
9. **James 4:13–15.** Segond: "nous trafiquerons, et nous gagnerons!" and "Si Dieu le veut" (NKJV "If the Lord wills"). Quoted Segond.
10. **Psalm 90:12.** Segond: "Enseigne-nous à bien compter nos jours, Afin que nous appliquions notre coeur à la sagesse." Fill8 answer "jours".
11. **Closing "He who has ears…"** Quoted Segond Matthieu 13:9 / Marc 4:9 ("Que celui qui a des oreilles pour entendre entende.").
12. **Title "Rich Man, Poor Soul"** → "Homme riche, âme pauvre". OK?

## Problems in the English source

- **MC10 `why`** ("It is difficult to regain lost attention") and **MC11 `why`** ("like the tail wagging the dog") give reasons the lesson never states. Translated; MC11's idiom was rendered by sense ("l'accessoire prend la place de l'essentiel").
- **b020:** "a parable usually makes one main point for each [main character]" is accurate to Unit 1, but "the sharecroppers, the servants, the son, and the settlement" refers to the wicked tenants outline, which is not among the outlines in b035. Minor.
- **Fill2 / Fill4 wording:** "insures" should be "ensures" (b017 also). Translated by meaning.
- **Fill5 English alternates** "shall/should" — "should" changes the meaning (an objective states what people will do). Not carried over.
- **SA2:** the English keywords are the eight interrogatives themselves, which the prompt asks the student to list; in English, "what", "how", "which" are also everyday words, so almost any answer passes. French groups use the *descriptions* (personnes, types/sortes, étapes, raisons, moments, lieux, procédés, quantité) plus some interrogatives.
- **SA10 model** uses Mark 4, while b035 does not name a text; harmless.
