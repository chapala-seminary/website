## Questions for Wayne

1. **Isa 5:1 "a song of my Beloved".** LSG has "Le cantique de mon bien-aimé". The phrase "love song" is not in it, and the lesson depends on that idea. I quoted LSG in b009 and added: "Le texte hébreu parle littéralement du « chant de mon bien-aimé », c'est-à-dire d'un chant d'amour." Elsewhere the prose says "chant d'amour" (MC2, SA1).
2. **Isa 5:2/5:4 "wild grapes".** LSG says only "mauvais" (raisins). The quotations use "mauvais". b013 explains *be'ushim* and adds that the Hebrew means literally wild or stinking grapes. Section VI is titled "Les mauvais raisins nommés". Fill 2's answer is *mauvais*, and it also accepts *sauvages*, *aigres*, *pourris* and *acides*.
3. **Isa 5:2 "the choicest vine" / "a very fruitful hill".** LSG has "un plant délicieux" / "un coteau fertile". I used both in b011, MC4, Fill 1 (answer *fertile*) and SA2.
4. **Isa 5:7 word order (most important).** NKJV pairs "justice → oppression" and "righteousness → cry for help". LSG pairs "**droiture** → sang versé" and "**justice** → cris de détresse". This means LSG's "justice" stands for *tsedaqah* and not *mishpat*. In b024 I followed LSG: *mishpat* = "la droiture (le droit, la justice rendue aux autres)" and *tsedaqah* = "la justice". MC14 and MC15 follow the same pairing. Fill 6's answer is *droiture*, and it also accepts *justice*, *droit* and *équité*. Fill 7 became "Dieu attendait la justice, mais il a entendu des ____ de détresse" (answer *cris*). Please confirm.
5. **Isa 5:6 "command the clouds".** LSG has "je donnerai mes ordres aux nuées". Fill 5's answer is *nuées*, and it also accepts *nuée* and *nuages*.
6. **Isa 5:18 "as if with a cart rope".** LSG has "comme avec les traits d'un char". I quoted that in b027 and used it in SA7.
7. **Hos 10:1.** The lesson says "a luxuriant vine that bears fruit for itself". LSG says the opposite in tone: "une vigne féconde, Qui rendait beaucoup de fruits". I quoted "une vigne féconde" and added that the Hebrew says it bore fruit for itself. Please check whether this note is acceptable or should be removed.
8. **Jer 2:21 "noble vine".** LSG has "une vigne excellente". **John 15:5 "vine/branches".** LSG has "cep/sarments". Fill 9's answer is *cep*, and it also accepts *vrai cep*, *vigne*, *cep de vigne* and *pied de vigne*. **Gal 6:7.** LSG has "Ce qu'un homme aura semé". Fill 10's answer is *semé*. **Gen 12:2 "you shall be a blessing".** LSG has "tu seras une source de bénédiction".
9. **Isa 5:3 "judge, please".** LSG has "Soyez juges entre moi et ma vigne". MC7 uses "Être juges".

## Problems in the English source

1. **Fill 8:** `accept_en` includes "Judah". Isa 5:7 says the vineyard is the *house of Israel*, and the men of Judah are the plant. I accepted *Jacob* but not *Juda*. Fill 5: "sky/heavens" are loose alternates, so I did not carry *cieux* over.
2. **b030 Hos 10:1:** "luxuriant vine" follows a different version's sense than NKJV ("empty vine") and LSG ("vigne féconde"). See Question 7.
3. **b024:** the transliteration "mispach" is unusual; the more common forms are *mispah* or *mishpach*. I kept it as written.
