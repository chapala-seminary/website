## Questions for Wayne

1. **"Unforgivable forgiveness" (title, b006 and throughout, MC1, Fill 1, SA1).** A literal « pardon impardonnable » sounds self-contradictory in French. Like the Spanish edition, I used **« le pardon de l'impardonnable »**. Fill 1 answer: **impardonnable**.
2. **"Enacted parable" (b006, MC2, SA1).** I translated it as « parabole en actes ».
3. **Hosea 1:2, "a wife of harlotry" (b011, b012, MC5, SA3).** Segond has "une femme prostituée", which seems to settle the interpretive question that b012 leaves open. I quoted Segond and added "le texte hébreu dit littéralement « une femme de prostitutions »". I used that literal phrase in b012 and MC5.
4. **Hosea 8:7, "whirlwind" (b017, MC9, Fill 5, SA5).** Segond has "ils moissonneront la tempête". I added a short gloss: the Hebrew means a violent wind, a whirlwind. Fill 5 answer: **tempête**. It also accepts tourbillon, ouragan and orage.
5. **Hosea 2:15, "a door of hope" (b019, MC10, Fill 6, SA6).** Segond has "comme une porte d'espérance". Fill 6 answer: **espérance**. It also accepts espoir. Segond's spellings are Acor and Acan; SA6 also accepts "achor".
6. **Hosea 2:19–20, "I will betroth you to Me" (b020, SA6).** Segond has "Je serai ton fiancé… par la justice, la droiture, la grâce et la miséricorde… par la fidélité, et tu reconnaîtras l'Éternel". I added a gloss: "je te fiancerai à moi pour toujours".
7. **Hosea 2:23 (b020, MC11).** Segond names the children ("Lo-Ruchama… Lo-Ammi: Tu es mon peuple! et il répondra: Mon Dieu!") instead of NKJV's "her who had not obtained mercy… those who were not My people". I added one sentence that spells out the reversal of the names.
8. **Hosea 3:1, "Go again" (b003, b022, MC12, Fill 7, SA7).** Segond has "Va encore". Fill 7 answer: **encore**. It also accepts de nouveau, à nouveau and une fois de plus.
9. **Hosea 3:2, "one and one-half homers of barley" (b024, MC13, Fill 8).** Segond has "un homer d'orge et un léthec d'orge". I added: "Un léthec valait un demi-homer : Osée a donc payé un homer et demi d'orge." Fill 8 answer: **orge**.
10. **Hosea 6:6, "mercy" (b032, MC19, Fill 10, SA10).** Segond has "j'aime la **piété** et non les sacrifices". Segond's Matthew 9:13 and 12:7, however, have "Je prends plaisir à la miséricorde". In b032 I quoted both and added that the Hebrew word means faithful kindness, or mercy. Fill 10 answer: **piété**. It also accepts miséricorde, bonté, amour and compassion. The heading of section VII keeps "La miséricorde, et non les sacrifices".
11. **Hosea 14:4, "I will heal their backsliding" (b027, SA9).** Segond has "Je réparerai leur infidélité". I added a gloss: the Hebrew says literally "je guérirai leur égarement". In b028 the English word "backslide" is rendered « rétrograder » (with « s'égarer » as the everyday equivalent). MC16 uses « égarement ».
12. **Matthew 18:22, "seventy times seven" (b029, MC17, Fill 9).** Segond has "septante fois sept fois". I added "c'est-à-dire soixante-dix fois sept fois", because "septante" is unfamiliar in Kinshasa. The Fill 9 prompt says "soixante-dix fois ____ fois". Answer: **sept**.
13. **Hosea 11:8 (b027, MC15).** Segond has "Que ferai-je de toi, Éphraïm? Dois-je te livrer, Israël?… Mon coeur s'agite au dedans de moi". This fits the lesson well.
14. **Names (b013, SA4).** I used Segond's spellings: Jizreel, Lo-Ruchama, Lo-Ammi. At first mention I added "(Jezreel)". SA4 keywords also accept "jezreel" and "ruhama". The "bore *him* a son" point works in Segond as well: 1:3 has "lui enfanta un fils", while 1:6 and 1:8 have no "lui".

## Problems in the English source

1. **The English SA1 prompt passes on its own keywords** ("unforgivable", "forgiv", "enacted"). SA8 hits 2 ("prodigal", "grace"). I reworded the French prompts for SA1, SA5 and SA8. No French prompt scores more than 1 hit.
2. **MC19 `why` and the SA10 model** say the Pharisees "would have *left* Gomer on the auction block". b032 says "*kept*". The meaning is the same, so there is no action needed. I translated both as « laissé ».
3. **MC5 `why`** does not explain why the correct option (a temple priestess) is wrong. It only restates the shared point. This is minor.
4. I found no wrong answer keys. Facts checked: Dahmer (17 victims, 16 life terms, baptized in prison), Margot Honecker (26 years as education minister), Holmer at Lobetal, and Exodus 21:32 (thirty shekels). All are consistent with the lesson.
