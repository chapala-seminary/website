# CTSParables Unit 4 — French draft review

## Questions for Wayne

1. **Amos 7:7–8, "plumb line" (b003, b012, b014, MC6, Fill 5, SA2, SA10).** Segond has "un niveau" (a level), not "fil à plomb". I kept "fil à plomb" as the lesson's image and unit title (as in Unit 1), quoted Segond exactly, and in b012 added that Segond's "niveau" is the mason's tool, traditionally understood as a plumb line. b014 mentions Segond's "niveau" next to "fil à plomb". SA keywords accept both "plomb" and "niveau".
2. **Amos 7:8, "I will not pass by them anymore" (b003, b012, b036, MC6, MC19, Fill 5, SA2, SA9).** Segond has "Je ne lui pardonnerai plus" (I will no longer forgive him). I quoted Segond everywhere and added in b012: the Hebrew literally says "je ne passerai plus à côté de lui", that is, "je ne fermerai plus les yeux". Fill 5 answer is **pardonnerai**; it also accepts "passerai" and "épargnerai".
3. **Amos 7:3, 6, "the LORD relented" (b011, MC5, Fill 4, SA2).** Segond has "L'Éternel se repentit de cela". I quoted it and added that the Hebrew means the LORD gave up the announced punishment, so readers do not think God had sinned. Fill 4 answer is **repentit** (also accepts "ravisa", "désista", "retint"). MC5's correct option reads "s'est repenti du châtiment annoncé". Is that wording all right?
4. **Amos 5:24, "Let justice run down like water, and righteousness…" (b022, MC11, Fill 6).** Segond reverses the nouns: "que la **droiture** soit comme un courant d'eau, Et la **justice** comme un torrent". I quoted Segond and added a note that the Hebrew word rendered "droiture" means the right/justice given to the poor. Fill 6 answer is **droiture**; it also accepts "justice", "droit" and "équité". Isaiah 28:17 has the same reversal ("de la droiture une règle, Et de la justice un niveau"); I quoted it as is.
5. **Amos 7:14, "sheepbreeder and a tender of sycamore fruit" (b008, MC2, Fill 1, SA1).** Segond has "berger, et je cultive des sycomores". I added a sentence that the sycamore is a kind of fig tree whose figs Amos tended. Fill 1 answer is **sycomores**; it also accepts "figuiers", "figues" and so on.
6. **Amos 2:6, "pair of sandals" (b009, b022, MC4, Fill 3, SA1).** Segond has "une paire de souliers". I used "souliers" throughout. Fill 3 also accepts "sandales" and "chaussures".
7. **Amos 5:12, "took bribes."** Segond has "vous recevez des présents"; I added "c'est-à-dire des pots-de-vin".
8. **Amos 9:11 / Acts 15:16, "tabernacle of David" (b037, MC20, SA9).** Segond has "la maison de David" in Amos and "la tente de David" in Acts. I quoted Amos as Segond, added that the Hebrew word means a hut or tent, and quoted Acts 15:16 as well. MC20's correct option reads "la maison (la tente) de David".
9. **Amos 8:1–2, "summer fruit."** Segond has only "une corbeille de fruits". I added that the Hebrew word means the ripe fruit of late summer, so the qayits/qets wordplay still makes sense.
10. **John 12:43, "praise of men" (b027, SA6).** Segond has "la gloire des hommes". I quoted it and added that the Greek word also means "louange". SA6 accepts both.
11. **Acts 10:34, "God shows no partiality" (b033, MC17, SA7).** Segond has "Dieu ne fait point acception de personnes". I quoted it and glossed it ("c'est-à-dire qu'il ne favorise personne").
12. **2 Kings 21:13, "plummet of the house of Ahab."** Segond has "le niveau de la maison d'Achab". Quoted as is.
13. **"Ushers who point to the throne" (b028).** There is no common French word for church ushers in this sense. I wrote "comme le protocole qui conduit les gens jusqu'au trône", using the word Congolese churches use for the people who seat the congregation. Please check that it reads well.
14. **Rick Warren's book.** I used the French edition's title, *Une vie motivée par l'essentiel*, and translated "It's not about you" as "Il ne s'agit pas de vous". Redman's song title stays in English with a French gloss.

## Problems in the English source

2. **SA2 and SA9 keywords "not pass by":** they depend on NKJV wording that Segond lacks. The French groups use "pardonnera / passera / passerai" instead.
