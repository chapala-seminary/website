## Questions for Wayne

1. **Luke 15:2, "sinners".** LSG has "gens de mauvaise vie" ("people of bad life"), not "pécheurs". Quoted verbatim in b006. Fill 1 answer is "gens de mauvaise vie"; "pécheurs/pécheur" also accepted. Elsewhere (MC1, MC16, b024) the lesson's own sentences use "pécheurs", since 15:7 and 15:10 in LSG do say "pécheur".
2. **Luke 15:17, "came to himself" / "hired servants".** LSG: "Étant rentré en lui-même… Combien de mercenaires chez mon père…". Fill 6 answer is "rentré" (also "revenu", "retourné"). "Mercenaire" is old-fashioned and today suggests a hired soldier, so in b017 I added one sentence: "Le mot « mercenaire » désigne ici un ouvrier payé à la journée", and the lesson's own sentences then say "ouvrier".
3. **Luke 15:20, "ran and fell on his neck".** LSG: "il courut se jeter à son cou et le baisa". Fill 7 answer is "courut" (also "a couru", "accourut", etc.).
4. **Luke 15:22, "sandals".** LSG has "souliers". The point that servants went barefoot still works; I used "souliers" in b018 and MC12.
5. **Luke 15:29–30, "I have been serving you" / "this son of yours".** LSG: "je te sers" and "ton fils" (no "this"). Because b023 relies on "serving" meaning slavery, I added (convention 1): "Le verbe grec employé ici signifie littéralement « servir comme un esclave »." MC15 now quotes "ton fils". In 15:31 LSG says "Mon enfant" rather than "Son".
6. **Luke 15:30, "harlots".** LSG has "prostituées"; quoted as is.
7. **Title of the third parable.** French tradition calls it "l'enfant prodigue"; I used that, with "le fils perdu" as the heading (matching the English heading). The lost coin is "la drachme perdue" (LSG word).
8. **b031 "willfulness".** Rendered "obstination" (stubborn self-will). SA10 also accepts "volonté propre". Please have the French reader confirm.

## Problems in the English source

1. **MC4 why** says the explorers got lost "within a quarter mile of home"; b009 says a restaurant a quarter mile from the Royal Geographical Society, not home. French says "de leur point de départ" to match the lesson.
2. **MC20** says Mrs. Jones forgave Bertram "before he could finish apologizing", but b029 never has Bertram start an apology ("you don't have to say a word"). Translated faithfully.
3. **SA9 model** calls Old Man Jones Mrs. Jones's husband; b029 implies but never states this. Kept in French.
4. **SA3 English prompt** hits three of its own keywords ("plan", "do not know", "joy"), so it passes by copying the question. Per convention 10, French groups were replaced with distinct lesson ideas; every French prompt reaches at most 1 hit, and every model hits all 8.
5. **b005 Orientation** (the Ark of the Covenant) is never connected to the lesson. Translated as is.

## Adaptations

- "a quarter mile" became "environ quatre cents mètres"; "586 BC" became "586 av. J.-C."
- "at least one famous Hollywood film" became "au moins un film célèbre".
- Added "aux États-Unis" after Indiana and Arkansas.
- "pumping gas" became "servait de l'essence dans une station-service"; "veterans' hospital" became "hôpital des anciens combattants"; "house shoe" became "pantoufle".
- "All hell will freeze over before I would do that!" became "Plutôt mourir que de faire une chose pareille !" (a natural French refusal; the literal "L'enfer gèlera" is not idiomatic French).
- Dollar amounts kept.
