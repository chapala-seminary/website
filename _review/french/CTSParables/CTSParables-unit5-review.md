## Questions for Wayne

1. **2 S 12:7 "You are the man!"** → LSG "Tu es cet homme-là!" (b003, b004, b017, b041, MC, SA4). b017 keeps "four of the most powerful words"; the French also has four words (Tu / es / cet / homme-là). Fill 1 answer: "homme".
2. **"Ewe lamb"** → LSG "petite brebis" (12:3). Unit title: "La petite brebis". Fill 2 answer: "brebis" (also accepts "agnelle" and "agneau").
3. **"Restore fourfold" (12:6)** → LSG "il rendra quatre brebis". Fill 4 now blanks the LSG wording: "Il rendra ____ brebis", answer "quatre" (also accepts "quadruple" and "quatre fois"). The "fourfold" echoes (b020, b035) use "quatre brebis" / "quatre pour un". Exodus 22:1 (LSG: "quatre agneaux pour l'agneau") is paraphrased, not quoted. Luke 19:8 is "je lui rends le quadruple" (Fill 10 answer "quadruple").
4. **2 S 12:8 "I also would have given you much more!"** → LSG "Et si cela eût été peu, j'y aurais encore ajouté". It is weaker, so following convention 1, I added one sentence in b018 giving the Hebrew sense ("encore autant et autant"). MC8 quotes the LSG wording.
5. **"A man after God's own heart" (b006).** The English gives no reference. I quoted 1 Samuel 13:14 LSG, "un homme selon son coeur", and added the reference. Please confirm.
6. **Ps 51:4 "Against You, You only, have I sinned"** → LSG "J'ai péché contre toi seul" (b030, MC14, Fill 8, SA7). The repeated "You" is lost, but the point the lesson makes still stands.
7. **Ps 32:3 "my bones grew old"** → LSG "mes os se consumaient". I added the Hebrew sense ("se sont usés", i.e. grew old) in b030. "When I kept silent" → "Tant que je me suis tu", so Fill 9 answer is "tu" (also accepts "tue", "resté silencieux", "silencieux" and "taisais"). Ps 32:5 "You forgave the iniquity of my sin" → LSG "tu as effacé la peine de mon péché".
8. **2 S 12:13 "The LORD also has put away your sin"** → LSG "L'Éternel pardonne ton péché".
9. **Other LSG wording:** Luke 18:13 "be merciful to me" → "sois apaisé envers moi"; Luke 15:17 "came to himself" → "étant rentré en lui-même" ("in the pigpen" → "au milieu des pourceaux"); Gen 3:12 "whom You gave to be with me" → "que tu as mise auprès de moi" (MC12 follows LSG).
10. **Preaching outline (b037, MC20, SA10).** "Sorry" → "attristé". I did not use "repentant" because step 4, "turned" → "s'en détourna", already carries the idea of repentance. Please confirm.
