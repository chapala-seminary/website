## Questions for Wayne

1. **Mark 4:3, "Listen! Behold, a sower…"** LSG has no "Behold": « Écoutez. Un semeur sortit pour semer. » I quoted LSG in b003, b004 and b007. Nothing in the lesson depends on "Behold".
2. **Mark 4:16–17, "endure only for a time".** LSG: « ils manquent de persistance ». I quoted LSG in b011 and kept the lesson's point about shallow roots.
3. **Mark 4:24, "and to you who hear, more will be given".** LSG has only « et on y ajoutera pour vous ». It does not include "to you who hear", because Segond did not follow the Greek manuscripts that have it. In b021 I quoted LSG and added one sentence saying that some Greek manuscripts add « à vous qui entendez » here. The lesson's point is unchanged. Fill 7 uses LSG's « Prenez garde à ce que vous entendez ».
4. **1 Corinthians 3:6, "God gave the increase".** LSG: « Dieu a fait croître ». Fill 8 answer is « croître ». It also accepts croissance, grandir and pousser. At the end of b024 I added « C'est Dieu qui donne la croissance » so that the noun appears in the lesson.
5. **Mark 4:28, "blade / head / full grain".** LSG: « l'herbe… l'épi… le grain tout formé dans l'épi ». I used LSG in b023, b025, MC18, fill 9 and SA8. Mark 4:29 "sickle" is « faucille » (fill 10).
6. **Isaiah 32:20.** LSG: « Heureux vous qui partout semez le long des eaux ». This is close to the NKJV, so I used it as it is.
7. **"Foxhole conversions" (b011).** French has no set phrase for this. I wrote « conversions de tranchée » and added a short explanation: conversions made under the threat of danger.

## Problems in the English source

1. **MC5 `why` and SA2 (model and keyword "mustard")** speak of the student's "mustard seed of faith". The lesson never mentions a mustard seed. I translated them faithfully. A student cannot get the SA2 "moutarde" keyword from the lesson.
2. **SA10 keywords** "wayside", "stony", "tribulation" and "good ground" are NKJV words from Mark 4:15–20 that the lesson does not quote, and the lesson never mentions "tribulation". In French I used the LSG words (chemin, pierreux, tribulation/persécution/épreuve, bonne terre). Students who read the verses can answer it.
3. **MC20 `why`** states as fact that farm fields were on the hillsides. The lesson (b007) says only that they were "probably" visible there. This is minor.
4. **b011.** "Three days in blindness, prayer, and fasting (Acts 9:9)". Acts 9:9 mentions the blindness and fasting. The prayer is in 9:11. I kept the reference as it is.
