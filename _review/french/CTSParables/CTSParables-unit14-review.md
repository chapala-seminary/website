## Questions for Wayne

1. **James 1:22, "doers of the word".** LSG has "Mettez en pratique la parole, et ne vous bornez pas à l'écouter...", with no noun "doers". b011, MC5 and Fill 3 rely on it. I quoted LSG and added in b011: "Le texte grec dit littéralement : « Devenez des faiseurs de la parole, et non pas seulement des auditeurs. »" Fill 3 is built on LSG ("Mettez en ____ la parole"), so the answer is "pratique" (also accepted: œuvre, application, action).
2. **1 Corinthians 4:2, "stewards".** LSG has "dispensateurs". b017 calls the servants stewards. I used "intendants" in the prose, quoted LSG, and added one sentence that Segond's "dispensateurs" are exactly such stewards. Fill 7 uses the LSG wording; the answer "fidèle" is unchanged.
3. **Matthew 25:21, 23, "I will make you ruler over many things".** LSG has "je te confierai beaucoup" ("I will entrust you with much"). Nothing in the lesson depends on "ruler", so I quoted LSG as it stands (b003, b020).
4. **Matthew 7:27–29.** "Great was its fall" is LSG "sa ruine a été grande". "Astonished at His teaching" is LSG "la foule fut frappée de sa doctrine". I used both verbatim in b009 and b013.
5. **Matthew 25:29.** LSG adds "et il sera dans l'abondance". I quoted the whole verse in b022. Mark 4:25 in LSG lacks that phrase, but the echo still works.
6. **Matthew 25:15, "according to his own ability".** LSG has "selon sa capacité". "Capacité" is the Fill 5 answer, with aptitude, habileté, compétence and moyens also accepted.
7. **"The least of these" (b015).** The English quotes it with no reference. I quoted LSG 25:40, "l'un de ces plus petits", and added "(25:40)". Please confirm.
8. **Wadi.** I wrote "oued (ou wadi)". "Oued" is the usual French word. Fill 2 answer: oued; also accepted: wadi, ouadi, wady.
9. **b023, "fall away through unbelief".** I wrote "tomber par l'incrédulité", and MC18's why says "un avertissement contre le risque de tomber". I kept the three views evenly weighted, as the English does. Please have the French reader confirm the tone.
10. **"Stewardship".** I used "gestion fidèle" in b017 and MC12, and "intendance" in the SA6 prompt. Please say which term you prefer course-wide.

## Problems in the English source

1. **b019 (Barings).** Leeson's losses were about £827 million, roughly US$1.3–1.4 billion, so "nearly a billion dollars" understates them. I translated it as written ("près d'un milliard de dollars").
2. **b015** quotes "the least of these" without a reference (see Q7).
3. **SA keywords (convention 10).** Several English groups are generic or not distinctive ideas: SA1 "given"; SA7 "same"; SA8 "never", "views"; SA10 "not how much". The English SA2 prompt also contains its own keyword "wadi". In French I used 8 distinct ideas from the lesson, such as "mêmes paroles", "jamais connu", "propriétaire" and "tentation". Each French model hits 8/8. Each French prompt hits at most 2. I reworded the SA6 prompt a little so it does not give away "compte" twice.
4. **Fill 1 / Fill 3 alternates.** "followers" (Fill 3) and "practitioners" are not really correct for "doers", so I did not carry them over. The French alternates follow LSG instead.
