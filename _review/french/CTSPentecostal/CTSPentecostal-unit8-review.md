# CTSPentecostal Unit 8 (Baptism in the Holy Spirit): French draft review

## Questions for Wayne

1. **1 Corinthians 12:13 (b024, b025, MC3, SA1, SA5).** NKJV has "by one Spirit we were all baptized into one body". LSG has "baptisés dans un seul Esprit, pour former un seul corps". The lesson's argument depends on "by"/"into", so following convention 1 I added one sentence at the start of b025: "Le texte grec dit littéralement « par (ou dans) un seul Esprit… baptisés en un seul corps »." MC3 quotes LSG.
2. **Acts 1:5 (b008, b022, fill 2).** LSG drops "truly" and has "baptisé d'eau" ("with water"). Fill 2 quotes LSG with the blank as "d'____". The answer is "eau", and "d'eau" and "de l'eau" are also accepted.
3. **Acts 15:9 (b034, fill 4).** NKJV has "purifying their hearts by faith", and LSG has "ayant purifié leurs coeurs par la foi". For "cleanses the heart" (b033, fill 4) I used "purifie", which matches LSG and MC6's "purifying". Fill 4 also accepts "nettoie", "sanctifie" and "lave".
4. **"Filling(s)" (b037, b041, fill 5, SA).** Following Eph 5:18 ("remplis de l'Esprit"), I translated this as "remplissage(s)". The Eph 5:18 present tense is given as "continuez à être remplis" (fill 7).
5. **"Empowerment".** I used "revêtement de puissance", from Luke 24:49 LSG "revêtus de la puissance d'en haut" and common in francophone Pentecostal usage. I also used "la puissance que donne l'Esprit". "Initial evidence" became "signe initial"/"preuve initiale" (the usual French Pentecostal terms). "Second blessing" became "seconde bénédiction".
6. **View names.** These are "Pentecôtiste classique", "Wesleyenne de sainteté", "conversion-initiation" (kept as a technical term, in quotation marks) and "Un seul baptême, plusieurs remplissages". Please confirm "conversion-initiation" can stay untranslated.
7. **"Finished work" (b063, fill 10).** I used "œuvre accomplie". Fill 10 also accepts "achevée", "consommée", "complète" and "terminée".

## Problems in the English source

1. **MC count.** The source has only 7 MC items, while the standard is 20. b085 ("0 / 7") matches 7. I translated all 7.
2. **b031: wrong reference.** "Told already-believing disciples to wait in Jerusalem for power from on high (Acts 1:8)": the waiting command is Acts 1:4 and Luke 24:49 ("power from on high"). Acts 1:8 is the promise of power. I kept "Actes 1:8".
3. **b030 / SA6.** "Paul asked the Ephesians" refers to some disciples at Ephesus (Acts 19:1-2), not the Ephesian church. I translated it as written.
4. **SA1, SA2, SA3: English prompts pass their own keywords.** SA1 hits conversion/baptism/spirit; SA2 hits baptism/body/filling/repeated; SA3 hits pentecostal/holiness/universal/treasure. The French keyword groups are rebuilt so that the prompts do not pass (convention 10).
5. **SA4–SA10: brittle English keywords.** Several English keywords are long exact phrases or references ("not truly received", "read them so differently", "not a second baptism", "acts 1:5"). The French uses stems and alternatives instead.
6. **b021.** John the Baptist's words are quoted with no reference. I added "(Matthieu 3:11)".

## Adaptations

- No US-specific examples in this unit. The footer (b084) adds "en français, de la Louis Segond 1910", and b007 names the LSG instead of the NKJV. The navigation labels are translated.
- "Description or prescription" (b046): I added a short gloss, "(une norme pour tous)", for readers without formal training. Fill 8 also accepts "norme", "normatif" and "prescriptif".
