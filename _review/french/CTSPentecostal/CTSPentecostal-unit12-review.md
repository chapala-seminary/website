# CTSPentecostal Unit 12 (A Biblical Assessment of the Movement): French review notes

Every quotation was checked with the offline LSG 1910 tool, and rechecked by the checker: all verbatim. The quotations are John 16:14, 1 Cor 12:7, 1 Thess 5:21, Eph 4:3 and Rom 14:5, plus the allusions Rom 8:16 and 1 Thess 5:20.

## Questions for Wayne

1. **"Decline" → "refuser" (in headings, prose, MC4, SA1-2 and SA8).** I chose "refuser" over "rejeter" because it is softer, and it carries the English sense of a reasoned "no" without contempt. The French reader should confirm that the tone is no cooler than the English.
2. **1 Corinthians 12:7 (b023, Fill 4).** Where NKJV has "for the profit of all," LSG has "pour l'utilité commune." The Fill 4 answer is "utilité," and it also accepts profit, bien, avantage and bien commun.
3. **Ephesians 4:3 (b043, Fill 10).** LSG prints "l'unité de l'esprit" with a lowercase e, while NKJV has "Spirit." I quoted LSG exactly. The lesson does not depend on the capital.
4. **Romans 14 (b033, Fill 8).** The NKJV phrase "fully persuaded in his own mind" is quoted from LSG 14:5 as « en son esprit une pleine conviction ». For "disputable matters" I wrote "questions discutables." LSG 14:1 has "opinions," so Fill 8 accepts discutables/débattues/contestées/disputées/secondaires.
5. **"Evidence" of Spirit-baptism (b027, MC4, Fill 6).** I rendered this as "signe obligatoire et universel," because unit 7's French uses "signe initial" for "initial evidence." Fill 6 also accepts preuve, marque and signe initial (the checker removed "évidence", which in French means "obviousness", not "proof").
6. **"Tares" (b017, Fill 2).** I used Segond's "ivraie" (Matt 13). Fill 2 also accepts mauvaise(s) herbe(s) and zizanie.
7. **"A distinct and later empowering" (b032, SA3).** I wrote "un revêtement de puissance distinct et ultérieur," which echoes LSG Luke 24:49 ("revêtus de la puissance d'en haut"). I avoided "seconde bénédiction" so as not to tilt toward one view.
8. **"Millennium" (b019, Fill 3).** I used "millénium," as the rest of the French edition does. Fill 3 also accepts millénaire and règne de mille ans.

## Problems in the English source

1. **b024 (minor factual framing).** The lesson says roughly 644 million people were "brought to name the Lord Jesus Christ" by the movement. Unit 1 presents the same figure as the movement's total size, not as a count of converts. I translated it as written.

No wrong answer keys, misplaced `why`s or untaught exam items were found.
