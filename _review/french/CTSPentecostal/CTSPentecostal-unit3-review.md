# CTSPentecostal Unit 3 (Pentecost): French draft review

## Questions for Wayne

1. **Acts 4:31, "boldness" (b038, b039, MC6, Fill 9).** Segond has "avec assurance". I quoted Segond, and following convention 1 I added one sentence to b039: the Greek word means literally boldness, a courageous freedom of speech. Fill 9 takes "assurance" as the answer and also accepts "hardiesse", "audace" and "courage".
2. **John 7:39, "the Spirit was not given until Jesus was glorified" (b046, Fill 10, SA10).** Segond reads "l'Esprit n'était pas encore, parce que Jésus n'avait pas encore été glorifié". It has no word for "given", and the NKJV's "given" is itself an addition. I quoted only the Segond clause "parce que Jésus n'avait pas encore été glorifié" and wrote "n'avait pas encore été donné" as plain prose outside the quotation marks. Fill 10 takes "glorifié" and also accepts "exalté" and "élevé".
3. **"Helper" (b016, b046; John 14:26, 16:7).** Segond has "consolateur", written in lowercase. I followed Segond.
4. **Acts 1:4, "the Promise of the Father" (MC1 why).** I used Segond's "ce que le Père avait promis".
5. **Acts 2:41, "about three thousand souls were added" (b033, Fill 7).** Segond has "le nombre des disciples s'augmenta d'environ trois mille âmes". The lesson paraphrases rather than quotes, so I kept "furent ajoutées" without quotation marks. Fill 7 depends only on the number.
6. **Terms.** "Spirit-empowered" / "empowerment" became "revêtue de la puissance de l'Esprit" / "revêtement de puissance", to match Unit 8 and Luke 24:49 in Segond. "Fillings" became "remplissages" (also as in Unit 8). For the two readings I used "position inaugurale" and "position du modèle", and gave "paradigm" as an accepted keyword in SA3. Please confirm that "position du modèle" reads naturally to a Congolese Pentecostal reader as a fair name for that view, and not as a dismissive one.
7. **Track label (b062, b063).** "Master's track" became "parcours de maîtrise (M.Div. et Th.M.)", as in Unit 8. Unit 10 has "parcours de master", so one of the two should be chosen for the whole course.

## Problems in the English source

1. **MC count.** This unit has only 7 MC items ("0 / 7"), where the standard is 20. I kept 7.
2. **b043.** "sons and daughters, young and old, servants and free, as Joel had said". Joel 2:28–29 (Acts 2:17–18) has menservants and maidservants. It does not say "free". I translated it as written ("serviteurs et hommes libres"). A suggested fix is "menservants and maidservants" ("serviteurs et servantes").
3. **SA keywords.** Several English keywords are long phrases ("gave them utterance", "stage by stage", "stands alone", "church was born", "all the lord's people", "all of them"), and in SA1, SA2 and SA7 the prompt already contains some keywords. I used shorter, distinctive French stems with alternates. No French prompt scores 3 on its own keywords.

## Checker's changes

- Scripture: every quotation checked against the offline Segond 1910 (Actes 1:4–5, 1:8, 2:1–6, 2:11, 2:17, 2:36–38, 2:41, 4:31; Jean 7:39, 14:26, 16:7). All verbatim; none needed correction.
- b016: capitalized the opening of the Acts 1:8 quotation ("Vous recevrez").
- b033 and SA8 model: "ramenés à la maison" (too literal for "brought home") became "ramenés à Dieu".
- b037: "la plus naturelle" became "plus naturelle", to match the English comparative ("the more natural") and not overstate the course's lean.
- Fill 2: added "moissons" as an accepted form.
- SA1, SA2: replaced keyword groups that the prompt already supplies (Pentecôte, Esprit, langues; Pierre, Christ) with distinctive lesson ideas (effusion, impétueux, s'exprimer, identifiable; phénomènes, élevé/exalté). Each now has 8 groups, the model hits 8, the prompt 0.
- SA3: prompt wording "les relier" became "les accorder" (closer to "reconciled"); added "compatibl" / "pas de conflit" to the reconciliation group.
- SA6: the "speaking" group ("parler") was hit by almost any answer; it is now "penche" / "plusieurs langues".
- SA7: groups for "all of them" and Corinth were weak or duplicated; now "effacer" (not erasing the languages) and Corinth / "même nature" / "question distincte". A wrong answer that only names Babel and Corinth no longer passes on loose words.
- SA4 keyword "120" kept despite the validator warning: a number that cannot collide with ordinary words.
- Fairness: rechecked the once-for-all / pattern section (b035–b040, MC5, SA3, SA9). The French is neither warmer nor cooler toward Pentecostal readers than the English.

## Adaptations

- **b007 and b066.** These now name the Louis Segond 1910 as the French Bible version, as in the other units.
- No US-specific examples needed adapting. I kept the tone of the once-for-all / pattern section exactly as in the English: the course "leans gently" toward the inaugural view ("penche légèrement"), "without binding your conscience" ("sans lier votre conscience"), and I added no extra warmth or caution.
