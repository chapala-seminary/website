## Questions for Wayne

1. **"Helper" (John 14:16, 15:26; b026, b034, Fill 2).** Segond has "consolateur". Following convention 1, b026 adds one sentence: the Greek word (Paraclet) literally means the one called alongside to help. Fill 2 has the answer "consolateur" and also accepts "Paraclet", "aide", "défenseur" and "avocat".
2. **"Grieved" (Eph 4:30; b026, MC 2, Fill 3).** Segond has "attrister". I used "attristé" throughout. Fill 3 has the answer "attristé" and also accepts "peiné", "affligé", "contristé" and "blessé".
3. **"Testify" (John 15:26; Fill 6).** Segond has "il rendra témoignage de moi". Fill 6 has the answer "témoignage" and also accepts "témoignages", "témoin" and "témoins".
4. **"Filled with the Spirit" (Eph 5:18; Fill 10).** Segond has "remplis de l'Esprit". Fill 10 has the answer "remplis" and also accepts "rempli", "remplie", "remplies" and "pleins".
5. **Lowercase "esprit" in Old Testament verses.** Segond writes "mon esprit" (Zech 4:6), "l'esprit de Dieu" (Gen 1:2; Job 33:4) and "l'esprit de l'Éternel" (1 Sam 16:13) in lowercase. The lesson treats these verses as speaking of the Holy Spirit. I quoted Segond exactly and kept "l'Esprit" capitalized in the lesson's own prose. Is that acceptable?
6. **Heb 9:14.** The lesson cites this verse as proof that the Spirit is eternal. Segond reads "par un esprit éternel", with lowercase and an indefinite article, so the French Bible makes this proof weaker. The verse is cited only, not quoted, so I left the citation as it is.
7. **Rom 8:9 (b039).** The NKJV says "in the Spirit"; Segond says "selon l'esprit", in lowercase. The lesson's point rests on the second sentence of the verse ("il ne lui appartient pas"), which matches the NKJV, so I added no note.
8. **Acts 5 "his gift" (b020, Fill 1).** I wrote "son offrande", as the Spanish edition does. The Acts 5:3–4 quotation is Segond with an ellipsis.
9. **"Getting more of the Holy Spirit" (b027, b060, SA 6).** I rendered it « recevoir davantage du Saint-Esprit ». "Water in a jar" became « eau dans une cruche ». "Yield" is translated « se soumettre ».
10. **"Down payment" (b041).** I wrote "les arrhes, le gage" because Segond uses "arrhes" in 2 Cor 1:22 and "gage" in Eph 1:14.

## Problems in the English source

1. **b017** says "We will consider four things in turn". The unit then counts six sections, from "First" to "Sixth and finally". I translated "quatre" faithfully. Should it be "six"?
2. **MC 6 why** says the later units will cover what "tongues and baptism" mean. The lesson (b045) says "tongues and prophecy". This is a minor mismatch, and I translated it as written, glossing "baptism" as « baptême dans l'Esprit ».
3. **Exam size.** The unit has only 7 MC items, not 20, although the footer counter "0 / 7" is consistent with that. No wrong answer keys were found. Every exam item is taught in the lesson.

## Adaptations

- No US-specific examples needed adapting. "The same as electricity" (MC 2) was kept.
- **SA keywords.** Some English keywords do not carry over to French, so I replaced them with ideas the lesson teaches. In SA 2, "upon" became "conception". In SA 5, "one" became « un seul / ne font qu'un ». In SA 7, "within" became « au-dedans / mettrai mon esprit ». In SA 10, "christlike" became « ressembl/semblable ». Every model answer hits 7–8 groups, and no prompt hits more than 2.
- **Validator warning.** The warning on Fill 9 ("fruit", with only "fruits" accepted) is a false alarm: no other genuinely correct word exists.

## Checker's changes

- SA 2: the prompt now says « et Christ » rather than « et le Fils », to match the English. The "exalted" stem now also catches « élève/élèvera », not only « élevé ».
- SA 3 ("every"): I removed the bare stem « chaque », which a wrong answer could hit by accident. The group now takes « chaque croyant / tout croyant / tous ceux qui sont à Christ ».
- SA 4 ("center"): « centre » became « au centre / le centre / son centre », so that « concentrer » no longer matches.
- SA 6, SA 10 ("power"): I dropped « pouvoir », which also matches the everyday verb « pouvoir » ("to be able"), and replaced it with « force », the lesson's word.
- SA 6 ("water"): I removed the form « de l'eau », which can never match because the grader replaces apostrophes with spaces. « d eau » now catches the lesson's « d'eau ».
- SA 9 ("christ"): this group now looks for Christlikeness (« semblable / ressembl / comme Christ »). The bare « christ » let a wrong answer reach 3 hits.
- b040 / SA 8: « une petite élite avancée » became « quelques croyants plus avancés ». The English ("an advanced few") is neutral, and « élite » sounded more pointed toward Pentecostal readers than the English does.
- MC 2 why: « avoir davantage » became « recevoir davantage », to match the lesson's phrase.
- Small wording: b012 (« qui avait été promis »), b017 (« fil conducteur demeurera »), b026 (Greek word given as paraklêtos), b046 (« garder l'équilibre » instead of « nous gardera stables »), b058 (« exaltation de Christ »).
