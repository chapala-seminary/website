## Questions for Wayne

1. **Num 11:29 (b008, Fill 9).** In this verse LSG says "Puisse tout le peuple de l'Éternel être composé de prophètes; et veuille l'Éternel mettre son esprit sur eux!" It writes "esprit" in lowercase. I quoted LSG exactly. Fill 9 now follows the LSG wording, with "prophètes" as the answer; it also accepts "prophète".
2. **1 Cor 12:13 (b020).** NKJV has "by one Spirit we were all baptized," and LSG has "baptisés dans un seul Esprit." "Dans" leans toward the "baptism in the Spirit" reading. The lesson doesn't comment on the wording, so I made no change. Please flag this if a later unit (Unit 8) builds an argument on this verse.
3. **"Initial evidence" (b030, SA8).** I used "signe initial," which is common in French-speaking Pentecostal usage. The SA8 keywords also accept "évidence initiale." In b033, "tongues as the evidence of Spirit-baptism" became "le parler en langues comme le signe du baptême dans l'Esprit." "Baptism in the Holy Spirit" became "baptême dans le Saint-Esprit."
4. **"Non-denominational" (b014, b026, b051, MC7).** In prose: "n'appartient à aucune dénomination" and "séminaire non confessionnel." For "fairly and non-denominationally" (b014, MC7 option D) the checker changed "sans esprit de dénomination" (which can sound dismissive of denominations) to "sans parti pris confessionnel." Please confirm.
5. **Names of movements.** I kept "Vineyard" and glossed it once as « la Vigne ». Fill 7 also accepts "Vigne." "Jesus People" stays in English with a gloss. For the Full Gospel Business Men's Fellowship I used "Hommes d'Affaires du Plein Évangile," the name used in French-speaking Africa, with the English name in brackets. "Power evangelism" became "évangélisation de puissance," "Global South" became "Sud global," and "Episcopal" became "épiscopalienne." In b019, "rector" is glossed as "(pasteur titulaire)."

## Problems in the English source

1. **b040.** The text says prosperity teaching was "examined in an earlier unit." In fact the prosperity gospel is covered in Unit 11, which comes later; Unit 1 only touches on it. I translated it as written. A suggested fix is "examined in a later unit."
2. **b048.** "In the next unit… healing, miracles, and spiritual warfare" is wrong. Unit 8 is "What Is the Baptism in the Holy Spirit?" and healing is Unit 9. I translated it as written.
3. **MC count.** This unit has only 7 MC items (b066 "0 / 7"), where the standard is 20. I kept 7.
4. **SA4–SA10.** These model answers are near-verbatim copies of lesson paragraphs, and several keywords are long phrases ("full gospel business men," "never tidy," "denominational maps"). In French I used shorter, distinctive stems so the grading still works. The meaning is unchanged.
