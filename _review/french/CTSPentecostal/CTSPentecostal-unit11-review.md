6. **Isaiah 53:5 / 1 Peter 2:24** (b033, SA7). NKJV "by His stripes"; Segond "par ses meurtrissures". The English says the context ties this verse to "the healing of our souls from sin". Many Pentecostal readers in Kinshasa hold "healing in the atonement" (cf. Matthew 8:17, which applies Isaiah 53:4 to bodily healings). I translated faithfully. The phrase "on demand" keeps the criticism aimed at the excess, but you may want to soften "the context ties" (e.g. "the context ties *first of all*") so the course does not read as cooler than intended toward a mainstream Pentecostal doctrine.
7. **Malachi 3:10** (b033, SA7). NKJV "windows of heaven"; Segond "les écluses des cieux". The SA7 keyword is "écluse".
8. **2 Corinthians 8:21** (b041). NKJV "providing honorable things… in the sight of men"; Segond "nous recherchons ce qui est bien… devant les hommes". Quoted from Segond, and the sentence was adjusted to fit.
9. **2 Corinthians 9:8** (b020). NKJV "make all grace abound toward you"; Segond "vous combler de toutes sortes de grâces". Quoted from Segond.
10. **Rich young ruler** (b027, SA5). I rendered it "un jeune chef riche", which combines Luke 18:18's "chef" with Matthew 19:22's "jeune homme… tout triste".
11. **"Prosperity gospel"**: rendered "évangile de la prospérité", with a lowercase *é* for the false teaching and "l'Évangile" capitalized for the true gospel (b044, b045, b046). Please confirm.
