# CTSPentecostal Unit 9 (Healing, Miracles, and Spiritual Warfare) — French draft review

## Questions for Wayne

1. **Matthew 12:39, "seeks after a sign".** LSG has "demande un miracle". The lesson's point is the sign-seeking heart, so I quoted LSG and added: "le mot grec traduit ici par « miracle » signifie littéralement « signe »." The heading uses "Le cœur avide de signes".
2. **1 Corinthians 12:9, "gifts of healings".** LSG has "le don des guérisons" (singular "don"). I quoted LSG in b022. Fill 2 now reads "le don des ____". The answer is "guérisons" and it also accepts "guérison".
3. **2 Corinthians 12:7, "thorn in the flesh".** LSG has "écharde dans la chair". Fill 4 uses the answer "écharde" and also accepts "épine" and "aiguillon".
4. **Ephesians 6:12, "principalities … powers".** LSG has "les dominations … les autorités". Fill 8 uses the answer "dominations" and also accepts "principautés". In b042 I quoted Col 2:15 the same way, "a dépouillé les dominations et les autorités".
5. **Exodus 15:26, "the LORD who heals you".** LSG has "l'Éternel, qui te guérit". Fill 1 uses the answer "guérit".
6. **Philippians 2:27, "almost unto death".** LSG has "tout près de la mort" and "Dieu a eu pitié de lui". The lesson says Paul called the recovery "the mercy of God"; b029 now reads "la miséricorde de Dieu (« Dieu a eu pitié de lui »)", since "la pitié de Dieu" alone can sound condescending in French.
7. **Hebrews 2:4.** LSG has "Dieu appuyant leur témoignage par des signes, des prodiges, et divers miracles". I quoted LSG.
8. **"not My will, but Yours" (b048).** The English quotes this with no reference. I quoted LSG Luc 22:42, "que ma volonté ne se fasse pas, mais la tienne", and added the reference.
9. **Ephesians 6:15, "the readiness of the gospel".** LSG has "le zèle que donne l'Évangile de paix". I used the LSG wording in b042 and SA 9.
10. **Fair tone.** Many readers in Kinshasa are Pentecostal or charismatic. I kept exactly the English balance and did not soften or sharpen it. For example, "the movement has sometimes stumbled" became "le mouvement a parfois trébuché", and "the prosperity teaching" became "l'enseignement de la prospérité". Please confirm that this tone is right.

## Problems in the English source

1. **Exam size.** The source has only 7 MC items (b069 reads "0 / 7"), not the usual 20. I translated all 7.
2. **SA 4–10 keywords** are long verbatim phrases from the lesson ("medicine is scarce", "live by", "not been failed", "wonders more than god", "saved and changed soul", "raise him up"). A student who answers correctly in their own words will rarely match them. In French I used short content stems with synonyms, 8 groups each.
3. **SA 1 and SA 5 prompts.** If these were translated literally, the French prompt alone would hit its own keywords, through words like "guérison" and "déformation", and through "apôtres" and "Exode". I reworded them without changing what is asked. SA 1 now reads "l'action de Dieu envers ceux qui souffrent dans leur corps … quel enseignement erroné". SA 5 now reads "depuis l'Ancien Testament jusqu'au ministère de Jésus et de ses disciples".
4. **b027** says "three times the answer was not the healing he sought". 2 Corinthians 12:8–9 records three requests but only one answer. This is a minor overstatement, and I translated it as written.
