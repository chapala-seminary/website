# CTSPentecostal Unit 10 — French draft review

## Questions for Wayne

1. **1 Thessalonians 5:21 (b008, b038, fill 1, MC 6):** NKJV "Test all things; hold fast what is good"; Segond "Mais examinez toutes choses; retenez ce qui est bon." I used "examiner" as the French verb for "test" throughout the lesson (heading "Examinez toutes choses"). Fill 1 asks for "prophéties" (Segond's word), so the answer key is unaffected.
2. **2 Timothy 3:16 (b020):** "given by inspiration of God" → Segond "inspirée de Dieu" (convention 3). The lesson does not dwell on the image, so I did not add "soufflée par Dieu".
3. **Ephesians 2:20 (b024, fill 4):** NKJV "chief cornerstone"; Segond "la pierre angulaire" (no "chief"). Fill answer is "pierre angulaire". I also accept "pierre d'angle", "pierre de l'angle", "principale pierre de l'angle" and "pierre angulaire principale".
4. **1 Corinthians 14:39 (b031, MC 4, SA 2, SA 7):** NKJV "do not forbid to speak with tongues"; Segond "n'empêchez pas de parler en langues". The lesson builds on "forbid" ("a refusal to forbid what the apostle told the church not to forbid"). I quoted Segond and added one sentence: the Greek verb rendered "empêcher" also means "interdire". SA keyword groups accept both "empêch-" and "interdi-".
5. **1 Corinthians 1:7 (b030, SA 7):** NKJV "the revealing of our Lord Jesus Christ"; Segond "la manifestation". I added a sentence that the Greek word literally means "révélation", because the unit's theme is revelation. SA 7 accepts "manifestation", "révélation de notre" or "apparition" (checker narrowed bare "révélation", which any answer about revelatory gifts would hit).
6. **1 Corinthians 14:1 (b029, SA 7):** NKJV "Pursue love… especially that you may prophesy"; Segond "Recherchez la charité… mais surtout à celui de prophétie". Quoted Segond as is; "charité" was left without comment.
7. **Hebrews 2:3–4 (b025):** Segond "Dieu appuyant leur témoignage" corresponds to NKJV "God also bearing witness". I quoted Segond and stopped before "distribués selon sa volonté", which matches the English cut.
8. **Jude 3 (b034, fill 7):** Segond "transmise aux saints une fois pour toutes". The NKJV's "once for all" is present, so the lesson's emphasis works without change.
9. **Deuteronomy 18:22 (b040, SA 10):** Segond phrases the test negatively ("Quand ce que dira le prophète n'aura pas lieu…"). The lesson paraphrases rather than quotes, so I kept the paraphrase "la parole d'un prophète devait s'accomplir".
10. **Terms "cessationniste / continuationniste":** I used these standard French theological terms (the Spanish edition used "continuista"). Fill 3 and fill 6 also accept spellings with one "n" and "continuiste". Please confirm this pair for the whole course.
11. **Fairness of tone:** I kept both positions at the same strength as the English. Both are introduced the same way ("Sous sa forme la plus solide…"), and "Le cessationniste soutient…" is parallel to "Le continuationniste soutient…". No wording was warmed or cooled for either side. (Checker re-read both sections against the English and agrees; the one change was "lecture simple" → "lecture directe" in b031, since "simple" can sound like "simplistic" in French.) b042 keeps the course's stated "no" on tongues as required evidence, exactly as the English has it.

## Problems in the English source

1. **MC count:** the source has 7 multiple-choice items, not the 20 the brief mentions; b064 also reads "0 / 7". I translated all 7. Please check whether 13 items are missing.
2. **b035 / SA 9 (Agabus):** the lesson says "the disciples" pleaded with Paul. In Acts 21:12 the people pleading are "we and those from that place" (Luke's party and the believers at Caesarea); the "disciples" who told Paul not to go are at Tyre (21:4). This is a small imprecision. I translated it faithfully ("les disciples"), and the SA 9 keyword "disciples" keeps it.
3. **SA 4 keyword "2 timothy 3:16":** a student who cites only "2 Timothée 3" or the phrase would be missed in English. In French the group accepts "timothée 3"/"timothee 3"/"inspirée de Dieu".
4. Several English keywords are weak or generic, for example "before", "christ", "relate" and "distinct". I made the French groups more distinctive where possible (SA 5 "pose une fois"). No answer keys are wrong.
