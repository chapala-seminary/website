# French Batch 1: what the reviews found

This covers four courses: How We Got the Bible, Pentateuch, Joshua Intensive and Psalms (44 units).

Every claim below about the English site was checked against the live course files. Claims that did not hold up were dropped.

## 3. Decisions that apply to the whole French edition

1. **When the lesson's argument depends on NKJV wording that Segond does not have.** Current practice is to quote Segond exactly and add a short note on the Hebrew or Greek. Examples:
   - Josh 6:2 "I have given": LSG has the present tense, "je livre."
   - Ps 22:31 "He has done this."
   - Ps 1:1: "Heureux," where the lesson wants "blessed."
   - Ps 23:4 rod and staff: LSG has "houlette."
   - Heb 6:18 "fled."
   - Josh 1:9 "wherever you go."

   Fill-in answers use Segond's word. Please approve this approach as policy, and say whether naming "other versions" is acceptable (Psalms 2 does it).
2. **The name of God.** The drafts write "Yahvé (l'Éternel)" where the lesson discusses the name itself, and "l'Éternel" everywhere else. The other options are "Yahweh" or "l'Éternel" only.
3. **"God-breathed."** The drafts are mixed. Some use "inspirée de Dieu" (Segond's wording), others "soufflée par Dieu," which keeps the image. Bible 10, Fill 5 depends on "soufflé." Pick one.
4. **Priests.** The drafts use Segond's "sacrificateur" and "souverain sacrificateur," not "prêtre" or "grand prêtre." Please confirm.
5. **Names.** The drafts use Segond spellings (Héthiens, Guilgal, Schinear, Melchisédek, Marie for Miriam, Yahvé). Fill-ins also accept the familiar forms. Please confirm.
6. **"The Word" (John 1).** The drafts use "la Parole," not "le Verbe."
7. **Fixed terms that are currently inconsistent between units:**
   - Gnat idiom: "couler le moucheron" (Segond) vs "filtrer."
   - Evidence: "preuves" vs "indices."
   - Cumulative case: "argument cumulatif" vs "dossier cumulatif."
   - Lord's Supper: "Sainte Cène" vs "sainte cène."
   - Pilgrim's Progress character: "Plein-d'Espoir."
8. **Offering names.** The drafts follow the lesson's headings ("sacrifice de paix," "bouc émissaire") and mention Segond's names ("actions de grâces," "Azazel") once. Please confirm.
9. **Track names and capitals.** Most units have "Parcours Certificat / Associé," but Pentateuch 5 has "Diplôme associé." Capitals are also mixed: Segond's lowercase "esprit," "seigneur," "agneau" vs the drafts' "Chef" and "Agneau."
10. **Short-answer grading in French is stricter than in English.** The French prompts were reworded so they cannot pass their own keywords, and the French keyword groups were rebuilt as 8 distinct ideas. Accept this as the standard? If so, the English should be fixed to match (see section 2).

Also, a smaller point: Psalm 51 verse numbers. Printed Segond Bibles number the psalm's title as verses 1–2, so the prayer starts at 51:3. The drafts keep the English numbering.
