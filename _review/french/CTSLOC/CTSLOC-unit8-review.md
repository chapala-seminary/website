# CTSLOC Unit 8 — French draft review notes

## Questions for Wayne

1. **Isaiah 53:9, "with the rich in His death."** Segond has "Son tombeau avec le riche." The lesson (b023) and the SA8 model depend on this verse. I quoted Segond and then added, following convention 1: « le texte hébreu dit littéralement : "avec le riche dans sa mort" ».
2. **Romans 3:25, "propitiation."** Segond has "victime propitiatoire," and its word for the mercy seat is "propitiatoire" (Exode 25:17; Hébreux 9:5). So in French the link to the mercy seat is visible in the word itself. In b020 I quoted "victime propitiatoire" and named the Greek word (hilastèrion). The answer for Fill 9 is "propitiatoire", and "propitiation" and "hilasterion" are also accepted.
3. **John 19:30, "It is finished."** Segond has "Tout est accompli." I used it in b016, b024, MC12 and SA6. MC12's option keeps "payé intégralement / accompli."
4. **Matthew 27:54.** Segond has "Assurément, cet homme était Fils de Dieu" (with no article before "Fils"). I used this in b019, MC16 and SA7.
5. **Luke 23:4, "I find no fault."** Segond has "Je ne trouve rien de coupable en cet homme." This affects MC4 and SA2, where the keyword group is "coupable" / "faute".
6. **Mark 14:61, "Son of the Blessed."** Segond has "le Fils du Dieu béni," which I quoted. For "He has spoken blasphemy!" I used Segond's Matthew 26:65, « Il a blasphémé! », because Mark 14:64 has no exclamation of that kind.
7. **Psalm 22:18 and the inscription.** For Psalm 22:18, Segond uses the present tense: "Ils se partagent mes vêtements, Ils tirent au sort ma tunique." For the inscription I used Matthew 27:37, "Celui-ci est Jésus, le roi des Juifs."
8. **"Crucify Him!"** I used Luke 23:21, « Crucifie, crucifie-le! ». For "scourged" I used Segond's "battre de verges" (John 19:1), and "flagellation" in b010.
9. **"Robbers" / "thief."** Matthew has "brigands" and Luke has "malfaiteurs." I used "brigand" throughout (MC8 and the lesson). The SA keywords accept brigand, malfaiteur, larron and voleur.
10. **Greek terms.** The Greek word for "propitiation" (hilastèrion) appears only in French, in b020. I added it so the mercy-seat link is clear. I also added a short gloss "(le couvercle de l'arche)" at the first mention of "propitiatoire."

## Problems in the English source

- **b017, MC12, Fill 7:** the claim that "in the old markets a paid debt was stamped with one word, tetelestai" is a popular sermon illustration. The papyrus evidence for it is thin, so a careful reader may object. I translated it faithfully.
- **b020:** "for nearly six hundred years". The time from 586 BC to the crucifixion is a little over 600 years. The detail that the room "held only a stone" comes from Jewish tradition (Mishnah Yoma 5:2), not from Scripture. I translated it as written.
- **b010:** the statement that He "could no longer carry the cross" is an inference. John 19:17 says He went out bearing His cross, and the Gospels do not say why Simon was compelled. I translated it as written.
- **SA prompts (convention 10):** the prompts for SA5 ("cry of dereliction"), SA6 ("It is finished… final word") and SA7 ("veil, centurion, pierced side") would have scored their own keywords in French. So I reworded them as follows. SA5 is now "le cri de Jésus en Matthieu 27:46". SA6 is now "la sixième et la septième parole". SA7 is now "les signes qui accompagnèrent la mort de Jésus".
- I found no wrong answer keys. All `why` references match their items.
