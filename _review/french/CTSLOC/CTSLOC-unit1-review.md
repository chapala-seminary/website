## Questions for Wayne

1. **"The Word" (John 1:1, 1:14).** Following convention 6, I used "la Parole" throughout, and the heading reads "La Parole a été faite chair". Segond writes "la parole" in lowercase in John 1:14. I kept that lowercase only inside verbatim quotations (b010, MC6, Fill 2).
2. **Philippians 2:7, "made Himself of no reputation".** Segond has "s'est dépouillé lui-même, en prenant une forme de serviteur". In b011 I added the note: "Le texte grec dit littéralement qu'il « s'est vidé lui-même » et qu'il a pris la forme d'un « esclave »". b012, MC7 and SA4 use "s'est dépouillé". Fill 3's answer is "serviteur", and it also accepts "esclave".
3. **Luke 2:12, "wrapped in swaddling cloths, lying in a manger".** Segond has "un enfant emmailloté et couché dans une crèche". I quoted that and adjusted the sentence to fit ("il était « un enfant emmailloté… »").
4. **Hebrews 1:3, "upholds all things by the word of His power".** Segond has "soutenant toutes choses par sa parole puissante". I quoted only "toutes choses par sa parole puissante", after "Celui qui soutient". The play on "upheld in His mother's arms" still works with "soutenir".
5. **John 8:58, "I AM".** Segond has "je suis" in lowercase. I quoted it verbatim. Where the lesson uses the phrase as a divine name (b011 "le grand « Je suis »", MC4 why), I wrote « Je suis » with a capital.
6. **Micah 5:2, "the Ruler".** Segond has "Celui qui dominera sur Israël". I quoted that phrase in b018 and in Fill 7.
7. **Luke 2:49, "about My Father's business".** Segond has "il faut que je m'occupe des affaires de mon Père". Fill 9's answer is "affaires" (accepts "affaire"). The checker removed "maison" from the accepted answers: the lesson never teaches the "house" reading, so it cannot be learned from the French lesson.
8. **Hebrews 2:17, "High Priest".** I used Segond's "souverain sacrificateur" (convention 4) in b025, MC20 and SA10.
9. **"God of very God" (b007).** I wrote "vrai Dieu issu du vrai Dieu". I avoided the Nicene French wording "né du vrai Dieu" so that "né" is not confused with the birth at Bethlehem in this lesson. Is that acceptable?
10. **"Kinsman" (b016, MC11, SA6, Fill 6).** I wrote "Proche Parent", capitalized as the English is. I did not use the Ruth term "celui qui a droit de rachat", because the lesson does not make the redeemer-kinsman point.
11. **"Condescension" (b012).** I wrote "abaissement" rather than "condescendance", because in modern French "condescendance" means haughtiness.
12. **Gentiles.** I used "païens", the usual Segond word (b019, SA7). SA7 also accepts "nations" and "non-Juifs".

## Problems in the English source

1. **b022, "in the very next verse".** Luke 2:51 is two verses after 2:49; verse 2:50 comes between them. I wrote "aussitôt après", which is accurate and keeps the point.
2. **SA keyword groups (convention 10).** Several English groups repeat the same idea: SA1 "creat"/"made"; SA2 "creat"/"made"; SA3 "divin"/"deity"/"claim"; SA4 "deity"/"fully". Some English stems are also not taught in the lesson: SA2 "sustain"/"univ" (the lesson says "consist" and "stars"), and SA5 "fulfil" (the lesson only says "citing Isaiah"). The French has 8 distinct ideas per question, all taken from the French lesson. Each model hits 7–8 groups, and no prompt hits more than 1. The checker tightened several groups so that typical wrong answers no longer pass (see Checker's changes).
3. **MC12 why vs. b015.** The lesson says Luke's line is "most likely through Mary"; the `why` says "often through Mary". This is minor and consistent in substance, so I translated both as written.

## Checker's changes

- Scripture: all quotations verified against Segond 1910; no corrections needed.
- Fill 2: removed "corps" from the accepted answers. Segond says "chair", and "corps" leans toward the idea that he only took a body.
- Fill 9: removed "maison" from the accepted answers. The French lesson never teaches it.
- SA1: the "created" group matched "pas créé" and "créé" alike, so "the Word was created" scored a hit. It now matches only negations ("pas créé", "non créé", "incréé").
- SA2: dropped the "que dieu" stem, which also matched "parce que Dieu…".
- SA3: dropped the bare "le nom" stem, which matched almost any sentence.
- SA5: the "near" and "over" groups rewarded the distractor answers (« Dieu près de nous / au-dessus de nous »). They are now "réellement/vraiment avec" and "partage", and the model was adjusted to hit them.
- SA6: merged David and Abraham into one group (one idea), and added a Salomon/Nathan group. The model now names both lines. A "same list" wrong answer now scores only 2 hits.
- SA9: replaced the "obéi" stem, which also matched "désobéi", with "unique / Fils de Dieu", and added "il obéissait" to the "soumis" group.
- SA10: "mort" and "homme" were bare common words. They are now "par la mort / anéantir / vaincre la mort" and "vraiment homme / Jésus-Christ homme".
- Wording: b012, smoother construction ("s'écrire lui-même dans la page"); b026, "là où tout le cours va marcher" became "comme tout le cours va avancer".
