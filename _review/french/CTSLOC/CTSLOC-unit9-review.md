## Questions for Wayne

1. **John 20:17, "Do not cling to Me".** LSG has "Ne me touche pas". The lesson's point is about holding on to Him. I quoted LSG and added "Le texte grec dit littéralement « ne me retiens pas »" (b011). SA2 accepts "touche", "retien" and "reteni".
2. **Matthew 28:18, "All authority".** LSG has "Tout pouvoir". I quoted LSG and added "le texte grec dit littéralement « toute autorité »" (b020). MC17 uses "pouvoir", and SA6 accepts "pouvoir" or "autorité".
3. **1 Corinthians 15:20, "firstfruits of those who have fallen asleep".** LSG has "les prémices de ceux qui sont morts". I quoted LSG and added a short note on the Greek "endormis" (b022). Fill 10 answer: "prémices". It also accepts "premiers fruits".
4. **Matthew 28:20, "to the end of the age".** LSG has "jusqu'à la fin du monde". The closing line "I am with you always" (b023, SA6, SA10) is rendered "Je suis avec vous tous les jours", following LSG. I added no note because the lesson does not depend on "age".
5. **Luke 24:39, "a spirit" / "ghost".** LSG has "un esprit". The lesson says "no ghost" (b016, MC11, Fill 7). I wrote "pas un « esprit », c'est-à-dire un fantôme". The exam items use "fantôme".
6. **Luke 24:30, "blessed and broke it".** LSG has "après avoir rendu grâces, il le rompit". b014 and MC10 use "rendit grâces".
7. **Luke 24:25, "O foolish ones".** LSG has "O hommes sans intelligence". I quoted LSG, and the SA3 model follows it.
8. **Luke 24:21, "redeem Israel".** LSG has "délivrerait Israël". I quoted LSG without a note.
9. **John 20:16, "Rabboni".** LSG spells it "Rabbouni". Fill 4 answer: "Rabbouni". It also accepts "Rabboni", "Raboni" and "Rabouni".
10. **John 20:18, "I have seen the Lord!"** The English quotes this as direct speech, but John 20:18 (NKJV and LSG) gives it as indirect speech. b011 quotes LSG's words "qu'elle avait vu le Seigneur".
11. **1 Corinthians 15:5, "Peter".** LSG has "Céphas". I wrote "Céphas, c'est-à-dire Pierre".
12. **John 20:25, "print of the nails".** LSG has "la marque des clous". I used that wording in b017, MC13 and SA5.

## Problems in the English source

1. **b019, MC16, SA7, "three times He charged him, 'Feed My sheep'".** John 21:15–17 says "Feed My lambs", "Tend My sheep" and "Feed My sheep" (LSG: "Pais mes agneaux", "Pais mes brebis", "Pais mes brebis"). I translated the lesson as written, using "Pais mes brebis". SA7 accepts both "brebis" and "agneau".
2. **b011.** The English puts "I have seen the Lord!" in quotation marks, but John 20:18 reports it as indirect speech (see Question 10).
3. **SA prompts reworded so they do not give away keywords.** Following convention 10, I changed the wording of the SA1, SA4, SA7, SA8 and SA10 prompts so that the French prompt does not contain answer ideas such as "paix", "corps", "rétablir", "ascension", "divin" or "humain". Each French prompt now reaches no more than 1 keyword hit. The meaning of each question is unchanged.
