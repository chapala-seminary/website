## Questions for Wayne

1. **Matthew 5:3-10, "Blessed are..." vs. LSG "Heureux...".** I quoted LSG. In b012 I added one sentence: the Greek word means "bienheureux", that is, blessed by God. This keeps the lesson's "blessing" language. MC6's option reads « les Béatitudes (« Heureux... ») ».
2. **Matthew 4:23, "healing all kinds of sickness" vs. LSG "guérissant toute maladie et toute infirmité".** I quoted LSG in b006. MC1's correct option is "guérir toute maladie".
3. **Matthew 5:17, "destroy / fulfill" vs. LSG "abolir / accomplir".** I quoted LSG in b014 and MC9. Fill 6 answer: "accomplir" (also accepts accompli, réaliser, accomplir la loi).
4. **Matthew 5:22, "But I say to you" vs. LSG "Mais moi, je vous dis".** I used the LSG wording everywhere (b011, b014, b025, MC10, SA3). In b011, "Thus says the LORD" became LSG's usual formula "Ainsi parle l'Éternel".
5. **Matthew 6:10, "Your kingdom come" vs. LSG "que ton règne vienne".** LSG does not say "royaume" here, and the unit's theme is the kingdom. In b015 I added a short note that the Greek word is the same one translated "royaume".
6. **Matthew 6:24, "mammon" vs. LSG "Mamon".** I added a one-line gloss in b015. Fill 7 answer: "Mamon" (also accepts mammon, argent, richesse, richesses).
7. **Matthew 6:33, "shall be added to you" vs. LSG "vous seront données par-dessus".** I quoted LSG in b015, MC13 and the SA9 model.
8. **Matthew 7:24, "and does them" vs. LSG "et les met en pratique".** In b017 I quoted the fuller clause ("entend ces paroles que je dis et les met en pratique"). "Mettre en pratique" is used in MC14 and SA4.
9. **Matthew 7:27-29.** "Great was its fall" became LSG's "sa ruine a été grande". "Astonished at His teaching... not as the scribes" became "la foule fut frappée de sa doctrine... non pas comme leurs scribes" (b017, MC15).
10. **Matthew 9:37-38, "laborers" became LSG "ouvriers"; "weary and scattered" became LSG "languissante et abattue".** Fill 3 uses LSG 9:37 ("peu d'____"). Answer: "ouvriers" (also accepts ouvrier, travailleurs, moissonneurs).
11. **Matthew 13, LSG terms.** "Tares" became "ivraie". "Mustard seed" became "grain de sénevé", glossed once as "graine de moutarde". "Dragnet" became "filet". "Householder" became "maître de maison". In LSG, Matthew 13:52 speaks of "tout scribe instruit", while the lesson says "a disciple". I kept the lesson's "disciple" because b022 paraphrases rather than quotes it.
12. **Mark 3:19, "who also betrayed Him" vs. LSG "celui qui livra Jésus".** I quoted LSG. 1 Corinthians 1:27, "put to shame", became LSG "confondre".
13. **Theological wording.** b008 "tax collector" became "publicain (un collecteur d'impôts)". b025 "the Rock" is capitalized ("le Roc") as in the English. "Communion before commission" became "la communion avant la mission".

## Problems in the English source

1. **b020** cites Matthew 13:30 for the owner's reason ("lest the wheat be uprooted"). That reason is in 13:29. I kept the reference as given.
2. **MC10 why** ("anger is murder, lust is adultery") goes further than the lesson, which says the angry heart is "already guilty". I translated it faithfully.
3. **SA5 model** uses "receptive" and "the proud". The lesson does not use these words; it says "seeker" and "scoffer". I translated the model; the keyword groups use the lesson's words.
4. **SA9 prompt** ("Explain 'Seek first the kingdom' and 'you cannot serve two masters'") contains most of its own keywords. In French I rephrased it to cite the verses without quoting them, so the prompt alone does not pass.
5. **SA keywords repeating the prompt** (SA1, SA2, SA4–SA8, SA10: e.g. "parable", "beatitude", "sower", "builder", "mustard"). Per the edition standard, the French groups replace these with other lesson ideas, so no French prompt hits more than one of its own groups.
