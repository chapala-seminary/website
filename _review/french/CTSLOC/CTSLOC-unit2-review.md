# CTSLOC Unit 2 — French draft review notes

## Questions for Wayne

1. **Isaiah 40:3 (b006, MC 1, Fill 1).** In LSG, Isaiah reads "Une voix crie: Préparez au désert le chemin de l'Éternel". That wording does not fit "the voice of one crying in the wilderness". So I quoted the LSG form of Matthieu 3:3 ("la voix de celui qui crie dans le désert : Préparez le chemin du Seigneur") and cited "(Matthieu 3:3 ; Ésaïe 40:3)". Fill 1 does the same. Its answer is "désert".
2. **"Remained upon Him" (John 1:32; b015, MC 14, SA 6).** LSG says "s'arrêter sur lui". I quoted LSG and added that the Greek verb literally means "demeurer". MC 14 and the SA 6 keywords use "demeure / resta".
3. **"Drove" (Mark 1:12; b017).** LSG says "poussa", which is less strong. I quoted it and added that the Greek literally means "chassa".
4. **"Fulfill all righteousness" (Matt 3:15; b013, MC 10, Fill 5, SA 4).** LSG says "accomplissions ainsi tout ce qui est juste". Fill 5 answer: "juste". It also accepts "justice" and "droit". MC 10 says "« accomplir tout ce qui est juste »".
5. **"In whom I am well pleased" (Matt 3:17; b014, MC 13, SA 5).** I used LSG's "en qui j'ai mis toute mon affection" throughout.
6. **"The LORD your God" (Matt 4:7, 4:10; b019).** LSG's Matthew has "le Seigneur, ton Dieu", while its Deuteronomy has "l'Éternel". I quoted Matthew, because the lesson quotes Jesus' words.
7. **Smaller LSG wording differences.** I quoted LSG and adjusted the surrounding prose where needed:
   - Hebrews 2:18 "aid" became "secourir".
   - Luke 4:14 "in the power of the Spirit" became "revêtu de la puissance de l'Esprit".
   - Luke 4:13 "until an opportune time" became "jusqu'à un moment favorable".
   - Genesis 3:6 "desirable to make one wise" became "précieux pour ouvrir l'intelligence".
   - Matt 3:7 "Brood of vipers" became "Races de vipères".
   - Malachi 3:1 "before the Lord's face" became a paraphrase: "préparer le chemin devant le Seigneur".
8. **"The Word" in b009 and b025.** I used "la Parole", following convention 6. In b025, "the eternal Word of Unit 1" became "La Parole éternelle de l'Unité 1, devenue homme".
9. **SA prompts.** I reworded SA 1, 2, 3, 4, 6 and 10 so that they do not contain their own keywords (convention 10). For example, SA 2 cites "Jean 3:30" instead of quoting it. The meaning of each question is unchanged.

## Problems in the English source

1. **MC 7 `why`** ("the sacrifice all the lambs foreshadowed") and the **SA 3 model** ("the true Lamb to whom all the Old Testament sacrifices pointed") make a point the lesson does not teach. b010 quotes John 1:29 but does not develop the sacrificial typology. I translated both faithfully.
2. **SA 7 keyword "appoint"** depends on the single word "appointed" in b017. I widened the French group to "prévu / voulu / hasard / accident".

No wrong answer keys found.
