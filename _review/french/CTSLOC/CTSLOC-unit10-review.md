## Questions for Wayne

1. **Philippians 2:6–7, "made Himself of no reputation".** LSG has "s'est dépouillé lui-même" ("emptied/stripped Himself"). The lesson adds "without laying aside His deity", and that reads awkwardly next to "dépouillé". To avoid a seeming contradiction, I wrote "voilant sa gloire sans *renoncer à* sa divinité" in b011, MC7, Fill 4 and SA4, not "sans se dépouiller de". "Did not consider it robbery" is LSG's "n'a point regardé comme une proie à arracher".
2. **Philippians 2:7, "bondservant".** LSG has "serviteur". Fill 4's answer is "serviteur", and it also accepts "esclave".
3. **Titus 2:13.** LSG has "la manifestation de la gloire du grand Dieu et de notre Sauveur Jésus-Christ", which can be read as two persons; the NKJV plainly calls Jesus God. Per convention 1, b025 now adds: "Le texte grec dit littéralement « de notre grand Dieu et Sauveur Jésus-Christ » : une seule personne." MC20 and Fill 10 quote LSG only.
4. **Matthew 28:18, "All authority".** LSG has "Tout pouvoir". I added "Le texte grec dit littéralement « toute autorité »" in b016. MC11 uses LSG, and the SA5 keyword accepts "pouvoir" or "autorit".
5. **"I AM".** John 8:58 in LSG is "avant qu'Abraham fût, je suis", in lowercase. I quoted it verbatim. For the sayings and the divine Name (b019, MC12, Fill 8), I wrote « JE SUIS », as earlier units did. For Exodus 3:14 (MC12 why), I used LSG's "Je suis celui qui suis".
6. **Hebrews 7:25, "make intercession".** LSG has "toujours vivant pour intercéder en leur faveur". Fill 7's answer is "intercéder", and it also accepts "intercession" and "faire intercession".
7. **Smaller LSG differences.** I quoted LSG verbatim in each case:
   - Hebrews 4:15 "sans commettre de péché" (Fill 3)
   - Matthew 28:20 "jusqu'à la fin du monde" for "end of the age"
   - Hebrews 10:14 "ceux qui sont sanctifiés" for "being sanctified"
   - Matthew 16:24 "qu'il renonce à lui-même, qu'il se charge de sa croix"
   - John 1:14, where LSG writes "la parole" in lowercase while John 1:1 has "la Parole"

## Problems in the English source

1. **SA keyword groups.** The English prompts of SA1, SA4, SA5, SA6, SA8 and SA10 each hit 3 or more of their own keywords. For example, SA5's prompt contains "prophet", "priest" and "king". Following convention 10, I replaced those groups with distinct ideas from the lesson. No French prompt reaches 3 hits.
2. **MC6, SA3, SA4: "two natures".** The phrase "two natures, undivided (and unconfused)" is used here, but the lesson (b011) never says "two natures" or "unconfused". It speaks only of "one Person" and "took our nature". This is minor, and I translated it as written.
