# CTSLOC Unit 7 (The Life of Christ — Passion Week): French review notes

All Scripture was checked with the offline LSG 1910 tool (re-verified by the checker: every quotation in lessons and exams matches Segond 1910).

## Questions for Wayne

1. **Mark 12:44 (b010, MC6, Fill 3).** NKJV: "out of her poverty put in all that she had." LSG: "elle a mis de son nécessaire, tout ce qu'elle possédait." I quoted LSG and added one sentence saying the Greek literally means "de sa pauvreté." Fill 3's answer is "nécessaire," and it also accepts "pauvreté," "indigence" and "besoin."
2. **Matt 21:5 / Zech 9:9 (b006).** NKJV has "lowly" and LSG Matt 21:5 has "Plein de douceur." I quoted LSG and then added the Greek sense ("doux, humble") plus LSG Zech 9:9 ("Il est humble et monté sur un âne"), so the "humble King" theme still rests on Scripture.
3. **Luke 22:44 (b019, b024, MC17, SA6, SA8).** NKJV has "like great drops of blood" and LSG has "comme des grumeaux de sang." I used LSG throughout and added a short note in b019 on the Greek sense (thick drops, like clots).
4. **John 15:1, 4 (b016, b024, MC15, Fill 8, SA5, SA10).** NKJV has "vine/branch" and LSG has "cep/sarment." I used LSG and added a one-line gloss for Kinshasa readers ("le cep est le pied de vigne"). Fill 8's answer is "cep," and it also accepts "vigne," "pied de vigne" and "cep de vigne."
5. **Matt 24:42 (b010, MC7).** NKJV has "what hour" and LSG has "quel jour." The MC7 option quotes LSG. Nothing depends on hour vs. day.
6. **Matt 26:50 (b021).** NKJV has "Friend, why have you come?" and LSG has "Mon ami, ce que tu es venu faire, fais-le," which is a different sense. I quoted LSG. The surrounding sentence ("friendship turned to treachery") still works.
7. **Matt 21:16 (b009).** NKJV has "perfected praise" and LSG has "tiré des louanges de la bouche des enfants." I reworded the sentence to match LSG.
8. **John 13:6, 8 (b013).** Peter's protest uses two verses: "Toi, Seigneur, tu me laves les pieds!… Non, jamais tu ne me laveras les pieds." The first half is John 13:6, but the English cites only 13:8.
9. **Matt 26:31 (b018).** NKJV has "made to stumble because of Me" and LSG has "Je serai pour vous tous… une occasion de chute." SA7 does not depend on this wording.
10. **Course and unit names (b026, b027).** I translated the course name as "La vie de Christ" because I found no earlier French precedent. "Passion Week" became "La semaine de la Passion," a literal rendering. "Semaine sainte" is the more common Catholic term, so I avoided it. Please confirm.
11. **SA5 prompt reworded.** A literal "the way, the Spirit, and the vine" scored 3 keyword hits by itself. The prompt now reads "ses grandes images et ses promesses." The meaning is the same.
12. **Diagram labels (b028–b084).** Following the CTSPent 7 / CTSMatt 11 precedent, the EN/ES pairs became FR/EN pairs, and the Spanish-only blocks (b036, b045, b047, b051, b057 … b084) now hold the English. Robert should decide whether the French site keeps the English half. The 3 validator warnings (b030, b081, b084) are these intentional English halves. LSG terms used: "parvis," "cuve" (laver), "autel des parfums," "pains de proposition," "la Belle Porte" (Acts 3:2 "la Belle").

## Problems in the English source

1. **b011.** "The hands that would be washed in the basin that night" is a slip: Jesus washed feet (John 13:5), not hands. Judas's *hands* taking the money is the intended image, but it is the feet that were washed. I translated it as written.
2. **b007.** "Palm branches" is cited to Matthew 21, but Matthew 21:8 has "branches of trees." The palms come from John 12:13. This is minor.
3. **SA4 prompt** cites 1 Corinthians 11, which the lesson never uses. Only Luke 22 is taught.
4. **SA10 keyword "follow."** The lesson's closing call (b024) never says "follow." The idea appears only in the model answer.
