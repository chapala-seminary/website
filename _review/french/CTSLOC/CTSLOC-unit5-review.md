# CTSLOC Unit 5 — French draft review

## Questions for Wayne

1. **John 6:26, "signs" (b009, MC6).** Segond has "des miracles". I quoted Segond and added: "Le mot grec traduit ici par « miracles » veut dire littéralement « signes »." MC6's correct option says "non parce qu'ils avaient vu des signes". John 6:14 ("le miracle") is not quoted, so it needed no note.
2. **Matthew 16:18, "on this rock" (b015, Fill 7, MC14).** Segond reads "je te dis que tu es Pierre, et que sur cette pierre je bâtirai mon Église". The French quotes this fuller clause so the Pierre/pierre wordplay makes sense, then adds: "Le mot grec traduit par « pierre » désigne littéralement un roc, un rocher." The lesson's reading (the rock = the confessed truth) is kept. Fill 7 answer: **pierre** (accept: roc, rocher, roche). MC14 distractor: "Tu es le roc".
3. **Luke 9:31, "decease" (b018, b021, MC19).** Segond: "son départ qu'il allait accomplir à Jérusalem". I added "Ce « départ », c'est sa mort." b021 echoes "le départ qu'il allait accomplir".
4. **John 6:63, "It is the Spirit who gives life" (b012).** Segond has a lowercase "esprit" ("C'est l'esprit qui vivifie"). I kept Segond. The lesson does not build a point on the capital letter.
5. **Matthew 17:5, "in whom I am well pleased".** Segond: "en qui j'ai mis toute mon affection". The command "Hear Him" became "écoutez-le!". The lesson's *Hear Him* / "hear Moses" contrast became *écoutez-le* / « écoutez Moïse ».
6. **Matthew 16:26, "gains the whole world".** Segond: "gagner tout le monde". A modern reader may take this to mean "everybody". I quoted Segond without a note. Should a gloss be added?
7. **Matthew 16:24.** "Deny himself / take up his cross" is Segond's "renonce à lui-même / se charge de sa croix". MC15 and Fill 9 follow it.
8. **"The eternal Word" (b018)** became "la Parole éternelle" (convention 6). "Elijah" became Élie (Segond). Fill 10 also accepts Elijah and Elias.
9. **"Cruciform" (MC15 why)** became "le disciple suit le chemin de la croix". "Bread-king" became "roi du pain".

## Problems in the English source

1. **b015 and Fill 8: "To them He gave the keys."** In Matthew 16:19 the promise is made to Peter (singular "you") and is in the future ("I will give"). I translated the lesson as written. The Fill 8 prompt says "Aux disciples, Jésus donna…".
2. **b019: "Moses would die and Elijah had been carried to heaven."** Moses had already died centuries before (Deut 34). I rendered it as "Moïse avait connu la mort". Please confirm.
3. **SA keywords that repeat the prompt.** In SA2 (bread/life/manna), SA3 (flesh/blood), SA5 (caesarea/confess/peter), SA6 (peter/rebuke), SA7 (cross/follow), SA9 (hear/jesus/only) and SA10 (turning), English keywords appear in the prompt itself. Under convention 10, the French groups were rebuilt with 8 distinct ideas each. No French prompt reaches 3 hits.
4. **MC3 why** ("A sign too large to leave out") gives no reference, unlike the others. I translated it as written.
