The MC distractors that quote Scripture (Jean 19:30, Marc 4:39, Marc 5:41, Jean 10:7, Jean 15:1) were checked too.

## Questions for Wayne

1. **Luke 9:51, "steadfastly set His face" (b004–b006, b023, MC1, Fill 1, SA1).** Segond says: "Jésus prit la résolution de se rendre à Jérusalem". There is no "face". I quoted Segond and added a line saying the Greek says literally "il affermit sa face". Fill 1's answer is "résolution", and it also accepts "face", "visage" and "décision". The section heading became "Il prit la résolution de se rendre à Jérusalem".
2. **Luke 9:56, "to save men's lives" (b008, MC3).** Segond says: "non pour perdre les âmes des hommes, mais pour les sauver". I used Segond's wording ("âmes") in both places.
3. **Luke 10:40, "distracted with much serving" (b012, MC8, Fill 4, SA3).** Segond says: "occupée à divers soins domestiques". I quoted it and added the literal Greek sense. Fill 4's answer is "domestiques", and it also accepts "service" and "ménagers". Please confirm this blank is acceptable, since it now tests Segond's adjective.
4. **Luke 15:2, "receives sinners" (b014, MC9).** Segond says: "accueille des gens de mauvaise vie". I added a line that the Greek says "des pécheurs".
5. **Luke 18:13, "God, be merciful to me" (b016, Fill 7).** Segond says: "O Dieu, sois apaisé envers moi". I added a line on the Greek sense ("sois propice"). Fill 7's answer is "apaisé", and it also accepts "propice", "miséricordieux", "favorable" and "clément".
6. **John 11:25, "though he may die, he shall live" (b018, SA6).** Segond reverses the order: "vivra, quand même il serait mort". The meaning is the same, and I quoted Segond as is.
7. **Luke 15:29, "young goat" (b016, Fill 6).** Segond says "chevreau". The fill also accepts "cabri", "jeune bouc" and "jeune chèvre".
8. **"Fifth great I AM" (b018, MC15, Fill 9).** Rendered "la cinquième grande parole « Je suis »" — consistent with Unit 5 ("grandes paroles « Je suis »").

## Problems in the English source

1. **SA10 model contradicts the lesson.** It says: "As God He wept at a grave; as man He set His face to die." The lesson (b019, b023) says the opposite: as God He is the resurrection, and as man He weeps. I translated it faithfully. I suggest fixing the English.
2. **MC14 `why`.** It says "that God be glorified", but the lesson cites John 11:15/11:40 ("that you may believe" / "see the glory of God") and never quotes John 11:4. This is minor.
3. **MC19 / Fill 8.** The lesson says "four days dead" but never cites John 11:17, which the `why` names. This is harmless.
4. **SA prompts 3, 5, 6 and 7 name their own keywords** (Martha/Mary, elder brother/Pharisee/tax collector, resurrection/life, wept/come forth). Following convention 10, I reworded the French prompts so they do not reach 3 hits by themselves. The meaning is unchanged.
