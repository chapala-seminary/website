## Questions for Wayne

1. **Psalm 23:6 (b022, MC 13, SA 7).** NKJV "dwell in the house of the LORD forever" is LSG "j'habiterai dans la maison de l'Éternel Jusqu'à la fin de mes jours" ("to the end of my days"). The lesson uses the verse for the "last" hour, and LSG still fits, but it loses the "forever" note. I quoted LSG.
2. **Psalm 1:3 (b017, MC 9, Fill 7, SA 4).** NKJV "rivers of water" is LSG "un courant d'eau" (singular). I used "près du courant d'eau" throughout. In Fill 7 the clause now reads "parce qu'il est planté près d'un courant d'eau", so the answer word "racine" no longer appears in the prompt.
3. **Psalm 1:6 (b016, SA 3).** NKJV "the way of the ungodly shall perish" is LSG "la voie des pécheurs mène à la ruine". "The way that perishes" is therefore rendered "la voie qui mène à la ruine".
4. **Psalm 55:22 (b013, b019, b028).** NKJV "Cast your burden on the LORD" is LSG "Remets ton sort à l'Éternel" ("your lot"). LSG has no "burden" and no "cast". I kept "fardeau" from the lesson and used LSG's verb, "remettre (à l'Éternel)".
5. **Psalm 119:11 (b016).** NKJV "hide Your word in my heart" is LSG "Je serre ta parole dans mon coeur". So "love and hide the Word" becomes "aimer la Parole de Dieu et à la serrer en lui".
6. **Psalm 103:1 echo (b033).** "Bless the LORD, O my soul" is quoted as LSG "Mon âme, bénis l'Éternel!", and I added quotation marks around it.
7. **"Saint" for an ordinary believer (b014, b022, MC 6, MC 13).** In Kinshasa, "saint" can suggest a canonized saint. I used "croyant mourant" and "vieux croyant" for these. For Rutherford I kept "le vieux saint écossais" because it is an established title for him. Is that acceptable?
8. **"Hopeful" in *Pilgrim's Progress* (b023, SA 7).** I used "Plein-d'Espoir". Units 10–11 have no French draft yet, so please make that name consistent across the units once it is chosen. (Some French editions use "Espérance".)
9. **"Capstone" (b002).** I rendered it as "synthèse finale". "Minister" is rendered "serviteur de Dieu", as in the earlier units.
10. **SA 1 and SA 6 prompts reworded.** French keyword stems would have appeared in the prompts, so SA 1 avoids "intérieur"/"nourriture" and SA 6 does not name Rutherford (it says "the old Scottish saint quoted in section 4"). The meaning is unchanged.

## Problems in the English source

1. **b011 / MC 3:** The lesson says the Psalms are "the only book of the Bible written entirely as words addressed *to* God". That is not accurate. Psalm 1 is addressed to the reader, and several psalms contain God's own speech (for example 46:10, 50, 81, 91:14-16 and 110:1). Unit 11 says more carefully "largely words addressed to God". I translated it faithfully.
2. **b020 / SA 6:** Rutherford "knew much of imprisonment". Historically, he was confined (exiled) to Aberdeen and died before he could be tried for treason. "Confinement/exile" would be more exact.
3. **English SA models miss some of their own keywords:**
   - SA 2 misses sin, afraid, silent and blessings, so it hits only 4 of 8.
   - SA 6 misses honest.
   - SA 7 misses sound.
   - SA 8 misses anger and fear.
   - SA 9 misses presence.

   Each French model hits all 8 groups.
4. **Fill 9:** The validator warns that "révérence" appears inside "irrévérence" in the prompt. This is a false alarm; the English prompt has the same pattern.
