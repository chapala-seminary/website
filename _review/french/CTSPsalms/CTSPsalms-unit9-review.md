## Questions for Wayne

1. **Psalm 103:5a (b025, b026, MC 16, Fill 8, SA 8).** NKJV "satisfies your mouth with good things" is LSG "rassasie de biens ta vieillesse" ("your old age"). LSG has no "mouth". I quoted LSG. Where the lesson paraphrases, I wrote "il nous rassasie de biens". Fill 8 answer: "provision" (accept "pourvoyance", "providence", "provisions").
2. **Psalm 103:5b (b028, b029, MC 18).** NKJV "your youth is renewed like the eagle's" is LSG "Qui te fait rajeunir comme l'aigle" ("makes you grow young again"). LSG has no noun "youth". I quoted LSG and wrote "force et jeunesse renouvelées" in the prose.
3. **Psalm 103:4a (b019, MC 11, SA 5).** NKJV "redeems your life from destruction" is LSG "délivre ta vie de la fosse". LSG already says "pit", which suits the lesson. However, LSG says "delivers", not "redeems". The heading and exam items use "délivrance"/"délivrer". SA 5 keywords also accept "rachet-/rédemp-".
4. **Psalm 103:4b (b022, MC 13, SA 7).** NKJV "lovingkindness and tender mercies" is LSG "bonté et miséricorde". I used the LSG pair throughout and "amour fidèle" for "steadfast love".
5. **Isaiah 40:31 (b029, Fill 9, SA 9).** NKJV "those who wait on the LORD" is LSG "ceux qui se confient en l'Éternel" ("trust in"). LSG also has "prennent le vol" for "mount up". The SA 9 keyword group accepts "confient/confiance/attendent".
6. **Romans 3:23 (b013, Fill 3).** NKJV "fall short of" is LSG "sont privés de" ("deprived of"). Fill 3 answer is still "gloire".
7. **Healing word to the bleeding woman (b017).** I used Matthieu 9:22 LSG, "Prends courage, ma fille, ta foi t'a guérie", which matches the NKJV well. LSG wording for the MC 7 distractors: "Ta foi t'a sauvée" (Luc 7:50) and "Va en paix, et sois guérie" (Marc 5:34).
8. **Hymn "Count Your Blessings" (b009, b010, b032, MC 1).** I gave the title as "Compte les bienfaits de Dieu", which follows the usual French hymnal title. The quoted refrain ("Comptez les bienfaits de Dieu, nommez-les un à un…") is my own translation, not a hymnal text.
9. **Eternal security wording (b019, b023, MC 14, SA 7).** The English says "Nothing can ever cause us to lose that love", "will never be cast into that everlasting pit" and (SA 7 model) "can never be lost". I translated these at the same strength, neither stronger nor weaker. Please confirm this is acceptable given the open question at CTS.
10. **"Thorn in the flesh" (b023, MC 15, SA 7).** LSG 2 Corinthiens 12:7 has "écharde dans la chair". I used that wording. SA 7 also accepts "épine".

## Problems in the English source

1. **SA 5:** The model says "the grave" is death from which God "will finally raise His people", and it explains "redeem" as buying back with Christ's blood. The lesson teaches neither: it treats the grave as a near-death crisis from which God pulled the psalmist back. The model also never uses its own keywords "trusted Jesus" or "never cast". I translated it faithfully and added those two ideas from b019.
2. **SA 1, SA 3, SA 7 and SA 10:** Each English model leaves out several of its own keywords. SA 1 omits "Alexander" and "no complaining". SA 3 omits "all have sinned" and "second chance". SA 7 omits "Paul" and "thorn". SA 10 omits "converted", "keep telling it" and "sixty years". I added these points, all taken from the lesson, to the French models.
3. **SA 4:** The model's last sentence ("the deepest healing is the wholeness He gives…") goes beyond the lesson. It is minor, and I translated it as written.
4. **SA 1:** The model says the man was "unable to do anything else", which the lesson does not say. It is minor, and I translated it as written.
5. **MC 8 `why`:** "in His time and way" is not in the lesson. **MC 19 `why`:** it says the young "may have no life at all", but the lesson says "no reason to live". Both are minor.
