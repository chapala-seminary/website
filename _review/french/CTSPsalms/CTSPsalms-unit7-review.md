5. **Psalm 119:89 (b026).** NKJV has "Your word is settled in heaven". LSG has "Ta parole subsiste dans les cieux". I quoted LSG.
6. **"Righteous Seed" (b024–b026, MC15, Fill 7, SA9).** LSG Genesis 3:15 uses "postérité", not "semence". I wrote "Postérité juste". The first time it appears, I added "(la « semence » promise)" and the reference "(Genèse 3:15)". The Fill 7 answer is "Postérité", and "semence", "descendance", "race" and "lignée" are also accepted. 7. **Terms.** I used "Covenantaires" (the usual French term) for the Covenanters. I rendered "God's will is our hiding place" (b020) as "la volonté de Dieu est notre refuge". "Armageddon" became LSG's "Harmaguédon".

## Problems in the English source

1. **b015, MC7–8, Fill 3, SA3: history.** Nineveh was near modern Mosul, not Baghdad, so MC8's answer key teaches an error. The lesson also merges two exiles. The northern kingdom went into Assyrian exile (722 BC) and did not return as a nation. Judah went to Babylon for about 70 years, then came back and rebuilt the Temple. "Nearly two hundred years" fits neither exile. I translated it as written.
2. **b023, MC13: hardening count.** The claim that God hardened Pharaoh's heart "four times" is too low. Exodus says so roughly nine or ten times (for example 9:12, 10:20, 10:27, 14:8). The claim that Pharaoh hardened his own heart "three times" (8:15, 8:32, 9:34) is correct.
3. **b023, MC14: Napoleon.** The quotation from Napoleon about Jesus is presented as something he wrote. It actually comes from disputed later recollections. You may want to soften it to "is reported to have said". MC14 already says "reportedly".
4. **SA6 model.** The model has "If I hadn't been delayed in the kitchen". The lesson has "if I hadn't heard you in the kitchen". I translated the model faithfully.
5. **SA5.** The model adds the sparrow and "Jesus said", and the keyword "individual" is never taught. The lesson cites only the numbered hairs.
6. **SA7.** The model's examples (creation, Daniel, the resurrection) only partly match the lesson's list. The lesson lists the Red Sea, the sun standing still, the floating iron, the ravens and the lions. Most of the keywords come from the lesson's list, so a student who follows the model would miss them.
7. **SA3 and SA10 models.** The English models leave out several of their own keywords: "never leave", "Assyria" and "mercy" in SA3, and "pray" and "moon" in SA10. In the French models I added a clause from the lesson for each so they hit more keyword groups. The meaning is unchanged.

- **Kept as written.** I kept the Gulf War, Deism among "America's founders" (rendered as "fondateurs des États-Unis") and Napoleon/Hitler, because the lesson and the exam depend on them.
- **Typography.** French spacing, with a non-breaking space before `: ; ? !` and inside « ».
- **Fill 5.** The blank is « si ». The validator's "answer appears in prompt" warning is a false alarm: it matches the letters "si" inside "Betsie". (Grading is exact match, so this does not affect scoring.)
