## Questions for Wayne

1. **Psalm 19:14 (b004, b010, Fill 2).** NKJV "my strength and my Redeemer" is LSG "mon rocher et mon libérateur" ("my rock and my deliverer"), and "meditation of my heart" is "sentiments de mon coeur". I quoted LSG verbatim. Fill 2 answer: "libérateur" (accept "rédempteur", "sauveur").
2. **Psalm 23:4 (b016).** The lesson quotes the KJV form ("Yea, though… Thou art with me"). I used LSG: "Quand je marche dans la vallée de l'ombre de la mort, Je ne crains aucun mal, car tu es avec moi". This is a plain, familiar wording rather than an old-fashioned one, so the "ancient cadences" point is a little weaker in French.
3. **Psalm 1:2-3 (b024, b035).** NKJV "planted by the rivers of water" is LSG "planté près d'un courant d'eau", and "His delight" is "qui trouve son plaisir". I quoted LSG in both places. In b014, "the blessed Man of Psalm 1" is rendered "l'homme heureux du Psaume 1", following LSG's "Heureux l'homme".
4. **Psalm 55:22 "cast your burden" (b009, b017, MC 10, SA 4).** LSG has "Remets ton sort à l'Éternel" ("commit your lot"), and the word "fardeau" (burden) is not in the verse. I kept "fardeau" in the prose ("se décharger de son fardeau sur l'Éternel"), as the earlier units did. The SA 4 keyword is "fardeau".
5. **Psalm 24:1 (b028).** I quoted the LSG parallel lines "À l'Éternel la terre et ce qu'elle renferme / Le monde et ceux qui l'habitent". The parallelism comes through clearly.
6. **"All things work together for good" (b031).** This is Romans 8:28, not a psalm. I quoted LSG ("toutes choses concourent au bien") and added the reference "(Romains 8:28)".
7. **Paul's "thorn" (b031).** LSG 2 Corinthians 12:7 says "écharde", and I used that word.
8. **"Closet" (b023, Fill 6).** I wrote "la chambre secrète de la prière", which echoes LSG Matthew 6:6 ("entre dans ta chambre"). Fill 6 answer: "classe" (from "salle de classe").
9. **"Capstone".** I rendered this as "synthèse" in the title, the MC and the SA items.
10. **SA prompts reworded.** In SA 8 and SA 9 the English prompts contain words that are also French keyword stems (parallelism, lament, Christ, caution, promises). I reworded these prompts in French so that the prompt alone cannot pass. The meaning is unchanged.

## Problems in the English source

1. **MC 3 / b011:** The lesson names four arenas, one of them "leading others in prayer", but it never has a section on that arena. The fifth section is about personal devotion instead.
2. **SA 9:** For the "cheapened comfort" caution, the model uses the advice about not rushing a lament (which is in b029). The lesson's actual example in b031 is tossing "all things work together for good" at a grieving person. The model also never uses two of its own keywords, "grief" and "thorn".
3. **Fill 5 / SA 4:** The "physician choosing the right bandage / remedy" image appears only in these exam items. The lesson does not use it, though the item can still be answered.
4. **SA 6:** The model does not mention three of its own keywords: music, Scripture and invitation. The lesson's exercise also calls for a written outline, which the model leaves out.
5. **SA 10:** The model leaves out most of its keywords ("understand", "sing", "grieving", "congregation"). It also attributes bedside, graveside and worship to "the close", whereas b034 speaks of the deathbed, the congregation and the secret place. The French model is faithful to the English and still reaches 4 hits.
6. **SA 3:** The keyword "bedside" does not appear in the English model. The French model uses "chevet" and passes anyway.
7. **MC 17 why / SA 8:** "The basic music of the Psalms / of Hebrew poetry" is not something the lesson says. This is minor.

## Adaptations

- **b021:** "First Baptist Church, Anywhere" became "une grande église bien établie de la ville". This avoids a US idiom and also avoids a denominational label.
- **b021:** "youth lock-in" became "une veillée de prière des jeunes, à minuit", since a lock-in is unknown in Kinshasa. The same change was made in MC 14 and SA 6.
- **b021:** The "invitation" (altar call) is rendered as "un appel". In SA 6 the keyword includes "invitation".
