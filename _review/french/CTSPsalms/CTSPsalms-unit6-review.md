# CTSPsalms Unit 6 — French draft review

## Questions for Wayne

1. **Ps 22:31 "that He has done this" vs. LSG "Elle annoncera son œuvre au peuple nouveau-né".** This is the largest LSG difference in the unit. b025, MC15, fill 9 and SA7 all link this phrase to "It is finished", and LSG does not contain it. In b025 I quote LSG and add one clause: "là où Louis Segond traduit « son œuvre », l'hébreu dit littéralement « qu'il a fait cela », avec l'idée d'une œuvre achevée". MC15, fill 9 and SA7 use « qu'il a fait cela » together with « son œuvre ». Is that clause acceptable?
2. **"It is finished" (John 19:30) = LSG "Tout est accompli".** Fill 9 answer: *accompli*. Accepted: consommé, achevé, fini, terminé. Because *accompli* is also the lesson's word for "fulfilled" (section 3), the play on words is stronger in French.
3. **Ps 22:27 "shall remember" vs. LSG "penseront à l'Éternel".** The lesson's closing triad (b031, MC20, SA10) is "remember, turn, worship". In b031 I wrote "se souvenir de l'Éternel (« penser à l'Éternel », dit le verset 27)". MC20 and SA10 use "se souvenir".
4. **Ps 22:14 "out of joint" → LSG "tous mes os se séparent"; "poured out" → "comme de l'eau qui s'écoule".** b021 and the SA6 keywords follow LSG and also accept "répandu" and "articulation".
5. **Ps 22:18 "for My clothing they cast lots" → LSG "Ils tirent au sort ma tunique".** Fill 7 answer: *sort*.
6. **Ps 22:16 "pierced" → LSG "Ils ont percé".** The b027 note reads « comme le lion » au lieu de « ont percé ». Fill 6 answer: *percé* (accepted: transpercé, percés).
7. **Acts 8:35 "preached Jesus to him" → LSG "lui annonça la bonne nouvelle de Jésus".** I used "annoncer Jésus" throughout, and the SA2/SA8 keywords accept both annon- and prêch-.
8. **MC12 option "Create in me a clean heart"** is Ps 51:10 in LSG, "Ô Dieu! crée en moi un cœur pur". The Ps 23:1 and 119:105 options are verbatim LSG.
9. **"Foreshadowing" → "préfiguration"** (fill 4). Accepted: figure, type, typologie.

## Problems in the English source

1. **b018 (and b025, MC9 `why`): "the first recorded words Jesus cried from the cross".** In the usual harmony of the seven sayings, "Father, forgive them" (Luke 23:34) comes first and the cry of dereliction is the fourth. b025 builds on this ("opened with the first cry of the cross … closes with the last"). I translated it faithfully.
2. **b027: the Ps 22:16 textual note is overstated.** The standard Masoretic Text reads "like a lion"; it is not just "some manuscripts". The Dead Sea Scrolls evidence (5/6HevPs) is debated, and the "pierced" reading rests mainly on the Septuagint. Jewish translations read "lion", so "nearly every translation" applies only to Christian ones. Translated as written.
3. **SA2 model** adds "the man believed and was baptized", and **SA6 model** adds "strength dried up, tongue cleaving, enemies staring". Neither is in the lesson. Both are true to Scripture, and I kept them.
4. **MC11 / SA5** add "we who believe". The lesson says only "so that we might never be forsaken". This is harmless, and I kept it.
