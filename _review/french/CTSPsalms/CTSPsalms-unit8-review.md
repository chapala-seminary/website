## Questions for Wayne

1. **Psalm 28:1 (epigraph b004, b010, Fill 1, SA 1).** NKJV "do not be silent to me" is LSG "ne reste pas sourd à ma voix" ("do not stay deaf to my voice"); LSG has no word "silent" in this verse, though "si tu t'éloignes sans me répondre" still carries the idea. LSG also puts "Éternel!" before "Mon rocher!". I quoted LSG verbatim, giving the full verse in b004 because the first half alone reads oddly in French. Fill 1 answer: "rocher" (accept "roc").
2. **Psalm 46:10 (b019, MC 11, Fill 5, SA 5).** NKJV "Be still" is LSG "Arrêtez" ("Stop"). I quoted LSG. In the prose, "be still" becomes "arrêtez-vous / faites silence / restez tranquilles". Fill 5 answer: "Arrêtez" (accept "arrêtez-vous", "soyez tranquilles", etc.).
3. **"Still, small voice" (b020, MC 12, Fill 6, SA 5).** LSG 1 Kings 19:12 is "un murmure doux et léger". I used that wording and added the reference "(1 Rois 19:12)". Fill 6 answer: "murmure" (accept "voix").
4. **Psalm 139:23-24 (b030, MC 19, SA 9).** NKJV "my anxieties" is LSG "mes pensées"; "wicked way" is "mauvaise voie"; "way everlasting" is "voie de l'éternité". I quoted LSG.
5. **Psalm 23:2 (b016).** NKJV "makes me to lie down in green pastures" is LSG "Il me fait reposer dans de verts pâturages". I wrote "Dieu la faisait « reposer dans de verts pâturages »".
6. **"Chastening" (b028–b029, MC 18, SA 8).** I used "châtiment", the LSG word (Hébreux 12:6 "le Seigneur châtie celui qu'il aime"). In modern French it can sound purely punitive, so the text pairs it with "discipline pleine d'amour" / "correction". Would "la loi de la discipline paternelle" be better?
7. **SA prompts reworded.** French keyword stems would have appeared in the prompts, so SA 5, SA 6 and SA 8 name the stories and laws less directly than the English (for example, "la loi de Galates 6:7"). The meaning is the same.
