# CTSPsalms Unit 4 — French draft review notes

## Questions for Wayne

1. **Psalm 51 verse numbers (b004, b012).** The LSG tool prints the superscription and "Ô Dieu ! aie pitié de moi…" together as 51:1, so I kept the English references 51:1 and 51:1-2. Printed LSG Bibles number the superscription separately as verses 1–2. In those Bibles the prayer begins at 51:3, and the old psalm text runs to 51:21. A Kinshasa reader with a printed Segond may look for "51:1" and find the title instead. Should we keep the English numbering, as I did, or change it to the printed LSG numbering?
2. **Psalm 51:1 wording.** NKJV has "lovingkindness … multitude of Your tender mercies … blot out". LSG has "dans ta bonté ; Selon ta grande miséricorde, efface". The lesson does not rely on the difference, and I used LSG throughout, including the closing echo in b033.
3. **"You are the man" (b013, mc6, fill3, sa3).** I used LSG 2 Samuel 12:7, « Tu es cet homme-là ! ». In fill3 the answer is "homme", and "homme-là" is also accepted.
4. **"Such a man should be put to death!" (b013).** I used LSG 2 Samuel 12:5, « L'homme qui a fait cela mérite la mort. » "Ewe lamb" is "petite brebis", following LSG 12:3.
5. **"Came to himself" (b014, mc8, fill4, sa4).** I used LSG Luke 15:17, « rentré en lui-même ». The fill4 answer is "lui-même".
6. **Adam's excuse (b017, mc11).** The English "This woman You gave me caused me to do it" paraphrases Genesis 3:12 but is presented as a quotation. I quoted LSG Genesis 3:12 verbatim instead: « La femme que tu as mise auprès de moi m'a donné de l'arbre, et j'en ai mangé. » The mc11 option is shortened to « …m'a donné de l'arbre ».
7. **"Lord, have mercy on me, a sinner" (b024).** This is the tax collector's prayer in Luke 18:13. LSG has « Ô Dieu, sois apaisé envers moi, qui suis un pécheur », which has a propitiation sense and not simply "have mercy". I used LSG. Please confirm this is acceptable, or I can paraphrase it without quotation marks.
8. **"I will remember your sins no more" (b024).** The English gives no reference. I quoted the LSG wording of Isaiah 43:25: « Je ne me souviendrai plus de tes péchés. »
9. **"A man after God's own heart" (b009, mc3).** This is rendered "un homme selon le cœur de Dieu", following LSG 1 Samuel 13:14 and Acts 13:22, "homme selon mon/son cœur".
10. **"What I have written, I have written" (b023)** is LSG John 19:22 verbatim. **"The sword shall never depart" (b029)** is LSG 2 Samuel 12:10. **Romans 3:23 (b032)** and **1 John 1:9 (b028)** are LSG verbatim.
11. **Scripture check (checker).** Every quotation was re-checked against the offline LSG tool: Psaume 51:1-2, 2 Samuel 12:5, 7, 10, 13, Genèse 3:12, Luc 15:17, Luc 18:13, Ésaïe 43:25, Jean 19:22, Romains 3:23, 1 Jean 1:9. All match LSG exactly; no corrections were needed.
12. **fill3** accepts only "homme" and "homme-là". This is a single-answer quotation blank, so no further alternates were added (validator warning left as a false alarm).

## Problems in the English source

- **sa1 model:** It says "for nearly a year", but b009 says "more than a year" and "an entire year". I translated it faithfully as « près d'un an ».
- **sa8 model:** It says Flora's father "ran to meet her". In Maclaren's story, as b026 tells it, the father wrote a letter and later recorded that he "fell upon her neck". He does not run; the running comes from the prodigal son and the section heading. I translated it faithfully.
- **sa5 model:** The model names "covered the sin more deeply" and "used his power to silence the matter". The lesson's four roads (b017) are lying, blaming, making excuses, and growing angry and killing Nathan. The English model does not use the keywords "lied", "angry" or "Adam". The French model adds "mentir" and "comme Adam avait accusé la femme" so that it follows the lesson.
- **sa4 and sa6 models:** They do not use several of their own keywords: Holy Spirit, prodigal, came to himself, need (sa4), and rock bottom, open arms (sa6). I added one sentence to each French model, taken from b014 and b021.
- **mc3 why:** It says David is called a man after God's own heart "because he truly repented". The lesson gives no reason. In Scripture, the title in 1 Samuel 13:14 comes before the Bathsheba sin. I translated it faithfully.
- **b024:** "The Bible is the only book of religion that says God completely forgives" is a sweeping comparative claim that a reviewer may want to soften. I translated it faithfully.
- **b023:** It says "Psalm 51 makes clear that David vowed to commit his life to the Lord". Psalm 51:13 ("J'enseignerai tes voies…") implies this at most. This is minor.
- No wrong MC answer keys were found.

## Adaptations

- **b023:** Lady Macbeth is introduced as « Dans la pièce de Shakespeare… », because many readers will not know the reference.
- **b026:** Ian Maclaren is introduced as « L'écrivain écossais ». "Lassie" became « Ma fille ». "Roll" became « registre des membres ».
- **b035–b037:** The exam instructions and buttons follow the wording of other French units ("Parcours Certificat", "phrases à compléter", "réponse courte").
- **SA keywords:** I rebuilt them as French stems and did not translate them literally. Every model hits 6–8 groups, and no prompt hits more than 2.
