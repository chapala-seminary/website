## Questions for Wayne

1. **Ps 23:4 "rod and staff" → LSG "Ta houlette et ton bâton me rassurent."** In modern French a *houlette* is a shepherd's crook, which is closer to the English "staff" than to "rod". The lesson depends on rod = club (defence) and staff = crook (guidance). In b026 I wrote "un gourdin, une sorte de massue (notre version l'appelle « houlette »)" for the rod and "le bâton, une longue crosse recourbée" for the staff. Fill 5's answer is *gourdin*, and it also accepts *houlette*, *massue* and *bâton court*. MC8 says "la « houlette » (le gourdin) et le « bâton »". I reworded the SA5 prompt as "les deux instruments que porte le berger (Psaume 23:4)" so that the prompt alone does not reach the keyword threshold. Please confirm.
2. **Ps 23:2 "He makes me to lie down" → LSG "Il me fait reposer."** LSG loses the idea of compulsion, and the Charles L. Allen story depends on that idea. The friend still quotes LSG ("Il me fait reposer"). The explanation around it says "Dieu doit nous obliger à nous coucher". MC3 and SA2 follow the same wording.
3. **Ps 23:6 "Surely goodness and mercy … forever" → LSG "Oui, le bonheur et la grâce m'accompagneront … Jusqu'à la fin de mes jours."** b033 and MC11 now explain "Oui" and add "(d'autres versions disent « Certainement »)". I glossed "bonheur et grâce" as "la bonté et l'amour fidèle de Dieu". b035 quotes LSG and adds "(D'autres versions traduisent : « pour toujours »)", because MC20 and SA9 rely on "forever". Is it all right to name other versions like this?
4. **Ps 23:5 "in the presence of my enemies" → LSG "En face de mes adversaires."** I quoted it verbatim. The surrounding prose uses both *adversaires* and *ennemis*, and SA6 accepts either word.
5. **"Cast down" / Ps 42:5 → LSG "Pourquoi t'abats-tu, mon âme".** The sheep is "renversée". "Downcast" became *abattu*, which matches the LSG verb. French *abattu* does not come from shepherding, so b019 and Fill 3 say that the word "évoque la même image" and do not claim it is derived from the sheep picture. The Fill 3 answer is *abattu*.
6. **The dying boy's five fingers (b036, MC14, Fill 9, SA9).** I used "Le Seigneur est mon berger" rather than LSG "L'Éternel est mon berger", because "L'Éternel est mon berger" has only four words. "Le Seigneur est mon berger" has five words, with "mon" in fourth place, so the story still works. Is this all right?
7. **John 10:11 "lays down his life" → LSG "donne sa vie."** The heading, MC15, Fill 10 and SA10 all use "donne sa vie".
8. **Isa 53:6 (b010)** is quoted in LSG's past tense: "Nous étions tous errants comme des brebis, Chacun suivait sa propre voie". The meaning is unaffected.
9. **Fill 9 / Fill 10 (checker):** I removed the alternates "mon berger", "le seigneur est mon berger" (Fill 9) and "propre vie", "existence" (Fill 10), because the blank is one word and the Fill 10 prompt is a verbatim LSG quotation. Only "mon" and "vie" are now accepted. Fill 6 likewise no longer accepts "plateau(x)", since the question asks what the plateaus are called.

## Problems in the English source

1. **MC16:** it attributes "the hardest struggle … to truly believe God will provide" to David. In b014 that struggle is the author's own ("my Christian life"). The lesson does not say this about David. I kept the item and the answer key as written.
2. **Fill 3 / b019:** the claim that the English word "downcast" comes from the cast-sheep picture is doubtful etymology. It was not carried into French (see Question 5).
3. **SA2 model:** it says a sheep will not lie down while "hungry, afraid, or harassed". The lesson gives different reasons: overheating and undigested grass. Translated as written.
4. **SA5 model:** it says "wolves and lions" and "pull back a wanderer". The lesson says "coyotes, wolves, cougars" and does not mention pulling back a wanderer. SA6 model: "checking the water" is not in the lesson. SA7 model: Lauder's "choice" between grief and healing is not in the lesson. All translated as written.
5. **SA8 model:** it uses "covenant love", but the lesson only says "steadfast love". This is minor, and I translated it as written.
6. **b024:** the predators named are coyotes and cougars, which are North American animals from Keller's ranch, yet they are placed in the valley near Jerusalem (see Adaptations).
