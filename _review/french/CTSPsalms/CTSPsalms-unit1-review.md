## Problems in the English source

1. **b009 / Fill 1:** "Many [psalms] were meant to be sung with … the gittith." Only three psalm titles (8, 81, 84) mention the gittith, and scholars disagree on whether it was an instrument or a tune. I translated it as written.
2. **b036:** "Jesus spoke of hell… and the chaff shall be burned with unquenchable fire." The saying about chaff comes from John the Baptist (Mt 3:12), not from Jesus. I translated it as written.
3. **b032 (Flack):** he speaks to "Jews and Arabs" in "their own Hebrew tongue", but Hebrew was not the Arabs' language. This is a minor issue. I translated it as written.
4. **MC8 explanation / SA5 model:** "abiding, not striving or busyness". The lesson never teaches this contrast; it only tells the Scottish lady story about peace. This is a mild overreach, but I translated it faithfully.
5. **SA4 model:** "not to empty the mind". The lesson never says this, although it does not contradict the lesson either.
