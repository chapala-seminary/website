## Questions for Wayne

1. **Psalm 55:22 (b004, b010, b012–b014, b031, b036, MC 1, MC 3, MC 16, Fill 2, SA 1–2, SA 9).** NKJV "Cast your burden on the LORD… He shall never permit the righteous to be moved" is LSG "Remets ton sort à l'Éternel, et il te soutiendra, Il ne laissera jamais chanceler le juste." LSG has "sort" (lot) where NKJV has "burden", and "remets" (hand over) is gentler than "cast". Section 2 says the verb is a forceful throwing, so I wrote "Le mot hébreu que Louis Segond traduit par « remets » est vigoureux ; d'autres versions françaises disent « rejette ton fardeau »" and added one sentence explaining that "sort" here means the load we carry. Everywhere else the lesson says "burden", I used "fardeau". Section headings use "remettre". Please confirm this framing.

3. **Psalm 55:6 (b010, Fill 1).** NKJV "wings like a dove" is LSG "les ailes de la colombe". Fill 1 answer: "colombe".

## Problems in the English source

1. **SA 1:** The model says part of David's anguish is "betrayal by a close friend" (Psalm 55:12-14). The lesson never mentions this. The keywords "fallen world" and "command" are not in the model.
2. **SA 7:** The model gives the grief seasons as "shock, sorrow, and slow healing". The lesson (b028) lists shock and disbelief, anger, bargaining and guilt, depression, and acceptance. Four of the model's own keywords (anger, guilt, depression, acceptance) are missing from the model. The French model still reaches 4 hits.
3. **SA 10:** The model says Frankl "survived the death camps" and that the "why" is "that God is good, present, and working out His purpose". The lesson says neither, and it applies Frankl's saying through b036's "a God who sustains, a heaven that waits, and a love…". The keywords "Person", "Shema" and "love" are not in the model.
4. **SA 8:** The model says Bayly "had lost children". The lesson says he buried three of his children. These do not conflict, but the model also invents "arguments", which the lesson does not mention.
5. **MC 7 distractor:** "The Cost of Discipleship" was by Bonhoeffer, who was not a missionary. This is harmless as a distractor. In French I used its French title, « Le prix de la grâce ».
