# CTSRev Unit 11 — French draft review

## Questions for Wayne

1. **Rev 19:6, "the Lord God Omnipotent reigns" (b004, b011, SA1, SA9).** Segond has "le Seigneur notre Dieu tout-puissant est entré dans son règne" ("has entered his reign"). I quoted Segond. The SA keywords accept "règne" and "régn-".
2. **Rev 19:1, "salvation and glory and honor and power belong to the Lord our God" (b011, MC1).** Segond follows a shorter Greek text: "Le salut, la gloire, et la puissance sont à notre Dieu". There is no "honneur" and no "Seigneur". I quoted Segond in the lesson, in MC1's correct option and in its `why`.
3. **"Marriage supper of the Lamb" (b011, MC4).** Segond has "festin de noces de l'agneau". I used that throughout, which also gives "grand festin de Dieu" in 19:17. Segond writes "agneau" in lowercase. I kept that inside quotations and capitalized "l'Agneau" in the lesson's own sentences (convention 9).
4. **"Fine linen is the righteous acts of the saints" (19:8, MC3, Fill 2).** Segond has "les oeuvres justes des saints". Fill 2's answer is "justes", and it also accepts "juste", "de justice" and "droites".
5. **Rev 19:11, "Faithful and True" (MC6, Fill 4).** Segond has "Fidèle et Véritable". Fill 4's answer is "Véritable", and it also accepts "vrai" and "véridique".
6. **Rev 19:13, "The Word of God" (MC8, Fill 5).** I used "la Parole de Dieu" (Segond, convention 6). Fill 5 also accepts "Verbe", because the Spanish edition uses "Verbo" and some students know that form.
7. **"Lake of fire" → Segond "étang de feu" (b015 onward, MC9, MC16, MC19, SA3, SA8).** Following convention 1, I added one sentence in b015: the Greek word means literally "lac". The SA keywords accept "étang", "etang" and "lac de".
8. **"Deceive" → Segond "séduire" (20:3, 20:8, 20:10).** I used Segond's verb. SA5 also accepts "tromper".
9. **Rev 20:4, "they lived and reigned" (b017, SA4).** Segond has "Ils revinrent à la vie" ("they came back to life"). Commentators of different millennial views read this verse differently: bodily resurrection or spiritual life. Segond's wording can sound like it leans toward bodily resurrection. To keep the passage as neutral as the NKJV, b017 quotes Segond and adds only "le texte grec dit littéralement « ils vécurent »" (convention 1). The SA4 model answer is not a quotation, so it now says "ils vécurent et régnèrent", as the English does. Please confirm this is acceptable.
10. **"Hades" → Segond "le séjour des morts" (b021, b023, MC19, SA8).** I added in b021 that this phrase translates the Greek "Hadès", following unit 2. SA8 accepts both forms.
11. **"Priests of God" (20:6).** Segond has "sacrificateurs" (convention 4).
12. **b023, "it can never be erased".** I translated this at the English's strength: "il ne peut jamais être effacé". The sentence is a homiletical image. I did not make it stronger or weaker. Please confirm, given that perseverance is an open question at CTS.

## Problems in the English source

1. **SA models add material the lesson never teaches.** SA8 has "the first death is the death of the body" and "eternal separation from God". SA9 has "the world-system". SA5 says "even at his freest Satan…". I translated them faithfully. None of these is a keyword.
2. **b021 and SA6–SA7: "a book that records the deeds."** Rev 20:12 has "books", plural, and it names the book of life in the same verse. The lesson's single "book of deeds" is a teaching image, not the text's own wording. This is not an error, but you may want to note it.
3. **Fill 1:** the lesson says "Alleluia" occurs "nowhere else in the New Testament". That is correct, since it appears only in Rev 19:1–6.
4. **SA10 keyword "views" is not in the English model.** The SA10 model never uses the word "views", so the English model misses that group. In French, the group accepts "positions / interprétations / plusieurs / plus d'une manière / points de vue". The draft added the clause "que les chrétiens comprennent de plus d'une manière" to the SA10 model. It restates b017's balance and does not favor any view. All MC and fill answer keys are correct. b017, SA4 and SA10 keep the English's even-handedness.
5. **The SA prompts were reworded** (SA2, SA3, SA4, SA6, SA7, SA8) so that the French prompt does not hit its own keywords (convention 10). The maximum is now 1 hit per prompt.

## Checker's changes

- Scripture: every quotation in the lesson, MC, fill-ins and models was checked against the offline Segond (Rev 19:1-20, 20:2-15). All were verbatim, so no corrections were needed.
- Eschatological balance: b017, SA4, SA10, b023 and MC12 were compared with the English. They are as neutral as the English, with no tilt toward any millennial view.
- SA4 model: Segond's "ils revinrent à la vie" changed to "ils vécurent", as in the English. A model answer should not carry Segond's resurrection-leaning wording when the lesson stays neutral.
- b017 and the SA4 model: "trois principales interprétations" changed to "trois principales positions", to match the titles of units 12-14 ("Les positions sur le millénium"). "positions" was added to the SA4 and SA10 "views" keyword groups.
- b017: "le grec dit" changed to "le texte grec dit" (convention 1 wording).
- MC2, MC4, MC12 distractors: "marquage du sceau" changed to plain "sceau" forms ("Le sceau posé sur les 144 000", "À recevoir le sceau", "Au sceau posé sur les saints"). The old forms were unnatural, and MC4's did not fit "appelés".
- MC11: the correct option and the `why` now quote 20:3 in Segond's own imperfect subjunctive ("séduisît… fussent"), matching b017 and Fill 7.
- MC17 stem: "Devant le grand trône blanc, devant la face…" changed to "Au grand trône blanc, devant la face de celui qui y était assis" to remove the repeated "devant".
- SA9 model: "la justification de son peuple" changed to "la justice enfin rendue à son peuple". "Justification" is the soteriological term and misreads "vindication".
- Validator: 0 errors. The 3 warnings remain (Fill 3, 8, 10 have few alternates). They are false alarms, because these single-word answers have no further genuine synonyms.
