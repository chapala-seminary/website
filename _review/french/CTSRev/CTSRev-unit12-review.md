# CTSRev Unit 12 — French draft review

## Questions for Wayne

1. **Rev 20:4 (b004, b009).** The NKJV has "they lived and reigned". Segond has "Ils revinrent à la vie, et ils régnèrent". "Revinrent à la vie" is a fair rendering of the Greek, but it can sound more bodily than "lived". The lesson's postmillennial reading (b017, MC11, Fill 9) still says "spiritual" exactly as the English does. I did not add a gloss, because a gloss could tilt toward one view. Please check that the passage reads as neutral.
2. **Rev 20:4, "judgment was committed to them" (b009).** Segond has "fut donné le pouvoir de juger". I quoted it as is.
3. **Matt 28:18-20 (b015, MC6, Fill 5, SA5).** The NKJV has "all authority" and "to the end of the age". Segond has "tout pouvoir" and "jusqu'à la fin du monde". I quoted Segond. The SA5 keywords accept both "tout pouvoir" and "toute autorité".
4. **Matt 13:31, "mustard seed" (b015, MC7, Fill 6, SA5).** Segond has "grain de sénevé". Following convention 1, I added "le texte grec dit littéralement « graine de moutarde »". Fill 6's answer is "sénevé", and it also accepts "moutarde". The SA5 keywords accept both.
5. **Rev 20:3, "deceive the nations" (b013, b017, MC10, Fill 8, SA6).** Segond has "séduisît". I used "séduire" throughout. Fill 8 also accepts "tromper" and "égarer".
6. **Objections passages (b021, SA9).** The NKJV has "perilous times", "evil men and impostors will grow worse and worse" and "a great falling away". Segond has "temps difficiles", "méchants et imposteurs avanceront toujours plus dans le mal" and "l'apostasie". I quoted Segond. The SA9 keywords accept "difficiles" and "périlleux".
7. **Ps 2:8 (b024).** Segond has "Demande-moi et je te donnerai", with lowercase pronouns, as Segond writes them. I quoted it as is.
8. **Isa 2:2 "exalted" / "streaming" (b015).** Segond has "s'élèvera par-dessus les collines" and "toutes les nations y afflueront". I quoted short phrases from Segond.
9. **Terms.** I followed unit 1's terms: "postmillénarisme / prémillénarisme / amillénarisme" and "millénium". I used "positions" for "views". The lesson's even-handed framing is kept sentence for sentence: b011 "n'en favorise aucune", b021 "l'équité demande qu'on les écoute", b023 "sans l'adopter pour autant", MC20 and SA10.
