## Questions for Wayne

1. **Rev 2:13, "My faithful martyr" (NKJV) vs. LSG "mon témoin fidèle".** I quoted LSG in b017 and added one sentence: the Greek word for "témoin" is the one that gave us "martyr". MC15 says "témoin fidèle" and its `why` quotes LSG.
2. **Rev 2:9 and 2:13, "I know your works".** LSG has "Je connais tes oeuvres" only at 2:2 and 2:19. At 2:9 it has "Je connais ta tribulation…", and at 2:13 "Je sais où tu demeures". So b021 ("Four times He begins, 'I know your works'") would be false against Segond. I rewrote it as "Quatre fois, il commence par dire qu'il connaît leur situation" and quoted all three LSG forms with references. SA10 is worded the same way, and its keywords ("connaî/connai") still match.
3. **Rev 2:23, "searches the minds and hearts" vs. LSG "sonde les reins et les coeurs".** I quoted LSG and added a one-line gloss: "reins" means one's innermost thoughts and feelings. SA7, SA8 and the SA8 keyword ("sonde") follow LSG.
4. **Rev 2:17, "a white stone" vs. LSG "un caillou blanc".** I used "caillou" in b017, MC17 and Fill 8 (answer "caillou"; it also accepts pierre, petite pierre, cailloux, galet).
5. **Rev 2:13, "where Satan's throne is" vs. LSG "là est le trône de Satan".** I quoted « est le trône de Satan » inside a "là où" sentence. Fill 7 answer is "trône"; it also accepts "siège" (the KJV sense).
6. **Rev 2:26-27, "power over the nations… rule them with a rod of iron" vs. LSG "autorité sur les nations… Il les paîtra avec une verge de fer".** I used "autorité" (b019, MC20, SA9) and "paître".
7. **Rev 2:8, "the First and the Last".** LSG writes it lowercase ("le premier et le dernier"). I kept LSG lowercase in the quotes and in MC11, even though convention 9 capitalizes Christ's titles. Please confirm.
8. **"Church" capitalization.** I followed LSG and the unit 4 draft: "Église" is capitalized for the churches throughout, including in general statements.
9. **Rev 2:20, "tolerate" vs. LSG "tu laisses… enseigner".** The lesson's word "tolérer" stays in the prose and the exam items, and the quote uses LSG.

## Problems in the English source

1. **MC13 (correct option and why) and SA5 model**: they define the second death as "the lake of fire". The lesson (b015) says only "final, eternal death". This is accurate (Rev 20:14), but the lesson does not teach it. I translated it as written.
2. **SA8 model**: "led Israel into Baal worship" and "repays each according to his works" (2:23b) are not in the lesson, which says only "the queen of old" and "searches the minds and hearts". This is harmless.
3. **b011**: the English says every letter closes with the refrain "and" the overcomer promise. In 2:7, 11 and 17 the refrain comes before the promise. In 2:26-29 (and in chapter 3) the promise comes first. This is minor, and I translated it as written.
