# CTSRev Unit 9 — French draft review

## Questions for Wayne

1. **Rev 15:3, "King of the saints" (b004, b009, b017, MC10 why).** Segond follows a different Greek reading: "roi des nations". I quoted Segond as it stands, including in the epigraph. The lesson does not build anything on the word "saints", so I added no gloss. Is that acceptable for the epigraph, or would you prefer a different epigraph verse?
2. **Rev 14:1 (b011).** Segond has "qui avaient son nom et le nom de son Père écrits sur leurs fronts", which includes the Lamb's name as well as the Father's. I quoted Segond. The lesson and MC1 still speak of "le nom du Père", which remains true.
3. **Rev 14:8, "Babylon is fallen… that great city" (b013, MC4, Fill 4).** Segond has "Elle est tombée, elle est tombée, Babylone la grande". I quoted Segond. The Fill 4 prompt now follows Segond's word order. MC4 keeps "la grande ville" in its option.
4. **Rev 14:12, "patience of the saints" (b013, b023, b024, SA2, SA10).** Segond has "persévérance". I used "persévérance" throughout. The SA keywords also accept "patience".
5. **Rev 14:13, "Blessed" → "Heureux" (Segond).** MC6 now reads "a déclaré heureux". The SA keywords accept "heureux" or "bénédiction".
6. **Rev 14:19–20, "winepress" (b015, MC8, Fill 5, SA3).** Segond has "la grande cuve". Following convention 1, I added one sentence: the Greek word means the press where grapes were trodden for wine. Fill 5's answer is "cuve", and it also accepts "pressoir". "Bridles" becomes "mors" (Segond).
7. **Rev 14:14, "One like the Son of Man" (b015, MC7).** Segond has "quelqu'un qui ressemblait à un fils d'homme", lowercase. I quoted it as it stands. The lesson does not identify the figure, and I did not either.
8. **Rev 15:1, "in them the wrath of God is complete" (b017, MC13).** Segond has "par eux s'accomplit la colère de Dieu". The MC13 option is "La colère de Dieu s'accomplit".
9. **Rev 16:2, "foul and loathsome sores" (b021, MC15, SA6).** Segond has "un ulcère malin et douloureux". I used Segond's wording.
10. **Rev 16:5–6 (b023, MC16, SA7).** The English uses an ellipsis. Segond's verse 5 reads "toi qui es, et qui étais; tu es saint, parce que tu as exercé ce jugement", and "it is their just due" becomes "ils en sont dignes". I quoted Segond in full.
11. **Rev 16:16, "Armageddon" (b021, MC18, Fill 9).** Segond has "Harmaguédon". The lesson adds "que beaucoup connaissent sous le nom d'Armageddon", and MC18 reads "Harmaguédon (Armageddon)". Fill 9's answer is "Harmaguédon", and it also accepts "Armageddon", "Armagédon" and "Har-Maguédon".
12. **Rev 16:17, "It is done!" (b009, b022, b023, MC19, Fill 10, SA8).** Segond has "C'en est fait!" Fill 10's answer is "C'en est fait", and it also accepts "c'est fait" and "cela est fait". I did not accept "c'est accompli", which is Segond's wording in John 19:30. Please decide whether it should be accepted.
13. **"Bowls" (Fill 7).** Segond has "coupes". Fill 7 also accepts "coupe", "bols", "bol" and "fioles" (the KJV-type "vials").
14. **Eschatology.** The 144,000 sentence in b011 and SA1 ("understood in more than one way… presented fairly") and the open question about the grain harvest in b015 and SA3 are translated with the same weight as the English. MC13 contains the wrong option "The church is raptured", rendered "L'Église est enlevée". It is a distractor in the English too, and it is wrong only because 15:1 is about wrath. I kept it but am flagging it in case readers take it as a ruling against a rapture view.

## Problems in the English source

1. **SA5.** The English prompt hits 3 of its own keywords (temple, angel, glory). Following convention 10, I reworded the French prompt. All the other French prompts reach at most 2 hits.
2. **SA8 model.** It adds details the lesson does not teach: hailstones "the weight of a talent", and "a later cry will mark the completion of all things" (Rev 21:6). Both are accurate. I translated them faithfully.
3. **SA6.** The keyword "total" never appears in the lesson, which says "in full" and "complete". The French group accepts "entier/entièrement", "complet/complète", "totale" and "tiers".
4. **SA5 and SA7 models.** SA5 says "golden bands across their chests" and SA7 mentions "the cry of the martyrs under the altar" (Rev 6:10, an earlier unit). Both go slightly beyond this lesson. They are not errors.
5. No answer-key errors found. All `why` notes match their questions.
