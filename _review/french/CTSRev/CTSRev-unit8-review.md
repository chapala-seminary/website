## Problems in the English source

1. **Perseverance wording (b019, b023, SA5, SA8, SA10).** The English says names are written "once and for all in indelible ink" and that the Lamb "has already secured His people forever". The SA8 model adds "those whose names are in the Lamb's Book of Life cannot finally be lost". The CTS brief treats eternal security as an open question, and these lines lean toward affirming it. Rev 3:5 ("je n'effacerai point son nom du livre de vie") is also in tension with "indelible". I translated these lines faithfully and did not strengthen or soften them.
2. **Balance in b021.** The first-century (preterist) reading gets a long, detailed paragraph. The futurist reading gets one sentence. The idealist (symbolic) reading of the beast gets no development in that paragraph. The English says "those who read the book against its first-century setting fill in this picture most fully", which signals the imbalance, but you may want to even it out. I translated it as is.
3. **Possible factual issue (b021).** The account of a person receiving a certificate after offering incense to the emperor describes the libelli of the Decian persecution (AD 250). These certificates are not documented under Nero or Domitian. The lesson presents them as the first-century background to the mark.
4. **SA8 model.** It says "His defeat was secured at the cross", but the lesson never mentions the cross in connection with Satan's defeat. The cross appears only at 13:8. This is not a keyword, so there is no grading impact.
5. **SA prompts.** Following convention 10, I reworded the SA1, SA4, SA5, SA6 and SA8 prompts so that they no longer reach 3 of their own keywords. Every French prompt now hits 0–2 keywords. Every model answer hits 8 of 8.
6. **Answer keys.** No answer-key errors found. Every `why` matches its question.
