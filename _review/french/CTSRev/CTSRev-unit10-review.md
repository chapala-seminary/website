# CTSRev Unit 10 — French draft review

## Questions for Wayne

1. **Rev 17:1, 5, "harlot" / "MOTHER OF HARLOTS" (b011, MC2, SA1).** Segond has "la grande prostituée" and "la mère des impudiques". I used "prostituée" throughout. Inside quotations and in MC2 I kept "impudiques", and in b011 I added that the Greek says literally "la mère des prostituées". The ALL-CAPS name is in Segond's normal case: "un nom, un mystère: Babylone la grande…".
2. **Rev 17:6, "martyrs of Jesus" (b011, MC3, Fill 3).** Segond has "témoins de Jésus". I quoted Segond and added one sentence: the Greek word is the source of the French "martyrs". Fill 3's answer is "témoins", and it also accepts "martyrs". MC3 reads "témoins (martyrs)".
3. **Rev 17:9, "mountains" (Fill 4).** Segond has "montagnes". In the Rome reading in b013 I wrote "ses sept collines". Fill 4 accepts "monts" and "collines".
4. **Rev 17:14, "the Lamb" (b004, b013).** Segond writes "l'agneau" in lowercase. I kept that inside quotations and wrote "l'Agneau" elsewhere, following convention 9.
5. **Rev 18:4, "Come out of her" (b009, b017, MC12, Fill 7, SA4, SA7).** Segond has "Sortez du milieu d'elle, mon peuple". I used that wording everywhere the lesson echoes the call.
6. **Rev 18:8, "in one day" (MC13).** Segond has "en un même jour". I quoted it in the `why`, and the lesson and the correct option say "un seul jour".
7. **Rev 18:10, "Alas, alas" (b019, SA5).** Segond has "Malheur! malheur!".
8. **Rev 18:11, 18:13, "cargo" / "bodies and souls of men" (b019).** Segond's genitive list reads "cargaison d'or, d'argent… de corps et d'âmes d'hommes", and I shaped the sentence in b019 to fit it.
9. **Rev 18:20, "God has avenged you on her" (b021, b024, MC17, SA6, SA8).** Segond has "Dieu vous a fait justice en la jugeant". The lesson leans on "avenged/vindication", so I added the literal Greek sense ("Dieu a jugé votre jugement sur elle") once in b021. The SA keyword groups accept both "justice" and "vengé".
10. **Rev 18:21, "millstone" (Fill 8).** Segond has "meule". Fill 8 accepts "meule de moulin" and "pierre de moulin".
11. **Rev 18:23, "sorcery" (b021, Fill 9).** Segond has "enchantements". I added that the Greek word means sorcery. Fill 9's answer is "enchantements", and it also accepts "sorcellerie(s)" and "magie".
12. **Rev 17:16, "make her desolate" (MC7).** Segond has "la dépouilleront". MC7's correct option reads "La dépouillent et la consument par le feu".
13. **Eschatology (b013, MC10, SA2).** The checker compared these line by line with the English, and the views come out balanced in the same way. The three readings of Babylon (Rome of John's day, a future world-system, the world-city of every age) appear in the same order and with the same weight, introduced by "les uns… d'autres… d'autres encore". Nothing is added or left out. "The course presents these readings fairly" and "What all agree on" are both kept.

## Problems in the English source
