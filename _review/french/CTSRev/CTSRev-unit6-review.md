# CTSRev Unit 6 — French draft review

## Questions for Wayne

1. **Rev 6:17, "who is able to stand?" (b004, b009, b017, b021, MC13, MC20, Fill 7, SA4, SA9).** Segond has "qui peut subsister ?" I quoted Segond and added once in b017 that the Greek verb means literally "se tenir debout", so the lesson's link to the multitude "standing" before the throne still works. Fill 7's answer is "subsister"; it also accepts "tenir debout", "se tenir debout", "rester debout" and "tenir".
2. **Rev 6:8, "Hades followed with him" (b013, MC6, Fill 3).** Segond has "le séjour des morts l'accompagnait". I quoted Segond and added that the Greek word is "Hadès", the world of the dead or the grave. Fill 3's answer is "séjour des morts"; it also accepts "Hadès", "enfer", "séjour" and "monde des morts". MC6's correct option is "La mort ; le séjour des morts (l'Hadès, la tombe)".
3. **Rev 6:5-6, "a pair of scales" / "a day's wheat at a day's wage" (b013, MC5, Fill 2).** Segond has "une balance" and "Une mesure de blé pour un denier". I added that a denier was a day's wage, so the lesson's point stays clear. Fill 2's answer is "balance".
4. **Rev 6:10, "O Lord, holy and true".** Segond has "Maître saint et véritable", and "tardes-tu à juger" instead of "until You judge". I quoted it as is. MC9's option follows Segond.
5. **Rev 6:11, "white robes" (b015, MC10, Fill 5).** Segond has "Une robe blanche fut donnée à chacun d'eux" (singular) and "se tenir en repos quelque temps encore" for "rest a little longer". 6. **Rev 6:12, "black as sackcloth of hair" (b017, Fill 6).** Segond has "noir comme un sac de crin". Fill 6's answer is "sac de crin"; it also accepts "sac", "cilice", "toile de sac" and "sac de poils".
7. **Rev 6:13, "late figs".** Segond has "figues vertes" (unripe figs), shaken by "un vent violent". I quoted Segond in b017 and did not add a comment. Please confirm that this is acceptable.
8. **Rev 6:14, "as a scroll".** Segond has "comme un livre qu'on roule". This is consistent with Unit 5, which uses "livre" for the scroll. I also used "le livre" in b011 and b023 for the Lamb's scroll.
9. **Rev 7:3-4, "sealed" (b019, MC14, MC15, Fill 8, SA5).** Segond has "marqués du sceau". I used that phrase throughout. Fill 8's answer is "front" (forehead).
10. **Rev 7:9, "great multitude" / "palm branches" (b009, b021, MC17, Fill 9, SA6).** Segond has "une grande foule" and "des palmes". I used "foule" throughout. The SA6 keywords accept both "foule" and "multitude". Fill 9's answer is "palmes"; it also accepts "branches de palmier" and similar forms.
11. **Rev 7:14, "come out of the great tribulation" / "an elder".** Segond has "ceux qui viennent de la grande tribulation" and "l'un des vieillards". I used "vieillards", as in Unit 5.
12. **Rev 7:17, "living fountains of waters".** Segond has "les sources des eaux de la vie". The SA7 keywords accept "source"/"fontaine" and "de la vie"/"vivant"/"vives".
13. **Lowercase "agneau" in Segond quotations.** I kept Segond's lowercase inside quotations and capitalized "l'Agneau" in the lesson's own sentences, as in Unit 5.
14. **Eschatology wording.** I kept these passages exactly as balanced as the English: the four ways of reading the seals (b011, SA1), the two readings of the white rider with "le cours laisse la question ouverte" (b013, SA2), and the three identities of the 144,000 plus literal vs. symbolic number (b019, MC16, SA5). Please confirm that "reste fidèle" works for "faithful remnant".

## Problems in the English source

1. **b021 (and SA6 model): "When John asks who they are, an elder tells him".** In Rev 7:13-14 the elder asks the question, and John replies "you know". I translated the lesson faithfully ("Quand Jean demande qui ils sont"). The French SA6 model avoids the point ("Un des vieillards explique").
2. **MC7.** The stem says "the four horsemen" were given power over a fourth of the earth. In 6:8 (and in the lesson) that power is given to Death and Hades ("leur"). The answer key is correct; the French stem now follows the English ("aux quatre cavaliers").
3. **SA1 prompt** reaches 3 of its own English keywords (lamb, seals, open). Following convention 10, the French prompt reaches 2. All other French prompts reach 0–2.
4. **SA7 model goes beyond the lesson.** It adds "will dwell among them", "the sun will not strike them", and the link to the new heaven and earth (Rev 21:4). These are true but not taught. I omitted the Rev 21 aside from the French model. The keywords are all taught.
5. No wrong answer keys found. The `why` explanations match their questions.
