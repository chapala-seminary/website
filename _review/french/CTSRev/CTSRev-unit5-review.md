# CTSRev Unit 5 — French draft review

## Questions for Wayne

1. **Rev 4:2, "in the Spirit" (b009, b011, MC2).** Segond has "je fus ravi en esprit", with a lowercase "esprit". I followed units 1–2: I quoted Segond, added that the Greek says literally "je fus en Esprit", and kept the lesson's statement that the Holy Spirit carries John.
2. **Rev 4:1, "Come up here" (b011, SA1).** Segond has "Monte ici". The two readings (the church caught up, or John's own call to ascend in vision) and "the course leaves the question open" are translated with equal weight. I used "enlevée" for "caught up", following 1 Thess 4:17 in Segond. Checker changed "annonce" (announcement) to "préfiguration" to match "foreshadowing" exactly, so neither reading is favored. Please confirm it reads as neutral.
3. **"Elders" → Segond "vieillards" (b015, MC4, Fill 3, SA3).** "Vieillards" can sound like "old men". I used Segond's word and added one sentence: the Greek says literally "anciens", meaning men with authority. Fill 3 also accepts "anciens", "presbytres" and the singular forms.
4. **Rev 4:5, 5:6, "seven Spirits of God".** Segond writes "sept esprits", lowercase. I kept Segond's spelling inside quotations and in MC5 and MC14, and kept the lesson's sentence that this pictures the fullness of the Saint-Esprit.
5. **Rev 4:11, "O Lord".** Segond has "notre Seigneur et notre Dieu". I quoted it as is.
6. **Rev 5:1–9, "scroll".** Segond has "livre". I used "livre" throughout and added once in b019 that the Greek word means a rolled scroll. MC10 says "le livre (le rouleau)". The SA5 and SA7 keywords accept both "livre" and "rouleau".
7. **Rev 5:5, "Root of David" / "prevailed".** Segond has "le rejeton de David, a vaincu". I used "vaincu" for "prevailed" and "conquer" throughout. Fill 8 answer: "Juda" (also accepts "Judah").
8. **Rev 5:6 "a Lamb", 5:12 "Worthy is the Lamb who was slain" (b004, b024, MC7, MC13, Fill 9).** Segond has "un agneau qui était là comme immolé" and "L'agneau qui a été immolé est digne…", both lowercase. I kept Segond's lowercase inside quotations and capitalized "l'Agneau" in the lesson's own sentences (convention 9). Fill 9's answer is "agneau".
9. **Rev 5:8, "bowls full of incense" (b021, MC15, Fill 10).** Segond has "coupes d'or remplies de parfums". I added that the Greek says literally "remplies d'encens". MC15's `why` mentions both words.
10. **Rev 5:9–10, "redeemed us… made us kings and priests".** Segond follows a different Greek text: "tu as racheté pour Dieu par ton sang des hommes…; tu as fait d'eux un royaume et des sacrificateurs". I quoted Segond. MC17's correct option is "Un royaume et des sacrificateurs pour Dieu". The SA8 keywords accept "sacrificateur" and "prêtre". Unit 1 used the same wording.
11. **Rev 5:11, "ten thousand times ten thousand" (b021, MC18).** Segond has "des myriades de myriades et des milliers de milliers". I added the short gloss "une myriade vaut dix mille". The MC18 option reads "Innombrable : des milliers de milliers".
12. **Rev 4:3, "rainbow" (Fill 2).** The answer is "arc-en-ciel". Fill 2 also accepts "arc", which follows Gen 9:13 in Segond ("mon arc dans la nue") and the English alternate "bow".

## Problems in the English source

1. **SA1–SA7 prompts reach 3–5 of their own English keywords.** For example, SA6 hits lion, judah, lamb, slain and conquer. Following convention 10, I reworded the French prompts. SA8 and SA9 reach 2 hits, and all the others reach 0.
2. **Model answers go beyond the lesson.** SA5 says "or even to look at it", and SA7 says "the horn is a symbol of power in Scripture", "alive and standing" and "all-seeing". SA6 says "won at the cross", and SA8 says "could not have been sung before the cross". The lesson never mentions the cross. These are all true, but the SA6 keyword "cross" is not taught. The French SA6 group also accepts "sacrific" and "mort".
3. **MC14.** The stem asks what both the seven horns and the seven eyes signify. The text explains only the eyes (as the seven Spirits). The lesson supplies "fullness of power" for the horns. The answer key is correct.
4. No answer-key errors found. On eschatology: b011 and SA1 ("Come up here") and b015 and SA3 (the elders) are kept exactly as balanced as the English. MC20's wrong option "the church will escape all trouble" is general and does not target a rapture view.
