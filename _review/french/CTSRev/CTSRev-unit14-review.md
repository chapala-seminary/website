# CTSRev Unit 14 — French draft review

## Questions for Wayne

1. **Rev 20:4-5, "lived and reigned" / "did not live again" (b021, MC16, SA7).** The main objection depends on the same word being used twice. Segond also uses the same verb twice, but it is "revinrent à la vie" / "ne revinrent point à la vie", which already sounds like a bodily return. I quoted Segond and added one sentence: the Greek says literally "ils vécurent" in both places. MC16's correct option quotes "revinrent à la vie". Please check that this does not make the objection sound stronger than it does in English.
2. **Rev 20:4, "judgment was committed to them" (b004).** Segond has "à ceux qui s'y assirent fut donné le pouvoir de juger". I quoted it as is.
3. **Rev 20:3, "deceive the nations" (b009 and throughout).** Segond has "séduire les nations". I used "séduire" everywhere, including MC4 and the SA models.
4. **Rev 22:20, "Even so, come, Lord Jesus!" (b024).** Segond has "Amen! Viens, Seigneur Jésus!" I quoted Segond.
5. **John 12:31, "ruler of this world" → "prince de ce monde"; Matt 13, "tares" → "ivraie"; Luke 10:18, "lightning" → "éclair"; 2 Cor 4:4, "blinding the minds" → "aveuglé l'intelligence".** These are all Segond's wording. Fill 4 and Fill 6 use Segond's words, and each also accepts common synonyms.
6. **"Present age" → "l'âge présent".** I followed unit 1 rather than Segond's "siècle présent" (Eph 1:21), because "âge" is clearer for these readers. Fill 2 also accepts "siècle", "ère" and "époque".
7. **Terms.** I used "amillénarisme / amillénariste" and "millénium", matching unit 1. For "binding of Satan" I wrote "le fait que Satan soit lié", which follows Segond's "il le lia" and avoids the awkward noun "liement".

## Problems in the English source

1. **MC12 uses "chiliasm", but the lesson never uses the word.** I glossed it in the French stem: "(l'attente d'un règne terrestre de mille ans)".
2. **Some model answers go beyond the lesson.** SA8 says the word "lived" carries different senses, that the souls describe the intermediate state, and that the single-coming pattern should govern the symbolic passage. SA6 says Satan can no longer "gather them to destroy the church", and also mentions tempting and accusing. The checker restored these points in the French models (see below), because cutting them made the amillennial replies thinner than the objections; the keywords do not depend on them.
3. **The SA4 English prompt reaches 3 of its own keywords** (recapitulation, binding, resurrection). Following convention 10, I reworded the French prompt. All French prompts reach 0–2 hits.
4. **MC18's `why` cites 1 Peter 5:8, but the lesson gives no reference.** The reference is correct, so I kept it.
5. **Answer keys.** No errors found.
