# CTSRev Unit 7 — French draft review

## Questions for Wayne

1. **Rev 8:3-4, "incense" (b009, b011, MC1, Fill 1, SA1).** Segond has "parfums". I quoted Segond and added that the Greek says literally "beaucoup d'encens". The rest of the lesson uses "encens". The MC1 stem says "parfums (d'encens)", and the SA1 keywords accept both words.
2. **Rev 8:10-11, "Wormwood" (b013, MC4, Fill 2, SA2).** Segond has "Absinthe". I added a gloss: wormwood is a very bitter plant. Fill 2's answer is "Absinthe", and it also accepts "Wormwood".
3. **Rev 8:13, "Woe" / "the three woes" (b013, b015, b017, b023, MC6, Fill 3).** Segond has "Malheur" and "Le premier malheur est passé". I used "malheur(s)" throughout. Fill 3 also accepts "calamités".
4. **Rev 9:4, "seal of God on their foreheads".** Segond has "sur le front" (singular). Fill 4's answer is "sceau", and it also accepts "marque", "signe" and "cachet".
5. **Rev 9:16, "two hundred million" (b017, MC11, SA4).** Segond has "deux myriades de myriades". I quoted it, explained that it means 200 million, and added "une myriade vaut dix mille". The MC11 option gives both forms.
6. **Rev 9:21, "sorceries".** Segond has "enchantements". I added "(c'est-à-dire la sorcellerie)".
7. **Rev 10:2, "little scroll".** Segond has "petit livre", which matches unit 5's choice. I added once that the Greek word means a small scroll.
8. **Rev 10:6, "there should be delay no longer" (b019, MC13, SA5).** Segond has "qu'il n'y aurait plus de temps", which could be read as "time itself ends". I quoted Segond and added a gloss saying the Greek word has the sense of "délai" (the checker reworded the gloss so it no longer presents Segond's wording as the literal Greek). The MC13 option reads "plus de délai (« plus de temps »)". This does not favour any eschatological view.
9. **Rev 10:9-10, "bitter in his stomach".** Segond has "entrailles" / "mes entrailles furent remplies d'amertume". I used "entrailles" throughout.
10. **Rev 11:3, "sackcloth" (b021, MC15, Fill 9, SA6).** Segond has "revêtus de sacs". I added a short gloss on what sackcloth was. Fill 9's answer is "sacs", and it also accepts "sac", "toile de sac", "étoffe de sac" and "cilice".
11. **Rev 11:11, "breath of life" (b021, MC18, SA6).** Segond has "un esprit de vie". I added that the Greek word also means "souffle", and I use "souffle de vie" in the lesson's own sentences.
12. **Rev 11:8, "where also our Lord was crucified".** Segond has "leur Seigneur" ("their Lord"). I quoted Segond. The lesson does not depend on this difference.
13. **Rev 11:15, "The kingdoms of this world have become the kingdoms of our Lord" (b004, b009, b023, b024, MC19, SA8, SA10).** Segond has "Le royaume du monde est remis à notre Seigneur et à son Christ" (singular, "remis"). I quoted Segond everywhere, including the masthead quote. I added once that the Greek says the royalty of the world "est devenue" his. MC19 uses Segond's wording.
14. **Rev 11:17, "taken Your great power and reigned".** Segond has "saisi ta grande puissance et pris possession de ton règne". I used this wording in b023 and in MC20 ("saisi sa puissance").
15. **"Elders" → "vieillards"**, following unit 5. The SA8 keywords also accept "ancien".

## Problems in the English source

1. **SA7 is only partly taught.** Its model says the temple is measured "and those who worship there", and that the measured worshipers "cannot be lost". The lesson (b021) says only that God marks out and preserves His own. "Cannot be lost" could read as more than the course's open position on perseverance. I kept the French model to "préserve ceux qui sont à lui" and did not render "cannot be lost". Please confirm.
2. **SA1 model refers to "the martyrs under the altar at the fifth seal".** That comes from an earlier unit. The lesson only says "under the altar". This is not an error.
3. **MC16 `why` cites 11:5-6.** The lesson describes those verses without a reference. The answer is correct.
4. **MC12 `why`, "judgment alone hardens the impenitent".** The lesson says that judgment "does not soften" a hardened heart, which is slightly different. I translated the English as written.
5. **The English models are long (7-10 sentences).** The French models are 4-6 sentences and hit all 8 groups. All the French SA prompts were reworded so that they reach at most 2 of their own keywords. The English SA1, SA2, SA6 and SA10 prompts reach 3 or more.
6. No answer-key errors found. On eschatology: b011 ("does not press one" → "n'impose aucune de ces lectures"), b021 and SA6 (the two witnesses as individuals or as the church, "présente ces points de vue avec équité") keep the English balance. The MC options "Mille ans de paix" and "Les rois de l'Orient" are wrong distractors in both languages and do not target any view.

## Adaptations

None needed. There are no US-specific examples. I added short glosses for "absinthe", "myriade", "sac" and "enchantements" for readers with little formal training.
