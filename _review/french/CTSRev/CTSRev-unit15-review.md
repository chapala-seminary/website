# CTSRev Unit 15 — French draft review

## Questions for Wayne

1. **Rev 21:1, 4, "passed away" (b011, b009, MC1).** Segond has "avaient disparu" / "ont disparu". I used "disparu" throughout. MC1's correct option is "Disparu".
2. **Rev 21:4, "nor sorrow, nor crying... no more pain".** Segond has "ni deuil, ni cri, ni douleur". MC4's option reads "Ni mort, ni deuil, ni cri, ni douleur".
3. **Rev 21:23, "the Lamb is its light" (b016 heading, b017, MC12, Fill 5).** Segond has "l'agneau est son flambeau". Following convention 1, I quoted Segond and added that the Greek says literally "sa lampe". The heading keeps "Dieu et l'Agneau sont sa lumière". Fill 5's answer is "flambeau", and it also accepts "lumière", "lampe" and "luminaire".
4. **Rev 22:3, "no more curse" (b019, MC15, Fill 7, SA5, SA10).** Segond has "Il n'y aura plus d'anathème". I quoted Segond and added a sentence saying that nothing will be cursed any more, so the curse has ended. MC15's correct option is "D'anathème (de malédiction)". Fill 7's answer is "anathème", and it also accepts "malédiction". The SA keyword groups accept both words.
5. **Rev 22:6, "faithful and true" (b021, SA6).** Segond has "certaines et véritables". I added the gloss "littéralement « fidèles et véritables »". The SA6 keywords accept both forms.
6. **Rev 22:16, "Root and Offspring of David, Bright and Morning Star" (b021, MC19, Fill 8).** Segond has "le rejeton et la postérité de David, l'étoile brillante du matin". "Root" became "rejeton", as in Unit 5. Fill 8's answer is "étoile".
7. **Rev 22:20, "Even so, come, Lord Jesus" (b022, b023, b024, MC20).** Segond has "Amen! Viens, Seigneur Jésus!" with no "Even so". The heading became "Viens, Seigneur Jésus !". In b023, "Surely I am coming quickly" became Segond's "Oui, je viens bientôt".
8. **Rev 22:21 (b024).** Segond has "Que la grâce du Seigneur Jésus soit avec tous!". It has no "Christ" and no closing "Amen" because it follows a different Greek text. I quoted Segond as it stands.
9. **Rev 21:7, "he who overcomes" (b013).** Segond has "Celui qui vaincra". I translated "He who overcomes shall inherit all things" with Segond's "héritera ces choses". The conditional wording is kept as it stands, with no added comment on perseverance.
10. **Rev 21:3, "tabernacle" (Fill 1).** Segond has "tabernacle", which is the answer. Fill 1 also accepts "demeure", "habitation" and "tente".
11. **"Good night" (b023, Fill 10).** I rendered it as "une « bonne nuit »", with "adieu" for "goodbye". Fill 10's answer is "nuit".
12. **Rev 22:7, 12, 20, "coming quickly" (b021).** Segond's "je viens bientôt" says "soon" (a time claim); the NKJV's "quickly" can mean "soon" or "suddenly", and readers of different eschatological schools lean on one sense or the other. To keep the French as open as the English, the checker added one sentence after the first quotation: the Greek word can also mean "promptement" or "soudainement" (convention 1). OK to keep?

## Problems in the English source

1. **SA prompts reach their own English keywords.** SA1 gets 4 hits, SA2 4, SA3 3, SA5 3, SA6 3 and SA8 3. Following convention 10, the French keywords were chosen so that every prompt gets 0–1 hits.
2. **SA9 and SA10 keywords are missing from their own English models.** The SA9 keyword "choose" does not appear in its model, and the SA10 keyword "creation" does not appear in its model. I replaced them with ideas taught in the lesson. The French SA10 model mentions "création renouvelée".
3. **SA9 model goes beyond this unit's lesson.** It says "drunk with the blood of the saints", "luxury, and cruelty" and "a summons to come out of Babylon". That material belongs to the earlier Babylon units, not to this lesson. I translated it faithfully. The keyword "sortir" depends on that content.
4. **MC7's `why` is looser than its answer.** It says "the unbelieving and wicked", while the answer gives the full list. This is acceptable.
5. **b015 and MC8.** The angel's showing of the bride is in 21:9, but the lesson quotes no verse there. I added the quote "l'épouse, la femme de l'agneau" (21:9). The answer keys are correct.
6. No answer-key errors were found. On eschatology, the unit makes no claims about the millennium, the rapture or timing, and the French adds none (see Question 12 on "bientôt").
