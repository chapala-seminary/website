# French Batch 2: what the reviews found

This covers four courses: The Gospel of John, Matthew Intensive, Acts Intensive and Romans (46 units).

Every claim below about the English site was checked against the live course files. Claims that did not hold up, or that were only matters of taste, were dropped.

---

## 1. Wrong answer keys on the live site

These affect students now, in English and Spanish.

**Clearly wrong**

| Course / Unit | Question | Keyed answer | Should be | Why |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Acts 11 | MC 3 (what makes Acts unique) | "It alone has no doxology, amen, or formal closing" | Reword, e.g. "It ends unusually open, mid-story" | Not true. Jonah ends with a question, and Judges, Ruth and Ezekiel have no formal closing either. The lesson (b016) says the same thing, so fix it there too. |
| Acts 11 | MC 13 (where Paul's chain reached) | "Caesar's own praetorian household (Philippians 4:22)" | "The praetorian guard (Phil 1:13) and Caesar's household (Phil 4:22)" | It mixes two verses. The guard is 1:13; 4:22 is Caesar's household. b032 has the same mix-up. |
| Acts 6 | MC 6 (what Luke calls the team after 13:13) | "Paul and his party" | Reword the stem | The lesson (b021) says Luke "stops calling them Barnabas and Saul" from 13:13 onward. He doesn't: Acts 14:12, 14:14, 15:12 and 15:25 still put Barnabas first. |
| Acts 8 | MC 12 (the Ephesian goddess) | "Diana / Artemis (one of the seven wonders)" | "…whose temple was one of the seven wonders" | The goddess was not a wonder of the world; her temple was. |
| Romans 1 | SA 9 (the two name orders) and MC 10 stem | Model says Paul opens with "Jesus Christ" and closes the greeting with "Christ Jesus" | Drop or rewrite the item | The NKJV's greeting ends with "the Lord Jesus Christ" (1:7), and 1:1 reads "Jesus Christ." The question rests on something the NKJV text doesn't show (b028). |

**Keys the lesson does not support**

- **Acts 1, MC 9.** The key says Pentecost was "primarily a miracle of hearing." The lesson (b029) says they "began to speak… in languages they had never learned." The "hearing" idea appears only in the SA 4 model.
- **Acts 11, MC 10 and MC 11.** The keys say Apollos was discipled at "their kitchen table" and Lydia's church met in "her living room." Acts says "took him aside" and "her house." These are the lesson's word-pictures, not facts a student can learn.
- **Romans 6, MC 6.** It marks "Paul's pre-conversion self" as wrong for the "I" of Romans 7. That is a live debate (see the theology flags in section 3), so a student giving the Wesleyan reading is marked wrong.

  - Unit 7, b033: "easier to meet a god than a man" is Petronius, not Pliny. b038: the "thousand prostitutes" belongs to old Greek Corinth, not Paul's Corinth.

## 3. Decisions that apply to the whole French edition

The eleven settled conventions are not repeated here. These are new.

1. **"Gentiles."** The drafts are split. Some use Segond's "païens," others use "non-Juifs" because "païen" can sound insulting in Congolese church usage. Current practice in most units: Segond's word inside quotations, and "non-Juifs" in the lesson's own sentences. Approve that, or pick one.
2. **"Helper" (the Holy Spirit).** Segond always says "consolateur." The John drafts use "le Consolateur" as His title and mention "Paraclet" once. Approve for all courses?
3. **"Septante" (seventy).** Segond says "septante fois sept fois." The drafts quote it and gloss it once ("septante, c'est-à-dire soixante-dix"). Approve as the standard?
4. **Course names in the header.** Use one pattern for every intensive: "Cours intensif sur Matthieu." The Joshua drafts still have three different forms.
5. **Diagram labels.** Where the English site shows English and Spanish side by side, the drafts show French and English. Should the French site keep the English half? (Robert to decide how.)
6. **Short answers where the English has fewer than 8 keyword groups (Acts 6).** The French can't follow convention 10 unless the English gets 8 groups, or Robert lets the French have more groups than the English.

**Theology flags the reviewers raised.** All are faithful translations of the English. The question is whether the English should change.

*May lean Calvinist*
- **John 2, b021 and MC 9:** "The new birth is His work before it is ever our choice." This can be read as new birth coming before faith.
- **Romans 1, b033:** "The faith that receives it is itself a gift."
- **Romans 4, b036, MC 16, SA 6:** Adam's *guilt* is imputed to all. This is the Reformed way of putting it. Many Wesleyans say we inherit a corrupted nature, not guilt.
- **Romans 6, b025, MC 6, SA 4, SA 6:** Romans 7 "I" is firmly the born-again Paul. MC 6 marks the Wesleyan/Arminian reading wrong.
- **Romans 8, b022–b025:** "sovereign election," "hardening is His prerogative."
- **Acts 6, b028 and SA 6 model:** "chosen before they ever responded… marked out for eternal life in advance."
- **Acts 7, b040 and SA 9 model:** "many elect people… already marked for salvation."

*May go further than "open question" on eternal security*
- **John 7, b025, MC 17, SA 9:** "What He buys at such a price, He does not lose."
- **Romans 10, b049 and SA 9:** "Able to establish you — the doctrine of perseverance."
- **Romans 3 SA 10, Romans 4 b015/b047, Romans 7 b016/b043/b051:** "rests on something already finished and unchangeable," "standing forever," "not now, not ever." The reviewers ask you to confirm this level of assurance.

*End times*
- **Romans 8, b043, MC 18, SA 8:** b043 fairly presents two readings, but MC 18's explanation then says flatly "there is no separate destiny for ethnic Israel," and MC 18 marks "a separate millennial program" wrong. Dispensational students will see a side being taken.
- **Matthew 11 and 13:** Segond has "la fin du monde" where the NKJV has "the end of the age." The drafts quote Segond and add the literal Greek. Please confirm this doesn't tilt the end-times teaching.

*Denominational and women-in-ministry*
- **Matthew 9, MC 7:** the wrong option "Peter made the first pope" is aimed at the Catholic reading. The lesson never mentions the papacy.
- **Matthew 12, b027:** "My great-grandfather left one church to become a Baptist…" Keep it?
- **Romans 10:** Segond calls Phoebe "diaconesse," which says more than the NKJV's "servant." Segond also spells Junia as the man's name "Junias." The drafts add a short neutral note. Please check both notes against your neutral stance.
