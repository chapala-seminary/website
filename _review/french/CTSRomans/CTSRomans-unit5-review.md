## Questions for Wayne

1. **"Reckon" (Rom 6:11).** LSG says "regardez-vous comme morts au péché", which loses the bookkeeping image the lesson builds on. Following convention 1, b028 quotes LSG and adds that the Greek literally says "comptez-vous" (logizomai). The prose uses "se regarder" and also "porter à son compte". The section 3 heading uses "Regardez-vous". Fill 5 answer: "regardez". It also accepts "comptez", "considérez", "tenez" and "estimez". MC 7 and MC 18 gloss it as "regardez-vous (littéralement « comptez-vous »)".
2. **Abraham "credited" (b027).** LSG Rom 4:3 says "imputé à justice". I added that LSG phrase in parentheses so students can connect it to chapter 4.
3. **"Newness of the Spirit / oldness of the letter" (Rom 7:6).** LSG says "dans un esprit nouveau, et non selon la lettre qui a vieilli". Here "esprit" is lowercase and the contrast nouns are lost. b047 quotes LSG and adds the literal Greek ("la nouveauté de l'Esprit… la vieillesse de la lettre"). b048 and MC 17 use those phrases. Fill 10 uses LSG's wording, with the answer "lettre".
4. **"Married to another" (Rom 7:4).** LSG says "pour que vous apparteniez à un autre", so there is no marriage word. The lesson depends on the marriage image, so b046 quotes LSG and adds that the Greek uses the same expression as verse 3 for the remarried wife. MC 16 says "pour appartenir à Christ, comme dans un nouveau mariage".
5. **"Freed from sin" (Rom 6:7).** LSG says "libre du péché". The MC 6 answer is "libre".
6. **"Gift" (Rom 6:23).** LSG says "le don gratuit". Fill 8 answer: "don gratuit". It also accepts "don", "cadeau", "cadeau gratuit" and "don immérité".
7. **"Unrighteousness" (Rom 6:13).** LSG says "instruments d'iniquité". The MC 8 answer is "d'iniquité".
8. **"The Way" (b036).** LSG never calls the faith "la Voie" with a capital letter. It has "la nouvelle doctrine" (Acts 9:2) and "la voie" (Acts 19:9, 24:14). I kept "la Voie" because the lesson quotes no verse there.
9. **Baptism (b022, b025).** I translated the immersion language faithfully and added nothing to it. Regeneration follows faith ("entré par la foi"). Perseverance does not come up in this unit, and nothing in the French affirms or denies it.

## Problems in the English source

1. **MC 6 (6:7), MC 11 (6:18), MC 12 (6:21).** The lesson does not quote or teach these verses directly. MC 11 is only touched on in b036.
2. **MC 12** stem calls the reader "unconverted", but Rom 6:21 is addressed to believers looking back on their former life. I translated it faithfully.
3. **SA 3 model** says baptism is "more than symbol — it is the believer's real identification". This is in some tension with b025, which says the water "does not create union with Christ" but only portrays it. I translated it faithfully. You may want to soften it in English.
4. **SA 8 keywords** include "sever", but the lesson never uses that word (it appears only in the MC 15 `why`). In French I used "rompt/met fin/brise".
5. **b015** says Romans has "seventy-some rhetorical questions". Counts vary by method, so please verify the number.
6. All answer keys look correct, and each `why` matches its question. All SA items have models.
