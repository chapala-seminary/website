# CTSRomans Unit 3 — French draft review

## Questions for Wayne

1. **Romans 3:22, "to all and on all who believe" (b017, b020, MC2).** Segond has only "pour tous ceux qui croient". I quoted Segond. Because b020 makes "to all and on all" its third point, I added one sentence: part of the Greek manuscripts reads literally "pour tous et sur tous ceux qui croient".
2. **Romans 3:22, "no difference" (b017, b021).** Segond says "Il n'y a point de distinction". I used "distinction" throughout b021.
3. **Romans 3:23, "fall short of" (b023, MC3).** Segond says "sont privés de la gloire de Dieu". I quoted Segond.
4. **Romans 3:25, "propitiation" / "set forth" (b023, b029, MC5, Fill 5, SA3).** Segond says "C'est lui que Dieu a destiné… à être… victime propitiatoire". The lesson says Christ is the *mercy seat*, so in b029 I added that Segond's "victime propitiatoire" renders *hilasterion*, which means the "propitiatoire" (Segond's word for the mercy seat in Exodus 25 and Hebrews 9:5). I also added that the Greek verb Segond renders "destiné" literally means "exposer publiquement". Fill 5 answer: **propitiatoire**. It also accepts "siège/trône de miséricorde" and "couvercle de l'arche".
5. **Romans 3:26, "just and the justifier" (b031, Fill 6, MC8).** Segond says "de manière à être juste tout en justifiant celui qui a la foi en Jésus", and has no noun "justifier". I quoted Segond and added "le texte grec dit littéralement « pour qu'il soit juste et justifiant »". Fill 6 answer: **justifiant**. It also accepts "justificateur" and "qui justifie".
6. **Romans 3:27, "boasting" (b032, b033, MC19, Fill 7).** Segond says "le sujet de se glorifier". Fill 7 answer: **glorifier**. It also accepts "vanter", "enorgueillir" and "targuer".
7. **Romans 3:31, "establish the law" (b034, MC15).** Segond says "nous confirmons la loi". I used "confirmer".
8. **Genesis 15:6 (b037, MC10, MC13, SA6).** Segond's Genesis reads "Abram eut confiance en l'Éternel, qui le lui imputa à justice", which is far from the lesson's wording. I quoted Paul's form in Romans 4:3 ("Abraham crut à Dieu, et cela lui fut imputé à justice") and tagged it "(Romains 4:3)". The MC10 option keeps "Genèse 15:6" as its label but uses the Romans 4:3 wording.
9. **"Accounted / imputed / credited."** Segond uses "imputer" throughout Romans 4. I used "imputé" for quotations and "porter au compte" for the lesson's bookkeeping explanation. In b045, Segond's "imputé" covers both of the NKJV's "imputed" and "accounted".
10. **Romans 4:5, "the ungodly"**: Segond has "l'impie". I used "l'impie" in b038, b039 and MC14.
11. **Romans 4:18, "contrary to hope, in hope believed"**: Segond has "Espérant contre toute espérance, il crut". The MC17 stem uses that phrase.
12. **Romans 4:25, "raised because of our justification" (b046).** The lesson glosses "raised" as "vindicated by the Father". I rendered it "approuvé publiquement par le Père" and avoided "justifié", so the text does not suggest that Christ himself needed justification.
13. **Theology, SA10 (assurance).** The English says the believer's standing "rests on something already finished and unchangeable". I translated it closely ("immuable") and did not strengthen it. The text says nothing either way about perseverance or eternal security. Please confirm that this matches the open position.

## Problems in the English source

1. **b014**: "after sixty-six verses of indictment". Romans 1:18–3:20 has 64 verses (15 + 29 + 20). Translated as written.
2. **b014**: the quotation of Romans 3:21-22 mixes KJV wording ("by faith of Jesus Christ unto all and upon all") with NKJV wording, and differs from the NKJV text in b017. The French uses Segond in both places.
3. **b037 / Fill 8**: the lesson calls *logizomai* the Greek word "in Genesis 15:6". Genesis is in Hebrew (*chashab*). *Logizomai* is the Greek of the Septuagint and of Paul's citation. In Fill 8 I wrote "en Genèse 15:6 tel que Paul le cite".
4. **MC4 / SA2**: the term *dikaioō* is never given in the lesson. The question is still answerable.
5. **SA4**: the keyword "instrum" and the model's statement that "faith is the instrument" are not taught in the lesson. Kept.
6. **SA prompts that pass their own keywords.** In the English, SA4 (grace / redemption / faith), SA5 ("Abraham") and SA6 ("Genesis") put keywords in the prompt. The French SA4 prompt keeps the English wording (grâce, rédemption, foi); it reaches only 2 hits because the faith group needs a phrase such as "par la foi". In SA5 I replaced the "Abraham" group with "païen". No French prompt passes its own keywords.
7. **SA10**: the English model hits only 5 of its 8 groups. The French uses 8 distinct ideas, and the French model hits 6.
