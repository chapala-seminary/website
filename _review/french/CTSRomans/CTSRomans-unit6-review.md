# CTSRomans Unit 6 — French draft review notes

Checker re-verified every Scripture quotation against the offline LSG tool (Romains 7:6, 7:7, 7:8, 7:12, 7:13, 7:15, 7:17, 7:21, 7:22-23, 7:24, 7:25, 8:1, 8:39, plus partial quotes in MC/fill/SA items): all match Segond 1910 exactly; no corrections needed.

## Questions for Wayne

1. **Rom 7:7 and 7:13, "Certainly not" / *mē genoito*.** LSG has "Loin de là!" I used it throughout: b015, MC 1, and as the gloss for *mē genoito*.
2. **Rom 7:13, "exceedingly sinful".** LSG has "afin qu'il se manifestât comme péché … il devînt condamnable au plus haut point". In b021 I quoted LSG and added "le texte grec dit littéralement… « excessivement pécheur »".
3. **Rom 7:8, "all manner of evil desire".** LSG has "toutes sortes de convoitises". I quoted it in b020.
4. **Rom 7:21, "evil is present with me".** LSG has "le mal est attaché à moi". I used it in b029.
5. **Rom 7:23 and 7:25, "law of my mind" / "with the mind".** LSG has "entendement". In b031 I added one sentence saying that the Greek word means mind or thought. MC 10 uses "la loi de l'entendement".
6. **Rom 7:24, "O wretched man that I am!"** LSG drops "man" and has "Misérable que je suis!" It also has "du corps de cette mort" rather than "this body of death". In b034 I added a sentence giving the literal Greek ("Homme misérable…", "ce corps de mort"). Fill 8 has the answer "misérable" and also accepts "homme misérable" and "malheureux". The heading and the lesson call it "le cri de l'homme misérable".
7. **Rom 7:25, "I thank God" / "serve".** LSG has "Grâces soient rendues à Dieu…", and in 7:25b "je suis … esclave de la loi de Dieu … esclave de la loi du péché" where the NKJV has "serve". b038, b039, b041, MC 15 and MC 16 follow LSG ("esclave"). The heading is "Grâces soient rendues à Dieu".
8. **Rom 7:22, "inward man".** LSG has "l'homme intérieur", which matches. Fill 6 has the answer "intérieur".
9. **Which "I" is speaking in Rom 7:14-25 (b025, b036, MC 6, MC 14, SA 4, SA 6).** The lesson says firmly that this is Paul as a regenerate believer. That is the Augustinian/Reformed reading. Arminius, Wesley and much of the Arminian/Wesleyan tradition read these verses as a person awakened under the law but not yet regenerate. MC 6 keys "pre-conversion self" as wrong. I translated the lesson faithfully and softened nothing. You may want to add one sentence acknowledging the other view, given the CTS guardrails.
10. **Perseverance (b040, b041).** The lesson says the deliverer "has secured the final victory" and that "nothing can separate him from the love of God" (Rom 8:39). I kept these at the English strength, quoting LSG 8:39 ("rien ne pourra … séparer"). I did not add or remove any claim about eternal security.

## Problems in the English source

1. **The "body of death" corpse custom (b034, MC 13, Fill 9, SA 7).** The lesson presents this as a known ancient punishment for murderers. The usual source is Virgil's account of the tyrant Mezentius (*Aeneid* 8), who chained living men to corpses. That is not a punishment for murderers, and it is disputed as background for Paul. The lesson states it as fact. I translated it faithfully.
2. **SA 10 and MC 19 `why`.** These depend on ideas the lesson never teaches: the "three tenses of salvation", being "declared righteous", "conforming to the image of Christ" and "the work of God". b032 teaches only that justification is instantaneous, sanctification is a war and glorification ends it. I also reworded the SA 10 prompt, because the English prompt names justification, sanctification and glorification, which on their own gave three keyword hits. The French asks for the three stages without naming them.
3. **SA 9.** The keywords "presumpt" and "despair" and the model's quotation of Rom 8:1 are not in the lesson. The lesson says only "There is no condemnation" (b041). The French model quotes LSG 8:1.
4. **SA 6.** The keywords "sensit" and "longing" are not taught in b036.
5. **SA 5.** The English model never hits its own keyword "believ". The French model hits 6 of its 8 groups, though it also misses "croyant".
