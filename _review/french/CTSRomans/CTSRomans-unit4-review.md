## Questions for Wayne

1. **Rom 5:2, "access ... in which we stand".** Segond has "à qui nous devons d'avoir eu par la foi accès à cette grâce, dans laquelle nous demeurons fermes". The offline Segond text leaves out the opening "à" ("qui nous devons…"). As the brief requires, b017 copies the tool exactly. Please have the French reader check a printed Segond 1910 and add "à" if it is there. b018, MC 3 and Fill 2 use "accès … dans laquelle nous demeurons fermes". Fill 2's answer is "accès", and it also accepts "entrée".
2. **Rom 5:3, "tribulations".** Segond has "afflictions / l'affliction". I used "affliction" throughout (b021, b023, MC 5, MC 6, SA 2).
3. **Rom 5:4, "character".** Segond has "la victoire dans l'épreuve", which hides the lesson's point about "proven character". In b023 I quoted Segond and then added "le texte grec dit littéralement « le caractère éprouvé »" before the metal-in-fire explanation. MC 6 option C reads "victoire dans l'épreuve (caractère éprouvé)". Fill 4's answer is "victoire" ("la ____ dans l'épreuve"), and it also accepts "caractère", "caractère éprouvé" and "valeur éprouvée".
4. **Rom 5:5, "hope does not disappoint".** Segond has "l'espérance ne trompe point". I used this in b024, MC 7 and SA 3.
5. **Rom 5:6, "powerless ... the ungodly".** Segond has "sans force … pour des impies". The four words in b026 are therefore "sans force, impies, pécheurs, ennemis".
6. **Rom 5:8, "demonstrates His own love".** Segond has "prouve son amour". The b025 heading became "Dieu prouve son amour".
7. **Rom 5:14, "type".** Segond has "la figure de celui qui devait venir". In b037 I added "le texte grec dit littéralement le « type »" and then explained what a type is. MC 17 and SA 8 use "figure (type)". Fill 9's answer is "figure", and it also accepts "type", "modèle" and "préfiguration".
8. **"Federal head".** "Chef fédéral" is the technical French term, but few Kinshasa readers will know it. I glossed it once in b036 as "chef fédéral — c'est-à-dire représentant". Fill 8's answer is "fédéral", and it also accepts "représentatif", "représentant" and "de l'alliance".
9. **Soteriology wording.** I translated b015 ("standing forever") and b047 ("settled it forever in our favor") literally ("qui demeure pour toujours", "l'a tranchée pour toujours en notre faveur"). I did not strengthen them toward eternal security, but a reader could take them that way. Please confirm that this matches the open-question stance.
10. **Original sin (b036, MC 16, SA 6).** The English teaches federal headship and the imputation of Adam's *guilt*. This is the Reformed formulation; many Arminian and Wesleyan evangelicals say instead that we inherit a corrupted nature rather than Adam's guilt. I translated it faithfully. Do you want a neutral sentence that mentions both views?
11. **"Forensic" (b016, Fill 1).** I rendered it as "juridique" (it is clearer than "forensique"). Fill 1 also accepts "judiciaire", "légale" and "forensique".

## Problems in the English source

1. **MC 11, MC 12, SA 10.** The lesson never teaches reconciliation (5:10-11), and it never contrasts reconciliation with justification. b026 only mentions "enemies" (v. 10). The SA 10 model's material (legal vs. relational standing, "enemies made friends") is not in the lesson.
2. **MC 4.** The lesson says "completed action" but never uses the term "aorist". The answer key is correct. In French, the stem says "dans le texte grec".
3. **MC 9, SA 4.** "asthenon" appears only in exam items, not in the lesson. Also, the Greek form in 5:6 is *asthenōn*.
4. **SA 9.** Keywords "reveal / expose / exceed" and the model's phrase "so sin could be fully seen" go beyond the lesson, which says only that the law entered "that the offense might abound". The French model follows the English.
5. **SA 5.** The keyword "unilat" is not a lesson term. b032 never says "unilateral".
6. All MC answer keys look correct, and each `why` matches its question.
