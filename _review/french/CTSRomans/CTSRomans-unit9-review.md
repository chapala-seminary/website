## Questions for Wayne

1. **Romans 12:1, "I beseech you".** LSG has "Je vous exhorte". The lesson (b016) contrasts *command* with *beseech*. I kept "exhorte" (LSG) and added that the Greek verb means both "exhorter" and "supplier", so the idea of pleading is still there.
2. **Romans 12:1, "by the mercies of God".** LSG has "par les compassions de Dieu". The lesson, MC1, SA1 and b050 use LSG's "compassions". b016 adds "c'est-à-dire à ses miséricordes". The SA1 keyword group accepts both "compassion" and "miséricord".
3. **Romans 12:1, "reasonable service".** LSG has "un culte raisonnable", so the sense of worship is kept. MC2 and b050 use "culte raisonnable".
4. **Romans 12:2, "this world" / "renewing of your mind".** LSG has "au siècle présent" and "le renouvellement de l'intelligence". The prose speaks of "le moule du monde" and "le renouvellement de l'intelligence". MC4 uses LSG's wording.
5. **Romans 12:3, "sober judgment".** LSG has "revêtir des sentiments modestes". b021 quotes LSG and adds "le texte grec dit littéralement « penser avec sobriété »". The SA3 keyword group accepts "sobr" or "modest".
6. **Romans 12:9, "Let love be without hypocrisy".** LSG has "Que la charité soit sans hypocrisie". b026 adds one sentence saying that Segond's "charité" means love (Greek *agapè*).
7. **Romans 12:19-20, "give place to wrath" / "heap coals of fire".** LSG has "laissez agir la colère" and "des charbons ardents que tu amasseras sur sa tête". The lesson, MC9, Fill 5 and SA4 all use "charbons ardents". The Old Testament verses cited in b030 have "charbons" (Ps 11:6), "charbons de feu" (Ps 18:13), "charbons embrasés" (2 Sam 22:9) and "charbons ardents" (Ps 140:10; Prov 25:22), so the link still works.
8. **Romans 12:21, "eight words" (b031, MC10).** The French sentence has more than eight words. I wrote "en une seule ligne" / "en une seule phrase" instead.
9. **Romans 13:1, "governing authorities".** LSG has "autorités supérieures". The prose says "les autorités qui gouvernent" or "les autorités". Fill 6 has the answer "soumis" (LSG "soumise/soumis").
10. **Romans 13:9, "false witness".** The lesson (b040) and SA6 list "false witness" among the commandments that love sums up. LSG's text of 13:9 does not include "Tu ne diras point de faux témoignage" (a textual variant the NKJV follows). I kept "le faux témoignage" in the prose, because it describes the commandments in general and is not a quotation. Please confirm.
11. **Romans 13:12, "armor of light".** LSG has "les armes de la lumière". Fill 9 has the answer "armes" and also accepts "armure".
12. **Romans 13:13 vice list.** b041 and SA7 use LSG's list: "les excès et l'ivrognerie, la luxure et l'impudicité, les querelles et les jalousies".
13. **Romans 14:1, "disputes over doubtful things".** LSG has "ne discutez pas sur les opinions". It is quoted as is. The prose uses "questions discutables" for "disputable matters".
14. **Romans 14:23, "whatever is not from faith is sin".** LSG has "Tout ce qui n'est pas le produit d'une conviction est péché". This verse is central to b047, MC19, Fill 10 and SA10. Following convention 1, I quoted LSG and added "le texte grec dit littéralement « tout ce qui ne vient pas de la foi est péché »". Fill 10 has the answer "conviction" (LSG) and also accepts "foi".
15. **Romans 14:4, "Master".** The lesson's word "Master" is rendered as LSG's "maître" ("cela regarde son maître"), followed by "le Seigneur a le pouvoir de l'affermir" (LSG).
16. **Perseverance.** b041 ("le salut est plus près de nous que lorsque nous avons cru") and b047 ("qu'il se tienne debout ou qu'il tombe… le Seigneur a le pouvoir de l'affermir") are translated literally, with no comment that would make them affirm or deny eternal security.

## Problems in the English source

1. **SA8 model vs. lesson.** The model lists "four foundations": the Lord's servant; giving account; the strong must not despise and the weak must not judge; and do not violate conscience. The lesson's four foundations (b047) are different: servant, giving account, the kingdom is not eating and drinking, and whatever is not of faith is sin. I translated the model as written.
2. **SA9 model.** It quotes Romans 14:20 ("do not destroy the work of God for the sake of food"), which the lesson never mentions or teaches. It also says the kingdom is "bigger than disputes that have divided churches throughout history", which is not in the lesson. This is minor. I translated it as written.
3. **b030, MC9, SA4, "coals of fire".** The lesson says the Old Testament background is "consistently" God's judgment, but the list includes Proverbs 25:21-22. Paul is quoting that passage, so it is not independent evidence, and its own context ("the LORD will reward you") is often read as leading the enemy to shame or repentance. 2 Samuel 22:9 and Psalm 18:13 describe a theophany more than judgment on a person. The view is defensible, but the lesson presents it as the only view. I translated it faithfully.
4. **MC3, distractor "Schematizō (to scheme)".** The verb Paul actually uses for "conformed" in 12:2 is *syschēmatizō*, which means "to conform to a pattern", not "to scheme". The distractor is still wrong as an answer, but its gloss is inaccurate. I translated it as "manigancer". **Updated 12 Oct 2026:** now « Syschēmatizō (se conformer à un modèle) », matching the corrected English.
5. **English SA keywords.** In SA1, SA2, SA3 and SA6, the English prompt alone reaches 3 or more keyword hits (for example, SA3 reaches 4 with "body/gift/tool/weapon"). Following convention 10, I rebuilt the French groups around ideas from the answers. Every French prompt reaches 0 hits, and every French model reaches 8.
