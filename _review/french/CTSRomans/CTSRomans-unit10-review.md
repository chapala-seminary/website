# CTSRomans Unit 10 — French draft review

## Questions for Wayne

1. **Romans 16:1 "servant" (b032, b033, MC7, fill6, SA5).** LSG translates *diakonos* here as « diaconesse », not "servant". So b033 now reads "the word Segond translates *diaconesse* is *diakonos*". Fill6 now says « Segond appelle Phoebé diaconesse… le mot grec est ____ », and the answer is still "diakonos". LSG's « diaconesse » goes further than the NKJV's "servant", so please confirm you are comfortable with it, given that CTS stays neutral on women's roles.
2. **Romans 16:7 Junia (b037, MC12, SA6).** LSG has « Junias », a man's name. The lesson counts Junia among the women. I kept LSG's spelling and added one sentence saying that the Greek name was most often read by ancient readers as the woman's name Junia. MC12 and SA6 use "Junias (Junia)". Please check that this gloss is acceptable.
3. **Romans 16:3 Priscilla (b037, MC9, SA6).** LSG has « Prisca » in Romans and « Priscille » in Acts. I used Prisca and noted Priscille once. The SA keyword "prisc" catches both forms.
4. **Romans 15:1 "scruples of the weak" (b015, MC1, SA1).** LSG has « supporter les faiblesses de ceux qui ne le sont pas ». I quoted LSG in the lesson and in MC1.
5. **Romans 15:3 / 15:5 (b016, b017).** "Reproaches" became « outrages ». LSG has "please" as « se complaire / complaire ». "God of patience" in 15:5 is « le Dieu de la persévérance » in LSG. That word means endurance there, not the doctrine of perseverance that b049 mentions. A French reader may link the two, so tell me if you want a short gloss.
6. **"Able to establish you — the doctrine of perseverance" (b049, SA9).** I translated this faithfully as « la doctrine de la persévérance ». Because CTS treats eternal security as an open question, please confirm the English wording itself. As written, it can be read as affirming eternal security. I did not strengthen or soften it.
7. **Romans 15:27 "material things" (b027, b028, SA4).** LSG has « choses temporelles ». I added one sentence saying the Greek literally says "carnal", meaning material goods. SA4 accepts "matériel" or "temporel".
8. **Romans 16:19 "simple concerning evil" (b042, MC14, fill8).** LSG has « purs ». I added one sentence on the Greek sense ("unmixed, simple, innocent"). The fill8 answer is "purs", and "pur", "simples", "innocents" and "sans mélange" are also accepted.
9. **Romans 16:25-27 doxology (b048, b049, MC18, MC19, fill10).** LSG differs from the NKJV in several places. It has « affermir » for "establish" and « caché pendant des siècles » for "kept secret since the world began". It also puts « par Jésus-Christ » after « aux siècles des siècles ». b049's phrase-by-phrase commentary now quotes the LSG phrases, and the MC19 correct option is LSG verbatim.
10. **Romans 1:1 "bondservant" (b050, MC20, SA10).** LSG has « serviteur ». I added « (le grec dit littéralement « esclave ») ». SA10 accepts "serviteur" or "esclave".
11. **"Gentiles".** I used LSG's « païens » in quotations and wherever the lesson echoes the text. Elsewhere I used « non-Juifs / non juifs », and glossed it once in b019.
12. **"The priesthood has been democratized" (b020, MC3).** I rendered this as « le sacerdoce est ouvert à tous ». "Démocratisé" sounds political in French.

## Problems in the English source

1. **MC12** has the option "fellow prisoners with Paul", but the lesson (b037) never mentions it. The correct answer is still identifiable.
2. **SA10** tests the structure of the whole letter (chs. 1–3, 3–5, 6–8, 9–11, 12–16; civil authority). This unit does not teach that structure. It depends on earlier units.
3. **SA6**: the English keyword "solo" never appears in the English model ("lone-ranger"). **SA9**: the keyword "worship" never appears in the English model ("praise"). The French models hit all 8 groups.
4. **Fill7** says "a body of many members, as in chapter 12". This unit says only "it produces a body". The blank is still answerable.
5. **b028** says the Jerusalem believers "were facing famine". Romans 15 speaks only of "the poor". The famine of Acts 11:28 was about ten years earlier. Consider softening this to "poverty".
6. **b049** quotes "for obedience to the faith among all nations" as if it were one phrase. In the doxology as quoted in b048, those words are not next to each other.

## Checker's changes

- Scripture: every LSG quotation and partial quotation checked with the offline Segond tool (Rom 15:1-3, 5, 13, 16, 18-20, 27, 30-31; 16:1-7, 13, 16-20, 25-27; 1:1). All were verbatim. No wording corrections were needed.
- b029: the Rom 15:30-31 quote was set inside a third-person sentence ("Il les exhorte à combattre avec moi…"), which mixed persons. It is now a clean LSG partial quote: « Je vous exhorte, frères, […] à combattre avec moi… de la Judée. »
- b019, MC3, SA2: "l'Esprit-Saint" changed to "le Saint-Esprit" (BRIEF convention; b019 paraphrases 15:16 here and does not quote it).
- MC20 why: "vivre de la même manière" was vague. It now says "vivre, lui aussi, en serviteur de Jésus-Christ", which matches the English "live as one".
- SA4: in the keyword group for "bond", "unir" was replaced with "rapproch" and "soudé/soude". "unir" also matches unrelated words such as "réunir" (collecting money) and "punir". The model still hits 8/8 groups.
- Theology: soteriology checked. b049/SA9 ("la doctrine de la persévérance") is exactly as strong as the English, no stronger (Question 6 stays open for Wayne). b050 and SA10 (justice offered freely, imputed apart from works) are faithful. No drift found.
- Validator: 0 errors. The remaining warning (fill10 has only "sages" as an alternate) is a false alarm, because the blank sits in a verbatim doxology line with one correct word.
