# CTSRomans Unit 8 — French draft review

Checker re-verified every quotation against the offline LSG (Rom 8:39; 9:1-6, 12, 15-17, 20-25, 27, 32-33; 10:4, 8-9, 13-15, 21; 11:1-5, 17-18, 25-26, 29, 32-36). Two small deviations corrected (see Checker's changes); all others exact.

## Questions for Wayne

1. **Rom 9:3 "accursed" (b015, MC1, Fill 1).** LSG: "anathème et séparé de Christ". Fill 1 answer **anathème**, accepting "maudit(s)", "anathèmes".
2. **Rom 9:12 "the older shall serve the younger" (b022, Fill 2).** LSG: "L'aîné sera assujetti au plus jeune". b022 quotes LSG and adds "c'est-à-dire que l'aîné servira le plus jeune". Fill 2 answer **plus jeune** (accept cadet, plus petit, puîné).
3. **Rom 9:21 "for honor / for dishonor" (b026).** LSG: "un vase d'honneur et un vase d'un usage vil". Nothing in the lesson depends on the exact word, so I quoted LSG without comment.
4. **Rom 10:4 "for righteousness" (b030, b031, Fill 4).** LSG: "pour la justification". Because the lesson says Christ "is the righteousness the law demanded", b031 adds that the Greek literally says "pour la justice". Fill 4 answer **fin** (accept but, terme, aboutissement, accomplissement, since the lesson teaches both senses of telos).
5. **Rom 10:15 "the gospel of peace" (b033, MC10, Fill 6).** LSG (critical text): "Qu'ils sont beaux Les pieds de ceux qui annoncent la paix, De ceux qui annoncent de bonnes nouvelles!" It has no single word for "gospel". I quoted LSG in all three places.
6. **Rom 11:17 "grafted in among them" (b038, b039).** LSG has "enté **à leur place**". That could suggest replacement, which is the opposite of the lesson's one-tree point. So b039 opens with: "le texte grec dit littéralement « parmi elles »". Segond's word "enté" is noted once in b037. Elsewhere the prose uses "greffé" (SA keyword "greff").
7. **Rom 11:25 "blindness in part" / "fullness" (b041, b042, MC17, Fill 9).** LSG: "une partie d'Israël est tombée dans l'endurcissement, jusqu'à ce que la totalité des païens soit entrée". b041 adds the literal sense ("un endurcissement en partie", comparable to blindness; "totalité" = literally "plénitude"). Fill 9 answer **totalité** (accept plénitude, nombre complet, pleine mesure).
8. **Rom 11:29 "irrevocable" (b044, MC19, Fill 10, SA9).** LSG: "Dieu ne se repent pas de ses dons et de son appel." b044 quotes LSG and adds that the Greek literally says "sans repentir", meaning irrevocable. Fill 10 answer **irrévocables** (accept irrévocable, sans repentir, sans repentance, définitifs). The prompt includes "(Dieu ne s'en repent pas)" so that it links to LSG.
9. **Perseverance (b014, b017, b039, b048, MC15 why).** Checker confirmed: b039 "could remove him if he turned from faith" = "pourrait le retrancher s'il se détournait de la foi", same conditional strength, nothing added. The chapter-8 "nothing can separate" lines are LSG quotations or close renderings and do not go beyond the English.
10. **Election wording (b022, b023, MC4, SA3).** "Sovereign election" and "those God had sovereignly chosen" are kept as "élection souveraine" and "souverainement choisis". I did not add conditional-election commentary, because the lesson itself ties the true Israel to Abraham's faith (b021, b023). "Hardening is His prerogative" (b025) is also kept as written.
11. **Eschatology tilt (b043, MC18, SA8).** The English says "There is no separate destiny for ethnic Israel apart from the church", and MC18 marks "a separate millennial program for ethnic Israel" as wrong. Dispensational readers will read this as taking a side. I translated it faithfully. Do you want this softened for even-handedness?
12. **"Gentiles".** The prose uses "non-Juifs". Quotations keep LSG's "païens". This matches the other Romans units.

## Problems in the English source

1. **SA3, SA4, SA10 prompts pass their own keywords in English.** SA3 names Isaac, Ishmael and Jacob, SA4 names potter, clay and mercy, and SA10 contains mystery, mercy and doxology. I reworded the French prompts, for example "les deux exemples tirés des patriarches", "l'image de Romains 9:20-21" and "un chant de louange", so they no longer pass on their own.
2. **SA4 keywords "author" and "maker".** Neither appears in the lesson or in the model answer. In French I used "droit/maître" and "créat-" (the model answer says "Créateur").
3. **SA6 model.** "The same gospel that elects also commissions" says the gospel elects, which is odd wording. I rendered it as "Le même Évangile qui parle d'élection envoie aussi en mission".
4. **b043 / MC18.** See Question 11. The lesson labels one reading "the most natural" but then states "no separate destiny" as settled. This may conflict with the even-handed eschatology standard.

- fullness of the Gentiles → la totalité des païens

- stumbling stone → pierre d'achoppement
- prerogative → prérogative
- doxology → louange / doxologie
- mē genoito → « Loin de là ! »
