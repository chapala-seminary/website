# CTSRomans Unit 2 — French draft review notes

Checker re-verified every quotation (blocks, MC stems/options/why, fills, SA models) against the offline LSG: Romains 1:18-32, 2:1-29, 3:1-23, Jacques 2:10, Matthieu 5:48, Galates 3:24. All match Segond exactly; none needed correction.

## Questions for Wayne

1. **Rom 1:18, "suppress the truth in unrighteousness".** LSG has "qui retiennent injustement la vérité captive". I quoted LSG in b020 and MC 1, then added: "Le texte grec dit littéralement qu'ils retiennent la vérité « dans l'injustice »". The first step reads "l'humanité retient la vérité captive dans l'injustice" in b020, MC 2 and SA 2. Fill 1 has the answer "retient" and also accepts "étouffe", "réprime", "supprime" and "retiennent".
2. **Rom 1:28, "debased mind".** LSG has "leur sens réprouvé". In b026 I added one sentence on the Greek sense ("une intelligence qui n'a pas résisté à l'épreuve… une pensée dépravée"). Fill 5 has the answer "réprouvé" and also accepts "dépravé", "perverti", "corrompu" and "pervers".
3. **Rom 2:11, "no partiality".** LSG has "il n'y a point d'acception de personnes". The heading word in b030 is "sans partialité", and the LSG verse follows it. Fill 6 has the answer "acception" and also accepts "partialité", "favoritisme" and "préférence". MC 8 gives both forms.
4. **Rom 2:29, "in the Spirit".** LSG has a lowercase "selon l'esprit". The lesson and MC 10 refer to the Holy Spirit. In b035 I added: "Le texte grec peut aussi se comprendre « par l'Esprit »…". MC 10 option B says "par l'Esprit". Please confirm this.
5. **Rom 3:9, "all under sin".** LSG has "sous l'empire du péché". I used this in b016 and the b036 heading. LSG's 3:9 begins differently ("Quoi donc! sommes-nous plus excellents?…"), so b016 quotes only from "Car nous avons déjà prouvé…".
6. **Rom 3:23, "fall short".** LSG has "sont privés de la gloire de Dieu". In b042 I added a note on the Greek sense ("manquent… restent en deçà").
7. **Rom 1:20, "Godhead / divine nature".** LSG has "sa puissance éternelle et sa divinité". "Without excuse" is "inexcusables". I used these in MC 3 and MC 15.
8. **Rom 1:21, "neither were thankful".** LSG has "ne lui ont point rendu grâces". The MC 4 blank now takes the adjective "reconnaissants", and the option order is unchanged.
9. **Rom 1:26, "vile passions".** LSG has "passions infâmes". I quoted only the opening clause of 1:26 and did not expand on the subject, as the English does not.
10. **"Gentiles."** LSG uses "païens" (Rom 2:14) and "Grecs" (3:9). I used "païens" throughout.
11. **b027, "the whole list is named 'wickedness'".** LSG heads the list with "toute espèce d'injustice", so the sentence now refers to that phrase.
12. **Rom 3:12, "unprofitable".** LSG has "pervertis". I quoted it as is. No exam item depends on it.

## Problems in the English source

1. **b041, "sixty-six verses".** Romans 1:18–3:20 is 15 + 29 + 20 = 64 verses. I translated it as written ("soixante-six"). **Updated 12 Oct 2026:** now « soixante-quatre », matching the corrected English.
2. **b037, "Can we sin so that grace may abound?"** This wording is Romans 6:1. The objection in chapter 3 is 3:8, "do evil that good may come". I translated it as written.
3. **b020, b022, b026, b027 and b029.** These quote an NIV-style wording, while the scripture blocks are labeled NKJV. This makes no difference in French, where everything is LSG.
4. **MC 4 (Rom 1:21, thankfulness).** The lesson never teaches this point.
5. **MC 19 (Rom 1:32, "deserving of death").** The lesson never mentions this. b026 only says the debased mind "approves" evildoers.
6. **MC 15 why ("anapologetos").** The Greek word does not appear in the lesson. This is minor.
7. **SA 8 model.** It introduces the law as "schoolmaster" (Galatians 3:24), which the lesson does not teach. I rendered it with the LSG word "pédagogue".
8. No wrong answer keys were found. Every SA item has a model answer. Nothing in this unit touches perseverance or election, and I rendered nothing with a stronger or weaker sense than the English.
