## Questions for Wayne

1. **Galatians 1:8, 1:9 "accursed".** LSG has "qu'il soit anathème!". Quoted LSG (b004, b009, b025) and added one sentence in b025: the Greek word "anathème" means "maudit", handed over to God's condemnation. SA6 accepts "anathème"/"maudit".
2. **Galatians 1:6-7 "a different gospel, which is not another".** LSG: "pour passer à un autre Evangile. Non pas qu'il y ait un autre Evangile". b025 now reads: what Paul calls « un autre Évangile », while adding at once « Non pas qu'il y ait un autre Évangile ». Same point, different wording. The heading "Why This Is 'Another Gospel'" works directly with LSG.
3. **Galatians 1:7 "pervert".** LSG has "renverser l'Évangile de Christ" (overturn). b025 keeps "pervertir l'Évangile"; SA6 model uses LSG's "renverser", and its keyword group accepts renvers-/pervert-/déform-.
4. **John 8:58 "I AM".** LSG has lowercase "je suis" with no visible link to God's name. Added in b021: "Le texte grec dit littéralement « moi, je suis », les mots mêmes par lesquels Dieu se nomme en Exode 3:14." Please confirm the Exodus 3:14 link is wording you accept.
5. **Ephesians 4:15 "the truth in love".** LSG: "professant la vérité dans la charité". Quoted LSG and glossed "c'est-à-dire dans l'amour", since "charité" in everyday French often means alms.
6. **Hebrews 1:8 "Your throne, O God, is forever and ever".** LSG: "Ton trône, ô Dieu, est éternel". No effect on the argument.
7. **Luke 24:39 "flesh and bones".** The English only uses the phrase in passing. I quoted LSG in full ("un esprit n'a ni chair ni os, comme vous voyez que j'ai"), which strengthens the point without changing it.
8. **2 Corinthians 6:14 "unequally yoked together with unbelievers".** LSG: "Ne vous mettez pas avec les infidèles sous un joug étranger". Quoted LSG and added "(ici, « infidèles » veut dire les non-croyants)", because "infidèle" now usually means "unfaithful".
9. **2 John 9-10 "transgresses … does not … greet him".** LSG: "Quiconque va plus loin … ne lui dites pas: Salut!". Fill 10 uses LSG's wording; the answer "doctrine" is the same in both. SA7 accepts "salue"/"dites pas".

(MC10 matches current Witness teaching, but the lesson does not teach it.) Fill 4 follows the lesson. Translated as written.

3. **SA keywords.** The English prompt alone reaches 3+ keywords in SA4 (christ, created, angel, god, eternal), SA5, SA6, SA8, SA9 (resurrection, body, deity, christ, gospel) and SA10. Following convention 10, the French groups are 8 distinct ideas from the lesson; no French prompt hits any of its own groups.

The lesson teaches none of these. They are sound but untaught; translated as written and not used as keywords.
