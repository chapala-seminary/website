# CTSGalatians Unit 6: French draft review

Checker re-verified every quotation against the offline LSG tool (Galates 5:1-26, Exode 20:15): all exact.

## Questions for Wayne

1. **5:1 word order (b009, b011, MC1, Fill 1, SA1).** Segond: « C'est pour la liberté que Christ nous a affranchis. Demeurez donc fermes… sous le joug de la servitude. » The heading is « Demeurez fermes dans la liberté ». Fill 1 now quotes Segond, so the answer is **joug** (also accepts "jougs"). The validator warns that there are fewer than 3 alternates. I did not add "fardeau" because it is not a correct synonym here, and the English source also has only one alternate.
2. **5:2 "profit you nothing" (b013, MC3, SA2).** Segond: « Christ ne vous servira de rien ». MC3 options follow Segond (« de tout / de beaucoup / de rien / d'un peu »).
3. **5:3 "a debtor" (b013, MC4, SA2).** Segond: « il est tenu de pratiquer la loi tout entière ». I added one sentence saying the Greek word means "débiteur". MC4 uses "devient débiteur, tenu de pratiquer". SA2 accepts débiteur / tenu de / obligé.
4. **5:4 "estranged… fallen from grace" (b012, b013, MC5, Fill 2).** Segond: « séparés de Christ… déchus de la grâce ». The heading is « Déchus de la grâce ». I kept the English's open wording on final security.
5. **5:6 "faith working through love" (b014–b016, MC6, MC7, Fill 3, SA3).** Segond has « la foi qui est agissante par la charité ». I added a sentence saying that the Greek word is *agapè*, self-giving love. Fill 3 answer: **charité**. It also accepts amour, agapè and amour de Dieu. The heading uses Segond's wording. Please confirm that "charité" reads well for Kinshasa readers, who may hear it as "almsgiving".
6. **5:7 "hindered" (b017, b018, MC8).** Segond: « qui vous a arrêtés, pour vous empêcher… ». The heading is « Qui vous a arrêtés ? ». The MC8 answer option is « arrêtés ».
7. **5:8 "persuasion" → Segond "influence".** SA4 accepts influence/persua.
8. **5:11 "the offense of the cross has ceased"** → Segond « le scandale de la croix a donc disparu ». Fill 5 and MC10 use "scandale".
9. **5:12** Segond softens the English ("Puissent-ils être retranchés"). The lesson's paraphrase about the knife is kept, and it uses "se retranchent", which echoes Segond.
10. **5:13 "opportunity for the flesh"** → Segond « un prétexte de vivre selon la chair ». MC11 is reworded around "prétexte".
11. **5:16 "Walk in the Spirit… lust of the flesh"** → Segond « Marchez selon l'Esprit… les désirs de la chair ». The heading, MC14 and Fill 7 follow Segond. Fill 7 accepts "nature pécheresse" because the English accepts "sinful nature".
12. **5:19-21, the vice list (b024, MC17, SA7).** Segond has no "adultery" and no "murders" (Textus Receptus vs. critical text), and its list differs ("inimitiés, animosités, sectes, excès de table"). I added one parenthetical: « Certains manuscrits grecs ajoutent aussi l'adultère et les meurtres ». The English MC17 key "outbursts of wrath and jealousies" became « les animosités et les jalousies ». SA7's "adultery"/"hatred"/"wrath" groups were widened to impudic/adult/fornic/impur, inimiti/haine and animosit/colère. Please confirm that the note on manuscripts is acceptable for this audience.
13. **5:21 "practice"** → Segond « commettent ». I added that the Greek verb means habitual practice. MC18 is restructured so that the option still completes the sentence.
14. **5:22-23** Segond: "longsuffering" = patience, "kindness" = bonté, "goodness" = bénignité, "self-control" = tempérance. "Against such there is no law" = « la loi n'est pas contre ces choses ».
15. **5:25-26** Segond: « vivons par l'Esprit, marchons aussi selon l'Esprit »; "conceited" = « chercher une vaine gloire ». The SA9 keyword group is vaine gloire/orgueil/vanit.

## Problems in the English source

1. **MC5 why vs. lesson.** The `why` says it is "not to forfeit a salvation grasped by faith". This leans on the eternal-security question, which b013 and SA2 explicitly leave open. Option A ("a debated conclusion about final security") is also an odd distractor. I translated it faithfully. Please consider softening the `why`.
2. **SA prompts.** Several English SA prompts (1, 2, 3, 5, 6, 8, 9) quote the verse and so hit their own keywords. I reworded the French prompts. None reaches more than 2 hits.
3. **SA3 keyword "badge".** The lesson uses this only as a metaphor ("the badge of the flesh"). In French it is "la marque dans la chair", and the keyword group is "marque dans la chair"/insigne.
4. **SA6 keyword "war".** It is in the lesson, but not in the verse. The group is guerre/combat/lutte.
5. All MC answer keys are correct, and every `why` belongs to its own question.

## Checker's changes

- SA3: tightened over-broad stems ("seul", "marque", "agit"/"oeuvre" alone) to "foi seule/jamais seul…", "marque dans la chair…", "elle agit/qui agit…" so a wrong answer cannot score on common words.
- SA6: "conduit" group narrowed to "conduits par/guidés par…" (bare "conduit" matched "conduite" etc.).
- Fill 1 warning kept: "joug" has only "jougs" as an alternate; no true synonym exists.
