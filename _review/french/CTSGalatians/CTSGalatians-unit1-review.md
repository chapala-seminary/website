## Questions for Wayne

1. **Gal 1:6-7, "a different gospel, which is not another".** Segond has "pour passer à un autre Évangile. Non pas qu'il y ait un autre Évangile", so the "different / another" contrast is gone. The contrast is the basis of the b016 heading, b017, MC8 and SA4. I quoted Segond and added in b017: "Le texte grec dit littéralement : « un Évangile différent, qui n'en est pas un autre »". MC8 and the SA4 prompt quote Segond's two phrases.
2. **Gal 1:7, "pervert the gospel".** Segond has "renverser l'Évangile". In b017 I added one sentence: the Greek verb also means "turn around, pervert". MC9's correct option is "Renverser l'Évangile de Christ". Elsewhere the lesson prose uses "déformer". SA4 accepts pervers/déform/renvers.
3. **Gal 1:4, "deliver us from this present evil age".** Segond has "nous arracher du présent siècle mauvais". MC4 uses "arracher", and Fill 4's answer is "siècle"; it also accepts "âge", "monde" and "époque".
4. **Gal 1:6, "I marvel… so soon".** Segond has "Je m'étonne… si promptement". This is used throughout (b009, b015, MC6, MC7, SA3).
5. **Gal 1:8-9, "accursed".** Segond has "qu'il soit anathème", which is the Greek word itself. So the lesson's "the word translated *accursed* is *anathema*" became: "the word *anathème* takes over the Greek word itself, *anathema*: devoted to destruction… that is, accursed" (b020). MC12's correct option is "Anathème". MC13's stem now asks for "the Greek word Paul uses for 'accursed'". Fill 7 now quotes "qu'il soit ____" (as the Spanish edition does); its answer is "anathème", and it also accepts "anathema" and "maudit".
6. **Gal 1:10, "persuade men, or God?" / "bondservant".** Segond has "est-ce la faveur des hommes que je désire, ou celle de Dieu?" and "serviteur de Christ". In b022 I added: "the Greek word translated *serviteur* literally means *esclave*". Fill 8's answer is "serviteur", and it also accepts "esclave".
7. **"Make Him Lord" (b024, b029)** is rendered "le reconnaître comme Seigneur" ("acknowledge Him as Lord"). This avoids the odd-sounding "le faire Seigneur". Please confirm.
8. **"Lord's Supper"** is rendered "la sainte cène" (convention 7). **"Infallibility"** is "infaillibilité". **"Investigative judgment"** is "jugement investigatif". **"Lesser light"** is "moindre lumière". Please ask the French reader to confirm "jugement investigatif", which is the usual French Adventist term.

## Problems in the English source

1. **b038, factual.** *Questions on Doctrine* (1957) was written by Adventist leaders. Their answers came out of the Martin–Barnhouse dialogues. Martin and Barnhouse's own conclusion appeared in *Eternity* magazine (1956) and in Martin's *The Truth About Seventh-day Adventism* (1960). The phrase "they concluded, in the resulting book *Questions on Doctrine*" therefore credits the conclusion to the wrong book. I translated it as written.
2. **SA prompts pass their own English keywords.** This happens in SA1 (apostl, author, men), SA3 (4 hits), SA4, SA6, SA7 and SA9 (3 hits each). In French, I chose keyword groups under convention 10. No French prompt has more than 1 hit, and every model has 6–8 hits.
3. **MC10's `why` and the SA5 model** call the Judaizers' teaching "the first false gospel" and "the pattern every later counterfeit follows". The lesson never says "first". This is minor, and I translated it.
4. **SA1 and SA7 keywords** include "author". The lesson never discusses authorship, so I did not use that group in French.
