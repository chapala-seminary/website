# CTSGalatians Unit 5 — French draft review

## Questions for Wayne

1. **Gal 4:7, "an heir of God through Christ."** LSG follows a different Greek text and reads "tu es aussi héritier par la grâce de Dieu". I quoted LSG in b004, b016 and MC 6, and added one sentence in b016: many Greek manuscripts read "héritier de Dieu par Christ". The MC 6 correct option is "héritier par la grâce de Dieu". The MC 6 `why` now uses the LSG wording too (checker's change), so it matches the correct option. Fill 5 has the answer "héritier" and also accepts "héritier de Dieu".
2. **Gal 4:4-5, "fullness of the time" and "adoption as sons."** LSG has "lorsque les temps ont été accomplis" and "l'adoption" (without "as sons"). In b013 I added: "Le texte grec dit littéralement « quand est venue la plénitude du temps », and the word for adoption means adoption as sons." MC 4 and Fill 3 use "l'adoption". Fill 3 also accepts "adoption filiale" and "adoption comme fils".
3. **Gal 4:6, "into your hearts."** LSG has "dans nos coeurs" (a textual variant). I quoted LSG, and MC 5 and Fill 4 say "nos cœurs".
4. **Gal 4:17, "they want to exclude you."** LSG has "ils veulent vous détacher de nous". In b022 I added a note that the Greek verb means "exclure". The MC 12 correct option reads "de les détacher (les exclure)…".
5. **Gal 4:2 and 4:3, "stewards" and "elements."** LSG has "administrateurs" and "rudiments". Fill 1 has the answer "administrateurs" and also accepts "intendants", "gérants" and "économes". Fill 2 has the answer "rudiments" and also accepts "éléments" and "principes élémentaires".
6. **Gal 4:25, "earthly Jerusalem."** LSG has "la Jérusalem actuelle". In b024 I wrote "la Jérusalem actuelle, la Jérusalem terrestre" so that both forms appear. The Fill 9 prompt uses "actuelle".
7. **Gal 4:12-13.** LSG puts the words in a different order: "Soyez comme moi, car moi aussi je suis comme vous. Frères, je vous en supplie." LSG 4:13 has "à cause d'une infirmité de la chair… annoncé l'Évangile" where the NKJV has "in physical infirmity… preached". b020 and MC 10 follow LSG.
8. **Gal 4:26 "the mother of us all" and 4:30-31 "bondwoman/freewoman."** LSG has "c'est notre mère" (used in MC 18) and "l'esclave / la femme libre" throughout. "Cast out the bondwoman" is "Chasse l'esclave". I used these forms in the headings and exam items.

## Problems in the English source

1. **SA 6 model:** the last clause is garbled: "and he is good to be zealous for them whether or not he is present". I translated the sense of the lesson instead (4:18: it is good to be zealous for what is good at all times, not only when Paul is present).
2. **MC 19 `why`:** it says believers are "born of God's word", but the lesson says "born of the promise of God". I translated it as written. This is minor.
3. **MC 1:** two of the distractors ("a stranger", "a foreigner") are near-synonyms. I rendered them as "un inconnu" and "un étranger".

## French glossary used

- heir → héritier
- guardians and stewards → tuteurs et administrateurs
- elements (of the world) → rudiments (du monde)
- fullness of the time → lorsque les temps ont été accomplis / plénitude du temps
- redeem / redemption → racheter / rédemption
- adoption as sons → adoption (condition de fils)
- bondwoman / freewoman → (femme) esclave / femme libre
- bondage → esclavage, servitude
- covenants → alliances
- symbolic / allegory → allégoriques / allégorie
- children of promise → enfants de la promesse
- false teachers → faux docteurs
- labor in birth → éprouver les douleurs de l'enfantement
