## Questions for Wayne

1. **Romans 14:5, "fully convinced in his own mind".** Segond has "Que chacun ait en son esprit une pleine conviction". I quoted Segond in b016 and b022, and in MC10 and SA9. Fill 5 answer: "esprit". It also accepts "propre esprit", "pensée" and "intelligence".
2. **Colossians 2:17, "the substance is of Christ".** Segond has "le corps est en Christ". In b016 I quoted Segond and added one sentence saying the Greek word for "corps" means the reality itself, as opposed to the shadow. MC9's option reads "une ombre, dont la réalité est Christ". Fill 4 ("ombre") is unaffected.
3. **John 19:30, "It is finished".** Segond has "Tout est accompli". Fill 9 answer: "accompli". It also accepts "achevé", "consommé", "terminé" and "fini". The lesson prose uses "achevée" for the finished atonement.
4. **Romans 15:7, "Receive one another, just as Christ also received us".** Segond has "Accueillez-vous donc les uns les autres, comme Christ vous a accueillis" ("accueillir", and "vous" where the NKJV has "us"). I quoted Segond in b024, b025 and MC20, and the b023 heading follows it. Fill 10 answer: "accueillis". It also accepts "accueilli", "accueillies", "reçus" and "reçu".
5. **Romans 14:4, "God is able to make him stand".** Segond has "le Seigneur a le pouvoir de l'affermir". MC16's correct option uses Segond's words.
6. **Galatians 1:8, "let him be accursed".** Segond has "qu'il soit anathème!". MC15 uses "anathème".
7. **Ephesians 4:15, "speak the truth in love" (b010).** I quoted Segond's phrase "la vérité dans la charité".
8. **b014, "made Him who knew no sin to be sin for us".** The English gives this quotation without a reference. I quoted 2 Corinthiens 5:21 from Segond and added the reference.
9. **Terms.** "Cult(s)" is "secte(s)". "Root-test" is "test de la racine". "Investigative judgment" is "jugement investigatif", "Great Disappointment" is "Grande Déception", "Spirit of Prophecy" is "Esprit de prophétie", and "lesser/greater light" is "lumière moindre / grande lumière". As far as I know, these match French Adventist usage. I translated the Adventist quotations in b014 (Trinity statement, "all of grace") from the English; I did not use an official French wording. "Lord's Table" is "sainte cène".
10. **Hebrews 10:14.** The NKJV has "those who are being sanctified", which is a progressive sense. Segond has "ceux qui sont sanctifiés". No exam item depends on the difference.

## Problems in the English source

1. **SA prompts pass their own keywords in English.** SA3 hits sabbath, day and worship. SA5 hits 1844, investigative, judgment and assurance. SA7 hits root, cults, gospel and brother. SA9 hits receive, brother, romans and secondary. In French I chose keywords so that no prompt reaches 3 hits (each scores at most 2), and I reworded SA9's prompt. SA1 also had weak keywords ("adventist", "church"), which I replaced with 1863 and sabbat/santé.
2. **MC18 stem and option don't fit together.** The stem "could be:" plus the option "never be saved" reads "could be never be saved". The French reads correctly.
3. **MC4 says "the Adventist Fundamental Beliefs state".** The lesson only says "their official statement" and "their own words". I could not verify that the quotation "Salvation is all of grace and not of works" comes from the Fundamental Beliefs. Please check the source.
4. **SA6 keyword "debated".** The lesson never calls these views "debated". It says they are "held by some other Christians" and "worth careful study". The French group accepts both ideas.
5. **b016, "Some Adventists" teach that Sunday worship will be the mark of the beast.** This has been standard historic Adventist teaching, drawn from Ellen White, not a fringe view. Wayne may want to rephrase. I translated it as written.
