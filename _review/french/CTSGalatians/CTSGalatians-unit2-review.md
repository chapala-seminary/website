## Questions for Wayne

1. **Gal 1:11 "not according to man"**: Segond has "n'est pas de l'homme". I used that wording in the heading ("Il n'est pas de l'homme"), in MC1 and in the short answers.
2. **Gal 1:12 "received... nor was I taught"**: Segond has "ni reçu ni appris". MC2's correct option is now "Ni reçu ni appris". I translated the distractor "Hear" as "Entendu", so the stem's blank still reads naturally.
3. **Gal 1:13 "tried to destroy it"**: Segond has "ravageais". MC3's option became "la ravageait". The lesson's paraphrase is otherwise unaffected.
4. **Gal 1:14 "exceedingly zealous"**: Segond has "animé d'un zèle excessif". "Excessif" sounds slightly negative in French, but I quoted it exactly as the brief requires.
5. **Gal 1:15 "separated me"**: Segond has "mis à part". The lesson's emphasis on *separated* became *mis à part*, and the SA2 keyword group accepts both "mis à part" and "sépar".
6. **"Gentiles"**: Segond has "païens" (1:16, 2:2, 2:9). I added one short gloss in b014: "Les « païens » sont ici les non-Juifs". Elsewhere I used "non-Juif" for "Gentile", as in Titus, the "Gentile believer".
7. **Gal 1:16 "confer with flesh and blood"**: Segond has "consulter la chair et le sang". MC7 uses that.
8. **Gal 1:18 "to see Peter"**: Segond has "faire la connaissance de Céphas". The lesson says "Peter", so in b016 I added "Céphas est le nom araméen de Pierre". MC9 says "Pierre (Céphas)".
9. **Gal 2:5 "not... even for an hour"**: Segond has "pas un instant". The lesson, MC17 and SA6 rely on "an hour". I quoted Segond and added in b020: "Le texte grec dit littéralement : « pas même pour une heure »". MC17, SA6 and the b020 lesson text say "un instant".
10. **Gal 2:6 "added nothing to me"**: Segond has "ne m'imposèrent rien". This verse is the basis of the "They Added Nothing" theme (heading, MC18, MC20, SA7). I quoted Segond and added "Le texte grec dit littéralement qu'ils « ne m'ajoutèrent rien »". MC18's correct option reads "Ne lui ont rien imposé ni ajouté". I kept the heading "Ils n'ont rien ajouté".
11. **Gal 2:9 "right hand of fellowship"**: Segond has "la main d'association". Fill 9 has the answer "association" and accepts "communion", "fraternité", "communion fraternelle" and "partenariat". I added one sentence in b022 on the Greek sense (koinōnia). Segond also has "colonnes" for "pillars".
12. **Gal 2:4 "bring us into bondage"**: Segond has "nous asservir". MC16 uses "Les asservir". Fill 8 has the answer "liberté", which matches Segond.
13. **Gal 2:1 "Titus"**: Segond spells it "Tite". Fill 7 accepts "Titus" as well.

## Problems in the English source

- **b014**: The English inserts "..." inside the Gal 1:14 quotation, which drops "and of my nation". I quoted the full Segond verse instead.
- **SA5**: The model says the false brethren wanted to "add circumcision and law-keeping". The lesson implies this in the Titus episode and in b020, which says "add the law to grace", but it never states it directly. This is a minor gap.
- **SA keywords**: The English groups include generic words ("gospel", "man", "christ", "paul", "nothing", "hour") that the prompts themselves already contain. As the brief requires, the French groups were rebuilt with 8 distinctive ideas each, and no French prompt hits its own keywords.
- I found no wrong answer keys.

## Adaptations

- None needed. The unit has no US-specific examples. I added two brief glosses for readers with little training: "païens" means non-Jews, and Céphas is Peter.
