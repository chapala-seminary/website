# CTSGalatians Unit 10 — French draft review

## Questions for Wayne

1. **Galatians 1:6-7 "a different gospel, which is not another" (b025).** LSG has « un autre Evangile. Non pas qu'il y ait un autre Evangile ». It uses "autre" twice and loses the NKJV's different/another contrast. I quoted LSG and added one sentence: the Greek literally says a "different" gospel, which is not "another" of the same kind.
2. **Galatians 1:8 "accursed" (b004, b009, b025, SA7).** LSG has « anathème ». In b025 I added « « Anathème » veut dire maudit ». SA7 accepts "anathème" or "maudit".
3. **2 Corinthians 6:14 "unequally yoked" (b027).** LSG has « Ne vous mettez pas avec les infidèles sous un joug étranger ». I quoted it and added that the Greek literally says "yoked together unequally".
4. **2 John 10 "nor greet him" (b027).** LSG has « ne lui dites pas: Salut! », and LSG 2 John 9 has « va plus loin » for "transgresses". Both are quoted verbatim. The lesson's gloss, "do not welcome him as a fellow worker", still fits.
5. **Colossians 1:16 (b021).** LSG lists « trônes, dignités, dominations, autorités », not the NKJV's "thrones or dominions or principalities or powers". I quoted LSG and reworded the lead-in to match. MC13 is unaffected.
6. **Isaiah 44:8 / Psalm 90:2 / Malachi 3:6 (b021, MC14, fill9).** LSG has « Il n'y a pas d'autre rocher », « D'éternité en éternité tu es Dieu » and « je suis l'Éternel, je ne change pas ». The fill9 answer is "change". The French blank is "je ne ____ pas", which keeps the grammar.
7. **John 5:24 / 1 John 5:13 assurance (b023, MC16, SA6).** I translated "present and certain possession" and "assured" literally (« possession présente et certaine », « assuré »). Given CTS's open position on eternal security, I did not strengthen or soften these. Please confirm the English is worded as you want.
8. **LDS terminology.** I used the church's own French names: « Saints des derniers jours », « l'Église de Jésus-Christ des Saints des Derniers Jours », « prêtrise », « ordonnances », « exaltation », « Doctrine et Alliances », « Perle de Grand Prix », « royaume céleste », « Père céleste ». I added « (souvent appelés « mormons ») » once in b013, because that is the name Congolese readers know. The Snow couplet is given as « Tel que l'homme est, Dieu a été ; tel que Dieu est, l'homme peut devenir », close to the church's French wording. Please confirm.
9. **"Spirit brother" phrasing (b021, b025, SA5 prompt).** "A created spirit brother of Lucifer" is rendered « un esprit créé, frère de Lucifer ». A literal « frère d'esprit créé » was awkward.

## Problems in the English source

1. **SA1 model** says "the Holy Ghost is a personage of spirit". The lesson (b015) never mentions this. I translated it.
2. **SA1 / SA2 / SA10 keywords** repeat ideas. SA1 has both "exalt" and "exaltation". SA2 has "jesus", which the prompt itself contains. SA2 also has "created", but b016-b017 never say the LDS call Jesus "created". The French uses 8 distinct ideas (convention 10).
3. **SA5 model** says angels' worship is something "an angel or a fellow creature could never receive". The lesson only says "The angels worship Him (Hebrews 1:6)". This is a small extension, not a contradiction.
4. **Fill3 accept_en "be"** turns the couplet into "man may be". That is a weak alternate. I accepted « être » and « devenir (un) dieu ».

## Adaptations

- None needed. The lesson has no US-specific illustrations. "In the United States" and "nineteenth century" are historical facts and were kept.
