# CTSGalatians Unit 11 — French draft review

## Questions for Wayne

1. **1 Timothy 6:5 "From such withdraw yourself."** Segond 1910 does not have this clause (it follows the critical text; NKJV follows the Textus Receptus). The lesson quotes it twice (b024, b028) and MC8 depends on it. I quoted Segond's verse exactly, then added: "Une partie des manuscrits grecs ajoute à ce verset un ordre : « Sépare-toi de tels hommes »", and tied it to Romans 16:17 ("Éloignez-vous d'eux"), which Segond does have. MC8's correct option is "« Sépare-toi de tels hommes »" (my translation, not Segond wording), and the `why` points to Romans 16:17 too. OK?
2. **Job 13:15 "Though He slay me, yet will I trust Him."** Segond reads "Voici, il me tuera; je n'ai rien à espérer; Mais devant lui je défendrai ma conduite", which says nearly the opposite. I quoted Segond and added a sentence giving the Hebrew sense: "Même s'il me tue, j'espérerai en lui." You might prefer to drop the verse in French.
3. **Galatians 3:3 "made perfect by the flesh"**: Segond has "voulez-vous maintenant finir par la chair?" Quoted as is; fill 6 still answers "chair".
4. **Galatians 1:8 "accursed"**: Segond has "anathème". I added "c'est-à-dire maudit". SA8 accepts both.
5. **Mark 11 "colt"**: Segond has "ânon" (young donkey). Used in b020, MC13, SA5.
6. **2 Corinthians 12:9 "strength is made perfect in weakness"**: Segond has "ma puissance s'accomplit dans la faiblesse". Fill 9 answer "faiblesse". "Thorn" is "écharde" (Segond, 12:7).
7. **2 Corinthians 8:9**: Segond word order is "qui pour vous s'est fait pauvre, de riche qu'il était, afin que par sa pauvreté vous fussiez enrichis" (old subjunctive kept verbatim).
8. **"Prosperity gospel" / "Word of Faith"**: rendered "l'évangile de la prospérité" (lowercase "évangile" for the false one, "Évangile" for the true one) and "Parole de Foi". "Positive confession" = "confession positive"; "seed faith" = "foi-semence"; "name it and claim it" = "nomme-le et réclame-le". Please confirm these match what Kinshasa readers hear locally.
9. **"Brass"** (gold vs brass): rendered "bronze" (as the Spanish has). Fill 4 also accepts laiton/cuivre/airain.

## Problems in the English source

1. **MC7, SA2, SA8, SA10** rely on "the root-test from Unit 8" (Christ at the root, grace as a free gift). This unit's lesson never states the test; it only uses the headings "Testing the Root". Answerable only from Unit 8.
2. **SA4 and SA5 models** say the Hebrews 11 saints were "destitute and afflicted" and SA5 has keyword "afflict"; the lesson cites only Hebrews 11:13, 39, not 11:37. Kept; French keyword group widened (dénué, maltrait, persécut, souffr…).
3. **MC15 why** ("in plenty and in want") draws on Philippians 4:12, which the lesson does not quote. Minor.
4. **b020** calls Luke 2:24 Jesus' "dedication"; the two birds were the purification offering of the poor (Leviticus 12:8) at the presentation. French says "présentation au temple".
5. SA6 model and lesson say "make merchandise of you" (2 Peter 2:3); Segond's "trafiqueront de vous" covers it — no problem, noted only.
