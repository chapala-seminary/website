# CTSGalatians Unit 3: French draft review notes

Every quotation was checked against the offline LSG: Galates 2:11-21 and 2 Pierre 3:15.

## Questions for Wayne

1. **Peter / Cephas (Gal 2:11, 14).** LSG has "Céphas" where the NKJV has "Peter". The lesson text uses "Pierre" throughout, and the quotations use "Céphas". In b011 I added that Paul calls him by his Aramaic name, Céphas. MC 1 and MC 2 say "Pierre (Céphas)" / "Céphas (Pierre)".
2. **"to be blamed" (2:11).** LSG has "répréhensible". I used it in b011 and MC 2.
3. **"fearing those who were of the circumcision" (2:12).** LSG has "par crainte des circoncis". Fill 2 has the answer "circoncis" and also accepts "circoncision", "parti de la circoncision", "partisans de la circoncision" and "Juifs circoncis". "Withdrew and separated himself" is LSG's "s'esquiva et se tint à l'écart" (MC 4).
4. **"played the hypocrite" (2:13).** LSG has "usèrent … de dissimulation". The end of the verse ("leur hypocrisie") still matches, so the lesson's explanation of *hypocrite* = actor works.
5. **"compel Gentiles to live as Jews" (2:14).** LSG has "forces-tu les païens à judaïser?" In b018 I added a one-line gloss of "judaïser". MC 9 keeps "vivre comme des Juifs", and its why mentions "judaïser". In the same verse, "not straightforward about the truth" is LSG's "ne marchaient pas droit selon la vérité" (MC 8).
6. **"Gentiles."** LSG uses "païens". I used "païens" throughout, with a gloss in b013 ("simplement les non-Juifs").
7. **"no flesh" (2:16).** LSG has "nulle chair", the same as the NKJV. b020 adds a short gloss ("aucun être humain"). Fill 7 has the answer "chair".
8. **"Certainly not!" (2:17).** LSG has "Loin de là!", used in b023 and MC 14. In SA 5 the keywords accept both "loin de là" and "certainement pas".
9. **"I make myself a transgressor" (2:18).** LSG has "je me constitue moi-même un transgresseur" (MC 15, Fill 8).
10. **"live to God" / "by faith in the Son of God" / "gave Himself for me" (2:19-20).** LSG has "vivre pour Dieu", "dans la foi au Fils de Dieu" and "s'est livré lui-même pour moi". MC 16, MC 18 and MC 19 use the LSG wording.
11. **"I do not set aside the grace of God" (2:21).** LSG has "Je ne rejette pas la grâce de Dieu". This verb gives the unit its title, so the French title is "Je ne rejette pas la grâce". Following convention 1, b028 adds a sentence saying the Greek verb means literally "mettre de côté, annuler". The SA 8 keywords accept "rejet", "de côté" and "annul". "Righteousness comes through the law" is LSG's "la justice s'obtient par la loi".
12. **"The old self" (b025).** I rendered it "le vieil homme", the familiar LSG term from Romains 6:6. "By grace through faith" (b032) follows Éphésiens 2:8 LSG: "par la grâce, par le moyen de la foi".
13. **SA prompts reworded so they do not pass their own keywords (convention 10).** SA 2 is now about how a leader's fault spreads and Barnabas as a warning. SA 4 quotes "Juifs de naissance". SA 7 asks how Paul died "par" what condemned him. SA 8 asks for Paul's reasoning and its conclusion about Christ's work. The meaning of each prompt is unchanged.

## Problems in the English source

1. **SA 4 model.** It calls 2:15-16 "the crushing answer to the Judaizers at Antioch". b030 says it is uncertain whether vv. 15-21 were spoken at Antioch. This is a small tension. I translated it as written, softened to "la réponse décisive aux judaïsants d'Antioche".
2. **SA 7 model.** It adds "He did not abandon obedience", which the lesson does not say. It is harmless, and I kept it.
3. **SA 2 keyword "exampl".** The lesson never uses the word "example". I kept it as the "exemple" group, because the model uses it.
4. No wrong answer keys were found, and no why belongs to a different question. Every exam item is taught in the lesson.

## Adaptations
