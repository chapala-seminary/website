## Questions for Wayne

1. **Gal 3:1, "O foolish Galatians! Who has bewitched you".** LSG: "O Galates, dépourvus de sens! qui vous a fascinés". "Fascinés" is weaker than "bewitched" in modern French. I kept LSG in b009/b011, the b010 heading "Qui vous a fascinés ?" and MC1. Fill 1 answer is "fascinés"; it also accepts "ensorcelés"/"envoûtés".
2. **Gal 3:2, 5, "the hearing of faith".** LSG: "la prédication de la foi" (the preaching, not the hearing). Quoted verbatim in b009, b011, MC2 and Fill 2. The Fill 2 answer stays "foi".
3. **Gal 3:6, "accounted to him for righteousness".** LSG: "cela lui fut imputé à justice", and the verse opens "Comme Abraham crut à Dieu…". I used "imputer" throughout (b013, MC4, Fill 3).
4. **Gal 3:8, "preached the gospel to Abraham beforehand".** LSG: "a d'avance annoncé cette bonne nouvelle à Abraham". The lesson depends on the word "gospel", so b013 adds: "le texte grec dit littéralement qu'elle « a d'avance annoncé l'Évangile »". LSG also has "les païens" for "the nations" in the first half of the verse. I kept that.
5. **Gal 3:10, "does not continue in all things".** LSG: "quiconque n'observe pas tout ce qui est écrit". The lesson stresses "continually", so b015 adds the literal sense "quiconque ne demeure pas dans tout ce qui est écrit".
6. **Gal 3:13, "hangs on a tree".** LSG: "pendu au bois". The Fill 6 answer is "bois", and it accepts "arbre", "croix", "bois de la croix" and "poteau". Please confirm that "croix" is acceptable as an answer.
7. **Gal 3:16, 19, 29, "Seed".** LSG uses "postérité" (singular vs. "postérités"), so Paul's singular/plural argument still works. Because the lesson builds on "Seed", b020 adds that the Greek word literally means "semence". In MC12 and the SA keywords, "postérité" and "semence" are both accepted.
8. **Gal 3:15, 17, "covenant".** LSG has "disposition". The lesson prose uses "alliance", and b020 notes that Segond says "disposition" in vv. 15 and 17 and that the Greek word means an alliance or testament.
9. **Gal 3:19, "It was added".** LSG: "Elle a été donnée ensuite". b022 adds that the Greek says "a été ajoutée".
10. **Gal 3:24-25, "tutor".** LSG: "pédagogue", which a Congolese reader may hear as "schoolteacher". b024 adds one sentence: in antiquity a pédagogue was a servant who guarded the child, not first a teacher. The Fill 9 answer is "pédagogue"; it also accepts "tuteur", "gardien", "précepteur" and "surveillant".
11. **Gal 3:14, 22, "the promise of the Spirit" / "the promise by faith".** LSG: "l'Esprit qui avait été promis" / "ce qui avait été promis". I used this in b017, b022, MC11 and MC16, and b018 has "l'Esprit promis".
12. **Gal 3:28, "male nor female".** LSG: "ni homme ni femme". I quoted it without comment, as in the English.
13. **"Father of the faithful" (b013)** is rendered "père des croyants".

## Problems in the English source

1. **MC2:** the correct option reads "the works of the law, or by the hearing of faith (answer: by faith)". It contains the whole question and points to itself, so it is the only option that matches the stem. I kept it, translated as written.
2. **SA prompts that pass their own keywords (English):** SA3 (curse, law, "justify" catches "just"), SA5 (promise, Abraham, law) and SA9 (faith, law, works) each give 3 hits. Under convention 10, I reworded the French prompts for SA5 and SA9 without changing what they ask, and I tightened the stems for SA3. Now every French prompt gets at most 2 hits, and every French model answer gets 8 of 8.
3. No wrong answer keys, mismatched `why` explanations or untaught exam items were found.
