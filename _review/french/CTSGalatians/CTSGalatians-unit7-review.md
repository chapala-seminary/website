# CTSGalatians Unit 7: French draft review

All quotations (Galates 6:1-18) were checked verse by verse against the offline Segond 1910 by the checker: all match exactly.

## Questions for Wayne

1. **6:1 "restore".** Segond has "redressez-le". The lesson's point about setting a broken bone is now attached to "redressez" (b011). The heading is "Redressez-le". Fill 2 asks for **os**. SA1 accepts redress-/restaur-/relev-.
2. **6:1 "considering yourself".** Segond has "Prends garde à toi-même". MC2 and Fill 1 follow Segond ("prendre garde à vous-même").
3. **6:2/6:5 "burdens" / "load".** Segond uses "fardeau" in both verses, so the English contrast between a crushing burden and a daily pack is lost. In b013 I added one sentence: the Greek uses one word for a crushing load (v. 2) and another for a traveller's pack (v. 5). The SA2 model repeats it. Please confirm you want that note.
4. **6:4 "test his own work… rejoicing".** Segond has "examine ses propres oeuvres… sujet de se glorifier". The prose follows Segond.
5. **6:7-9 "reap" / "due season" / "lose heart".** Segond has "moissonner", "au temps convenable" and "si nous ne nous relâchons pas". Fill 4 answer: **moissonnera** (also accepts récoltera). MC10's correct option is "ne nous relâchons pas".
6. **6:10 "household of faith".** Segond has "les frères en la foi". I quoted Segond and added in b018 "le texte grec dit littéralement « ceux de la maison de la foi »". MC11, Fill 7 (answer **foi**) and SA4 use "frères en la foi".
7. **6:12 "make a good showing in the flesh".** Segond has "se rendre agréables selon la chair". b022 adds "le texte grec dit littéralement « faire bonne figure dans la chair »". SA6 accepts agréable/bonne figure/apparence/spectacle.
8. **6:14 "God forbid that I should boast".** Segond has "loin de moi la pensée de me glorifier d'autre chose que de la croix… le monde est crucifié pour moi, comme je le suis pour le monde". MC16, MC17 and Fill 9 follow Segond. I used "se glorifier" throughout for "boast" and "glory".
9. **6:15 "avails anything… new creation".** Segond has "ce n'est rien que d'être circoncis ou incirconcis; ce qui est quelque chose, c'est d'être une nouvelle créature". b026 notes that the Greek word also means "création". Fill 10 answer: **créature** (also accepts création). The heading and MC18 use "nouvelle créature".
10. **6:16 "walk according to this rule".** Segond has "suivront cette règle". The prose says "suivent cette règle".
11. **6:17 "the marks of the Lord Jesus".** Segond has "les marques de Jésus" (without "du Seigneur"), and "let no one trouble me" is "ne me fasse de la peine". MC20 follows Segond.
12. **"Judaizers"** is translated as "judaïsants", and "circumcision party" as "partisans de la circoncision".

## Problems in the English source

1. **SA prompts contain their own keywords.** SA1 (spiritual/trespass/brother), SA3 (reap/flesh/spirit), SA5 (large/letters/hand) and SA8 (circumcision/avails/new/creation) each pass on the prompt alone. SA2, SA4, SA7 and SA9 come close. The French prompts are reworded so that none passes (convention 10); no French prompt hits more than one of its own keywords.
2. **The SA8 model** adds "born again" and "recreated by the Spirit". The lesson's 6:15 paragraph says only "made new by the Spirit", although b026 does mention "the new birth" for 6:16. This is minor. I translated it faithfully.
3. All MC answer keys are correct, and each `why` matches its own question. Every exam item is taught in the lesson.

## Checker's changes

- Scripture: all 18 verses of Galates 6 (and 5:11 for "scandale de la croix") verified against Segond; no corrections needed.
- MC3: stem ends at "accomplirez ainsi… ?" and each option carries its own article ("la loi de Moïse", "les traditions des hommes", "les œuvres de la chair", "la loi de Christ"); this avoids the ungrammatical "la œuvre" and restores the plurals of the English. Correct option unchanged (index 3).
- Fill 1: removed "délicatesse" from accepted answers (not the biblical word, too loose); kept "mansuétude", "gentillesse".
- Wording: b015 "ne change pas seulement de maîtres" → "ne fait pas que changer d'enseignants" (English "teachers"; "maîtres" read as masters); b024 and SA7 model "ses performances" (anglicism) → "ce qu'il accomplit".
- SA keyword false positives tightened: SA1 "tente" (matched "attente", "tentent") → tentat-/tenter/être tenté forms; SA2 "mesur" → "se mesur"; SA4 "courage" (matched "encourage") → perdre courage/décourag-; SA5 "grande" and "sa propre" (matched any "grande"/"sa propre lettre") → "grandes", "gros caract", "propre main"/"sa main"; SA6 "impos" (matched "impossible") and "pratiqu" (any practice) → specific verb forms and "pratiquer la loi"; SA8 "valeur"/"quelque chose" (very common) → "ne vaut rien", "compte pour rien", "sans valeur", etc. All models still hit 8/8; tested plausible wrong answers hit at most 2.
