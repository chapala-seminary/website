# CTSBible Unit 5 — French draft review notes

## Questions for Wayne

1. **Matthew 23:24 allusion (b023, SA9).** The lesson alludes to "strain a gnat ... the skeptic's camel" without quoting the verse. Checked against the offline LSG: "qui coulez le moucheron, et qui avalez le chameau" — the draft's "couler le moucheron" / "le chameau du sceptique" match LSG wording. No other Bible verses are quoted in this unit.
2. **"Received Text" / "Critical Text".** I chose "Texte reçu" and "Texte critique", the usual French terms, rather than the Latin "Textus Receptus". The SA keyword group also accepts "textus".
3. **Codex names.** I kept the Latin names "Codex Sinaiticus" and "Codex Vaticanus", which French scholarship normally uses, rather than "Codex du Vatican" or "Sinaïtique". The fill-in for Vaticanus also accepts "Vatican".
4. **Dead Sea Scrolls.** I used "manuscrits de la mer Morte", the standard French name, and "rouleau(x)" for an individual scroll ("le grand rouleau d'Ésaïe"). For the place name I used "Qumrân", and the fill-in also accepts "Koumrân".
5. **Track names.** I translated "Certificate" as "Certificat" and "Associate" as "Associé" and left Th.M./M.Div. as they are. Please confirm that these labels match the names used elsewhere in the French site.

## Problems in the English source

1. **SA4 model**: it dates P75 to "about AD 175 to 225". The lesson (b016) only says P75 is "a little earlier" than P66 (c. 200) and says elsewhere that the text was already there "in the second century". The range is not taught and partly runs into the third century. I translated it as written.
2. **SA9**: it asks how the gallery applies the "concede, refuse, stand" method. This lesson never names that method or uses the word "concede", although b023 follows the pattern. Students have to rely on Unit 1.
3. **SA1 model**: it says "corroboration is the honest workhorse of evidence", which comes from Unit 1 and is not in this lesson. Minor.
4. **MC1 stem**: it quotes Unit 4 as meeting the manuscripts "in the mass, as a number". This lesson says "as a crowd — a number". It is a close paraphrase but not a real quotation, so I kept the quotation marks as in the source.
5. **b014 (P46) factual detail**: "Thessalonians" is listed among its contents. P46 has only part of 1 Thessalonians, not 2 Thessalonians, and it lacks the Pastoral Epistles and Philemon. "Most of the Pauline corpus" is fair, but the list overstates a little.
6. **b021 / MC17 / fill 8**: "every OT book but Esther" is the usual popular claim. Nehemiah is also missing as a separate text and is counted only because Ezra–Nehemiah was one book. This is acceptable, but a careful reader may raise it.

## Adaptations

- b009: "no larger than a bank card" became "pas plus grand qu'une carte d'identité", an object every Congolese reader handles.
- b012: I added "en Angleterre" after Manchester.
- b018: I added short glosses for the King James Version ("la grande traduction anglaise") and the Reina-Valera ("la grande traduction espagnole"), which are unfamiliar in Kinshasa. b025 and the exam items keep the plain names.
- "AD 125" became "l'an 125", and "BC" became "avant Jésus-Christ" or "av. J.-C.".
- Fill 9: the French prompt names "le livre du prophète ____" so that "Ésaïe" is answerable without the English title "Great Isaiah Scroll". The fill also accepts "Isaïe", the Catholic/TOB form many Congolese know.
