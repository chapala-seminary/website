# CTSBible Unit 4 — French draft review notes

## Questions for Wayne

1. **"Article of the faith" (b021, mc18, sa7).** Rendered "article de la foi chrétienne", the standard French theological term. Kept.
2. **"Reading" as a text-critical term (b024, b025, mc19, sa8).** Kept "leçon", the technical French term; the checker added a gloss on first technical use in b024 (« une leçon — c'est-à-dire une formulation particulière du texte »). Where the English uses "reading" loosely (b016 "a hard reading", b021 "garbled readings"), it is now "formulation". Confirm this is acceptable for Kinshasa readers.
3. **"Viable variants" (mc18).** Rendered "variantes significatives et plausibles". The lesson says "meaningful and genuinely in doubt", not "viable", so I followed the meaning.

## Problems in the English source

- **mc18:** The stem says "meaningful, viable variants", but the lesson (b021) never uses the word "viable". It is answerable, but the term is not taught.
- **sa7 model:** It says the many manuscripts make the text "more secure, not less". b018 supports this ("our security"), but b021 does not say it, so it is a mild stretch. Translated faithfully.
- **sa10 model:** It says the chain of copies is something "God did not promise". This comes from b025. Fine, noted only because b027 does not say it.
- **mc1/mc20:** No problems. The answer keys check out against the lesson. I found no wrong answer keys or mismatched `why` explanations.

## Adaptations

- **b016:** The English homophone example "their/there" became « ses » / « ces » (the Spanish edition did the same with haya/halla).
- **b025:** I added short glosses: "King James (la Bible anglaise)" and "Reina-Valera (la Bible espagnole)". Congolese readers may not know either version. LSG itself is not mentioned. Wayne may want a note saying whether Louis Segond follows the Received Text here. (LSG prints both passages.)
- **Exam items:** I did not translate the SA keyword lists literally. Each group was rebuilt as French stems, and English/Spanish stems such as "hand/mano" became "la main/les mains". Each French model hits 7–8 groups, and no prompt hits more than 2.
- **fill7:** The fixed phrase « embarras de richesses » allows only one real alternate (singular "richesse"). The validator warning was accepted.
