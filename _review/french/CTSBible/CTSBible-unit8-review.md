# CTSBible Unit 8 — French draft review

## Questions for Wayne

1. **LSG verification (checker).** All Scripture wording was checked against the offline LSG 1910 text: Daniel 5:16 (« la troisième place dans le gouvernement du royaume »), 2 Rois 20:20 (« l'étang et l'aqueduc »), Matthieu 23:24 (« moucheron… chameau »), 2 Timothée 3:16 (« inspirée de Dieu »), plus the LSG name forms (Sanchérib, Méscha, Caïphe, Gallion, Aï). All match.
2. **Daniel 5:16 (b014, MC 8, fill 4, SA 3).** NKJV has "the third ruler in the kingdom". LSG has "tu auras la troisième place dans le gouvernement du royaume". I followed LSG ("la troisième place dans le gouvernement du royaume") and built fill 4 on *troisième*, so the argument still works.
3. **2 Kings 20:20 (b011, MC 5, SA 2).** NKJV has "made a pool and a tunnel". LSG has "l'étang et l'aqueduc". The lesson says Hezekiah "dug a tunnel". The checker changed this to "Ézéchias fit creuser un tunnel (« l'aqueduc », dit Segond)", so the lesson's "tunnel" and the fill-in answer are taught explicitly. Fill 2 answer is *tunnel*; accept includes *aqueduc*, *canal*, *conduit*, *galerie*.
4. **Proper names in LSG form:** Sanchérib (I added "que les historiens appellent Sennachérib" once), Ézéchias, Belschatsar, Méscha, Omri, Caïphe, Gallion, Aï, Esdras. For non-biblical figures I used the standard French forms: Nabonide, Cyrus, Merneptah, and "inscription de Siloé".
5. **"High priest"** is rendered *souverain sacrificateur*, as LSG has it, not *grand prêtre*. Is that your preference?
6. **Exodus 1:11 "store-city" (b020).** LSG does not say *villes d'approvisionnement*; it says Pithom and Ramsès were built « pour servir de magasins à Pharaon ». The checker changed it to *ville-entrepôt*.
7. **"The mechanism is disputed" (b020, MC 17).** *Le mécanisme* reads oddly in French, so I wrote "la manière dont il s'y est installé est débattue". Please confirm this keeps the intended neutrality among the conquest and settlement models.
8. **"a book breathed out by God" (b025).** I wrote "qu'un livre est inspiré de Dieu" rather than a literal "soufflé par Dieu". LSG 2 Timothée 3:16 has "inspirée de Dieu".
9. **Gnat/camel (b022, b023, fill 10).** I kept *moucheron*, as in the LSG wording of Matthieu 23:24 and in the French Unit 7. Fill 10 also accepts *moustique*.

## Problems in the English source

1. **MC 2 and SA 1:** both quote the phrase "credibility anchor", but it never appears in the lesson. I translated it as « ancre de crédibilité » in quotation marks.
2. **MC 5:** the name "Siloam Inscription" is never used in the lesson, which only says "an inscription left by the two teams of diggers". This is minor.
3. **SA keyword groups:** the English prompt alone already reaches 3 hits in SA 2 (prism, corrobor, hezekiah), SA 6 (conquest, silence, silenc) and SA 10 (confirm, trust, honest). In SA 6, "silence" and "silenc" are separate groups that one word satisfies at once. Several English groups are Spanish stems (enigma, confianz, ezequ, asedi, muro…). The French groups are rebuilt, and no French prompt reaches 3.
4. **SA 4 model:** it says Caiaphas "tried Jesus", but the lesson says "questioned". I translated the model as "interrogea", following the lesson.
5. **SA 8 keyword:** "prob" (3 letters effectively, from the Spanish "probar") is a false-positive risk. In French it became `prouv/preuv`.

## Adaptations

- None needed. The lesson has no US-specific examples. "Second Kings nineteen" and similar spoken references became numerals (2 Rois 19). Dates use "av. J.-C." / "apr. J.-C.".
