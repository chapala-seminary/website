## Problems in the English source

1. **The SA keyword groups split one concept into English and Spanish groups (all of SA1–SA10).** For example, SA1 has "text" and "texto" as separate groups, and "archaeolog" and "arqueolog" as separate groups. Because "text" is a substring of "texto", a Spanish answer that writes "texto" scores 2 hits. An English answer, meanwhile, really has only about 4 concepts to hit. For French I built 8 distinct concept groups per item, each with its accented and unaccented forms.
2. **Some English words hit two groups at once.** In SA4 and SA9, the word "evidence" matches both "evidence" and "evidenc". In SA3, "investigat…" matches both "investigat" and "investig". One word therefore counts as 2 of the 3 required hits.
