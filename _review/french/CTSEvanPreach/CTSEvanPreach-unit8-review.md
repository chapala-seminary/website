## 1. Questions for Wayne

1. Acts 16:30-31 example: 'Be baptized' is the third of 'Three Steps to Salvation' (« Trois étapes vers le salut »), though Paul's answer in 16:31 is only « Crois au Seigneur Jésus » (baptism comes in 16:33), and the sample invitation itself calls only for belief. Translated faithfully. In Kinshasa, where baptismal-regeneration teaching is common in some churches, this may read as baptism being a condition of salvation. Keep as is, or retitle (e.g. « Trois étapes de la réponse au salut ») or add a sentence that baptism follows faith?
2. Sample invitations address the congregation with 'vous' (the Spanish uses 'tú'). 'Vous' is normal from a Congolese pulpit; the exam items use 'sa bouche / son cœur'. OK?
3. Pulpit refrain 'The decision is yours. The time is now.' rendered « La décision vous appartient. Le moment, c'est maintenant. » It appears in three invitations, MC 12 and SA 7. OK, or prefer another wording?

## Problems in the English source

- SA 2: the English model answer lists seven steps that do not match the lesson's seven steps (b013-b019): it adds 'give the invitation, press the appeal, call for an immediate response' and merges question/keyword and subject/points. The French model follows the lesson's actual seven steps.
- SA 7: the English prompt nearly gives the answer ('the decision is his and the time is now', 'pressing'), hitting its own keywords (decision, now, press). The French prompt is reworded so it does not give the answer (convention 10).
- SA 6: keyword 'remember' is not taught anywhere in the lesson (b047 gives the reasons); the model's 'unified moment' is loosely drawn from b011/b047.
- SA 10: keywords 'gentle' and 'honest', and the theme of 'manipulation', are not taught in the lesson; the model also says the appeal 'presses for decision', while the lesson stresses 'urgency without pressure'.
- b011 says the keyword approach was 'used in the first seven units of WiseSpeak', but this is unit 8 of Evangelistic Preaching; it probably means the first seven units of this course (or of WiseSpeak teaching). Translated literally.
- SA 5: the model says the invitation calls the listener to 'come forward and confess Christ'; the sample invitation (b045) says only 'come forward as we sing'. Minor; kept.

## Adaptations for Congolese readers

- SA 2 model answer rewritten from the lesson (see English-source problems) rather than translated.
- SA 7 and SA 8 prompts reworded so the prompt alone does not reach its keywords.

## 2. Bible quotations (Louis Segond 1910)

Items marked **⚠** differ from the English (NKJV) wording in a way the lesson or exam depends on.

- **Jean 3:16** — « Car Dieu a tant aimé le monde qu'il a donné son Fils unique, afin que quiconque croit en lui ne périsse point, mais qu'il ait la vie éternelle. »  
  _Referenced only (not quoted). The example's question uses 'sent' (envoyé), which reflects John 3:17; Segond 3:16 says 'donné', as the NKJV says 'gave'. Kept 'envoyé'._
- **Actes 16:30-31** — « il les fit sortir, et dit: Seigneurs, que faut-il que je fasse pour être sauvé? Paul et Silas répondirent: Crois au Seigneur Jésus, et tu seras sauvé, toi et ta famille. »  
  _The example's question is given in Segond's words: « Que faut-il que je fasse pour être sauvé ? »._
- **Romains 10:9** — « Si tu confesses de ta bouche le Seigneur Jésus, et si tu crois dans ton coeur que Dieu l'a ressuscité des morts, tu seras sauvé. »  
  _Points 1-2 follow Segond's wording ('confesser de… bouche', 'croire dans… cœur'), addressed with 'votre' in the sermon and 'sa' in exam items._

## 3. Terms for the reviewer to confirm

- **invitation** → invitation
- **appeal** → appel — 'appel' also means a call/vocation; context makes it clear, and 'exhortation' is used once in the definition.
- **keyword (plural noun)** → mot-clé (un nom au pluriel)
- **probing question** → question exploratoire — Matches the WiseSpeak French drafts.
- **subject** → sujet
- **evangelistic objective** → objectif évangélistique
- **worked example** → exemple commenté
- **come forward** → s'avancer
- **urgency without pressure** → l'urgence sans la pression
- **The decision is yours. The time is now.** → La décision vous appartient. Le moment, c'est maintenant. — Chosen to sound natural from a pulpit; an alternative is « La décision est entre vos mains. C'est maintenant le moment. »

**b009**

> EN: Every evangelistic sermon must end with a clear invitation and appeal. The invitation is the moment the preacher asks the listener to respond to the gospel. The appeal is the urgent call that presses the heart to decide today. WiseSpeak teaches that without a strong invitation the sermon remains incomplete. The listener may understand the truth but never take the step of faith. A well-crafted invitation turns knowledge into action.
