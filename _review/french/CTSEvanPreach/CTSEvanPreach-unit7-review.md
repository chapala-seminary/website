## Problems in the English source

- John 3:16 example (b022-b026, MC5, MC7, fill 5, SA3): the question 'Why did God SEND His Son?' and the point 'Save the world' come from John 3:17; verse 16 says God 'gave' His Son. Translated as written.
- Romans 10:9 example (b033, MC10, SA4): 'Receive God's salvation' is not a condition stated in the verse; the verse gives salvation as the result ('you will be saved'). Translated as written.
- SA1: the English prompt passes its own keywords ('textual', 'expositor', 'text'), and 'text' overlaps 'textual'. French replaced 'text' with 'pluriel' so the prompt does not pass.
- SA6: the keyword 'verse' is listed twice and the English prompt passes its own keywords ('textual', 'expositor', 'scope'). French uses a distinct 8th group ('long' = longer passage) and a prompt that does not pass.
- SA2 model answer lists only six actions for 'seven steps' (it merges points and objective). French model lists all seven.
- SA10 keywords 'authorit' and 'decision' and the model's 'God's own authority ... toward a decision' are not taught in the lesson (it says 'aimed at the heart'). Translated as written.

## 3. Terms for the reviewer to confirm

- **Evangelistic Preaching (course name)** → Prédication d'évangélisation — 'évangélique' would mean 'evangelical'; 'évangélistique' is rare. Other units of this course may have been drafted in parallel; keep one form course-wide.
- **Textual Approach / Expository Approach** → approche textuelle / approche expositive — 'prédication expositive' is current in francophone evangelical circles; lowercased as ordinary French style.
- **probing question** → question exploratoire — WiseSpeak technical term; no settled French. 'question d'approfondissement' was too heavy for a fill-in. Fill 4 accepts essentielle/principale/clé/de fond.
- **keyword** → mot-clé
- **plural noun** → nom au pluriel
- **subject** → sujet
- **evangelistic objective** → objectif d'évangélisation
- **invitation** → invitation — Many francophone churches say 'appel'; kept 'invitation' to match WiseSpeak.
- **supporting texts** → textes d'appui
- **outline** → plan
- **Reason One / Condition One** → Première raison / Première condition — Ordinals instead of 'Raison un', which is unnatural French.
