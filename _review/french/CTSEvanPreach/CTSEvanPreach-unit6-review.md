## Problems in the English source

- b045 / fill 10: 'form the subject by placing the keyword at the end' contradicts step 4 (b016 'Form the subject around the keyword') and all three examples, where the keyword comes second ('Three Sources of Real Satisfaction'). Translated faithfully; fill 10 tests this statement.
- b039-b040 (Romans 5:8 example): 'While we were sinners' and 'God demonstrates love' are not 'clean singulars' of the keyword 'proofs' as b017/b020 require; they are phrases from the verse rather than proofs.
- MC 14: the correct option says the keyword is 'a plural noun drawn from the text'. The lesson never says the keyword comes from the text, and in all three examples (sources, reasons, proofs) it does not appear in the text.
- SA 2: the model answer splits and groups the seven steps differently from b013-b019 (it makes 'imagine the interview' and 'draw out the question' two steps and merges 'points' and 'objective' into one). French model follows the lesson's seven steps.
- SA 7 and SA 8: keyword 'remember' / models 'memorable', and SA 8's 'parallel' and 'three points required' are not taught in this unit (the lesson requires neither exactly three points nor parallel form). Translated anyway.

## 3. Terms for the reviewer to confirm

- **keyword** → mot-clé — Same as WiseSpeak French draft.
- **subject** → sujet — Same as WiseSpeak French draft.
- **unsaved person** → une personne non sauvée — Common in francophone evangelical usage; alternative 'non convertie'.
- **(clean) singular of the keyword** → un singulier (net) du mot-clé — Unusual phrase in French; glossed once in b020 as 'un seul élément de la catégorie qu'il nomme'.
- **supporting texts** → textes d'appui
- **evangelistic objective** → objectif évangélistique
- **invitation** → invitation
- **inductive approach** → approche inductive
- **prodigal son** → le fils prodigue

**b009**  
WiseSpeak enseigne que l'une des façons les plus efficaces de préparer un sermon évangélistique est la méthode de l'entretien. Cette méthode commence par des personnes réelles et leurs histoires. Nous imaginons un entretien avec quelqu'un qui a besoin de Christ, ou nous nous souvenons d'un tel entretien. Nous écoutons ses questions, ses combats et ses besoins. > EN: WiseSpeak teaches that one of the most effective ways to plan an evangelistic sermon is the Interview Method. This method begins with real people and their stories. **b010**  
L'approche inductive

> EN: The Inductive Approach

**b011**  
La méthode de l'entretien suit l'approche inductive des sept premières unités de WiseSpeak. Nous partons des faits précis du texte et nous remontons pour découvrir le mot-clé et le sujet. La méthode de l'entretien rend le sermon personnel et pertinent. Elle oblige le prédicateur à parler directement de la situation réelle de l'auditeur. Associée à la méthode du mot-clé, elle produit un message évangélistique clair et unifié, qui semble avoir été écrit pour la personne assise devant vous.

> EN: The Interview Method follows the inductive approach from the first seven units of WiseSpeak. We start with the specific facts in the text and work backward to discover the keyword and subject. The Interview Method makes the sermon personal and relevant. It forces the preacher to speak directly to the listener's actual situation. When combined with the keyword approach, the result is a clear, unified evangelistic message that feels like it was written for the person sitting in front of you.
