## Questions for Wayne

1. **Sun/Son wordplay (b013)** — The pun does not work in French ("soleil" / "Fils"). I kept the sentence and added one short parenthesis explaining that the two words sound alike in English. Alternatively it could simply be dropped.
2. **John 1:5 "comprehend"** — Segond: "les ténèbres ne l'ont point reçue" ("did not receive it"). Quoted as Segond. No exam item depends on it.
3. **John 3:19 "loved darkness rather than light"** — Segond: "ont préféré les ténèbres à la lumière". Quoted as Segond; MC5 stem uses "ont préféré". Segond's "dévoilées" (3:20) fits the lesson's "expose". F4 answer is "dévoiler" (accept "exposer", "révéler", "démasquer", "mettre en lumière").
4. **John 8:58 "I AM"** — Segond prints "je suis" in lowercase. Quotations follow Segond; the lesson's own commentary, the heading and MC10's "why" use « JE SUIS » to keep the emphasis. MC10's correct option is "je suis". I added the reference Exode 3:14 (Segond: « Je suis celui qui suis ») after "burning bush".
5. **John 9:38 "and he worshiped Him"** — Segond: "Et il se prosterna devant lui". Quoted as Segond, followed by one sentence (checker reworded): "Le verbe grec employé ici signifie à la fois « se prosterner » et « adorer » : il l'adora." MC16 option is "il se prosterna devant lui pour l'adorer"; SA8 keyword group accepts "prostern" or "ador".
6. **John 9:41 "your sin remains"** — Segond: "votre péché subsiste". F10 answer "subsiste" (accept "demeure", "reste", "persiste"); MC19 option uses Segond.
7. **John 9:6–7, 11** — Segond has "boue" (clay → mud), "réservoir de Siloé" (pool of Siloam), and "L'homme qu'on appelle Jésus" (a man called Jesus). All used throughout; F8 accepts "Siloam"; SA7 accepts "siloam".
8. **Monogenes (b021, MC8, F6)** — Kept the Greek word as the lesson does and added "(Segond dit « le Fils unique », Jean 1:14, 18)". F6 accepts "monogène", "Fils unique", "unique", "seul Fils".
9. **"Followers of the Way" (b015, F3)** — Segond's Acts has "la voie du Seigneur" / "la voie" (and "la nouvelle doctrine" in Acts 9:2). I wrote « la Voie » (capitalized as a name). F3 accepts "chemin".
10. **"Disciple = learner" (F2)** — Answer "apprenant"; accepts "apprenti", "étudiant", "écolier", "apprenante". Please confirm "apprenant" is the word you want stressed.

1. **MC20, SA10** — The seven "I AM" sayings are never taught in this unit (only "light of the world" and John 8:58 appear). Translated faithfully. I removed "seven" from the French SA10 prompt so the prompt does not give away a keyword.
2. **MC11 (why), SA4 (model)** — "Blasphemy" is the reason given for the stoning, but the lesson never uses the word (it says "a carpenter had just called Himself God"). Minor; translated faithfully.
3. **SA3 model** — Adds material not in the lesson (God as "just and almighty"; "we no longer slavishly follow rules but imitate the Light, who is a person"). Translated faithfully.

11. **MC20 stem** — Reworded by the checker into two sentences ("… fait partie des grandes déclarations « JE SUIS » … Combien y en a-t-il ?"); "l'une des combien de" was not natural French. Same question, same options.
