## Questions for Wayne

1. **John 3:17, "to condemn".** LSG has "pour qu'il juge le monde". In b031 I quoted LSG exactly and added one sentence: "Le mot grec traduit ici « juger » a le sens de « condamner »." The MC 19 answer is "juge" (option order unchanged). SA 9's keyword group is now "juge"/"condamn", so a student who writes "condamner" also scores.
2. **John 3:19, "condemnation" / "loved darkness rather than light".** LSG has "Et ce jugement c'est que… les hommes ont préféré les ténèbres à la lumière". I quoted LSG in b032 and Fill 10, and the MC 20 stem paraphrases it. Fill 10 has the answer "ténèbres" and also accepts "obscurité".
3. **John 3:16, "only begotten Son" / "everlasting life".** LSG has "son Fils unique" and "la vie éternelle". I used LSG in b031, MC 18 and SA 8. "Begotten" is not discussed in the lesson, so I added no note.
4. **Bronze serpent (b030, Fill 8, MC 15, SA 7).** In Nombres 21:8 LSG, God tells Moses to make a "serpent brûlant", and in verse 9 Moses makes a "serpent d'airain". The English says God told Moses to lift up a bronze serpent. I wrote "Dieu dit à Moïse de placer un serpent sur une perche ; Moïse fit un serpent d'airain (de bronze)". Fill 8 has the answer "airain" and also accepts "bronze", "cuivre" and "laiton".
5. **John 3:2 "signs" and "teacher".** LSG has "miracles" and "docteur". I kept LSG in the quotation and used "docteur d'Israël" in the prose to match Jean 3:10.
6. **John 3:4 "womb".** LSG has "sein de sa mère". I used it in b017 and MC 5. In b016, where Cook tells the playful story of his own birth, I used "ventre".
7. **Theology, b021, MC 9 and SA 5.** "The new birth is His work before it is ever our choice" could be read as regeneration coming before faith. That differs from the CTS order of faith first, then new birth. I translated it faithfully ("La nouvelle naissance est son œuvre avant d'être notre choix") and did not soften it. You may want to reword it in all editions, for example as "God's drawing comes before our choice".
8. **"You must be born again."** I used the LSG wording "Il faut que vous naissiez de nouveau" for the unit title and throughout. "Essential" became "indispensable".

## Problems in the English source

1. **Fill 8 and b030.** These say God told Moses to make a bronze serpent. In Numbers 21:8 God commands a "fiery serpent", and Moses makes it of bronze (v. 9). This is a minor imprecision (see Q4).
2. **SA 5.** It uses "grace" as a keyword stem, but the lesson never uses the word "grace", and "gift" appears only once (b032). I kept both ideas in the French keywords.
3. **SA 10 model.** It adds "his relationship with God, with others, and with himself", which the lesson does not teach. I translated it as written.
4. **MC 20 why.** "The one who comes to the light is born of God" comes from b032. John 3:21 itself says the deeds are "faites en Dieu" (LSG). The lesson's reading is defensible, but it is not the wording of the verse.
5. **b021.** It quotes John 6:44 without a reference, so I added "(Jean 6:44)".
6. No wrong answer keys were found. Every SA item has a model answer.
