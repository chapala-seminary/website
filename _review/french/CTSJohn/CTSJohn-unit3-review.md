# CTSJohn Unit 3 — French draft review notes

Checker re-verified every quotation against the offline LSG (tools/lsg.py): Jean 4:7, 4:10, 4:11, 4:13–14, 4:16–18, 4:24, 4:26, 4:28–29, 4:34–36, 4:39, 4:42, Philippiens 2:13, Actes 4:36. All match; one quotation (Philippiens 2:13) had a stray capitalized "Car" at the start, now dropped.

## Questions for Wayne

1. **John 4:42, "the Christ, the Savior of the world".** LSG follows the critical Greek text and reads only "il est vraiment le Sauveur du monde", without "le Christ". In b024 I quoted LSG and added one sentence: "Une partie des manuscrits grecs porte ici : « vraiment le Christ, le Sauveur du monde »." MC 15 keeps "le Christ, le Sauveur du monde" as the correct option, because the woman already asks "ne serait-ce point le Christ ?" (4:29). Its `why` quotes 4:42 as LSG has it and adds 4:29. Fill 9 uses LSG and has the answer "Sauveur" (the checker removed "Rédempteur" from the accepted answers, as it is not the verse's word).
2. **John 4:14, "fountain".** LSG has "une source d'eau qui jaillira jusque dans la vie éternelle". I used "source" throughout. Fill 3 has the answer "source" and also accepts "fontaine".
3. **John 4:35, "already white for harvest".** LSG has "les champs qui déjà blanchissent pour la moisson". The heading is "Les champs blanchissent". In MC 16 the correct option is "blanchissent". Fill 10 has the answer "blanchissent" and also accepts "blancs", "mûrs" and "sont blancs".
4. **John 4:28, "waterpot".** LSG has "cruche". I used it in the lesson, MC 11, Fill 7 and SA 5. Fill 7 also accepts "jarre".
5. **John 4:39, "because of the word of the woman".** LSG has "à cause de cette déclaration formelle de la femme". I quoted it in b024. MC 14 says "le témoignage de la femme", which matches the lesson.
6. **Acts 4:36, Barnabas "son of encouragement".** LSG has "fils d'exhortation". In b029 I used it and added "(le mot grec veut dire aussi « encouragement »)".
7. **John 4:17–18.** The English elides part of the quotation ("You have well said... for you have had"). I quoted LSG in full: "Tu as eu raison de dire : Je n'ai point de mari. Car tu as eu cinq maris…"
8. **"Divine Partnership".** I rendered it "Partenariat divin". It is capitalized where it names the concept, as in the English. Fill 1 has the answer "Partenariat"; the checker removed "collaboration", "association" and "coopération" from the accepted answers, since the blank is masculine ("le ____ divin") and asks for the lesson's own term.

## Problems in the English source

1. **b025, SA 8: "three years under the Lord".** John 4 takes place early in Jesus' ministry. The disciples had been with him for months, not three years. I translated it as written.
2. **MC 8 and SA 3 model: "Mount Gerizim".** The lesson never names the mountain. It says only "this mountain". MC 8's distractor is harmless. The SA 3 model names it too, and I kept "le mont Garizim" there.
3. **SA 3 model.** "The Father seeks such worshipers" (4:23) is not in the lesson. I translated it.
4. **MC 12 vs. b024.** The option reads "Is this not the Christ?" while the lesson quotes "Could this be the Christ?". In French, both follow LSG: "ne serait-ce point le Christ ?"
5. **MC 13.** It says the disciples "told no one in the town". The lesson says only that "there is no sign they spoke to a soul". This is a slight overstatement, and I translated it as written.
6. **SA keywords.** Several English groups repeat words from the prompt, for example "water", "living", "samaritan", "woman", "spirit" and "worship". Following convention 10, the French uses 8 distinct ideas from the lesson that are not in the prompt.
7. No wrong answer keys were found. Every SA item has a model answer.
