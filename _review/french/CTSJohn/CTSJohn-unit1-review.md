# CTSJohn Unit 1 — French draft review notes

Checker re-verified every quotation against the offline LSG (Jean 1:1, 1:3, 1:6–8, 1:11–15, 1:18, 3:7, 3:30, 20:31; allusions to Genèse 1:1, Matthieu 11:11, Hébreux 1:3, Apocalypse 1:18): all match Segond exactly; no corrections needed.

## Questions for Wayne

1. **"The Word" and the pronouns for it.** I used "la Parole", following settled convention 6. LSG refers to the Word with feminine pronouns ("par elle", "sans elle", "Elle est venue chez les siens… ne l'ont point reçue", "à tous ceux qui l'ont reçue… elle a donné"). Quotations and the headings taken from them (II, V) therefore say "elle". When the prose speaks of the Person of Christ, it uses "il". In MC 9–11 the options are in the feminine form so that they match LSG ("reçue", "méritée", "gagnée").
2. **John 1:14 "dwelt".** LSG has "elle a habité parmi nous". Following convention 1, b021 adds "Le mot grec… signifie littéralement « dresser sa tente »". Fill 5 has the answer "tente" and also accepts "tabernacle" and "demeure".
3. **John 1:14 word order.** LSG puts "pleine de grâce et de vérité" before "nous avons contemplé sa gloire". It also has "comme la gloire du Fils unique venu du Père" where the NKJV has "only begotten of the Father". In b030 I quoted the LSG clause in its own order. I rephrased the MC 12 stem so that the blank still falls on "grâce et de vérité".
4. **John 1:18 "declared".** LSG has "l'a fait connaître". b030 adds "Le texte grec dit littéralement qu'il l'a « expliqué » : c'est le mot d'où vient « exégèse »". The MC 13 option reads "fait connaître / expliqué". Fill 10 has the answer "explication" and also accepts "exégèse", "révélation" and "interprétation".
5. **John 1:12 "the right".** LSG has "le pouvoir de devenir enfants de Dieu". The SA 5 keywords accept both "pouvoir" and "droit". Fill 8 has the answer "enfants" and also accepts "fils".
6. **John 1:15 "is preferred before me".** LSG has "m'a précédé". I used it in b025 and MC 7. The lesson's point (before me / after me) still works.
7. **John 3:30.** LSG has "Il faut qu'il croisse, et que je diminue". Fill 7 has the answer "diminue" and also accepts "diminuer", "décroisse" and "décroître".
8. **"The infinite became an infant" (b021, b023, Fill 6).** I wrote "L'Infini est devenu un nourrisson", with a capital, as a title for God. The French loses the English sound play of "infinite/infant".
9. **"Light" when it refers to Christ.** LSG keeps "lumière" lowercase, so the quotations do too. The prose capitalizes "Lumière" where the English capitalizes "Light".

## Problems in the English source

1. **MC 20.** The distractor "keep the schemes of timing" looks as if it was copied from an eschatology unit. It has nothing to do with this lesson. I translated it as "s'attacher aux calendriers des événements à venir". The answer key (1) is correct.
2. **b026.** It quotes "He must increase, but I must decrease" without a reference. I added "(Jean 3:30)".
3. **b019 and b022.** They borrow wording from outside John without saying so: "upholds all things by the word of His power" comes from Hebrews 1:3, and "the keys of death" from Revelation 1:18. These are fine as allusions. In French I followed the LSG wording ("par sa parole puissante", "les clefs de la mort").
4. No wrong answer keys were found. Every SA item has a model answer.
