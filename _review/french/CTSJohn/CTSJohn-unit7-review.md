# CTSJohn Unit 7 — French draft review

## Questions for Wayne

1. **John 10:10 "more abundantly"** — LSG: "et qu'elles soient dans l'abondance". Quoted LSG in b017; MC7 stem now ends "qu'elles soient ___" with options reworded to fit ("à bon marché / pour peu de temps / dans l'abondance / dans le silence"). Fill 5 answer "abondance" (accepts "surabondance", "abondamment").
2. **"Hireling" (10:12–13)** — LSG "mercenaire", which in modern French sounds like a hired soldier. Quoted LSG and added one sentence in b023: "Le « mercenaire », c'est l'homme payé à la journée, le simple salarié." Lesson prose then uses "salarié". Fill 8 answer "mercenaire", accepts "salarié", "employé", etc.
3. **"Snatch" (10:28–29)** — LSG "ravir". MC18 correct option is "ravir"; lesson prose elsewhere uses "arracher" (b026, SA9). SA9 keyword group accepts both.
4. **"I and My Father are one" (10:30)** — LSG "Moi et le Père nous sommes un" (no "My"). Used in b025, MC19, Fill 10 (answer "un").
5. **Isaiah 53:6** — LSG "Nous étions tous errants comme des brebis, Chacun suivait sa propre voie" (past "were wandering", not "have gone astray"). MC1 option reworded as "brebis errantes, dont chacune suit sa propre voie"; Fill 1 answer "brebis" (accepts "moutons").
6. **John 10:14 "am known by My own"** — LSG "et elles me connaissent". Quoted as is.
7. **John 10:11/18 "lays down" vs. LSG "donne sa vie" / "je la donne de moi-même"** — Section III heading and MC14/MC16 use "donne"; the "lay down" image is not separately kept.
8. **Eternal security (b025, MC17, SA9)** — "ce qu'il a acquis à un tel prix, il ne le perd pas" and "la sécurité du croyant" translate the English exactly; I did not strengthen or soften. Flagging only because the English itself leans toward security more than the "open question" guardrail; you may want to review the English.
