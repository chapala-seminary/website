## Questions for Wayne

1. **John 6:26 "signs"** — Segond has "non parce que vous avez vu des miracles". b027 and MC16 use "miracles" (Segond); b027 adds one sentence: "(Le texte grec dit littéralement « des signes ».)" SA8 accepts "miracle" or "non pour les signes".
2. **John 6:20 "It is I"** — Segond: "C'est moi; n'ayez pas peur!" The lesson's point that the Greek is "I AM" is kept as « JE SUIS ». The fill-in (F8) answer is "JE SUIS" (accept "ego eimi").
3. **Matthew 14:14** — NKJV "moved with compassion for them, and He healed their sick"; Segond "fut ému de compassion pour elle, et il guérit les malades" ("elle" = la foule). Quoted as Segond. I wrote "Jésus fut ému de compassion" for "felt compassion" throughout.
4. **John 6:66** — NKJV "went back and walked with Him no more"; Segond "se retirèrent, et ils n'allaient plus avec lui". Quoted as Segond. SA10 keyword is "se retir".
5. **"Grace upon grace"** (b020, MC8) — rendered with Segond's John 1:16 wording, « grâce pour grâce ». SA5 also accepts "grâce sur grâce".
6. **Augustine** (b027, F10) — I used the standard French wording of *Confessions* I.1: « Tu nous as faits pour toi, Seigneur, et notre cœur est inquiet jusqu'à ce qu'il repose en toi. » The fill answer is "inquiet" (accept "sans repos", "agité").

## Problems in the English source

1. **b012/b013, MC1, F1, SA1** — "The only miracle in all four Gospels." This is true of Jesus' miracles during his ministry, but the resurrection is also recorded in all four Gospels. You may want to add "of His ministry".
2. **b017, F4, SA3** — "Six times the Gospels say that Jesus felt compassion." The verb *splanchnizomai* is used of Jesus about eight times (for example Matthew 9:36; 14:14; 15:32; 20:34; Mark 1:41; 6:34; 8:2; Luke 7:13). Please check the number. I translated "six" faithfully.
