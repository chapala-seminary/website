## Adaptations

- "Drive a neighbor to the doctor" became "accompagner un voisin chez le médecin", because most readers do not own a car.
- SA8 model: "We do not wear sandals today" became "nous ne lavons plus guère les pieds des invités à la porte". Sandals are common in Kinshasa.
- SA prompts were reworded slightly so that no prompt passes its own keywords (convention 10). Some keyword groups were replaced where the French word was too short or too generic:
  - "wheat" (blé is 3 letters) became moisson/récolte.
  - "come" became espoir/espérance.
  - "one" was split into "les uns" and "autres".
- MC15 "a priest" became "un sacrificateur" (convention 4).
