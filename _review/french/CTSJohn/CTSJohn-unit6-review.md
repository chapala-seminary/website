## Questions for Wayne

1. **John 20:18, "I have seen the Lord!"** LSG gives this in indirect speech: "Marie de Magdala alla annoncer aux disciples qu'elle avait vu le Seigneur". In b028 I quoted LSG and added one sentence: "Le texte grec rapporte ses paroles mêmes… « J'ai vu le Seigneur ! »". MC 17 keeps the direct cry « J'ai vu le Seigneur ! » as option A.
2. **"The Way" (b034, MC 20, Fill 10).** LSG does not have "la Voie" as a name. Acts 9:2 has "la nouvelle doctrine", Acts 19:23 has "la voie du Seigneur", and Acts 22:4 and 24:22 have "doctrine". I wrote "disciples de « la Voie »" and added a short note in parentheses giving Segond's wording and the Greek sense. Fill 10 has the answer "Voie" and also accepts "voie du Seigneur", "Chemin" and "nouvelle doctrine".
3. **John 5:40, "not willing".** LSG has "Et vous ne voulez pas venir à moi pour avoir la vie!" Fill 9 has the answer "voulez" and also accepts "vouliez" and "désirez". The lesson's phrase "unwillingness of the will" became "un refus de la volonté".
4. **John 15:26, "Helper" / "proceeds".** LSG has "le consolateur" (lowercase) and "qui vient du Père". I quoted these as they stand in b023 and Fill 6.
5. **"Nobleman" (John 4:46).** LSG has "officier du roi". I used that name throughout: b028, MC 14 and 15, and SA 8.
6. **John 5:35, "burning and shining lamp".** LSG has "la lampe qui brûle et qui luit". Fill 3 has the answer "lampe" and also accepts "lumière", "flambeau" and "torche".
7. **John 9:25.** LSG has "je sais une chose, c'est que j'étais aveugle et que maintenant je vois". Fill 7 has the answer "vois". The paraphrase "Once I was blind, now I see" in b034 became "J'étais aveugle, et maintenant je vois".
8. **John 5:18, "broke the Sabbath".** LSG has "violait le sabbat". I used it in b015, MC 5 and SA 2.

## Problems in the English source

1. **b028 and SA 7 say the blind man was "hauled before the council".** In John 9:13 he is brought to the Pharisees, not the Sanhedrin. In French I used "les autorités", which is neutral, and did not write "le sanhédrin". *(Updated 12 Oct 2026 after the English correction; see "Updated after English corrections" above.)*
2. **b031 says "The council warned him, then threatened him".** John 9 shows him being questioned, reviled (9:28) and cast out (9:34). It records no warning followed by a threat. I translated it faithfully.
3. **MC 11 and the SA 4 model.** Both rely on "if you believed Moses, you would believe Me" (5:46a). The lesson (b022) quotes only "for he wrote about Me". The answer itself, "wrote", is taught.
4. **SA 10.** The keyword "spirit" and the model's line "the Spirit testifies through such witness" do not appear in the verdict section (b033–b034). The Spirit is taught only in b023.
5. **b029.** "A woman out of whom seven demons had gone" comes from Luke 8:2, not from John. This is not an error, but the unit does not say where it comes from.

## Adaptations

- "Juror/jury" → "juge" (b013), and "unlikely a jury" → "combien ces témoins sont inattendus" (b029). Courts in DR Congo use judges, not juries.
