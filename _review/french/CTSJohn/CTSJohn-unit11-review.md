# CTSJohn Unit 11 — French draft review notes

Checker re-verified every quotation against the offline LSG tool (Jean 14:16-18, 14:26, 15:26, 16:7-8, 16:12-14, 16:20-23, 17:1, 17:3, 17:15-17, 17:20-21, 17:24, Philippiens 2:13): all exact except one added word in the 14:26 quote, now corrected.

## Questions for Wayne

1. **"Helper" (John 14:16, 26; 15:26; 16:7).** LSG says "consolateur" every time. I used "le Consolateur" (capitalized in the prose, lowercase inside LSG quotations) as the Spirit's title throughout. I kept the lesson's list of meanings in b018: "le Consolateur, celui qui aide, l'Avocat". Fill 4 uses "Paraclet", the standard French form.
2. **"Keep them from the evil one" (John 17:15).** LSG says "les préserver du mal" (from evil), not "from the evil one". In b025, MC 16 and the SA 8 model I quoted LSG and added "(du Malin)", with one short note in b025 that the Greek can also be read as the devil.
3. **"Convict" (John 16:8).** LSG says "il convaincra le monde en ce qui concerne le péché, la justice, et le jugement". "Convaincre" is weaker than "convict". Following convention 1, I added one sentence in b018 saying the Greek means to prove guilt and bring the fault into the light. Fill 6 uses LSG's "justice" and also accepts "droiture" (NKJV "righteousness").
4. **"Abide with you forever" (14:16).** LSG says "demeure éternellement avec vous". Fill 5 answer: "éternellement"; it also accepts "pour toujours", "à jamais", "toujours" and "pour l'éternité".
5. **"It is to your advantage" (16:7).** LSG says "il vous est avantageux". Fill 2 answer: "avantageux"; it also accepts "utile", "bon", "profitable" and "avantage".
6. **"A woman in labor" (16:21).** LSG says "lorsqu'elle enfante". The lesson's prose and Fill 3 use "une femme en travail" (answer "travail"). The fill also accepts "couches", "enfantement" and "accouchement".
7. **"High-Priestly Prayer."** I used "la prière sacerdotale", the usual French name. "High Priest" follows convention 4 and is "souverain sacrificateur". Fill 7 answer: "sacerdotale"; it also accepts "du souverain sacrificateur" and "du grand prêtre".
8. **"Your word is truth" (17:17).** LSG says "ta parole est la vérité". Fill 9 answer: "parole" (also accepts "paroles", "Parole de Dieu").

## Problems in the English source

1. **SA 6** cites John 16:25-31, but the lesson (b023) never discusses those verses. It builds the point from 16:12 and 16:16-19 ("a little while"). I kept the reference as it is.
2. **b015** quotes "John 16:20–22", but the words quoted come only from 16:20. This is minor, and I kept the reference.
3. **MC 12** has the option "instantly, like a chatbot", but the lesson says "a machine". I rendered it "comme une machine qui répond tout de suite" so that it matches the lesson.
4. I found no wrong answer keys and no mismatched `why` explanations. Every SA item has a model answer.
