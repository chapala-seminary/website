## Questions for Wayne

1. **John 14:2, "many mansions"**: Segond has "plusieurs demeures". The lesson explains that the word means "rooms, abiding places", and Segond's word already says this, so I wrote: Le mot traduit par « demeures » signifie simplement des chambres, des lieux où l'on demeure. I also skipped "Si cela n'était pas, je vous l'aurais dit" with […], where the English has "...".
2. **"abide" / "abode" (b023, MC14, Fill 8, SA6)**: in French this pair becomes "demeurer" / "demeure". The pair works well, and it ties back to the "demeures" of 14:2. Fill 8 has answer "demeure". The validator warns that the answer is a substring of "demeurer" in the prompt. That warning is a false alarm, and I left the item as it is. (Checker: removed "habitation" and "logis" from the accepted answers, since the blank asks for the specific word behind "demeurer"; only "demeures" is accepted as an alternate.)
3. **John 15:1, 5, "vine" / "branches"**: Segond has "cep" and "sarments". I quoted Segond and added one gloss line in b021: Le cep, c'est le pied de vigne. Outside the quotations, the vine as a plant is called "vigne", and the titles say "le vrai Cep" / "le Cep". Fill 10 has answer "sarments" and also accepts "branches". The SA keywords accept both cep and vigne, and both sarment and branche.
4. **John 15:2, "prunes"**: Segond has "il l'émonde". I added "Émonder, c'est tailler." and used "taille/tailler" for "pruning" in the rest of the text. "Love with a knife" became "l'amour qui tient le couteau". Fill 7 has answer "couteau".
5. **Matthew 7:13-14**: the lesson paraphrases the verses and does not quote them. I used Segond's vocabulary: "chemin spacieux", "perdition", "chemin resserré", "que peu trouvent".
6. **John 15:8**: the English gives a loose wording ("to My Father's glory, that you bear much fruit") with no italics. I quoted Segond 15:8a with a reference and no added tags.
7. **"I AM" sayings**: I wrote « JE SUIS », as in the earlier units. Inside Segond quotations I kept Segond's spelling "coeur" (14:1). Everywhere else I wrote "cœur".

## Problems in the English source

1. **MC1**: the lesson never names "John 14–17" or the term "Upper Room Discourse". It says only "the upper room".
2. **MC20**: the lesson never says that the "I AM" sayings aim at belief that Jesus is the Christ (John 20:31). This unit does not teach that point.
3. **MC17**: the stem says the Polish believers spent "much of the night" on their knees. The lesson says only that they "stayed on their knees long after he finished".
4. **MC7**: the correct option adds "service", but the lesson says "humility and obedience". The option also says "few take", while the lesson says "few find". The item can still be answered.
5. **SA9**: the model adds "persistent prayer and selfless service to others" and "drawn to Christ". The lesson teaches none of these, and the keyword "service" has no basis in the lesson.
6. **SA6 and SA10**: the keyword "word" in SA6 ("as His word remains in us", John 15:7) and the keyword "follow" in SA10 do not appear in the lesson.
7. **b021, factual**: the Great Vine at Hampton Court is usually reported as about 36 m (120 ft) long, not 200 ft. I translated faithfully as "une soixantaine de mètres".

## Adaptations

- I converted all measurements to metric. "Two feet thick" became "environ soixante centimètres". "Two hundred feet" became "une soixantaine de mètres". "Hundreds of pounds" became "plus d'une centaine de kilos".
- "Pie in the sky" became "un beau rêve en l'air".
- "Graduation… keynote speaker" became "cérémonie de fin d'études… orateur principal".
- The phone-charging illustration was kept as written. It works well for readers in Kinshasa.
- SA prompts were reworded so that no prompt passes its own keywords (convention 10). Some short English keywords were replaced by French phrases:
  - "is" became "en personne/réel/réalité".
  - "nothing" became "rien faire" and similar phrases.
  - "gift" became "les dons" and similar forms.
