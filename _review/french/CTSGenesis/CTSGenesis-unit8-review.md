# CTSGenesis Unit 8 — French draft review

## Questions for Wayne

1. **Genesis 22:14, "The-LORD-Will-Provide" / Jehovah-jireh.** Segond does not translate the name; it writes "Abraham donna à ce lieu le nom de Jehova-Jiré." Following convention 1, b022 quotes Segond and then adds: "Le texte hébreu dit littéralement « l'Éternel pourvoira » (ou « l'Éternel verra »)." The "will see" note is my addition, because the Hebrew verb means "see". Please confirm or strike it. The b021 heading is "L'Éternel pourvoira". MC11's correct option is "L'Éternel-pourvoira (Jehova-Jiré)". Fill 8 was reworded: "…un nom qui signifie « l'Éternel pourvoira » ; Segond l'écrit ____." The answer is "Jehova-Jiré", and the fill also accepts Jéhovah-Jiré, Jehovah-jireh and Yahvé-Yiré.
2. **Genesis 22:2, word order.** Segond has "ton fils, ton unique, celui que tu aimes, Isaac" (Isaac comes last). I quoted Segond exactly in b006 and in Fill 2, whose answer is "aimes". MC3 quotes "ton unique".
3. **Genesis 22:12, "now I know".** Segond has "je sais maintenant que tu crains Dieu". The b017 heading and MC9 use "Je sais maintenant". Fill 6's answer is "crains".
4. **Hebrews 11:19, "raise Isaac from the dead".** Segond has "Dieu est puissant, même pour ressusciter les morts". Fill 4 now follows Segond's wording ("…même pour ____ les morts"). Its answer is "ressusciter", and it accepts "relever" and "faire revivre".
5. **Moriah.** Segond spells it "Morija" in Genesis 22:2 and 2 Chronicles 3:1. I used Morija throughout. Fill 9 also accepts Moriah and Moria.
6. **Genesis 22:1, "tested".** Segond has "Dieu mit Abraham à l'épreuve". Fill 1's answer is "éprouvé". It also accepts "mis à l'épreuve", "mit à l'épreuve" and "testé".
7. **"Allegiance"** (b018, b034, MC9). I rendered it "fidélité" because "allégeance" sounds political in French. Please confirm.
8. **"The Binding of Isaac"** (b015 heading). I rendered it "Isaac lié sur l'autel" rather than a technical term such as "la ligature d'Isaac".
