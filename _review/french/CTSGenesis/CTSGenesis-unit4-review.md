# CTSGenesis Unit 4 — French draft review

## Questions for Wayne

1. **Genesis 6:6, "the LORD was grieved in His heart" (b022, MC10).** Segond says "L'Éternel se repentit d'avoir fait l'homme… et il fut affligé en son coeur". I quoted only "fut affligé en son coeur" in b022 and did not quote "se repentit". The lesson's warning about divine grief ("not that God discovered something He had never anticipated") applies even more to Segond's "se repentit". Do you want a sentence on it?
2. **Rainbow (b034, MC16, Fill 9, SA8).** Segond 9:13 says "mon arc dans la nue", not "arc-en-ciel". I used the ordinary word "arc-en-ciel" in the lesson, as the English says "rainbow". Fill 9 answer is "arc-en-ciel" and it also accepts "arc", "arc dans la nue" and "mon arc".
3. **Mark of Cain (b010, MC4, Fill 4).** Segond 4:15 says "un signe". I used "signe protecteur". Fill 4 answer is "signe" and it also accepts "marque".
4. **"And he died" (b016, MC7, Fill 6, SA4).** Segond's refrain in Genesis 5 is "puis il mourut". I used that wording throughout. Fill 6 answer is "mourut".
5. **Genesis 8:21, "inclination of the heart" (b032, MC15, SA7).** Segond says "les pensées du coeur de l'homme sont mauvaises dès sa jeunesse". The lesson only paraphrases this, so I wrote "le penchant du cœur". SA7 accepts "penchant", "inclination" or "pensées".
6. **Names.** I used Segond's spellings: Lémec (Lamech), Hénoc (Enoch), Seth, Sem, Cham, Japhet, Canaan. The fill-ins also accept Lamech/Lamek and Enoch/Hénoch/Enoc.
7. **Theological wording.** "De-creation" is rendered « dé-création » (in quotation marks). "Remnant" is « un reste ». "Stewardship" is rendered "une gestion fidèle de la création". "Sanctity of human life" is "le caractère sacré de la vie humaine". Please confirm these.
