# CTSGenesis Unit 9 (Isaac and the Wells): French review notes

Every quotation was checked against the offline LSG 1910 tool (re-verified by the checker; all exact): Genèse 26:1-33 (the whole chapter), and also 12:10, 16:11, 16:13, 20:2, 21:31, 22:14, 25:34 and 28:18 for the MC distractors.

## Questions for Wayne

1. **Gen 26:22, "the LORD has made room for us" (b022, MC 9, fill 7, SA 5).** LSG has "l'Éternel nous a maintenant mis au large, et nous prospérerons dans le pays". I quoted that in b022 and added one sentence: "Le texte hébreu dit littéralement : « l'Éternel a fait de la place pour nous »". MC 9's correct option is "L'Éternel nous a mis au large". The headings use "espace" for "room".
2. **Gen 26:24 (b002 masthead).** NKJV has "Do not fear, for I am with you". I quoted LSG: "Ne crains point, car je suis avec toi."
3. **Gen 26:3 (b008, b042, fill 2).** I used LSG "je serai avec toi, et je te bénirai". It matches the NKJV.
4. **Names.** I used Segond's spellings: Guérar, Abimélec, Rebecca, Sitna (the English has "Sitnah"), Esek, Rehoboth and Beer-Schéba. Fill 7 also accepts Rehobot and Rehovot.
5. **Gen 26:15, "filling them with earth" (b014).** LSG says "les remplirent de poussière". This is a paraphrase, not a quotation, so I kept "de terre".
6. **Abimelech's "treaty" (b030, fill 10, SA 9).** LSG 26:28 says "que nous fassions alliance avec toi", so I used "alliance" for "treaty". In b030 I also quoted "Nous voyons que l'Éternel est avec toi" (26:28). I used "festin" (LSG 26:30) for "feast", and fill 10 accepts banquet and repas. I translated "the ruler" as "le roi" because LSG calls Abimelech "roi des Philistins".
7. **"Restraint" (b018, b042, fill 8).** I used "retenue". Fill 8 also accepts maîtrise de soi, contrôle de soi, modération and patience. Please confirm that "patience" should count.
8. **"Gathered worship / attendance" (b016, b032, b038, MC 17).** I used "culte communautaire" and "assiduité aux réunions de l'Église" / "la seule présence aux réunions".

## Problems in the English source

1. **SA 1–10 have no keywords and no real model answer.** `keywords_en` is null in all ten. Every model is the same generic sentence ("A strong answer should integrate…"), not an answer to the question. Following settled convention 12, I wrote 8 keyword groups and a model answer for each question from the lesson. Each model hits 7–8 groups, and no French prompt hits any of its own groups. Because of this, the validator reports 10 ERRORs ("8 keyword groups, English has 0"). These come from the null English keywords, not from a defect in the draft. Robert: the English needs keywords too. Without them the site passes any answer to these questions.
2. **MC 1–20 `why`.** Every explanation just repeats the correct option. None is wrong, but none explains anything. I translated them as written.
3. **b016, b022, b032, b034, b036, b038: "the recovered sermon".** The unit refers to this sermon throughout but never introduces it: no title, preacher or date. A reader cannot tell which sermon is meant.
4. **Fill 2's English alternates.** They accept "promise" for "covenant ____ rather than Isaac's exceptional ability". "Covenant promise" is not what the lesson says, so I did not carry it over. The French accepts fidélité, loyauté, constance and fiabilité.
