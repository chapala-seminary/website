# CTSGenesis Unit 7 — French draft review

## Questions for Wayne

1. **Gen 18:14, "Is anything too hard for the LORD?" (b002, b017, b018, b038, b042, fill 6, SA4, MC19).** LSG has « Y a-t-il rien qui soit étonnant de la part de l'Éternel ? ». I quoted LSG and added that the Hebrew literally means "too extraordinary", i.e. too difficult. Where the lesson paraphrases rather than quotes (b042, MC19), I used « trop difficile ». Fill 6 answer: « étonnant »; it also accepts difficile, trop difficile, extraordinaire, impossible.
2. **Gen 18:25, "Shall not the Judge of all the earth do right?" (b020, MC9, fill 7, SA5).** LSG has « Celui qui juge toute la terre n'exercera-t-il pas la justice ? ». I quoted LSG and added that the Hebrew literally says "the Judge of all the earth". Fill 7's blank is now the verb: answer « juge » (also jugera, jugeait, juges). MC9 keeps the noun « le Juge de toute la terre », as a description.
3. **Gen 19:16, "the LORD being merciful to him" (b026, MC12, fill 9).** LSG has « car l'Éternel voulait l'épargner ». I quoted LSG and added that the Hebrew literally says "because of the LORD's compassion for him". MC12 option A reads « L'Éternel a compassion de lui et veut l'épargner ». Fill 9 answer: « épargner » (also sauver, délivrer, préserver, protéger).
4. **Gen 21:1, "the LORD visited Sarah as He had said… did for Sarah as He had spoken" (b030, MC14).** LSG has « L'Éternel se souvint de ce qu'il avait dit à Sara, et l'Éternel accomplit pour Sara ce qu'il avait promis ». I quoted LSG and added that the Hebrew verb literally means "visited".
5. **Gen 16:13, "the God who sees" (b008, fill 2, MC5).** LSG transliterates the name as « Atta-El-roï ». I added one sentence giving the name and its literal sense (« tu es le Dieu qui me voit »). MC5's distractor is « Le Dieu qui voit, El Roï ».
6. **Gen 17:1, El Shaddai (b012, MC5, fill 3).** LSG has « le Dieu tout-puissant ». I kept « El Shaddai » beside it, as the English does. Fill 3 also accepts Shadday / Chaddaï.
7. **Gen 17:5, "father of many nations" (b014, fill 4).** I quoted LSG « père d'une multitude de nations ». Fill 4 answer: « nations ».
8. **MC5 distractors.** I used LSG's titles: « Le Dieu Très-Haut » (Gen 14:18) and « Le Dieu de l'éternité » (Gen 21:33), with the Hebrew names kept.
9. **"Ministers" (b020, b040)** is rendered « serviteurs de Dieu », because « ministres » mostly means government ministers in Kinshasa (same as Unit 1).

Checker re-verified every quotation against the offline LSG tool (all exact; none needed correcting), including the MC5 titles (Gen 14:18, 21:33): Gen 16:2, 16:11, 16:13-14, 17:1, 17:5, 17:15-17, 17:19-20, 18:12, 18:14, 18:25, 19:16, 20:2, 21:1, 21:3, 21:6, 21:10, 21:12-13, 21:17-20; 2 Pet 2:7-8; Ezek 16:49.

## Problems in the English source

1. **SA1–SA10.** Every `keywords_en` is null, and every `model` is a placeholder ("A strong answer should integrate … while distinguishing God's actual promises from human attempts to control outcomes"), not a model answer. The same closing clause is pasted into all ten, even where it does not fit (SA6 Sodom, SA7 Lot). Per convention 12, the French file has 8 real keyword groups in `keywords` and a real French model answer (3–5 sentences, from the lesson) for each item.
2. **MC1–MC20 `why`.** Each one only repeats the correct option. I translated them as they are.
3. **Masthead b002** cites the verse "(NKJV)"; the French says "(LSG)".
4. **b014** says Sarah "will become mother within the promised line". Gen 17:16 says more: she will "be a mother of nations" (LSG « elle deviendra des nations »). Minor; translated as written.

No answer keys are wrong.
