## Problems in the English source

1. **MC count.** The source has 18 MC items, but b115 says "(20 · 90%)" and the brief expects 20. Is the 90% pass mark calculated on 18 items?
2. **Name meaning inconsistency.** b078 says Elimelek means "My God is King", but b087 says "Our God is King". I translated both as written ("Mon Dieu est roi" / "Notre Dieu est roi").
3. **b076 word study.** The claim that the Hebrew of "lived there" (Ruth 1:2, literally "they were there") carries a sense of "belonging to and adoption" is doubtful. MC13 and SA4 depend on it. I translated it faithfully.
4. **b085 Moab history.** Three problems:
   - "Both nations… joined to become a part of Assyria, who then conquered Israel" overstates Psalm 83:8, which only says Assyria joined the children of Lot.
   - Ben-Ammi is the ancestor of the Ammonites, not a nation by that name.
   - Abraham's intercession in Genesis 18 was for the righteous in Sodom; it does not name Lot.
   - Also, "Lot… leaving Abraham the worst" goes beyond Genesis 13.
5. **b082.** "Ephrathites: … some say descendants of Ephraim" is weak. Ephrathah is an old name for Bethlehem (Genesis 35:19; Micah 5:2).
6. **b083.** "One must cross the Dead Sea" is odd; travellers went around it or crossed the Jordan. I translated it as written.
7. **Minor typos** (no effect on the French): b021 "had to be pay"; b016 "mete out" (probably "live out"); b108 "Give us this our daily bread".
