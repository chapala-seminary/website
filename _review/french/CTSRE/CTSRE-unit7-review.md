## Problems in the English source

1. **b012: Xerxes' assassination date.** Esther 3:7 is in Xerxes' 12th year (c. 474 BC). Xerxes was killed in 465 BC, about nine years later, not "only a couple of years after this". The killer, Artabanus, is usually described as commander of the royal guard rather than "prime minister". I translated it as written.
2. **b015: Bathsheba and Nathan before Solomon.** Bathsheba and Nathan bowed before **David** (1 Kings 1:16, 23). In 1 Kings 2:19 it is Solomon who bows to Bathsheba. I translated it as written.
3. **b011: Captivity and arrival at Susa.** The lesson says Mordecai and Esther were "brought into the citadel of Susa during the Babylonian captivity". The deportation was to Babylon, not Susa. Also, if Mordecai himself came with Jehoiachin in 597 BC, he would be well over 100 years old by Xerxes' reign. Many commentators read Esther 2:6 as referring to his great-grandfather Kish. This may deserve a note.
4. **b010: Authorship.** The claim that God's absence "tells us a non-Jew most likely wrote it" is a minority view. Most scholars, and Jewish tradition, hold that the author was Jewish. I translated it as written.
5. **Short answers.** The English keyword lists are thin and partly non-distinctive, such as "anti" and "jew". As the convention requires, I built 8 distinct French idea groups for each question. Every French model hits at least 7 groups, and no prompt hits more than 2.
