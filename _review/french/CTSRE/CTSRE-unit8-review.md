## Problems in the English source

1. **b012 / MC3, the "bribe":** The lesson says Haman offered the king a bribe that the king "refused". Esther 3:9 describes payment into the royal treasury. The king's reply in 3:11 ("the money is given to you") is read in more than one way, and 4:7 and 7:4 ("sold") suggest the money stayed in play. The lesson presents a disputed reading as fact. I translated it as written.
2. **b029 / MC19:** "Within a foot of the king's harem meant instant death" is presented as fact, but no source is given. The text itself (7:7-8) shows only the king's anger.
3. **SA8:** The keywords "expose" and "enemy" (and the model answer) rely on Esther 7:6, where Esther names Haman as the enemy. The lesson never teaches this. It moves from 7:4 straight to the king going into the garden.
4. **SA9:** The keywords "irony", "justice" and "evil" are not in the lesson. b030 only says Haman was hanged on the gallows he built.
5. **MC17:** The stem quotes "gut kick", which does not appear in the lesson (it is minor). I translated it as "un coup terrible".
