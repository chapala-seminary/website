## Questions for Wayne

1. **Isaiah 7:14 vs Matthew 1:23, "virgin".** In Ésaïe 7:14, Segond has "la jeune fille deviendra enceinte". In Matthieu 1:23 it has "la vierge sera enceinte". The lesson quotes only Matthew 1:23 (b009, b020), so all quotations use "la vierge". If you later want Isaiah 7:14 quoted in b023, the wording difference would need one sentence of comment.
2. **Immanuel.** Segond spells it "Emmanuel", and I used that everywhere. In the short answers, "immanuel" also counts as a keyword.
3. **"Out of Egypt I called My Son."** Segond words the two verses differently. Osée 11:1 reads "j'appelai mon fils hors d'Égypte" and Matthieu 2:15 reads "J'ai appelé mon fils hors d'Égypte". I quoted each verse in its own wording (b024 and MC9 use Hosea; b029 and MC10 use Matthew). Segond also writes "fils" in lowercase in Matthew 2:15, where the NKJV capitalizes "Son". I kept Segond's lowercase.
4. **"The LORD saves."** I rendered this as « l'Éternel sauve » (b018, MC4 why, Fill 5, SA3).
5. **Gentiles.** I used "païens", which is Segond's usual word. At its first use in b026 I added "c'est-à-dire des non-Juifs". In b016, Rahab is "une païenne".
6. **Joseph, Matthew 1:19.** The lesson says "righteous man" and "put her away quietly". Segond has "homme de bien" and "rompre secrètement". The lesson only paraphrases this verse, so I kept "homme juste" and used Segond's "rompre secrètement".
7. **Matthew 2:18.** Segond reads « On a entendu des cris à Rama, Des pleurs et de grandes lamentations: Rachel pleure ses enfants… ». I quoted it in b027 and Fill 10. The Fill 10 answer is "Rama" and it also accepts "Ramah". In MC17, "Rachel weeping for her children" became « Rachel pleure ses enfants ».
8. **Names (convention 5).** I used Segond's spellings: Thamar (Matthieu 1:3), Bath-Schéba, Achaz. Fill 7 also accepts "Ahaz" and "Acaz".
9. **Gifts.** "Frankincense" became "encens", Segond's word in Matthieu 2:11. For myrrh, "used to anoint the dead" became « servait à embaumer les morts ».
10. **Exile.** In b014, "to the exile" became « l'exil à Babylone ». Segond's Matthieu 1:17 says "déportation à Babylone", but the lesson does not quote that verse.

## Problems in the English source

1. **SA1, SA3, SA4 and SA5: the English prompts pass their own keywords.** SA1 hits genealog, david and abraham. SA3 hits jesus, immanuel and name. SA4 hits five groups. SA5 hits egypt, hosea, son and recapitul. Under convention 10, I replaced those groups in French with ideas taken from the lesson. For example, SA4 uses assyri, refus, saint esprit and délivr. No French prompt reaches 3 hits.
2. **MC3 / b016.** MC3 lists Bathsheba among the women Matthew "names". Matthew 1:6 actually says "the wife of Uriah", which the lesson itself acknowledges. This is a minor inaccuracy in the stem. I translated it faithfully.
3. **b026.** The lesson says "Jerusalem … sleeps", but Matthew 2:3 (and b027) says all Jerusalem was troubled. I read this as a figure of speech for spiritual indifference and translated it as written ("dort").
4. **SA8 (English).** The English model hits only 7 of its 8 keywords. The French model hits all 8, so nothing needed fixing.
