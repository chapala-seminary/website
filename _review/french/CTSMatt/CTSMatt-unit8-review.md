# CTSMatt Unit 8 — French draft review notes

## Questions for Wayne

1. **Matthew 13:39, 40, 49, "end of the age."** Segond has "la fin du monde." The lesson (b015, b023, b029), MC6, MC7 and MC10 (with their `why` notes), Fill 5 and SA3/6/9 all rely on "end of the age." I quoted Segond and used "fin du monde" throughout so everything matches. Following convention 1, I added one sentence in b015: « Le texte grec dit littéralement "l'achèvement de l'âge", c'est-à-dire la fin du temps présent. »
2. **Matthew 13:31, "mustard seed."** Segond has "grain de sénevé." The first mention in b019 adds "(c'est-à-dire de moutarde)." The answer for Fill 6 is "sénevé", and "moutarde" is also accepted.
3. **Matthew 13:25, "tares."** Segond has "ivraie" (darnel). This word fits the lesson's point that darnel looks like wheat. I left out "bearded darnel" as a separate botanical name, because "ivraie" already means darnel. Fill 4 also accepts "zizanie" and "mauvaise herbe."
4. **Matthew 13:33, "hid."** Segond has "mis" ("put"). The lesson's theme is a hidden kingdom, so I added in b019: « Le texte grec dit littéralement qu'elle "cacha" le levain dans la farine. » Segond's ending is "jusqu'à ce que la pâte soit toute levée."
5. **Matthew 13:57, "A prophet is not without honor."** Segond has "Un prophète n'est méprisé que dans sa patrie…" I changed the heading for b026 to "Un prophète méprisé dans sa patrie." For "they took offense," Segond has "il était pour eux une occasion de chute." I quoted this in b027, and the lesson says « ils se scandalisent ». The SA8 keywords accept honneur/mépris and scandal/occasion de chute/offens.
6. **Matthew 13:58, "mighty works."** Segond has "miracles." MC13 and Fill 10 use this wording. The Fill 10 answer "incrédulité" is the same as in Segond.
7. **Matthew 13:22 and 13:21.** For "cares of this world," Segond has "les soucis du siècle," and I used it in b013, MC4 and Fill 3. For "trouble," Segond has "tribulation."
8. **Matthew 13:52.** For "householder," Segond has "maître de maison," and for "old" it has "anciennes." The answer for Fill 9 is "anciennes", and "vieilles" is also accepted.
9. **Short-answer prompts.** SA2–SA8 point to the passage (e.g. "la parabole de Matthieu 13:24-30") instead of naming the parable. Otherwise the prompt alone would score its own keywords (convention 10). Is that acceptable?
