## Questions for Wayne

1. **Matthew 6:1, "charitable deeds" (NKJV) vs. LSG "pratiquer votre justice".** LSG follows the Greek text that reads "righteousness". This actually fits the lesson's heading ("Righteousness Not for Show") better. I quoted LSG and kept the sentence that follows: alms, prayer, fasting.
2. **Matthew 6:22, "good" / "single" eye.** LSG has "en bon état". Following convention 1, I added in b015: « le grec dit littéralement "simple", c'est-à-dire sans partage ». MC18 quotes "en bon état" and adds "(littéralement « simple »)".
3. **Matthew 6:24, "mammon".** LSG has "Mamon". I added one gloss in b015 ("Mamon, c'est la richesse, l'argent, présenté comme un maître"). Fill 3: answer "Mamon"; it also accepts mammon, argent, richesse, richesses.
4. **Matthew 6:27.** The lesson says "worry adds not a single hour to our lives". LSG reads "ajouter une coudée à la durée de sa vie". The lesson does not quote the verse, so I kept "une seule heure" as the lesson's paraphrase.
5. **Matthew 6:33, "shall be added to you" vs. LSG "vous seront données par-dessus".** LSG is quoted in b004, b009, b017, Fill 4 and MC7's explanation. Fill 4 answer: "premièrement" (also accepts d'abord, en premier, en premier lieu, avant tout).
6. **Matthew 6:32, "Gentiles" vs. LSG "païens".** I used "païens" throughout (b017, MC19, SA4).
7. **Matthew 7:12, "do to you" vs. LSG "fassent pour vous".** I quoted LSG. "Golden Rule" became "la règle d'or" (Fill 7; accepts "règle dorée").
8. **Matthew 7:13-14, "difficult is the way" vs. LSG "resserré le chemin".** I quoted LSG. MC11's correct option became "Étroit et resserré, et peu le trouvent". Fill 8 answer: "étroite".
9. **Matthew 7:3-5, "plank / speck".** LSG has "poutre / paille". Fill 5 answer: "paille"; it also accepts fétu, brindille, écharde.
10. **Matthew 7:28-29, "astonished at His teaching" vs. LSG "la foule fut frappée de sa doctrine"; "the scribes" vs. LSG "leurs scribes".** I quoted LSG in b025. MC17 now quotes LSG exactly ("frappée de sa doctrine", "non pas comme leurs scribes").
11. **Matthew 7:24, "does them" vs. LSG "les met en pratique".** I used "mettre en pratique" for the lesson's key word "doing" everywhere (b025, MC15, SA8). I think it is the stronger French term.

## Problems in the English source

1. **SA9 model** mentions "prophesying and casting out demons". The lesson says only "mighty works". This is accurate to Matthew 7:22, so I kept it.
2. **SA2 model** ends with "an audience of One". The lesson does not use this phrase. I kept the idea ("un seul spectateur : Dieu").
3. **MC17 explanation** ("the word of the King to obey") does not really say why the people were astonished. The lesson's reason is the King's authority. I translated it faithfully.
4. **MC18** says the single eye pictures "a heart undivided and rightly fixed". The lesson never says this directly. It is implied, and the added Greek note in b015 ("sans partage") now supports it.
