## Questions for Wayne

1. **Matthew 6:7, "vain repetitions" vs. LSG "vaines paroles".** I quoted LSG in b011 and added a sentence on the Greek sense ("ne répétez pas sans fin les mêmes mots"). Fill 2 answer: "paroles". It also accepts répétitions, redites. MC3 option: "leurs nombreuses paroles / vaines paroles".
2. **Matthew 6:9-10, "kingdom" vs. LSG "règne".** LSG has "que ton règne vienne". The lesson defines a kingdom. In b023 I added one line saying the Greek word Segond renders "règne" also means "royaume", and I used "royaume" after that.
3. **Matthew 6:12, "debts / debtors" vs. LSG "offenses / ceux qui nous ont offensés".** I quoted LSG in the b037 heading and in b038, and added the literal Greek ("remets-nous nos dettes, comme nous remettons à nos débiteurs"). Fill 9 answer: "offensés". It also accepts débiteurs.
4. **Psalm 46:10, "Be still" vs. LSG "Arrêtez".** I quoted LSG and added the Hebrew sense ("Cessez, restez tranquilles") in b019. MC12's correct option is "Arrêtez".
5. **Luke 15:19, "Make me as one of your hired servants" vs. LSG "traite-moi comme l'un de tes mercenaires".** The "Give me / Make me" contrast in b029 depends on "make". I quoted LSG, added the literal Greek ("fais-moi comme l'un de tes ouvriers"), and kept the contrast as « Donne-moi » / « Fais de moi ».
6. **Luke 17:21, "within you" vs. LSG "au milieu de vous".** I quoted LSG and added that the Greek can also mean "au-dedans de vous" (b023).
7. **John 17:15, "the evil one" vs. LSG "du mal".** I quoted LSG and noted that the Greek can mean "du malin" (b040). Matthew 6:13 LSG already has "du malin".
8. **Doxology (b030–b032).** LSG prints the doxology in the text of Matthew 6:13, while the lesson says it is missing from the oldest manuscripts. I quoted LSG as it is, and the lesson's note still stands. I took the b030 heading from the LSG wording ("À toi appartiennent le règne, la puissance et la gloire").
9. **"Hallowed".** French has only "sanctifié". I wrote: « Sanctifié » vient d'un mot qui signifie « être différent », mis à part. Fill 5 answer: "différent". It also accepts distinct, mis à part, séparé.
10. **Name of the prayer.** I used « Notre Père » (the usual French name) alongside "la prière du Seigneur", "la prière modèle", "la prière des disciples".

## Problems in the English source

1. **MC2** asks how "we give, pray, and fast". This unit teaches only praying in secret; giving and fasting were taught in Unit 4. I translated it as written.
2. **SA7** has the keyword "Judges", and its model says "in the days of the Judges". The lesson never mentions the Judges. I kept the idea in the model and used ["juges","samuel"] as the keyword group.
3. **SA1** has the keyword "babbling", but the lesson says "vain repetitions". I used a "répét / vaines paroles" group.
4. **b031** says "Moses asked, 'Show me your glory,' and God hid His face (Exodus 33:18)". The hiding of His face is in 33:20-23, not 33:18. I kept the reference.
5. **b027** says "drops of blood fell from His face". Luke 22:44 says His sweat became *like* drops of blood. I translated it faithfully.
6. **b031** says "Jesus told the disciples to wait at Pentecost for power". He told them to wait for the power that came at Pentecost (Acts 1:4-8), so I rendered it that way.
