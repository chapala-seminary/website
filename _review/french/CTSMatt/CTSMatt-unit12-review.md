## Questions for Wayne

1. **Matthew 26:28, "new covenant."** Segond follows the shorter Greek text: "le sang de l'alliance" (no "nouvelle"). The lesson (b013), MC3, fill 3 and SA2 rely on "new covenant." Segond is quoted exactly, followed by one sentence: several Greek manuscripts of Matthew read "the blood of the new covenant," and Luke 22:20 (Segond) says "la nouvelle alliance en mon sang." This is a text-critical note, not an "other versions say" note. Acceptable? (Unit 6 raised the same question for Matthew 9:13.)
2. **Matthew 27:54, "Truly this was the Son of God!"** Segond: "Assurément, cet homme était Fils de Dieu" (no article). Used in b029, b032, MC19, fill 9. The lesson treats it as a full confession echoing Matthew 14:33 and 16:16. Add a sentence saying the Greek has no article but Matthew presents it as a true confession?
3. **Psalm 22 (b025).** The English only says the psalm moves toward "the ends of the earth remembering and turning to the Lord." The French quotes Psalm 22:27 from Segond, which adds a verse reference not in the English. Keep?
4. **"Priest" in the great-grandfather story (b027).** "prêtre" for the clergyman in the story; "sacrificateur" for temple priests and for "every believer is a priest" (MC16, 1 Pierre 2:9). OK?

## Problems in the English source

- **b027 (the "Three Curtains" sermon):** "My own great-grandfather left one church to become a Baptist…" This personal story names a denomination. It is translated faithfully. It does not call CTS Baptist, but please check that you want it kept in an edition meant for all denominations.
- **b027:** the courts are said to separate "the high priest from the priests." The temple courts did not do that; only the Holy of Holies did. This is minor and was translated as is.
- **MC4:** three of the four options ("Left the garden", "Refused the cup", "Fell silent") do not fit the stem's "and then: …" as well as the quoted option does. I reworded the stem to "Ensuite :" so all options read naturally. The order is unchanged.
- **MC20 `why`:** "witnesses to the resurrection." The lesson says only "witnesses to the very thing they fear." This is minor.
- **SA4 model:** "exactly as the prophets had foretold." This does reflect Matthew 26:56, but the lesson says only "the Scriptures would not be fulfilled." Minor.
- **SA6 model:** the contrast between Peter and Judas (repentance against despair) is not taught in the lesson. Translated as is.
- **SA5 model:** "who will come again in glory" goes beyond the lesson ("coming on the clouds of heaven"). Minor.
