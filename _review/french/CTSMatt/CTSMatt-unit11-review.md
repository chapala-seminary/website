## Questions for Wayne

1. **Matthew 24:3, "end of the age" (NKJV) vs. LSG "la fin du monde".** I quoted LSG in b011 and added « Le texte grec dit littéralement "l'achèvement de l'âge" ». MC2 and SA1 use "fin du monde". The SA1 keyword also accepts "fin de l'âge" and "fin des temps".
2. **Matthew 24:42, "what hour" vs. LSG "quel jour".** I quoted LSG verbatim in b004 and b009. No exam item depends on the word.
3. **Matthew 24:14, "as a witness" vs. LSG "pour servir de témoignage"; "the end will come" vs. "Alors viendra la fin".** Fill 3 answer: "témoignage" (also accepts témoin, témoins, témoignages). In MC5 the correct option reads "Viendrait la fin".
4. **Matthew 24:21, "great tribulation".** LSG has "la détresse sera si grande…". In b015 I wrote « une détresse si grande… (ce qu'on appelle la "grande tribulation") ».
5. **2 Corinthians 5:8, "absent from the body … present with the Lord" vs. LSG "quitter ce corps et demeurer auprès du Seigneur".** I quoted LSG in b021 and added the literal Greek sense. In MC15 the stem says "quitter ce corps" and the correct option reads "Demeurer auprès du Seigneur". The SA8 keywords accept both wordings.
6. **1 John 3:2, "when He is revealed" vs. LSG "lorsque cela sera manifesté".** The lesson paraphrases it as "when He appears". In b023 I quoted LSG and added that the Greek can also read « lorsqu'il sera manifesté ». b026 is verbatim LSG.
7. **"Two thieves" / "the thief".** I used LSG "brigands" (Matthew 27:38) and "malfaiteur" (Luke 23). The SA8 keywords also accept "larron".
8. **Millennial terms.** I used "prémillénarisme / amillénarisme / postmillénarisme", the standard French terms. Fill 6 now reads "L'____ comprend…", answer "amillénarisme" (also accepts "amillénariste", "position amillénariste", "amillénaire").
9. **Diagram labels (b031–b116).** Following the CTSPent unit 7 precedent, the EN/ES pairs became FR/EN pairs, and the Spanish-only blocks now hold the English. Robert should decide whether the French site keeps the English half. The 3 validator warnings (b031, b032, b116) are these intentional English halves.

## Problems in the English source

1. **MC9, MC10 (why), SA4 (last sentence), SA5.** These rely on material the lesson never teaches. The lesson does not say that Jesus "gave parables and images instead of a date". It does not cover "one taken, one left", the "ordinary circumstances" point, the delayed bridegroom, or "He answered the question they should have asked". The lesson's b025 mentions the ten virgins only briefly. I translated them faithfully.
2. **SA5 model, "two are sleeping in a bed".** That image comes from Luke 17:34, not Matthew 24, which has only the field and the mill (24:40-41). I kept it. *(Updated 12 Oct 2026 after the English correction; see "Updated after English corrections" above.)*
3. **MC4 why** ("social order collapsed… the only priority") is not in the lesson. It is harmless.
4. **b023, "He ate fish on the shore".** In Scripture Jesus ate fish in the room (Luke 24:42-43). On the shore (John 21:13) he gave the fish to the disciples. I translated as written ("au bord du lac"). *(Updated 12 Oct 2026 after the English correction; see "Updated after English corrections" above.)*
5. **b021, C. S. Lewis attribution** (history split into before and after the second coming, the present age as "preface"). I could not verify the wording. Please check the source.
