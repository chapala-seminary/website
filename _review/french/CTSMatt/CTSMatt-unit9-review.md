# CTSMatt Unit 9 — French draft review notes

## Questions for Wayne

1. **Matthew 16:18, "gates of Hades."** Segond has "les portes du séjour des morts." b015 quotes Segond and adds one sentence: the Greek says literally "les portes de l'Hadès." The rest of the lesson keeps "portes du séjour des morts" / "portes de l'enfer," as the English does. Fill7's answer is "séjour des morts"; it also accepts "Hadès" and "enfer."
2. **Matthew 16:18, the rock / Petros-petra.** Segond has "tu es Pierre, et ... sur cette pierre," so the French has no separate word for "rock." In b015 and MC7 I keep the lesson's argument: Petros = "une petite pierre," petra = "le roc, le rocher solide." Fill6 now refers to "le nom grec de l'apôtre, Petros" so that the answer "pierre" is not already in the prompt. Please check that the play on words reads clearly.
3. **Matthew 18:22, "seventy times seven."** Segond has "septante fois sept fois." "Septante" is understood in Kinshasa but is not standard modern French, so b027 glosses it once ("septante, c'est-à-dire soixante-dix"). MC17 uses Segond's wording, and SA10 accepts "septante" or "soixante-dix."
4. **Matthew 18:6, "causes to stumble."** Segond has "scandalisait." b023 uses "fait tomber" and adds the note "(Segond dit « scandalise »)." SA8 accepts "scandalis," "tomber" and "chute."
5. **Matthew 17:5, "Hear Him!"** Segond has "écoutez-le !" Fill8's answer is "écoutez" (in "____-le !"); it also accepts "entendez." "Well pleased" is Segond's "en qui j'ai mis toute mon affection."
6. **Matthew 15:28, "O woman, great is your faith!"** Segond has "Femme, ta foi est grande," with no "O." I used Segond in b013 and MC4.
7. **Matthew 14:31 and 14:33.** Segond has "Homme de peu de foi" (not "O you of little faith") and the disciples "se prosterner devant" him (not "worship"). SA1 accepts "prostern" and "adora."
8. **Matthew 16:23, "You are an offense to Me."** Segond has "tu m'es en scandale" and "tes pensées ne sont pas les pensées de Dieu" (not "not mindful of"). I quoted Segond in b017.
9. **Matthew 17:4, "three tabernacles."** Segond has "trois tentes." I used "tentes" in b021, MC12 and SA7.
10. **Matthew 17:15, "epileptic."** Segond has "lunatique." The lesson does not quote the verse, so I wrote "un garçon épileptique," which is clearer to readers today. Is that OK?
11. **b019, "not my will."** The English quotes this without a reference. I cited Segond's Matthew 26:39, "non pas ce que je veux," and added the reference.
12. **Short-answer prompts.** SA1, 4 and 5 are reworded ("l'épisode où Jésus vient vers la barque," "l'apôtre," "la parole de Jésus en Matthieu 16:18") so that the prompt does not give away its own keywords.

## Problems in the English source

- **MC7, option 0 ("Peter made the first pope"):** the lesson never mentions the papacy. The option is a deliberate distractor against the Roman Catholic reading. Translated as is. Please decide whether it fits your goal of keeping CTS courses usable across denominations.
- **b015 and the keys:** Matthew 16:19 gives the keys to Peter ("je te donnerai"), but the lesson says the King gives them "to His people." This is an interpretation, presented as if it were the text. Translated as is.
- **MC8 `why` and SA5 model:** "batters down hell's defenses" is stronger than the lesson, which says only that the gates "will not hold out" or "will not stand." This is minor.
- **SA10 keyword "confess":** the prompt asks about the parable, which has nothing to do with the confession. Only an answer that also pulls in the unit's theme will hit this keyword. Kept.
- **SA9 keyword "correct":** b025 says "discipline." "Correction" appears only in the summary (b029). Kept. The French group also accepts "disciplin."
- **MC6:** option 1 begins with a lowercase letter ("the Father"). This is only a typo.
- **MC20 `why`:** "who went to the cross for it" is not in the lesson, which says the King "is going to the cross." This is harmless.
