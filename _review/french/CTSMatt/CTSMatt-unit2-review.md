## Questions for Wayne

1. **Matthew 3:15, "to fulfill all righteousness" vs. LSG "accomplir tout ce qui est juste".** I quoted LSG. Following convention 1, I added in b019: « Le texte grec dit littéralement "accomplir toute justice" ». The heading became "Accomplir tout ce qui est juste". Fill 6 answer: "juste" (also accepts justice, toute justice, ce qui est juste). The MC7 option and SA4 use the LSG wording. The MC7 explanation adds the literal sense.
2. **Matthew 3:7, "brood of vipers" vs. LSG "races de vipères".** I used LSG in b015, Fill 3 and SA2. Fill 3 answer: "vipères".
3. **Matthew 3:8, "fruits worthy of repentance" (plural) vs. LSG "du fruit digne de la repentance" (singular).** I used LSG in b015, MC4 and SA2. The heading "Repentance with Roots" became "Une repentance qui porte du fruit". A literal "with roots" sounds odd in French.
4. **Matthew 3:17, "in whom I am well pleased" vs. LSG "en qui j'ai mis toute mon affection".** I used LSG in b004, b020, MC9 and SA5.
5. **Matthew 3:12, "winnowing fan … chaff" vs. LSG "van … paille".** I quoted LSG and added a short gloss for "van" ("l'outil qui sépare le grain de la paille"). Many urban readers may not know the word.
6. **Matthew 3:3 / 4:7 / 4:10, "the LORD".** In Matthew, LSG has "le Seigneur", but at Isaiah 40:3 it has "l'Éternel". I kept "Seigneur" wherever Matthew is quoted. The lesson makes no point about the divine name.
7. **Matthew 4:12, "John had been put in prison" vs. LSG "Jean avait été livré".** The lesson paraphrases this verse, so I kept "mis en prison" (b025, MC17, Fill 10).
8. **Matthew 4:16, "light has dawned" vs. LSG "la lumière s'est levée".** I used LSG in b025 and the heading ("La lumière s'est levée"). I glossed "Galilée des Gentils" as "des nations païennes".
9. **Name spellings.** I used the LSG forms: Capernaüm (Fill 10 also accepts the familiar "Capharnaüm"), Ésaïe, Zébédée and Jacques. "Fishers of men" became "pêcheurs d'hommes" (LSG).
10. **"Recapitulation".** I translated it as "la récapitulation", the standard French theological term. "Gets it right" became "et cette fois sans faillir".
11. **SA prompts reworded (SA4, SA6, SA8).** A literal translation of each of these prompts already reached 3 keyword hits by itself (convention 10). I kept the meaning and changed the wording. For example, SA6 now reads "Expliquez le terme « récapitulation » appliqué à l'épreuve de Jésus…".

## Problems in the English source

1. **SA5 model** names "the Trinity". The lesson says "one God in three Persons" but never uses that word. I translated it faithfully, and the keyword group also accepts "trois personnes".
2. **SA6 model** calls the pattern "double-fulfillment from Unit 1". The Unit 2 lesson (b022) calls it "recapitulation" and only says the pattern was "named in Unit 1". The two terms are slightly blurred. I kept them as written.
3. **SA4 model** says "fully obeyed and embraced God's whole saving plan". The lesson never gives this definition. It teaches only identification with sinners. I translated it faithfully.
4. **b011**: "the wise men knelt at the cradle". Matthew 2:11 places the visit in a house, with "the young child", so the timing is loose. I translated it as "auprès du berceau".
5. **SA2 keyword "turnin"** is an English stem. In French I used a group for "tourn/retour".
