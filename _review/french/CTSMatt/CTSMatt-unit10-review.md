# CTSMatt Unit 10 — French draft review notes

## Questions for Wayne

1. **Matthew 21:5, "lowly."** Segond has "plein de douceur" (gentle). The lesson and MC6 rely on "lowly." I quoted Segond in b017 and added one sentence: the Greek word also means "humble," and Zechariah 9:9 (Segond) says "Il est humble et monté sur un âne." Fill 4 quotes "plein de douceur." MC6, SA4 and b029 use "humble/humblement."
2. **Matthew 21:42, "chief cornerstone."** Segond has "la principale de l'angle." I quoted Segond, then glossed it as "la pierre angulaire" in b019. MC9's correct option is "La principale de l'angle." Elsewhere (b021, MC9 `why`) I use "pierre angulaire."
3. **Matthew 19:14, "Of such is the kingdom of heaven."** Segond has "le royaume des cieux est pour ceux qui leur ressemblent." I quoted Segond in b011.
4. **Matthew 19:6.** Segond reverses the word order: "Que l'homme donc ne sépare pas ce que Dieu a joint." I used this in b011, MC1 and Fill 1. The answer is "sépare."
5. **Matthew 23:38, "desolate."** Segond has "déserte" (b027). The SA9 keywords accept both "désert-" and "désolé."
6. **Matthew 21:31, "harlots."** I used Segond's "prostituées" (b019, MC16 `why`). MC16's "pleased the father" became "a fait la volonté du père," following Segond 21:31.
7. **Matthew 22:40, "hang."** Segond has "dépendent" (b023, MC12, Fill 7).
8. **Matthew 23:25, "greed."** Segond has "rapine" ("plunder"). I used "rapine" in b025 and the SA8 model. This is slightly stronger than "greed."
9. **Matthew 20:28, "a ransom for many."** Segond has "la rançon de plusieurs." I kept it verbatim. In current French, "plusieurs" can sound like "several." Given CTS's unlimited-atonement position, is it acceptable without a note?
10. **"Woes."** I rendered this as « malheur à vous » (b025, MC13, MC14, SA8) because French has no natural plural noun for it.

## Problems in the English source

1. **SA6 model** adds things the lesson does not teach: the riddle about seven brothers, and "you know neither the Scriptures nor the power of God." Both are accurate to Matthew 22. I translated them.
2. **SA7 model** ends with "even as they tithed their herbs." The lesson never mentions tithing. I translated it.
3. **SA3 model** includes "whoever would be first must be a slave." The lesson's quotation leaves this out with "...". It is accurate to Matthew 20:27.
4. **MC10 `why`** ("neither denies the state…"), **MC11 `why`** ("the patriarchs live to God") and **MC17 `why`** ("on the King's terms") are not taught in the lesson. They are minor and accurate.
5. **MC19:** the lesson says He silences His challengers in chapter 22 (b023). It does not say "throughout these chapters," though b029 is close ("answers every challenge with perfect wisdom"). This is minor.
6. **SA5 model** says the tenants "beat and killed" the servants. The lesson (b019) says only "beat." Matthew 21:35 supports "killed."

## Adaptations

- "Sharecroppers" became "métayers," a familiar word for farmers who work someone else's land.
- I added "près de Londres" after Hampton Court (b021).
- I added a short gloss to "la principale de l'angle" (b019).
- The SA prompts for 2, 3, 6, 7 and 9 are paraphrased so that they do not give away their own keywords (convention 10).

- SA10: last group "peuple" (too common; not in the English) replaced by "rassembl" (English "gather").
- SA4: donkey group also accepts "un âne" / "un ane".
