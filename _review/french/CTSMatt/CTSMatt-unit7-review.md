# CTSMatt Unit 7 — French draft review notes

## Questions for Wayne

1. **Matthew 10:16, "harmless as doves."** Segond has "simples comme les colombes." I quoted Segond in b013, MC3 and Fill 2, and added a sentence giving the Greek sense ("sans mélange": innocent, without guile).
2. **Matthew 10:29-31, "sparrows."** Segond has "passereaux." I added a short gloss in b013 ("de petits oiseaux comme les moineaux"). Fill 3's answer is "passereaux" and it also accepts "moineaux" and "oiseaux".
3. **Matthew 10:39, "He who finds his life."** Segond has "Celui qui conservera sa vie la perdra." I quoted Segond in b015 and MC7 and added a sentence saying the Greek literally reads "trouvera". The MC7 answer, "retrouvera", matches Segond.
4. **Matthew 11:6, "not offended because of Me."** Segond has "pour qui je ne serai pas une occasion de chute." I added a sentence in b019 linking the Greek word to "scandale". SA4 accepts "chute", "scandal" and "offens".
5. **Matthew 11:28-30.** For "labor and are heavy laden," Segond has "fatigués et chargés." For "learn from Me," it has "recevez mes instructions." For "My yoke is easy," it has "mon joug est doux." I quoted Segond and added one sentence in b021 explaining "apprenez de moi" and the well-fitting yoke. Fill 5's answer is "léger".
6. **Matthew 12:8, "Lord even of the Sabbath."** Segond has "maître du sabbat" and has no "even." The lesson heading, MC11's option and Fill 6 use "maître". b023 adds that the Greek word also means "Seigneur", and SA6 accepts both words.
7. **Matthew 12:38-39, "sign."** In these verses Segond has "miracle," while the lesson's heading, MC18, MC19 and Fill 9 speak of the "sign of Jonah." b027 explains that the Greek word literally means "signe". The exam items use "signe".
8. **Matthew 12:40, "belly of the great fish ... heart of the earth."** Segond has "ventre d'un grand poisson" and "sein de la terre." I used Segond's wording throughout, including MC18's `why` and the SA9 model.
9. **Beelzebub.** I used Segond's spelling, "Béelzébul". Fill 7 also accepts "Béelzébub", "Belzébul", "Belzébuth" and "Beelzeboul".
10. **Matthew 10:22, "endures."** Segond has "persévérera." The SA2 keyword group includes "persévér" and "endur".
11. **Matthew 12:20, "smoking flax."** Segond has "lumignon qui fume". Since that is an archaic word, I added a short gloss ("la mèche qui fume encore").
12. **Unpardonable sin (b025, MC16).** "Settled, willful, final rejection" is rendered as "rejet arrêté, volontaire et définitif". I kept the English force exactly and did not tilt it toward or against eternal security. Please confirm that you are happy with this wording.
13. **"A trust to be carried" (b011)** is rendered as "un dépôt sacré à porter", which echoes the biblical idea of a "dépôt" (2 Timothy 1:14).
14. **Short-answer prompts.** SA1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 7, 8, 9 and 10 are paraphrased so that no prompt passes its own keywords (convention 10). For example, SA8 avoids "blasphème", "Esprit" and "pardonné".

## Problems in the English source

- **SA8 prompt:** the English prompt contains three of its own keywords ("blasphemy", "Spirit", "forgiven"). A student would pass by restating the question. The French prompt has been reworded to fix this.
- **SA3 keyword "losing":** this stem does not match "lose", "loses" or "lost", so it rarely hits in English. The French group uses "perd".
- **SA1 keyword "demons":** b011 quotes only "unclean spirits"; the lesson's Matthew 10 section never uses the word "demons". The French group accepts "esprits impurs" as well.
- No wrong answer keys or mismatched `why` explanations were found.

## Adaptations

- **Stagecoach illustration (b017):** I added a brief explanation ("ces anciennes voitures tirées par des chevaux") because Congolese readers may not know the image. The illustration itself is unchanged.
- **Branding iron (b017):** kept as written. Cattle branding is understandable in a Congolese setting.

## Checker's changes

- Scripture: every Segond quotation in the lesson and exams was checked with the offline LSG tool. All of them were already exact, and no corrections were needed.
- b021: "la ville qu'il avait adoptée" became "sa propre ville d'adoption" (clearer French).
- b025 and MC16 `why`: "une simple parole qui échappe" became "un simple écart de langage" (more natural French for "a single slip of the tongue").
- SA7: the keyword group "contre" matched almost any answer, because it is a common word. It is now ["folie","absurd","insens"], which follows the lesson's "Jesus shows the folly of it". Before this fix, a wrong answer ("by Moses' power… whoever is not with me is against me") reached 3 hits.
- SA8: "esprit" and "pardonn" were replaced, because any answer naming the sin gets them for free (a wrong answer such as "a single careless word that God never forgives" hit 3 groups). The new groups test the lesson's own definition: "volontair/délibér" (willful) and "définiti/non-retour" (final, point of no return). The model answer still hits 8 of 8.
- SA9: "prophète" was replaced by "ninive", because any answer mentioning Jonah also says "prophète". The model answer still hits 8 of 8.
