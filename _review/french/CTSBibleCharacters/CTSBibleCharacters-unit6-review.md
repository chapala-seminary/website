## Questions for Wayne

1. **Exodus 3:14, "I AM has sent me to you".** LSG has "Celui qui s'appelle \"je suis\" m'a envoyé vers vous". I quoted LSG in b014 and added in b015: "Le texte hébreu dit littéralement : « JE SUIS m'a envoyé vers vous. »" Fill 2 answer is "je suis" (accepts "je suis celui qui suis", "celui qui suis"). MC4 option uses LSG "Je suis celui qui suis".
2. **Exodus 4:10, "slow of speech and slow of tongue".** LSG: "je ne suis pas un homme qui ait la parole facile ... j'ai la bouche et la langue embarrassées". I quoted LSG and added in b017 that the Hebrew says literally "lourd de bouche et lourd de langue". MC1's correct option became "qu'il avait la bouche et la langue embarrassées".
3. **Exodus 4:13, "Send someone else".** Quoted as LSG "Envoie qui tu voudras envoyer" (b017).
4. **Exodus 14:13, "see the salvation of the LORD".** LSG has "regardez la délivrance que l'Éternel va vous accorder". I quoted LSG and added that the Hebrew word also means "salut".
5. **Exodus 33:18, "Please, show me Your glory".** LSG "Fais-moi voir ta gloire!" — used for the masthead verse, the section III heading, MC7, SA3.
6. **Exodus 33:22-23, "cleft of the rock" / "My back".** LSG: "un creux du rocher", "tu me verras par derrière". Used "le creux du rocher" and "ne se laissa voir que par derrière" throughout (b026, b036, MC11, MC12).
7. **Exodus 34:6, "longsuffering, and abounding in goodness and truth".** LSG: "lent à la colère, riche en bonté et en fidélité". Quoted LSG in b028; "bonté" keeps the goodness link.
8. **Galatians 3:24, "schoolmaster/tutor".** LSG has "pédagogue". b022 says "Paul compare la loi à un pédagogue, ou précepteur", plus one sentence on the Greek sense. Fill 7 answer "pédagogue" (accepts "précepteur", "tuteur", "gardien", "surveillant").
9. **1 John 3:2, "when He is revealed".** LSG has "lorsque cela sera manifesté" (impersonal), while the lesson and MC14/MC16 assume Christ appears. b030 quotes LSG; b029 now ends with one sentence noting the Greek can also read "lorsqu'il sera manifesté", and the MC16 stem paraphrases ("quand Christ paraîtra") before quoting only the exact LSG words.
10. **John 14:2, "many mansions".** LSG: "plusieurs demeures". Used "demeure" throughout.
11. **Numbers 20:12, "had not honored God".** Followed LSG's sense: "il n'avait pas sanctifié Dieu aux yeux du peuple". Fill 9 uses LSG "Meriba" (accepts "Meribah", "Meriba-Kadès").
12. **Fill 10.** Answer "Nebo"; accepts "Pisga" (LSG for Pisgah, Deut 34:1) as the English did.
13. **"the inadequate".** Rendered "ceux qui ne sont pas à la hauteur" (b009, b017, b036, MC20). Please have the French reader confirm it reads naturally.

## Problems in the English source

1. **MC14 why** cites 1 John 3:2 for "we will see Christ's glory", but that verse says "we shall see Him as He is"; the lesson (b029) says "the face of Christ". Minor; translated faithfully.
2. **SA keywords (English) include words the prompts themselves contain** (e.g. SA2 "law", "moses", "jesus", "christ"; SA3 "moses"; SA4 "goodness", "glory", "god"; SA5 "holin", "glory", "hidden", "shini"). Under convention 10 I replaced these in French with distinct lesson ideas; no French prompt passes its own keywords.
3. **SA5 model** introduces "concealed/revealed" and holiness as "set-apart"; the lesson only gives Bengel's line and "God's holy goodness unveiled". Translated faithfully.
4. **SA8 model and b034** say Moses "stood with Christ ... inside the land at last". Fine theologically, but the exam relies on it while the lesson cites no verse (Matthew 17:3). Not changed.
5. **b018** says "the angel of death passed over"; Exodus 12:23 speaks of the LORD passing over and "le destructeur". I used "l'ange destructeur".
