## Questions for Wayne

1. **Genesis 25:23, "the older shall serve the younger" (b029, b030, b033, MC15, Fill 10, SA5, SA8).** Segond has "le plus grand sera assujetti au plus petit". I quoted Segond. Following convention 1, I added one sentence in b030: the Hebrew says literally "le grand servira le petit", meaning the elder and the younger. Elsewhere the prose says "le plus grand serait assujetti au plus petit, c'est-à-dire que l'aîné servirait le cadet". MC15's correct option (B, unchanged) gives both forms. Fill 10 blanks Segond's **plus petit**, and also accepts "cadet", "plus jeune", "petit" and "fils cadet".
2. **Rebekah's words at the well (b015).** The lesson quotes "Drink, and I will draw for your camels also" as one sentence. Segond splits it between 24:18 and 24:19. I quoted both parts with an ellipsis and gave the reference: « Bois, mon seigneur… Je puiserai aussi pour tes chameaux » (Genèse 24:18-19).
3. **"I will go" (b003, b019, all headings, MC6, Fill 4, SA1, SA3, SA10).** Segond has "J'irai", which works well. Fill 4 has the student supply the whole reply **J'irai** (the blank is inside the quotation marks). It also accepts "irai", "jirai" and "oui j'irai".

5. **"Goatskins on his arms" (b033, MC18, SA7).** Segond 27:16 says "la peau des chevreaux" on his hands and neck. I kept the lesson's "bras" and wrote "peaux de chevreau". Should it be "mains et cou"?

## Problems in the English source

1. **Birthright vs. blessing (b030, MC17, SA6).** The lesson says "Esau lost his blessing" by despising his birthright (stew, Gen 25). But the blessing was taken separately, by Jacob and Rebekah's deception (Gen 27). MC17 asks why Esau "lost his blessing". The keyed answer is "despised his birthright", but the distractor "was deceived" is arguably also correct (Isaac, not Esau, was deceived, but the blessing was lost through deception). I translated faithfully and kept key A. Consider rewording the stem to "lost his birthright".
2. **Rebekah's death (b033, MC19, SA7).** "Rebekah … would die without ever seeing her son again" is a common inference. Genesis never records her death or says she never saw Jacob again; it only mentions her burial (Gen 49:31). The `why` for MC19 cites Gen 27:43-45, which only shows Rebekah planning to bring Jacob back. Consider softening this to "Scripture never records that she saw him again".
3. **SA4 keyword "church".** The lesson never uses the word "church"; it speaks only of "a bride for His Son". I kept "Église" in the French model and keywords.
4. **MC15 "contrary to custom".** The lesson never says this, though it is true. Minor.
5. **SA2 prompt and SA6 prompt** gave away keywords. Following convention 10, I reworded the French prompts. Each now hits at most 2 groups, and every French model hits all 8.
