## Questions for Wayne

1. **Genesis 15:6 (b018, b019, MC2, Fill 3, SA1).** LSG has "Abram eut confiance en l'Éternel, qui le lui imputa à justice" ("trusted", not "believed"). The lesson depends on "believed God". I quoted LSG and added in b019 that the Hebrew verb means "to believe", which is why Paul quotes it as "Abraham crut à Dieu" (Romains 4:3). Fill 3 is unaffected (answer "justice").
2. **Genesis 18:14 (b025, MC5).** LSG has "Y a-t-il rien qui soit étonnant de la part de l'Éternel?" ("astonishing", not "too hard"). I quoted LSG and added at the start of b026 that the Hebrew word also means "too difficult". In MC5 the correct option is now "étonnant"; the other options stay in the same order.
3. **Genesis 12:1 (b012, b013, MC16).** LSG has "de ton pays, de ta patrie, et de la maison de ton père" ("homeland", not "family"). I used "patrie" throughout and added in b012 that the Hebrew word also means kinship or clan.
4. **"Friend of God" in Isaiah 41:8 and 2 Chronicles 20:7 (b033).** LSG says "Abraham que j'ai aimé" and "Abraham qui t'aimait", so the word "ami" does not appear in those two verses. The lesson claims that Scripture calls him friend three times. I added a short note that the Hebrew word means both "one who loves" and "friend". Only James 2:23 says "ami" in LSG. Fill 10 still works from James 2:23.
5. **"The-LORD-Will-Provide" (b031).** LSG has "Jehova-Jiré" (Genèse 22:14). I wrote « Jehova-Jiré », c'est-à-dire « l'Éternel pourvoira », as the Genesis course already does.
6. **"Seed of Abraham" (b035, b037, MC19, SA6, SA9).** LSG uses "postérité" (Galates 3:16, 29). I wrote "la Postérité d'Abraham" / "la Postérité promise", capitalized as the English capitalizes "Seed". In b035 I added that the Greek word literally means "semence". The SA keyword groups accept postérité, semence and descendan.
7. **Genesis 15:17 (b020).** In LSG it is "une fournaise fumante, et des flammes", not "a smoking oven and a burning torch". I followed LSG.
8. **"Father of all those who believe" (b007, MC4).** LSG Romans 4:11 says "le père de tous les incirconcis qui croient". In b007 and MC4 I kept "le père de tous ceux qui croient" without quotation marks. MC4 `why` quotes LSG 4:11 and 4:16 ("notre père à tous").
9. **"Father of the faithful" (title, b008).** I rendered it "père des croyants", which reads more naturally than "père des fidèles". Please confirm.

## Problems in the English source

1. **b027.** The intercession count leaves out a step. Genesis 18 goes 50, 45, **40**, 30, 20, 10, but the lesson skips forty. I translated it as written.
2. **MC18.** The lesson (b023) presents Genesis 17 as the name change, El Shaddai and circumcision. It does not say that the promise of a son through Sarah was given there; it teaches that promise at Mamre (Genesis 18, b024). The key is biblically correct (Genesis 17:19), but the lesson does not teach it.
3. **MC6 `why`** cites Genesis 21:6 for the meaning of "Isaac". That verse is Sarah's word about laughter, not a gloss of the name. This is minor.
4. **SA keywords.** Under convention 10, I rebuilt all ten groups as eight distinct French ideas. I reworded the SA4 prompt so that it no longer contains its own keywords ("foi"/"œuvres"). No French prompt reaches more than 2 hits. I added one sentence to the SA5 model (intercession for Sodom, from b027) and one to the SA9 model (Galates 3:9) so they reach all 8 groups.
