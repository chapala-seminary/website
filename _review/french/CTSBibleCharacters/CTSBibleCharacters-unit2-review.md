## Questions for Wayne

1. **Job 13:15, "Though He slay me, yet will I trust Him."** This verse is the unit's motto (b003, b009, b012, b023, b024, MC 8, MC 20, Fill 2, SA 3, SA 10). Segond reads "Voici, il me tuera; je n'ai rien à espérer; Mais devant lui je défendrai ma conduite", which says nearly the opposite. Where the lesson quotes the verse (b003, b012), I quoted Segond and added: "Le texte hébreu peut aussi se lire : « Même s'il me tue, j'espérerai en lui »". This follows the same choice made in CTSActs 10 and CTSGalatians 11. Where the lesson uses the verse as a confession of faith (b009, b024, MC 8, MC 20, SA 3, SA 10), I used only the Hebrew sense, marked "(Job 13:15, d'après l'hébreu)". The heading b023 is « Même s'il me tue ». **Fill 2** cannot use Segond, which has no "trust". I rebuilt it as "D'après le sens du texte hébreu de Job 13:15, Job dit en somme : même s'il me tue, je garderai ma ____ en lui". The answer is "confiance"; it also accepts "espérance", "espoir" and "foi". Please confirm this approach, or replace the motto verse in French.
2. **Job 22:21, "Now acquaint yourself with Him, and be at peace".** Segond has "Attache-toi donc à Dieu, et tu auras la paix". I quoted Segond in b017 and MC 16, and in b017 I added: "Le texte hébreu dit littéralement « familiarise-toi avec lui »".
3. **Job 42:6, "I abhor myself, and repent in dust and ashes".** Segond has "je me condamne et je me repens Sur la poussière et sur la cendre". Fill 8 answer: "repens". It also accepts "repentis", "repentirai" and "repentir".
4. **Boils (Job 2:7).** Segond has "un ulcère malin", and I used "ulcère" throughout (b011, MC 5, SA 1).
5. **Uz.** Segond spells it "Uts". Fill 1 answer: "Uts". It also accepts "Uz".
6. **John 9:3, "the works of God".** Segond has "les oeuvres". Fill 5 answer: "oeuvres". It also accepts "œuvres", "oeuvre" and "œuvre".
7. **"Permissive will".** I translated this as "volonté permissive". Fill 3 answer: "permissive".
8. **Discipline/spank (b015, MC 12).** I translated this as "corriger"/"correction" ("Quels enfants corrigez-vous ?") rather than a word for physical punishment.

## Problems in the English source

1. **MC 18, MC 19, SA 8, SA 9** refer to "point B" and "point C". The lesson numbers its points First/Second/Third and never uses letters. I kept the letters in French and added "la deuxième réponse" / "la troisième réponse".
2. **MC 16** quotes "Acquaint now yourself". The lesson (b017) and the NKJV say "Now acquaint yourself".
3. **b017 / MC 16**: the lesson says Eliphaz "finally said rightly" in Job 22:21. That verse comes in Eliphaz's harshest speech, where he accuses Job of great wickedness (22:5), and God later rebukes all three friends (42:7). The counsel is sound in itself, but "rightly" may need softening.
4. **b020, SA 7, Rockefeller**: he died in May 1937 at 97, not at 98. The story that at 53 he had "a year to live" and then began giving is a popular sermon illustration and is not well documented. He tithed and gave to churches from his youth.
5. **b021, SA 8, Jerry McAuley**: the usual accounts place his conversion in Sing Sing prison. He was restored later through a mission, and he founded the Water Street Mission in 1872. "Saved one cold night in a mission" simplifies this.
6. **b022, SA 9, Finney**: the "then what? … then the judgment" dialogue is a traditional anecdote and is not in Finney's *Memoirs*. His prayer in the woods (1821) is documented.
7. **SA 2, SA 4, SA 5**: several English keyword groups overlap or are trivial ("not", "god", "sin"/"sinned", "permit"/"permissive"/"permission"). Following convention 10, I wrote 8 distinct French ideas for each. Prompts for SA 6 and SA 8 were lightly reworded so they do not hit their own keywords; SA 2 and SA 7 now follow the English question again (checker).
8. The answer keys are correct. Every `why` matches its question.

## Adaptations
