## Questions for Wayne

1. **Hannah = "Anne"; Peninnah = "Peninna"; Eli = "Éli"; Elkanah = "Elkana"; Miriam = "Marie" (Segond).** I used Segond's spellings everywhere. Fill 1 accepts "Peninnah" and "Penina" as well. In MC 18, Segond calls both Miriam and Mary "Marie". To keep the options distinct I wrote "Marie, mère de Jésus, dans le Magnificat" and "Marie, sœur d'Aaron (Miriam)".
2. **1 Samuel 1:27-28, "lent" (b003, b023, b024).** Segond has "prêter… prêté", which matches the NKJV, so nothing changed there. In b024 the English paraphrases the verse. I quoted Segond in full in its place.
3. **1 Samuel 1:18, "her face was no longer sad" (b019, b020).** Segond reads "son visage ne fut plus le même". Following convention 1, b020 quotes Segond and then adds one sentence on the Hebrew sense ("son visage n'était plus triste"). In b019 the narrative sentence keeps "son visage ne fut plus triste".
4. **1 Samuel 1:6-7 (b013).** Segond's "prodiguait les mortifications… la mortifiait" is old-fashioned for "provoked her severely". I quoted it verbatim. The lesson prose, Fill 1 and SA 2 use "se moquait / provoquait".
5. **"Coat" (1 Samuel 2:19).** Segond has "une petite robe". The lesson, MC 14 and Fill 7 use "robe". Fill 7 also accepts tunique, manteau, habit and vêtement.
6. **"Speak, for Your servant hears" (1 Samuel 3:10).** Segond has "Parle, car ton serviteur écoute". Fill 10's answer is therefore "écoute", and it also accepts "entend".
7. **1 Samuel 2:9 "saints".** Segond has "ses bien-aimés". I quoted it as is (b033), and no exam item depends on it.
8. **Hannah's song (b029).** The English paraphrases 2:4-8. I used Segond's exact lines from 2:4, 2:7 and 2:8 ("L'arc des puissants est brisé… Du fumier il relève l'indigent, Pour les faire asseoir avec les grands"). The Magnificat line is Luke 1:52 in Segond.
9. **MC 7, "the Lord's maidservant".** The stem now reads "en se présentant devant l'Éternel comme sa : … servante" so that it fits French grammar. The options are unchanged.
10. **Last sentence of b034** ("the King who came at last from her son's anointing"). The English is slightly opaque. I wrote "le Roi qui est enfin venu de la lignée que son fils a consacrée par l'onction". Please confirm this is the sense you meant.
