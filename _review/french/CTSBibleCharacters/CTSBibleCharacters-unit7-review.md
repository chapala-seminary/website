# CTSBibleCharacters Unit 7 (Gideon): French draft review

## Questions for Wayne

1. **Judges 6:12, "mighty man of valor" (b013, b014, MC4, fill 2).** LSG has « vaillant héros ». I quoted LSG, and b014 reads « Un vaillant héros… caché dans un pressoir ! ». Fill 2's blank is « vaillant ». It also accepts valeureux, brave, courageux and puissant.
2. **Judges 6:15, "my clan is the weakest in Manasseh" (b015, MC5, fill 3).** LSG has « ma famille est la plus pauvre en Manassé ». I quoted LSG and added a sentence at the start of b016 saying the Hebrew word also means "the weakest". MC5's correct option reads « la plus pauvre (la plus faible) ». Fill 3 is unchanged (answer « Manassé »).
3. **Judges 6:16, "Surely I will be with you, and you shall defeat the Midianites as one man" (b016, MC6).** I quoted LSG: « Mais je serai avec toi, et tu battras Madian comme un seul homme. »
4. **Judges 7:2, "My own hand has saved me" (b007, b019, MC8, SA1).** I used LSG throughout: « C'est ma main qui m'a délivré ».
5. **Zechariah 4:6 (b003, b021, MC9, fill 4, SA3).** LSG has « Ce n'est ni par la puissance ni par la force, mais c'est par mon esprit », with "esprit" in lowercase. The lesson reads it as the Holy Spirit, so I added one sentence at the start of b022: « Ici, "mon esprit" désigne l'Esprit de l'Éternel lui-même. » Please check that you are happy with this note. Fill 4's answer is « esprit ». It also accepts Saint-Esprit, Esprit Saint and Esprit de Dieu.
6. **Judges 7:3, "afraid" (b022, MC10, fill 5).** LSG has « craintif et qui a peur ». Fill 5's answer is « craintif ». It also accepts craintifs, effrayé, peureux and apeuré.
7. **Judges 7:15, "he bowed and worshiped" (b031, MC15, SA4).** LSG has only « il se prosterna ». In b031 I wrote « il s'est prosterné pour adorer » and added that the Hebrew verb means both "bow down" and "worship".
8. **Judges 7:20, "The sword of the LORD and of Gideon!" (b034, MC17, fill 9, SA5).** LSG has « Epée pour l'Éternel et pour Gédéon! » ("A sword for the LORD and for Gideon"). I quoted LSG everywhere. I did not add a Hebrew note, because the meaning is the same. Fill 9 is unchanged (answer « Gédéon »).
9. **Judges 7:14 (b030).** The English quote leaves out part of the verse with "...". I quoted the whole LSG verse, which includes « fils de Joas, homme d'Israël ».
10. **Judges 7:13 and 7:16 terms.** I used LSG's words: « gâteau de pain d'orge » (fill 7, answer « orge »), « cruches » for pitchers, « flambeaux » for torches (fill 8, answer « flambeau », which also accepts torche and lampe), and « trois corps » for the three companies.
11. **Course title.** I rendered "Bible Characters" as « Personnages bibliques ».

Checker re-verified every quotation against the offline LSG (Judges 6:2, 6:12, 6:15-16, 7:2-3, 7:5-7, 7:13-15, 7:20; Zechariah 4:6; 1 Thessalonians 5:18; Psalm 20:7; Joshua 1:9): all match exactly.

## Problems in the English source

1. **MC11 `why` and SA2 model** say the 300 lapped "staying alert". But b024 says that "Scripture does not explain why this particular manner of drinking mattered to God." I translated both as written. You may want to remove "staying alert" from them, or soften it.
2. **SA5 model** says plainly that "Israel struck no blow with a sword." In b035 and MC18, the lesson limits this to the first rout, and it notes the fighting that followed. This is minor, and I translated it as written.
3. **b003** labels the verse "(NKJV)". The French says "(LSG)".
4. **SA keywords.** Several English groups are generic ("god", "lord", "many"). Following convention 10, the French uses 8 distinct ideas from the lesson for each item. Each French model hits 8 of 8 groups. Each prompt hits 2 groups at most.

No answer keys are wrong.
