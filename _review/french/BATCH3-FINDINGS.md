# French Batch 3: what the reviews found

This covers four courses: The Life of Christ, 1 Peter Intensive, Galatians Intensive and Revelation Intensive (49 units).

Every claim below about the English site was checked against the live course files. Claims that did not hold up, or that were only matters of taste, were dropped. The settled conventions and the questions already raised in Batches 1 and 2 are not repeated.

---

## 1. Wrong answer keys on the live site

These affect students now, in English and Spanish.

**Clearly wrong**

| Course / Unit | Question | Keyed answer | Should be | Why |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Revelation 12 | MC 19 (how postmillennialists reply to objections) | Option 4, "The Bible is unreliable" | Option 2, "Setbacks do not disprove the gospel's long-term triumph…" | The key is off by two. The `why` describes option 2. As things stand, a student who answers correctly is marked wrong. |

No other wrong keys were found in these four courses. A scan comparing every key with its own explanation turned up nothing else.

**Keys that are weak or not taught by the lesson**

- **Galatians 4, MC 2.** The correct option reads "the works of the law, or by the hearing of faith (answer: by faith)". The answer is printed inside the option, so it gives itself away.
- **Galatians 9, MC 10.** The key says the *Governing Body* is "the faithful and discreet slave, God's channel". The lesson (b017) gives that title to "God's organization", and it never uses the word "channel".
- **1 Peter 7, MC 19 and SA 9.** Both items refer to "the grandfather's account". The lesson (b017) mentions no grandfather; it only says "a godly woman in one congregation".
- **Galatians 6, MC 5.** One wrong option reads "a debated conclusion about final security", which is not really an answer. The `why` then says "not to forfeit a salvation". The lesson (b013) deliberately leaves that question open. See the theology flags in section 3.

## 2. Other English-site problems worth passing to Robert

**Exam items the lesson never teaches**
- Life of Christ 7: SA 4 cites 1 Cor 11. The lesson teaches only Luke 22.
- Life of Christ 10: MC 6 and SA 3–4 use "two natures, undivided". The lesson never says "two natures".
- 1 Peter 2: MC 3, MC 6, SA 1 and SA 2 call the first picture "Regeneration". The lesson (b015) calls it "New birth".
- Revelation 3: MC 13 and SA 5 define the second death as "the lake of fire". The lesson says only "final, eternal death".
- Revelation 14: MC 12 uses "chiliasm". The word is not in the lesson.

**Model answers and explanations that disagree with the lesson**
- Life of Christ 6, SA 10: "As God He wept at a grave; as man He set His face to die". The lesson (b019) says the reverse: as God He is the resurrection, and as man He weeps.
- Galatians 5, SA 6: the last clause is garbled ("and he is good to be zealous for them whether or not he is present").
- Galatians 8, MC 11 `why`: it quotes the words of James 2:26 but cites 2:17.
- Galatians 12, MC 18: the stem and the keyed option together read "could be never be saved".
- Revelation 11, SA 10: the keywords "views" and "judgment" do not appear in the model answer.
- Revelation 15: the keyword "choose" is not in the SA 9 model, and "creation" is not in the SA 10 model.

**Keyword sets that let a wrong answer pass.** The site needs 3 keyword hits to pass an answer.
- **Students can pass by copying the question.** 205 of the 490 English short-answer prompts score 3 or more of their own keywords:
  - Life of Christ: 33 of 100
  - 1 Peter: 78 of 120
  - Galatians: 56 of 120
  - Revelation: 38 of 150
- **Tested wrong answers that pass:**
  - 1 Peter 2, SA 6: "Sanctification is earned by our own effort and works, not by grace or faith in Christ" scores 6.
  - Revelation 8, SA 8: "The dragon is not defeated and has plenty of time; the blood of the Lamb cannot preserve the church" scores 5.
  - Revelation 11, SA 8: "There is no second death; the lake of fire is only a symbol and no one is cast there" scores 5.
  - 1 Peter 12, SA 4: "God does not care about our worry; we must carry our own burden and anxiety" scores 5.
  - Galatians 6, SA 2: "Fallen from grace means a Christian who sins loses his salvation" scores 4.
  - Galatians 10, SA 1: "The Latter-day Saints believe in the Trinity just as other Christians do; the Father has no body" scores 3.
- **One word counted twice:** 1 Peter 12 SA 4 ("care", "cares"); 1 Peter 8 SA 2 ("bless", "blessing"); Galatians 10 SA 1 ("exalt", "exaltation").

**Leftovers in the lessons**
- 1 Peter 1: b016 and b017 say the same thing twice (the five provinces, "now Turkey", resident aliens). b024 and b026 both retell Exodus 24. They look like two drafts that were both kept.

**Factual errors in the lessons**
- **Life of Christ:**
  - Unit 1, b022: "in the very next verse". Luke 2:51 comes two verses after 2:49.
  - Unit 4, b020: the owner's reason ("lest the wheat be uprooted") is in Matt 13:29, not 13:30.
  - Unit 5, b015 and Fill 8: "To them He gave the keys". In Matt 16:19 the promise is made to Peter ("I will give you").
  - Unit 5, b019: "Moses would die". Moses had died centuries before the Transfiguration.
  - Unit 7, b011: "the hands that would be washed in the basin". Jesus washed feet.
  - Unit 7, b007: palm branches are credited to Matthew 21, but they come from John 12:13.
  - Unit 8, b017, MC 12, Fill 7: the "tetelestai stamped on paid debts" claim is a sermon illustration with thin evidence. b020's "nearly six hundred years" is a little over 600.
  - Unit 9, b019, MC 16, SA 7: "three times He charged him, 'Feed My sheep'". John 21 has lambs, sheep, sheep.
- **1 Peter:**
  - Unit 2, b015: Acts 3:19 is quoted as if it were said to Saul on the Damascus road. It is from Peter's sermon in Jerusalem.
  - Unit 5, b017: says Paul calls the authorities "sent by God". Romans 13:1 says "appointed". "Sent" is 1 Pet 2:14.
  - Unit 6, b025 and Fill 9: "George Wilson was hanged". The record of *U.S. v. Wilson* (1833) does not show this.
  - Unit 6, b012: "some sixty million slaves" is far above current estimates, which run to about 5–10 million.
  - Unit 10, b021: C. T. Studd was not clearly "the finest cricket player in all of England".
  - Unit 12, b017: the Louis XIV funeral story. The real line is Massillon's "Dieu seul est grand, mes frères" (1715). The golden coffin and the single candle are legend, and French readers may know the real version.
- **Galatians:**
  - Unit 1, b038 and Unit 12, MC 18: *Questions on Doctrine* (1957) was written by Adventist leaders. Martin and Barnhouse published their own conclusion in *Eternity* magazine (1956) and in Martin's 1960 book.
  - Unit 11, b020: Luke 2:24 is called Jesus' "dedication". The two birds were the offering for Mary's purification.
  - Unit 12, MC 4: the stem credits "all of grace and not of works" to the Adventist *Fundamental Beliefs*. The lesson says only "their own words". Please check the source.
- **Revelation:**
  - Unit 1, b018 and Unit 8, b021: the incense certificates that let people trade belong to the Decian persecution (AD 250), not to John's day.
  - Unit 3, b011: "every letter closes the same way, with the refrain… and a promise". In letters 1–3 the promise comes after the refrain; in 4–7 it comes before.
  - Unit 4, b019: "boiling, or 'zesty'" implies a link between the Greek *zestos* and English "zest". There is none.
  - Unit 6, b021 and SA 6: "When John asks who they are, an elder tells him". In Rev 7:13–14 the elder asks the question.

## 3. Decisions that apply to the whole French edition

These are new; they are not covered by the eleven settled conventions or by Batch 2.

1. **Where Segond follows a different Greek text.** Examples:
   - Rev 1:5–6 "délivrés… un royaume, des sacrificateurs"
   - Rev 15:3 "roi des nations"
   - 1 Pet 4:14b and 1 Tim 6:5, which are missing in Segond
   - Gal 4:7
   - 1 Pet 5:11

   Convention 1 ("le texte grec dit littéralement…") does not fit these. The drafts quote Segond and, only where the lesson depends on the wording, add "Une partie des manuscrits grecs ajoute / lit…". Approve this wording?
2. **"Charité" for *agapè*** (1 Pet 4:8, Gal 5:6, Eph 4:15). In everyday French "charité" means almsgiving. The drafts quote Segond, gloss it once ("c'est-à-dire l'amour"), and use "amour" in the lesson's own sentences. Approve for all courses?
3. **Segond's lowercase inside quotations.** Examples: "l'agneau", "sept esprits", "un fils d'homme", "le premier et le dernier", "ravi en esprit". The drafts keep Segond's lowercase inside quotations and capitalize in the lesson's own sentences. Approve?
4. **Segond words that are odd or awkward for today's readers.** The drafts quote these and add a short gloss: "vieillards" (elders), "collyre", "pédagogue", "infidèles" (2 Cor 6:14) and "sexe plus faible" (1 Pet 3:7). The open question is whether such a word may be the graded fill-in answer. 1 Peter 7, Fill 7 currently grades "sexe". Should it grade "vase" instead?
5. **Other groups' own terms.** For the cults units, the drafts use each group's official French vocabulary: the Jehovah's Witnesses' "Traduction du monde nouveau" and "Collège central", the Latter-day Saints' "Saints des derniers jours" and "exaltation", and the Adventists' "jugement investigatif". Approve, with a check by a French reader?
6. **Fixed end-times vocabulary for all courses.** The list is: prémillénarisme / postmillénarisme / amillénarisme, dispensationaliste, millénium, enlèvement, chiliasme, and "étang de feu" (with one note that the Greek means "lac"). Approve as the standard list?

**End-times balance flags.** All of these are faithful translations of the English. The question is whether the English should change.
- **Revelation 4, b016 heading: "Kept *Through* the Hour of Trial".** This heading takes one side of a question that b017 says "the course leaves open". The Spanish heading ("Guardada *de* la Hora") is already neutral. A suggested fix is "Kept in the Hour of Trial".
- **Revelation 8, b021.** The first-century (preterist) reading of the beast and the mark gets a long, detailed paragraph and the remark that it "fills in this picture most fully". The future reading gets one sentence, and the idealist reading gets none.
- **Rev 20:4 in Segond (Revelation 11–14).** Segond's "ils revinrent à la vie" sounds like a bodily resurrection, which favours the premillennial reading. Units 11, 13 and 14 add "le texte grec dit littéralement « ils vécurent »". Unit 12 (postmillennialism) leaves the note out on purpose. Should all four units handle it the same way?
- **Rev 22:7, 12, 20 (Revelation 15).** Segond's "je viens bientôt" makes a claim about time. The checker added that the Greek can also mean "promptly" or "suddenly". Keep?
- **Revelation 9, MC 13.** The wrong option "The church is raptured" could be read as a ruling against a rapture view. It is wrong only because 15:1 is about wrath.

**Eternal-security flags** (the course treats this as an open question)
- **Revelation 8.** b019 says names are written "once and for all in indelible ink". b023 says "already secured His people forever". The SA 8 model says they "cannot finally be lost". This sits in tension with Rev 3:5.
- **Revelation 11, b023:** "it can never be erased".
- **Revelation 7, SA 7 model:** "they cannot be lost". The French model leaves this out. Please confirm.
- **Galatians 6, MC 5 `why`:** "not to forfeit a salvation grasped by faith". The lesson (b013) says "Christians differ" on this point.
- **Galatians 10, b023 and Galatians 8, MC 19:** "a present and certain possession"; grace "always bears fruit". These are milder.

**May lean Calvinist**
- **Galatians 8, b017 and SA 3:** "even that faith is itself a gift of God".
- **1 Peter 3, b030 (1 Pet 1:20):** Segond's "prédestiné" sounds stronger than "foreordained". The drafts add "littéralement « connu d'avance »". Approve?

**Other**
- **1 Peter 10, b013:** one reading of 1 Pet 4:6 (believers now dead) is given as settled. Unit 9 presented the views on the nearby verses even-handedly.
- **Galatians 12, b016:** "Some Adventists" teach that Sunday worship will be the mark of the beast. This was standard historic Adventist teaching, not a fringe view. Rephrase?
