# CTSBibleCharacters2 — Unit 1 (Jonah) — French review notes

## Questions for Wayne

1. **Joppa (Jonah 1:3).** Segond has "Japho". The lesson text says "Japho (Joppé)". Fill 3 answer: "Japho"; it also accepts Joppé, Joppa and Jaffa.
2. **"From the presence of the LORD" (Jonah 1:3; b003, b013, mc3).** Segond: "loin de la face de l'Éternel". Quoted as is. No note added, since the meaning is the same.
3. **"Sign" in Matthew 12:39 (b032, b033, mc20, sa9).** Segond translates "miracle" ("demande un miracle… celui du prophète Jonas"). I quoted Segond in b033 and added one sentence to b032: the Greek says literally "signe". The lesson and exam items use "signe", and sa9 accepts both "signe" and "miracle".
4. **"Abundant in lovingkindness" (Jonah 4:2; b028, fill 8).** Segond: "riche en bonté". Fill 8 answer: "bonté". It also accepts amour, miséricorde, bienveillance and bonté fidèle. Elsewhere "gracious and merciful" became "compatissant et miséricordieux", following Segond (mc17, sa6).
5. **"Relented" (Jonah 3:10, 4:2; b024, mc15).** Segond says God "se repentit du mal qu'il avait résolu de leur faire". I quoted Segond, then glossed it as "il renonça au malheur dont il les avait menacés". The mc15 option uses "a renoncé au malheur…". Please confirm that you are comfortable printing "Dieu se repentit" without more explanation.
6. **Sackcloth (b024, mc14, fill 7).** Segond: "se revêtirent de sacs". Fill 7 answer: "sacs". It also accepts sac, toile de sac, sacs et cendre and habits de sac.
7. **The plant (Jonah 4:6).** Segond says "ricin". I kept the lesson's "plante feuillue" and mentioned "ricin" once in b029 (and in the mc18 why). sa7 accepts plante or ricin.
8. **"Overthrown" (Jonah 3:4; b024, mc13).** Segond: "Encore quarante jours, et Ninive est détruite!" The mc13 option became "détruite".
9. **"Salvation is of the LORD" (Jonah 2:9).** Segond: "Le salut vient de l'Éternel". It is used in b019 and mc10.
10. **"Heathen captain" (b020).** Segond (Jonah 1:6) calls him "le pilote". I wrote "le pilote païen du navire".
11. **"A greater than Jonah is here" (b034, b036, sa8).** Segond (Matthew 12:41): "il y a ici plus que Jonas". I quoted it with the reference added, and used "plus que Jonas" or "plus grand que Jonas" in the headings and keywords.
12. **Jonah 3:2 (b023).** Segond's wording "proclames-y la publication que je t'ordonne" is archaic. I quoted it verbatim as required.

## Problems in the English source

1. **sa1 and sa5:** the keyword "arrepent" is a Spanish stem. An English answer containing "repent" will never match it, so the English grading is weaker than intended. **sa3:** "salvaci" has the same problem (Spanish "salvación"). In French I used "repent" and "salut".
2. **sa7:** the English prompt ("plant, worm, and wind") contains 3 of its own keywords (plant, worm, wind), so a student who simply copies the prompt passes. The French prompt matches only 2 groups.
3. **b034:** "a greater than Jonah is here" comes from Matthew 12:41, but only 12:39-40 is quoted or cited. I added "(Matthieu 12:41)" in French.
4. **mc5:** the "why" cites Psalm 139:7-10, which the lesson never quotes. This is minor; I kept it.
