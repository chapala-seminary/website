# CTSBibleCharacters2 Unit 10 — French draft review notes

## Questions for Wayne

1. **John 20:17 "Do not cling to Me".** LSG has "Ne me touche pas". In b020 I quoted LSG and added one sentence: "Le verbe grec a ici le sens de « ne me retiens pas »" (convention 1). Fill 6 also uses LSG: "je ne suis pas encore ____ vers mon Père". The answer is "monté", and the fill also accepts "remonté", "élevé", "retourné" and "allé".
2. **John 20:15 "Sir".** LSG has "Seigneur, si c'est toi qui l'as emporté…". A reader could think Mary already recognised Jesus as Lord. In b018 I added a sentence saying that the Greek word is a simple polite form of address here, like "Monsieur". MC 9 quotes LSG as it stands.
3. **John 20:18 "I have seen the Lord!"** LSG gives this as indirect speech: "alla annoncer aux disciples qu'elle avait vu le Seigneur". b024 quotes LSG, then adds "Le texte grec rapporte ses propres mots : « J'ai vu le Seigneur ! »". MC 16 `why` and the SA 6 model now use indirect speech (no direct quotation presented as Scripture). MC 7 keeps "J'ai vu le Seigneur" only as a distractor.
4. **John 20:16.** LSG adds "en hébreu" and spells the word "Rabbouni" (Maître). I used "Rabbouni" everywhere. Fill 5 also accepts "Rabboni", "Rabouni" and "Raboni".
5. **John 10:3.** LSG has "il appelle par leur nom les brebis qui lui appartiennent, et il les conduit dehors". I used it in b020, MC 13 and Fill 7. Fill 7 has the answer "brebis" and also accepts "moutons".
6. **John 20:2 (b012).** LSG has "Ils ont enlevé du sépulcre le Seigneur". I used it as it stands.
7. **1 Corinthians 15:3-4.** LSG has "enseveli" (buried) and "selon les Écritures". I used these in b026, MC 18 and SA 8.
8. **Name.** I used the Segond form "Marie de Magdala" throughout (convention 5). At the first mention (b007) I added "que l'on appelle souvent Marie-Madeleine". The SA 1 keyword group accepts both forms.

## Problems in the English source

1. **Fill 1.** The English prompt gives a clue, "another w-word" (weeping → worship). It does not work in French, so I dropped it.
2. **b010 / MC 4 / SA 2.** These say Mary "was the first to come to the tomb". That comes from John 20:1. The other Gospels have her arriving with other women. This is minor.
3. **MC 2 `why`.** "Her love for Him flowed from how much He had done for her" does not explain the answer (supporting Jesus out of their own means). It fits loosely, but it reads like it belongs to MC 1.
4. **SA 1 and SA 2.** The keywords "devoted" and "faithful" are not lesson vocabulary. **SA 7.** The keyword "acts" is a book name (Acts 2:32). I kept it as the group ["actes","apôtres"].
5. No wrong answer keys were found.
