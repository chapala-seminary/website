# CTSBibleCharacters2 Unit 7 — French draft review notes

## Questions for Wayne

1. **Matthew 16:18 "On this rock".** LSG has "sur cette pierre je bâtirai mon Église" and "les portes du séjour des morts" (for "Hades"). The lesson's "rock" theme depends on this. In b020 I quoted LSG and added one sentence: the Greek word (petra) literally means "rock" and echoes Peter's name (Petros). Elsewhere, for "the rock who feeds the sheep", I used "le roc". Fill 4 has the answer "pierre" and also accepts "roc", "rocher" and "roche". In SA 2 the keyword group is ["rocher", "cette pierre", "le roc", "ce roc"], because a bare "pierre" would match the name Pierre.
2. **John 21:15-17 "Feed / Tend / Feed".** LSG has "Pais mes agneaux... Pais mes brebis... Pais mes brebis". Segond uses the same verb all three times, so the feed/tend distinction is lost. The lesson does not depend on that distinction, so I added no note. Fill 8 has the answer "agneaux".
3. **1 Peter 5:2.** LSG has "Paissez le troupeau de Dieu qui est sous votre garde". It does not include "serving as overseers". b028 adds right after the quote, in parentheses: "le texte grec ajoute littéralement : « en veillant sur lui »" (settled convention 1).
4. **Luke 5:8.** LSG has "Seigneur, retire-toi de moi, parce que je suis un homme pécheur". Fill 2 has the answer "pécheur". Students may confuse it with "pêcheur" (fisherman). The grader folds accents, so "pecheur" is accepted either way. The French also makes a nice wordplay here.
5. **Luke 5:10.** LSG has "désormais tu seras pêcheur d'hommes" ("you will be a fisher of men", not "you will catch men"). I quoted LSG in b015.
6. **Matthew 14:30 "boisterous wind".** LSG has "voyant que le vent était fort". Fill 5 has the answer "vent". MC 7 quotes "vit que le vent était fort".
7. **Matthew 26:74, "with curses".** I rendered this as "avec des imprécations" (LSG's word).
8. **Revelation 1:17-18.** LSG's verse division and wording differ: it has no "Amen", and "les clefs de la mort et du séjour des morts" puts Death before Hades. I quoted LSG verbatim. MC 19 follows LSG's order.
9. **Acts 10:34.** I quoted LSG "Dieu ne fait point acception de personnes" for "God shows no partiality". This phrase is old-fashioned but familiar to Segond readers.
10. **Heading "Coward into a Crusader".** "Croisé" (crusader) has bad associations in Africa and in Islamic contexts. I used "héraut intrépide" (fearless herald) instead, in b029, MC 16 and SA 7. Please confirm.
11. **"Witch doctor" / "voodoo" (Malamba).** In Kinshasa "vaudou" is not the local term. I used "féticheur" (the usual Congolese French word) and "sorcellerie". Please confirm this fits the original story.

## Problems in the English source

1. **SA 5.** The keyword "failure" is not in the lesson's account of the denial. Only the model's last sentence supplies it. I kept it as "échec/chute".
2. **SA 2.** The English keyword list has "peter", which will be in almost every answer. In French a bare "pierre" would also match the name. I replaced it with a "bâti" (build) group to keep 8 distinct ideas.
3. **SA 6.** The keyword "commission" relies on "recommissioned" in b028. That is fine. "pastor" only appears in the heading III. This is minor.
4. **MC 5 option "Come and see".** This is John 1:39 and is unrelated to Peter's confession. It is harmless as a distractor.
5. No wrong answer keys were found, and every `why` matches its question.
