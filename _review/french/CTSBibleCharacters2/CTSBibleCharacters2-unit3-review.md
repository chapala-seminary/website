# CTSBibleCharacters2 — Unit 3 (Jeremiah) — French review notes

## Questions for Wayne

1. **"Sanctified" (Jeremiah 1:5; b003, b013, fill 1).** Segond: "je t'avais consacré". Fill 1 answer: "consacré"; it also accepts sanctifié and mis à part.
2. **"I am a youth" (Jeremiah 1:6-7; b014, mc2, fill 2).** Segond: "je suis un enfant". The mc2 option became "seulement un enfant". Fill 2 answer: "enfant"; it also accepts jeune, jeune homme, garçon and adolescent.
3. **"Burning fire" and "His word" (Jeremiah 20:9; b029, b030, mc15, fill 7, sa6).** Segond: "Il y a dans mon coeur comme un feu dévorant". It does not say "His word was in my heart", and the lesson and mc15 depend on the fire being God's word. Following convention 1, I added one sentence at the start of b030: the Hebrew literally says "cela était dans mon cœur comme un feu brûlant", and that "cela" is the word Jeremiah wanted to silence. The mc15 option uses Segond's "feu dévorant".
4. **"Law in their minds" (Jeremiah 31:33; b034, sa8).** Segond: "Je mettrai ma loi au dedans d'eux". I quoted it as is and wrote "au dedans" in the sa8 model. No exam item depends on "minds".
5. **"Stocks" (b023, sa5).** Segond's Jeremiah 20:2 says "la prison", not stocks. The lesson does not quote the verse, so I kept the lesson's idea with the traditional French "mis aux ceps". sa5 accepts ceps or entraves.
6. **Clay and the potter (Jeremiah 18:4, 6; b019, b021, fill 3).** Segond has "l'argile" and "le vase... ne réussit pas". In b021 I followed Segond's wording ("il en refit un autre vase, tel qu'il trouva bon de le faire"). Fill 3 answer: "argile"; it also accepts glaise and terre.
7. **2 Peter 3:9 allusion (b021).** The English alludes to the verse without quotation marks. I kept it as an unquoted allusion that matches Segond's wording ("ne veut pas qu'aucun périsse, mais veut que tous arrivent à la repentance").
8. **"Last Supper" (mc20).** I used "la sainte cène" (settled convention 7).
9. **"Lord GOD" (Jeremiah 1:6; b014, mc2).** Segond: "Ah! Seigneur Éternel!". I quoted it with the accent added.

## Problems in the English source

1. **sa7:** the English prompt ("What sign of hope did God give through the field at Anathoth?") contains 4 of its own 8 keywords (sign, hope, field, anathoth), so a student who copies the prompt passes. In French I reworded the prompt ("Expliquez pourquoi Dieu demanda à Jérémie d'acheter une terre pendant le siège de Jérusalem") so it hits only one keyword ("achet"). Please confirm the change.
2. **mc12:** the "why" cites Lamentations, which the lesson never mentions. This is minor; I kept it.
3. **mc9:** the "why" cites Jeremiah 2:13, which the lesson does not cite (but the idea is taught in b018). This is minor; I kept it.
4. **b021:** the clause "not willing that any should perish, but that all should come to repentance" quotes 2 Peter 3:9 without giving the reference. I kept it unreferenced, as in the English.
