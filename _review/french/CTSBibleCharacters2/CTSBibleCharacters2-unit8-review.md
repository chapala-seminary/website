# CTSBibleCharacters2 Unit 8 (Thomas): French draft review notes

## Questions for Wayne

1. **John 20:29 "Blessed".** LSG has "Heureux ceux qui n'ont pas vu, et qui ont cru!" (b003, b027, MC 12, Fill 9). In the prose I called it "béatitude" (blessing). In b028 I added one sentence saying the Greek word rendered "heureux" means the happiness of someone God blesses (convention 1). The SA 5 keyword group accepts "heureux", "béni" and "bénédiction".
2. **John 20:27 "Do not be unbelieving, but believing".** LSG has "ne sois pas incrédule, mais crois" (a verb, not an adjective). I used this in b021 and MC 9. Fill 6 has the answer "crois" and also accepts "croyant", "croyante" and "croire".
3. **"First Lord's Day" (b015).** I rendered it "ce tout premier jour du Seigneur". LSG's John 20:19 says "le premier de la semaine".
4. **"Faith would come by hearing" (b030).** I echoed Romains 10:17 in LSG ("la foi vient de ce qu'on entend").
5. **"Deity".** I used "divinité" (b024, MC 11, SA 4). Fill 8 has the answer "divinité" and also accepts "déité", "nature divine" and "nature de Dieu".
6. **"Doubting Thomas".** I used « Thomas l'incrédule », the usual French label. Headings use "celui qui doutait" to avoid repeating "incrédule".

## Problems in the English source

1. **MC 1.** The lesson never mentions Thomas's other name, Didymus ("the Twin"). It appears only in the exam. LSG does have "Thomas, appelé Didyme" (Jean 11:16; 20:24).
2. **MC 14 and SA 6.** Both cite a section called "Eyes of the Heart", but the lesson has no such heading. The material is in "III. The Blessing" (b028). I translated the title literally as « Les yeux du cœur ».
3. **MC 4.** The correct option ("He was absent when Jesus first appeared") restates the question rather than giving a reason. The lesson says "for some reason". This is minor.
4. **b032 and MC 17.** They say Thomas "fell in awe at the feet of his Savior". John 20:28 records his confession but not that he fell at Jesus' feet. This is minor.
5. **SA 9.** The keyword "risk" is not in the lesson. I kept a group ("risque", "enjeu"), and the model answer hits it through "enjeu" (the lesson's "the stakes").
6. No wrong answer keys were found. Every `why` matches its question.

## Adaptations
