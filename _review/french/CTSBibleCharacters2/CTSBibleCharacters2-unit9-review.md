# CTSBibleCharacters2 Unit 9 — French draft review notes

## Questions for Wayne

1. **John 11:25 word order.** LSG has "Celui qui croit en moi vivra, quand même il serait mort", which puts "though he may die" after "he shall live". I quoted LSG in b003, b018, MC 9, MC 10, fill 4 and SA 5. The MC 10 option follows LSG's order: "vivra, quand même il serait mort… et ne mourra jamais".
2. **"The triumph of death" (b010, b016, MC 3).** In English, "the triumph of death" could be read as death triumphing. I wrote "le triomphe sur la mort" (the triumph over death) for the heading, the three movements and MC 3. The first two movements stay "la tragédie / l'épreuve de la mort". Is that acceptable?
3. **John 11:16.** LSG has "Allons aussi, afin de mourir avec lui". Fill 3 uses the answer "mourir" and also accepts "mourions", "périr" and "mourir aussi".
4. **John 11:11.** LSG has "Lazare, notre ami, dort ; mais je vais le réveiller". I used it in b024, MC 14, fill 7 and SA 7.
5. **John 11:43 "come forth".** LSG has "Lazare, sors !". For the remark in b023 and MC 13 that every dead person might have risen, I used the plural "Sortez !".
6. **"Groaned in His spirit" (b020).** I quoted John 11:33 from LSG: "frémit en son esprit, et fut tout ému".
7. **Scripture allusions turned into quotations.** I made three allusions into short LSG quotations with references, so French readers can find them:
   - "the last enemy" in b012 → 1 Corinthiens 15:26
   - "not grieve as those who have no hope" in b028 → 1 Thessaloniciens 4:13
   - "wipe away every tear" in b028 → Apocalypse 21:4

   I also quoted John 11:6 ("il resta deux jours encore…") in b014. Please confirm that is acceptable.

8. **Scripture check (checker).** Every LSG quotation in lessons and exams (Jean 11:3, 4, 6, 11, 14, 15, 16, 25-27, 33, 35, 43, 44; Jean 3:16; 1 Co 15:26; 1 Th 4:13; Ap 21:4; MC 11 options from Jn 19:30 and Mc 4:39) was checked against the offline Segond: all exact.

## Problems in the English source

1. **SA 5.** The model calls Jesus's words "the great claim of His deity", but the lesson never discusses His deity. In French I wrote "la grande déclaration de qui il est".
2. **SA 10.** The keyword "fear" does not appear in the English model answer. The French model also does not use it, but it still passes on the other keywords.
3. **SA 3 and SA 4.** Both questions draw on the same paragraph (the delay, neglect and glory) and test the same keywords. The overlap is minor.
4. **MC 13.** The stem asks why Jesus called Lazarus by name. The lesson presents this only as something "someone once observed", not as the reason. The French keeps the hedge ("comme on l'a dit").

## Adaptations

- **b012.** I added "aux États-Unis" after "Dakota du Sud" so Congolese readers know where it is.
- **MC 5.** I changed "funerals in winter" to "pendant la saison froide", since Kinshasa has no winter. The option is a distractor, so the answer is not affected.
