# CTSBibleCharacters2 — Unit 2 (Hosea): French review notes

## Questions for Wayne
1. **Hosea 14:4 (epigraph b003, b026, b027, b031; MC 14; fill 9; SA 6, 10).** Segond reads "Je réparerai leur infidélité, J'aurai pour eux un amour sincère" ("I will repair their unfaithfulness, I will have a sincere love for them"). It has neither "heal" nor "backsliding" nor "freely". I quoted Segond and added in b027: "Le texte hébreu dit littéralement : « je guérirai leur égarement, je les aimerai de plein gré », c'est-à-dire gratuitement." The lesson's "He heals the very backsliding" became "Il guérit l'infidélité même qui l'a blessé". Fill 9 answer = "infidélité" (accepts "égarement", "apostasie").
2. **Hosea 1:2 (b013, MC 3, SA 1).** NKJV "a wife of harlotry" = Segond "une femme prostituée"; "harlotry" throughout rendered "prostitution". Segond also ends "il abandonne l'Éternel!", which fits the lesson.
3. **Hosea 3:2 (b024, fill 7).** Segond: "un homer d'orge et un léthec d'orge" (no "homer and a half"). I wrote "un homer d'orge et un léthec d'orge, c'est-à-dire un homer et demi". Fill 7 prompt uses "un homer et un léthec d'____" (answer "orge"). Also "shekels" = "sicles".
4. **Hosea 2:14 (b024).** NKJV "speak comfort to her" = Segond "je parlerai à son cœur". Quoted Segond; the lesson's point (tender wooing) still works.
5. **Hosea 3:1 (b022, SA 5).** Segond "aime-la comme l'Éternel aime les enfants d'Israël" (not "just like the love of the LORD"). Quoted Segond up to that point and truncated with an ellipsis (the source block also stops mid-verse).
6. **Names (b014, MC 5–7, fill 3–4, SA 2).** Segond spellings: "Jizreel", "Lo-Ruchama", "Lo-Ammi". Fill answers/keywords also accept "Jezreel", "Ruhama". Jezreel's meaning kept as the lesson's "Dieu dispersera". Fill 3 also accepts "semera" (the literal "God sows", matching the English alternate "sow").
7. **Matthew 18:22 (b030, MC 19, fill 10, SA 8).** Segond "septante fois sept fois". I added "c'est-à-dire soixante-dix fois sept fois" in b030, since "septante" is less familiar in Kinshasa. SA 8 accepts both.
8. **Hosea 2:23 (b028).** Segond has "Je dirai à Lo-Ammi: Tu es mon peuple! et il répondra: Mon Dieu!" (it names Lo-Ammi, unlike NKJV "those who were not My people"). Quoted Segond. This actually strengthens the lesson's "names reversed" point.
9. **"Unforgivable forgiveness"** rendered "pardon impardonnable" (a deliberate paradox, as in the English). Please confirm you want it kept rather than softened to "pardon de l'impardonnable".

## Problems in the English source
1. **b014 / MC 5 / SA 2:** "Jezreel, meaning 'God will scatter'". Jezreel literally means "God sows/will sow"; "scatter" is a derived sense. Also the dogs ate Jezebel "in the plot/field of Jezreel" (2 Kings 9:36), not "the courtyard". I wrote neutrally "des chiens à Jizreel" and kept "Dieu dispersera" for the meaning.
2. **b024 / fill 7:** "the going price of a slave" — Exodus 21:32 sets a slave's price at 30 shekels; Hosea's 15 shekels plus barley is usually reckoned as roughly equal to that. The claim is defensible but stated without support.
3. **MC 19:** the `why` (Stephen's prayer) does not explain the answer about Matthew 18:22. Translated faithfully.
4. **b021 / b023:** "sold into slavery" and "the slave market" are traditional interpretation; Hosea 3 says only that he bought her. Translated as written.

## French glossary used
- harlotry → prostitution
- backsliding → infidélité (Segond, Os 14:4); égarement (literal sense)
- love freely → aimer d'un amour sincère et gratuit
- relentless grace → grâce inlassable
- unforgivable forgiveness → pardon impardonnable
- redeem / buy back → racheter
- slave market → marché aux esclaves
- covenant → alliance
- spiritual adultery → adultère spirituel
- wanderer → celui qui s'égare
- woo → reconquérir (avec tendresse)
- shekel → sicle
- Not My People / No Mercy → Pas mon peuple / Pas de miséricorde
