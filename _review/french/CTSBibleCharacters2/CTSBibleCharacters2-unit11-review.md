# CTSBibleCharacters2 Unit 11 — French draft review notes

## Questions for Wayne

1. **Romans 10:2 "zeal without knowledge" (b014, Fill 3).** LSG has "du zèle pour Dieu, mais sans intelligence". In b014 I quoted LSG and added "c'est-à-dire sans vraie connaissance". Fill 3 has the answer "intelligence" and also accepts "connaissance", "vraie connaissance" and "discernement".
2. **Acts 9:15 "chosen vessel" (b018, Fill 6).** LSG has "un instrument que j'ai choisi". I added that the Greek literally says "un vase de choix". Fill 6 has the answer "instrument" and also accepts "vase", "vaisseau" and "outil".
3. **Philippians 3:8 "rubbish".** LSG has "comme de la boue". I used "boue" in b021, MC 9 and SA 4, with a note that the Greek word means refuse. The SA 4 keyword group also accepts "ordure" and "déchet".
4. **1 Timothy 1:15 "chief of sinners".** LSG has "dont je suis le premier". I used "le premier des pécheurs" throughout (b031, b035, MC 17, MC 20, SA 9, SA 10). Fill 9 has the answer "premier" and also accepts "chef", "pire" and "plus grand".
5. **2 Timothy 4:7 "race".** LSG has "la course". Fill 8 has the answer "course" and also accepts "carrière" and "parcours".
6. **1 Corinthians 9:16 "necessity is laid upon me".** LSG has "la nécessité m'en est imposée". MC 13 and SA 6 use that wording; the English "compulsion" keyword maps to ["nécessité", "contraint", "imposée"].
7. **1 Corinthians 15:8 "one born out of due time".** LSG has "comme à l'avorton". I quoted it as LSG has it (b030). Please confirm this is acceptable for readers.
8. **Acts 9:1 and Acts 8:1.** b012 and MC 2 quote LSG "respirant encore la menace et le meurtre". For "consenting" I used "approuvait", following LSG's "avait approuvé" (Actes 8:1).
9. **Acts 9:6 "Lord, what do You want me to do?"** It is quoted from LSG ("Seigneur, que veux-tu que je fasse ?"). Note that LSG puts this question in 9:6, the same as the NKJV; some critical-text versions omit it.
10. **"Thirty-nine lashes" (b023).** I quoted LSG's "quarante coups moins un" (2 Corinthiens 11:24). "The love of Christ compelled him" (b025) is rendered with LSG's 2 Corinthiens 5:14, "l'amour de Christ nous presse".
11. **"Master" (b007, b009, MC 20).** I rendered "master and send any life" as "conquérir et envoyer n'importe quelle vie". "Master his hatred" (b035) became "vaincre sa haine".

## Problems in the English source

1. **b028 heading** "The Resurrection Took Away His Ignorance". The section under it never speaks of ignorance; it is about the risen Lord explaining his transformation. I translated it faithfully.
2. **b018** says Saul "was filled with the Spirit" through Ananias. Acts 9:17 promises this, but the text does not narrate it explicitly. This is minor.
3. **b023** says "From Antioch he sailed across Asia Minor". Much of Paul's travel in Asia Minor was on foot. I rendered it as "il a traversé l'Asie Mineure". This is minor.
4. **SA 3.** The keyword "obey" is not in the lesson, and the model says "surrendered" instead. I used ["obéi", "soumi", "soumet"] so that the model hits it.
5. **SA 7.** The model calls the passage "one of the most triumphant farewells in all of Scripture". The lesson does not say this. It is harmless.
6. No wrong answer keys were found. Every `why` matches its question.
