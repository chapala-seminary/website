# CTSBibleCharacters2 Unit 6 — French draft review notes

## Questions for Wayne

1. **John 3:30 "increase / decrease".** LSG has "Il faut qu'il croisse, et que je diminue". It is used in b003, b015, b031, MC 6 and SA 1. Fill 1 has the answer "croisse" and also accepts "croître", "grandisse", "grandir" and "augmente".
2. **Matthew 11:3 "the Coming One".** LSG has "Es-tu celui qui doit venir, ou devons-nous en attendre un autre?". In b020 the prose follows it ("celui qui doit venir"). MC 9 quotes it. The SA 2 keyword group is ["doit venir", "celui qui vient"].
3. **Matthew 11:6 "not offended because of Me".** LSG has "Heureux celui pour qui je ne serai pas une occasion de chute!". It is quoted in MC 12. The SA 4 keyword group accepts "chute", "scandal" and "offens".
4. **Matthew 11:5 "the poor have the gospel preached".** LSG has "la bonne nouvelle est annoncée aux pauvres". I used "bonne nouvelle" in MC 11 and in the SA 4 model. The keyword group also accepts "évangile".
5. **Hebrews 13:5.** LSG has "Je ne te délaisserai point, et je ne t'abandonnerai point" (leave → délaisser, forsake → abandonner). Fill 10 has the answer "abandonnerai" (accepts "abandonnerais", "abandonner", "abandonne"); "délaisserai" is no longer accepted, since it is the other verb already printed in the prompt. In SA 8 there are separate groups for "délaiss" and "abandonn".
6. **1 John 5:13.** LSG puts "vous qui croyez au nom du Fils de Dieu" at the end of the verse. Fill 9 follows LSG's order.
7. **Elijah's "juniper tree" (b024).** LSG has "genêt" (broom tree). I used "genêt". For the "gentle voice" I quoted LSG's "un murmure doux et léger" (1 Rois 19:12).
8. **Allusions given in LSG wording.** "Races de vipères", "la cognée est mise à la racine des arbres" (Matthieu 3), "je ne suis pas digne de délier la courroie de ses souliers" (Luc 3:16), "son van à la main… son aire" (b020), Psaume 103:14 (b024, which I put in quotation marks), and "ne brise point le roseau cassé" (Ésaïe 42:3). b012 says "Préparez le chemin du Seigneur" because LSG has "Seigneur" in Matthieu 3:3, not "l'Éternel".
9. **"Standing" (assurance of one's standing).** I rendered it "position devant Dieu" and "lui appartenir". Please check that this does not read as more (or less) of an eternal-security statement than the English.
10. **Zacharias the "priest".** I used "sacrificateur" (convention 4) and the LSG spelling "Zacharie".

## Problems in the English source

1. **SA 7.** The model answer begins "When my wife was asked how she dealt with doubt". The lesson (b024) says the opposite: she asked Wayne how *he* dealt with doubt. I translated it faithfully. Please fix the English.
2. **SA 7.** Its keyword "feelings" is not part of the wife's story in the lesson. The French replaces it (and the generic "god"/"doubt" stems) with ideas the lesson does teach (convention 10).
3. **SA 5.** The keyword "esteem" and the model's "nor strip him of his honor" go slightly beyond the lesson. This is minor.
4. **SA 6 model.** It says Christ "does not change and cannot lie". The lesson never says this. This is minor and harmless.
5. **SA 9.** The keyword "martyr" never appears in the lesson. I kept it.
6. No wrong answer keys were found. Every `why` matches its question.
