# CTSBibleCharacters2 — Unit 5 (Ezra) — French review notes

## Questions for Wayne

1. **Ezra 7:10 (b003, b025, mc13, fill 7, sa4).** The NKJV has "prepared his heart to seek the Law… and to do it, and to teach". Segond has "avait appliqué son coeur à étudier et à mettre en pratique la loi de l'Éternel, et à enseigner". I quoted Segond. In b025 I added one sentence: "Le texte hébreu dit littéralement qu'il avait « préparé » son cœur". That keeps your point that he prepared his heart and not just his arguments. The fill 7 answer is "enseigner". sa4 accepts both "prépar" and "appliqu".
2. **"Stirred up" (Ezra 1:1, 1:5).** Segond has "réveilla l'esprit". I used "réveiller" all through the lesson and exam (b015, b020, b035, mc2, mc8, fill 2, sa2).
3. **Ezra 3:12-13 (b019, mc6).** Segond has "chefs de famille âgés, qui avaient vu la première maison". I quoted it as written. The lesson prose still says "temple".
4. **"Skilled scribe" (Ezra 7:6; b024, mc12).** Segond has "un scribe versé dans la loi de Moïse". I quoted that in the mc12 why. The stem uses "habile" ("skilled").
5. **"Sat down astonished" (Ezra 9:3; b029, mc17).** Segond has "je m'assis désolé". The lesson prose uses "consterné".
6. **"Adversaries" (Ezra 4:1; b020, mc7).** Segond has "les ennemis de Juda et de Benjamin". I kept "adversaires" in the prose and in the mc7 option.
7. **"If your hand causes you to sin, cut it off" (b030, sa9).** The English gives no reference. I quoted Segond, Mark 9:43 ("Si ta main est pour toi une occasion de chute, coupe-la"), and added the reference.
8. **John 4 (b035).** I put the words "en esprit et en vérité" in quotation marks and added the reference (Jean 4:23). The rest stays a paraphrase, as in the English.
9. **"Husks of the far country" (b035).** I used Segond's "carouges" (Luke 15:16) and "pays éloigné" (Luke 15:13).
10. **"Bible doers" (mc14, sa5).** Segond's James 1:22 says "Mettez en pratique la parole", so "doers" became "pratiquants". In mc14 I replaced the English blank "___" with "…" so it is not confused with a fill-in. sa5 accepts both "pratiquant/pratiquer" and "mettre en pratique".
11. **Priest/cleanse (b022, b023, mc11, fill 8).** I used "sacrificateur", following the convention. "Cleanse" became "purifier". Fill 8 also accepts nettoyer, laver, rendre pur and purification. In b035 Christ is "notre grand Souverain Sacrificateur", with capitals as in the English title.
12. **"Intermarried" (fill 9).** French has no single verb for this, so the prompt reads "avaient contracté des ____". The answer is "mariages". It also accepts mariage, mariages mixtes, unions and alliances matrimoniales.

## Problems in the English source

1. **b023:** "more than sixty years later" is loose. Ezra came in 458 BC, about 58 years after the temple was finished (516 BC) and about 80 years after the first return (538 BC). I translated it as written.
2. **mc7:** the correct option says "(Samaritans)", but the lesson never uses that name. It says only "adversaries of the land". This is minor; I kept it.
3. **mc14 and sa5:** the phrase "Bible doers" and James 1:22 (cited in the mc14 why) never appear in the lesson. The lesson only teaches "study, do, teach".
4. **sa9:** the keywords "covenant" and "separate" are not taught in the lesson. The lesson does not mention the covenant of Ezra 10:3. In French I used "alliance/engag" and "sépar", and the French model answer avoids "covenant", as the English does.
5. **sa6:** the keyword "world" does not match the lesson's "idolatry of the land". In French the group is "idolâtr/monde".
6. **sa10:** the keyword "gospel" never appears in the lesson. It does appear in the model answer.

## Adaptations

- **b007:** the English opening addresses expatriates who moved to a new country and left behind "cars and houses". I widened it to "quitter le village pour la grande ville, fuir la guerre, ou partir vivre dans un autre pays", and "cars" became "biens". The point (a fresh start) is unchanged.
