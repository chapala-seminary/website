## Questions for Wayne

1. **1 Peter 5:2, "serving as overseers".** LSG has only "qui est sous votre garde" (no separate verb for overseeing). b011 quotes LSG and adds: "Le texte grec dit littéralement qu'ils doivent le paître « en exerçant la surveillance »." MC2 stem says "exercer leur garde".
2. **1 Peter 5:4, "the Chief Shepherd" / "crown of glory that does not fade away".** LSG: "le souverain pasteur" (lowercase) and "la couronne incorruptible de la gloire". Kept lowercase inside the quotation; in the lesson's own prose capitalized "le Souverain Pasteur" (convention 9). Fill 2 answer "gloire".
3. **1 Peter 5:7, "casting all your care … He cares for you".** LSG: "déchargez-vous sur lui de tous vos soucis, car lui-même prend soin de vous". The lesson stresses the verb *cast*; I used "se décharger" throughout and added in b019: "Le texte grec dit littéralement « jeter » nos soucis sur lui." The heading b018 uses LSG. Fill 5 answer is "soin" (from "prend soin"), accepts "soins", "souci", "grand soin".
4. **1 Peter 5:9, "steadfast in the faith" / "your brotherhood in the world".** LSG: "avec une foi ferme" and "imposées à vos frères dans le monde". Fill 7 is now "avec une ____ ferme" (answer "foi"). The lesson's prose uses "fraternité" for "brotherhood"; MC17 uses LSG's "frères".
5. **1 Peter 5:10, "perfect, establish, strengthen, and settle".** LSG: "vous perfectionnera lui-même, vous affermira, vous fortifiera, vous rendra inébranlables". The lesson's "settle them on a firm foundation" became "les rendre inébranlables sur un fondement solide".
6. **1 Peter 5:11, "the glory and the dominion".** LSG has only "la puissance" (Segond follows a text without "glory"). b025 says Peter ascribes "la puissance", with a parenthesis: "dans de nombreux manuscrits grecs : « la gloire et la puissance »". The SA10 model follows LSG. Please confirm this parenthesis is acceptable.
7. **1 Peter 5:12-13, "Silvanus" / "She who is in Babylon".** LSG: "Silvain" and "L'Église des élus qui est à Babylone". Since LSG already says "Église", the lesson's "(which he calls Babylon)" still fits. Fill 10 answer "Silvain", also accepts Silvanus, Silas, Sylvain, Sylvanus.
8. **"Kiss of love"** → LSG "baiser d'affection"; used in b027.
9. **"Undershepherd"** rendered "sous-berger"; "shepherd" (pastoral role) as "berger", with "pasteur" kept for Christ (LSG 2:25 "le pasteur … de vos âmes").

## Problems in the English source

1. **SA prompts pass their own keywords in English:** SA1 (elder, shepherd, flock), SA4 (cast, care, god), SA6 (polycarp, brotherhood, suffer, resist, steadfast, faith), SA7 (god, grace, suffer). Following convention 10, the French prompts were reworded and the keyword groups use ideas from the lesson; each French prompt now gets at most 2 hits.
2. **Weak English keywords:** SA3/SA7/SA4 use "god"; SA4 has "care" and "cares" as separate groups (the same word). Replaced with distinct ideas in French.
3. **b017, Louis XIV funeral:** the story is a sermon-illustration version. Historically, Jean-Baptiste Massillon opened his funeral oration (1715) with "Dieu seul est grand, mes frères"; the golden coffin, the darkened "great cathedral" and the single snuffed candle are not well documented. Translated as written. French readers may know the real quotation.
4. **b023, Uganda story:** gives no names or date. It is probably the 1973 Amin-era execution told by Festo Kivengere. I kept it as vague as the English.
