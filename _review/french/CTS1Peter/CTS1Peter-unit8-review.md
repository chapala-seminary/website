# CTS1Peter Unit 8 — French draft review

## Questions for Wayne

1. **1 Peter 3:8, the five marks (b011, MC1, Fill 1, SA1).** Segond: "soyez tous animés des mêmes pensées et des mêmes sentiments, pleins d'amour fraternel, de compassion, d'humilité". The order and words differ from NKJV ("of one mind, having compassion, love as brothers, tenderhearted, courteous"). I quoted Segond, added one sentence giving the Greek sense literally, and matched the lesson's five explanations to Segond's terms: mêmes pensées = one mind; mêmes sentiments = compassion (sympathy); amour fraternel = love as brothers; compassion = tenderhearted; humilité = courteous. MC1's correct option is "De compassion et d'humilité". Fill 1 answer **pensées** (accept: pensée, esprit).
2. **1 Peter 3:9 (b014, Fill 2).** Segond uses the imperative "bénissez, au contraire" and "injure pour injure". Fill 2 answer **bénissez** (accept: bénir, bénissant, bénédiction, bénis). The echo of 2:23 ("injurié, ne rendait point d'injures") is Segond's wording.
3. **1 Peter 3:14, "blessed" (b018, MC9, Fill 5, SA4).** Segond: "vous seriez **heureux**". I quoted it and added a note that the Greek word is the Beatitudes' word, meaning blessed by God. I also explained that "N'ayez d'eux aucune crainte" means literally "do not fear their fear", i.e. their threats (NKJV "their threats" is what MC10 and SA4 use). Fill 5 answer **heureux** (accept: bienheureux, bénis, béni, heureuses). This is two short sentences rather than one; trim if you prefer.
4. **1 Peter 3:15, "sanctify the Lord God" (b009, b020, b021, MC11, MC19, Fill 6, SA5).** Segond (critical text) reads "sanctifiez dans vos coeurs **Christ le Seigneur**", not "the Lord God". This actually matches the lesson's explanation (set Christ apart as Lord) and its heading, so I used it everywhere without comment. Fill 6 answer **coeurs** (accept: cœurs, coeur, cœur).
5. **1 Peter 3:15, "give a defense… with meekness and fear" (b004, b009, b022, b023, MC12, MC14, Fill 7, Fill 9, SA5–7).** Segond: "étant toujours prêts à **vous défendre**, avec **douceur et respect**". The lesson's "meekness and fear… or gentleness and respect" became Segond's wording plus a note that the Greek says literally "avec douceur et crainte". Fill 9 answer **respect** (accept: crainte, révérence). Fill 7 answer **apologie** (accept: apologétique, apologies).
6. **1 Peter 3:16, "ashamed" (b025, MC17, SA8).** Segond: "soient **couverts de confusion**"; "décrient votre bonne conduite". MC17's correct option is "Couverts de confusion (de honte)".
7. **1 Peter 3:10–12 vs Psalm 34.** Quoted 1 Pierre 3:10-12 as Segond renders it (Seigneur, not l'Éternel, since Peter's Greek has Kyrios). Psalm 34 itself was not quoted. "Refrain his tongue" = "préserve sa langue".
8. **SA prompts 5–9** were reworded (not changing the question) so the prompt alone does not hit 3 keywords, per convention 10.

## Problems in the English source

1. **b009/b020 and MC11, MC19, Fill 6.** The lesson quotes NKJV "sanctify the Lord God" but then explains it as setting apart *Christ* as Lord (and MC19's correct option says "Set Christ apart as Lord"). The critical text reads "Christ as Lord"; the English is internally mixed. The French follows Segond and is consistent.
2. **SA2 keywords "bless" and "blessing"** are effectively one idea (and "blessing" contains "bless"). The French uses two separate groups (bénédiction / bénir forms) so all 8 are distinct.
3. **SA1 model** says "household of faith" and "not a business or a club"; neither is in the lesson (MC2 has "business"). Minor; translated.
