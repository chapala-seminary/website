## Questions for Wayne

1. **1 Peter 3:20, "who formerly were disobedient".** LSG has "qui autrefois avaient été incrédules" (unbelieving). The lesson, MC6 and Fill 2 speak of disobedience. I quoted LSG and added in b014: "Le mot grec traduit « incrédules » signifie littéralement « désobéissants »." The lesson and exam then use "désobéissants"; MC6 adds "(« incrédules »)".
2. **1 Peter 3:21, "antitype".** LSG has "Cette eau était une figure du baptême"; there is no word "antitype". I used "figure" (Segond) and added in b022 one sentence on the Greek *antitupos* (impression matching the seal). Fill 7 answer "figure"; also accepts "antitype", "antitupos", "image", "préfiguration".
3. **1 Peter 3:21, "the answer of a good conscience".** LSG has "l'engagement d'une bonne conscience" (pledge). The lesson and SA7/SA8 say "answer". I used LSG's "engagement" throughout and added in b023: the word "can also mean « réponse » or « demande »". Please confirm.
4. **1 Peter 3:21, "the filth of the flesh".** LSG has "la purification des souillures du corps" (body, not flesh). I quoted LSG and added "Le texte grec dit littéralement « la saleté de la chair »" (b023). MC17 uses LSG's wording.
5. **1 Peter 3:21, "now saves us".** LSG has "maintenant vous sauve, vous aussi" (you, not us). The heading and MC17 quote use "vous sauve". No doctrinal effect.
6. **1 Peter 3:18, "made alive by the Spirit".** LSG has "rendu vivant quant à l'Esprit" ("with respect to the Spirit", not "by"). Quoted as LSG without comment; the lesson does not build on "by". MC4 uses LSG's words.
7. **1 Peter 3:20, "eight souls … saved through water".** LSG: "huit, furent sauvées à travers l'eau" ("personnes", not "souls"). MC7 and the lesson say "huit personnes".
8. **"Descended into hell" (Apostles' Creed).** The standard French form is "il est descendu aux enfers", where "enfers" (plural) historically means the realm of the dead, not hell. The lesson's warning still works (readers hear "l'enfer"), but I added a clause saying so. b019's "In our English Bibles a single word 'hell'…" does not fit Segond, which already distinguishes "séjour des morts" (Sheol) and "géhenne". I wrote "in everyday speech and several Bible translations" and added a parenthesis noting Segond's two terms. Please confirm.
9. **Ephesians 4:8 ("led captivity captive").** b016 only alludes to it; Segond has "il a emmené des captifs" ("led captives"). b016 quotes Segond 4:8-9 with the reference added.
10. **2 Peter 2:5, "a preacher of righteousness".** Segond: "ce prédicateur de la justice". Used in b016 and MC9, reference added.
11. **"Sheol"** rendered "Shéol" (common French evangelical spelling); Fill 5 also accepts "Schéol", "séjour des morts", "lieu des morts".
12. **MC9 option "A priest"** rendered "Un sacrificateur" per convention 4.

## Problems in the English source

1. **SA keywords.** In the English, SA1 ("christ, once, suffer, just, unjust, bring, god"), SA2 ("spirit, prison, difficult, …"), SA4 and SA5 the prompt alone reaches 3+ keywords. Following convention 10, the French groups use ideas from the lesson's answers; each French prompt now reaches at most 2 hits.
2. **SA8 keyword "regeneration" / model "baptismal regeneration".** The lesson (b023) never uses the term; it says "the act of baptism itself conveys salvation". The French model uses "régénération baptismale" and explains it; the keyword group also accepts "confère"/"transmet".
3. **SA10 model** says "all agree the passage proclaims Christ's victory" — the lesson says the passage proclaims it whatever view one takes, but not that all interpreters agree. Minor; translated as written.
4. **b012** "Peter set before us when he said Christ bore our sins" refers to 1 Peter 2:24 (an earlier unit). I quoted LSG there.
