## Questions for Wayne

1. **1 Peter 2:11, "sojourners and pilgrims" (b004, b009, b011, MC1, MC3, Fill 1, SA1, SA8).** Segond reads "comme étrangers et voyageurs sur la terre". I quoted Segond and added one sentence to b011 saying the Greek literally means "résidents étrangers et pèlerins". The lesson's own word "pilgrim" stays "pèlerin". Fill 1 answer: **voyageurs** (accept: pèlerins, voyageur, pèlerin, exilés).
2. **1 Peter 2:11, "fleshly lusts which war against the soul".** Segond: "convoitises charnelles qui font la guerre à l'âme". Fill 2 answer: **guerre** ("font la ____ à l'âme"; accept: lutte, combat, bataille).
3. **1 Peter 2:12, "the day of visitation" (b009, b014, MC5, Fill 3).** Segond: "au jour où il les visitera". I added "Le texte grec dit littéralement « au jour de la visite »" to b014, and the lesson's discussion uses "le jour de la visite". Fill 3 answer: **visite** (accept: visitation, visite de Dieu). "Gentiles" in this verse is Segond's "païens" throughout.
4. **1 Peter 2:14, "for the praise of those who do good" (b016, MC7).** Segond: "pour approuver les gens de bien". MC7's correct option is "Approuver les gens de bien".
5. **1 Peter 2:15, "the ignorance of foolish men" (b020, MC11, Fill 6).** Segond: "réduisiez au silence les hommes ignorants et insensés". I added the literal Greek sense ("l'ignorance des hommes insensés"). Fill 6 answer: **ignorants** ("les hommes ____ et insensés"; accept: ignorant, ignorance, ignares).
6. **1 Peter 2:16, "a cloak for vice… bondservants of God" (b023, MC13, MC14, Fill 7, Fill 8, SA5).** Segond: "un voile qui couvre la méchanceté… comme des serviteurs de Dieu". I added "Le texte grec dit littéralement « comme des esclaves de Dieu »". Fill 7 answer: **voile** (accept: manteau, prétexte, couverture, masque). Fill 8 answer: **serviteurs** (accept: esclaves, serviteur, esclave, servantes).
7. **1 Peter 2:17, "Love the brotherhood" (b025–b028, MC16–19, Fill 9, SA6).** Segond: "aimez les frères"; "Honor all people" is "Honorez tout le monde". I added a sentence to b026 saying the Greek word means "la fraternité", the whole family of believers. Fill 9 answer: **frères** (accept: frère, fraternité, frères et sœurs, croyants).
8. **"Fear God"** is rendered "Craignez Dieu" (Segond), explained as "une crainte pleine de respect". I avoided "peur" so it would not read as terror. Is that what you want?
9. **"Pastoral epistles" (b017, MC10).** The lesson paraphrases 1 Timothy 2:1-2 without quoting it, so I paraphrased in French too ("prier pour les rois et pour tous ceux qui exercent l'autorité").

## Problems in the English source

1. **b017 / MC9 / SA3**: the lesson says Paul taught that the authorities are "sent by God" (Romans 13). Romans 13:1 says they are "appointed" or "ordained" by God (Segond: "instituées de Dieu"). "Sent" is the wording of 1 Peter 2:14, about governors sent by the king. Translated as written.
2. **SA3** keyword "roman": the lesson never mentions Rome or the Roman state. I replaced it in French with a group for Romans 13 / Paul ("romains", "apôtre paul").
3. **SA9** keyword "gospel": the lesson uses "gospel" only once, in passing (b023). I kept a French group for it ("évangile"), but students are unlikely to use it from the lesson.
4. **SA keywords that repeat the prompt.** In SA1 (sojourn, fleshly, lust, abstain), SA3 (submit, authority, lord, anarchist), SA4 (good, silence, foolish), SA7 (honor, king, god, allegiance) and SA9 (submit, conduct, good, witness), the English prompt alone passes its own keywords. Under convention 10, all the French prompts were reworded, and each has 8 distinct groups. No French prompt reaches 3 hits; the highest is SA5, with 1 hit.
