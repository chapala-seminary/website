# CTS1Peter Unit 2 — French draft review notes

Checker re-verified every quotation against the offline LSG tool (1 Pierre 1:3-12, 1:16; Jean 3:3; 2 Corinthiens 5:19; Romains 3:24, 8:30; Actes 3:19; Philippiens 2:12; Éphésiens 2:8-9; 1 Jean 3:2): all match Segond exactly. Romains 4:5 is cited, not quoted.

## Questions for Wayne

1. **"Begotten us again" (1 Pet 1:3).** LSG says "nous a régénérés". I quoted LSG and added a note that the Greek literally means "fait naître de nouveau". The prose uses "nouvelle naissance" throughout. MC 1 says "régénérés (fait naître de nouveau)".
2. **"Incorruptible, undefiled, does not fade away" (1:4).** LSG uses verbs: "qui ne se peut ni corrompre, ni souiller, ni flétrir". I quoted that wording once in b017, then used the adjectives "incorruptible", "sans souillure" and "ne se flétrit pas" to follow the lesson's word-by-word comment. Fill 6 answer: "incorruptible". It also accepts "impérissable" and "inaltérable".
3. **"Genuineness of your faith… found to praise, honor, and glory at the revelation" (1:7).** LSG says "l'épreuve de votre foi… ait pour résultat la louange, la gloire et l'honneur, lorsque Jésus-Christ apparaîtra". I added a sentence explaining that the Greek means faith that has been tested and found genuine. MC 13 and 15 use LSG's wording.
4. **"Receiving the end of your faith" (1:9; b022, b029).** LSG says "vous obtiendrez le salut de vos âmes pour prix de votre foi" and has no word for "end". The lesson explains the word "end", so I added the literal Greek ("recevant la fin de votre foi") and kept that explanation. MC 17 says "prix (littéralement la « fin » : le but…)".
5. **"Searched and inquired carefully" and "the glories that would follow" (1:10-11).** LSG says "l'objet de leurs recherches et de leurs investigations" and "la gloire dont elles seraient suivies", in the singular. I used LSG in b026 and MC 19.
6. **"Angels desire to look into" (1:12).** LSG says "les anges désirent plonger leurs regards". I used this in b026, MC 20 and Fill 9 (answer "anges").
7. **"Be holy, for I am holy" (1:16).** LSG uses the future tense: "Vous serez saints, car je suis saint". I added "Ce futur a la force d'un commandement : soyez saints."
8. **"Work out your own salvation" (Phil 2:12).** LSG says "travaillez à votre salut". The lesson's contrast ("we cannot work out our conversion") depends on "work out". I quoted LSG, explained that the Greek means "mettre en œuvre, mener à son terme", and used "mettre en œuvre" for the contrast. MC 18 uses this too.
9. **"When He is revealed" (1 John 3:2).** LSG says "lorsque cela sera manifesté". The subject is "cela", not "he". I quoted LSG as it stands. The SA 8 model follows LSG.
10. **Security wording (b011, b017).** I translated "can never finally die" as "ne peut jamais mourir définitivement". I translated "Their security does not rest on their own strength, but on His power" literally, and I neither strengthened nor weakened it, given the open question on perseverance.
11. **Three tenses.** I rendered "I am being saved" as "je suis en train d'être sauvé", which is clear but a little heavy. Fill 2 answer: "temps". It also accepts "étapes" and "phases".

## Problems in the English source

1. **MC 3, MC 6, SA 1 (keyword "regenerat") and SA 2 (model)** call the first picture "Regeneration". The English lesson never uses that word. It calls the picture "New birth". In French, LSG's "régénérés" (1:3) makes the link, so the problem is smaller there.
2. **b015 (Conversion).** The lesson presents Saul on the Damascus road and then quotes Acts 3:19. That verse is Peter preaching in Jerusalem, not anything said to Saul. I translated it as written.
3. **SA 4 model** ends with "so the church can even rejoice in trials", which the lesson does not teach (v. 6a is not discussed). **SA 8**: the keyword "heaven" and the model's link between glorification and "the inheritance reserved in heaven" are not made in b028. Both points are minor. The French models are written from the lesson.
4. **Fill 2** introduces "like the forms of a verb". The lesson only says "three tenses". The answer can still be found.
5. All answer keys and `why` texts match their questions.

## Adaptations

- "Maternity room" became "maternité", and "highway" became "grande route". Fill 5 accepts "route", "grand-route", "autoroute" and "chemin".
- "Courtroom" became "tribunal" (Fill 4).
- SA prompts were reworded slightly so that they do not hit their own keywords (convention 10). The French models are shorter than the English ones but meet 8 out of 8 keyword groups.
