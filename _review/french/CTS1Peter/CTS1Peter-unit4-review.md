# CTS1Peter Unit 4 — French draft review

## Questions for Wayne

1. **1 Peter 2:2, "pure milk of the word" (b011, b012, MC2, MC3, Fill 1, SA1, SA8).** Segond has "le lait spirituel et pur" and does not mention "the word". I quoted Segond and added one sentence: the Greek word translated "spirituel" belongs to the same family as the word for "word", so this is the milk of the word. Fill 1 blanks **lait**.
2. **1 Peter 2:1, the list of sins (b011, b012, MC1).** Segond has "toute malice et toute ruse, la dissimulation, l'envie, et toute médisance". I used Segond's words and glossed "dissimulation" as "c'est-à-dire l'hypocrisie" in b012. Hypocrisy is the point of the lesson.
3. **1 Peter 2:5, "are being built up" (b017, b027).** Segond uses an imperative: "édifiez-vous pour former une maison spirituelle". I quoted Segond and added: "Le texte grec peut aussi se lire « vous êtes édifiés »." The lesson's point is that God builds ("divinely composed"). Is this note enough?
4. **1 Peter 2:5, "spiritual sacrifices" (b009, b019, MC9, SA3, SA9).** Segond has "victimes spirituelles". I quoted it and added that the Greek word means "sacrifices". In the lesson's own prose I use "sacrifices spirituels". MC9's correct option gives both forms.
5. **1 Peter 2:7, "He is precious" (b022, MC12, SA4).** Segond has "L'honneur est donc pour vous, qui croyez". I quoted it and added that the Greek word also means "prix, valeur", and the prose then says "pour ceux qui croient, il est précieux".
6. **1 Peter 2:6, "put to shame" (b022, b023).** Segond has "ne sera point confus". I quoted it as is. The prose explains it as "ne sera jamais couvert de honte".
7. **1 Peter 2:9, the four titles (b004, b024, b025, MC13–16, MC20, Fill 9, SA5).** Segond has "une race élue … un peuple acquis". "Chosen generation/people" became **race élue**. For "His own special people / peculiar people", I quoted "peuple acquis" and added: the Greek says literally "un peuple pour sa possession"; in older language, "un peuple *particulier*". MC16 now asks what "peuple acquis" means, with the correct option (A, unchanged) "Un peuple particulier, qui appartient à Dieu". In MC14 the option "A special people" became "Un peuple acquis". Fill 9 now blanks Segond's **acquis**, and "particulier", "spécial" and "pour sa possession" are also accepted (the prompt no longer prints "particulier"). The English blanked "peculiar".
8. **1 Peter 2:9, "proclaim the praises" (b004, b025, MC17, SA6).** Segond has "annoncer les vertus". I quoted it and added that the Greek word means God's praiseworthy perfections and mighty deeds.
9. **John 1:11–12 (b014, MC6).** Segond uses "Elle" (la Parole): "Elle est venue chez les siens…". I added one sentence: "Jean parle ici de la Parole, c'est-à-dire du Christ lui-même."
10. **Outline terms (b017, b022, MC10, SA3).** "Divinely chosen / composed / capped" became "choisies / assemblées / couronnées par Dieu". "Chief cornerstone" became "pierre angulaire", or "la principale de l'angle" (Segond, Psalm 118:22).
11. **Priests (b019, b020, Fill 6, SA9).** Following convention 4, priests are "sacrificateurs" and "the one great High Priest" is "l'unique grand Souverain Sacrificateur". Fill 6 also accepts "prêtre". "Beelzebub" became Segond's **Béelzébul**, and "Belzébuth" and "Beelzebub" are also accepted.

## Problems in the English source

1. **Short-answer prompts give away their own keywords.** SA1 has milk and word. SA2 has stone, reject and chosen. SA3 has stone, house, priest and Sparta. SA4 has cornerstone, reject and stumbl. SA5 has chosen, royal, priest, holy, nation, people and special. SA6 has darkness, light, called and marvel. SA7 has people, god and mercy. SA8 has newborn, milk, word and born. SA9 has priest, priesthood, believer and royal. Following convention 10, I reworded every French prompt (except SA10) so that it gives away no keyword. None of the French prompts hits a keyword now.
2. **Weak English keywords.** SA7 has "god", "once" and "now". SA10 has "christ" and "people". Following convention 10, I used 8 distinct ideas in each French item.
3. **Keywords and model content the lesson never teaches.** SA6 uses "witness", SA7 "belong" and "identity", and SA9 "mediator", "no human mediator" and "come boldly". The lesson says only "no human priest". I kept these points in the French models and keywords (SA9 also accepts "intermédiaire"). Consider adding a sentence to the lesson.
4. **b014 and Fill 4.** The lesson says "His own nation … charged that His power came from Beelzebub". In the Gospels this charge came from the Pharisees and scribes (Matthew 12:24; Mark 3:22), not from the whole nation. I translated it as written, with an impersonal "on".
5. **MC13–MC16** spend four questions on the order of the titles in verse 9, and MC20 and SA5 cover the same verse. That is heavy coverage for one verse. Consider replacing one of them.
