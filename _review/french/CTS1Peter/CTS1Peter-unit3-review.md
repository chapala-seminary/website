# CTS1Peter Unit 3 — French draft review

Checker verified every quotation against the offline LSG (1 Pierre 1:13-25, Lévitique 11:44, Romains 3:23, Matthieu 6:33): all exact, no corrections needed.

## Questions for Wayne

1. **1 Peter 1:16, "Be holy, for I am holy."** Segond has a future tense: "Vous serez saints, car je suis saint". Leviticus 11:44 in Segond also has "vous serez saints". In b013 I quoted Segond and added one sentence explaining that this future works as a command ("Soyez saints !"). The headings and the title use "Soyez saints". MC4 and Fill 2 quote Segond's wording.
2. **1 Peter 1:13, "at the revelation of Jesus Christ".** Segond has "lorsque Jésus-Christ apparaîtra". In b011 I added that the Greek literally says "à la révélation de Jésus-Christ". MC2 follows Segond, and its `why` gives the literal sense. SA5 accepts both "apparaîtr-" and "révél-".
3. **1 Peter 1:13, "gird up the loins of your mind".** Segond has "ceignez les reins de votre entendement". Fill 1 answer: **entendement**. It also accepts esprit, pensée(s) and intelligence.
4. **1 Peter 1:17, "in fear" / "sojourning".** Segond has "avec crainte pendant le temps de votre pèlerinage". The lesson's "sojourners" became "pèlerins", and "étranger de passage" keeps the Unit 1 link. MC17's correct option is "Avec crainte (une crainte respectueuse)".
5. **1 Peter 1:20, "foreordained".** Segond has "prédestiné". Because CTS holds to conditional election, I added in b030 that the Greek literally means "connu d'avance". The same gloss is in MC19, and SA8 accepts both forms. Please confirm.
6. **1 Peter 1:22, "through the Spirit… with a pure heart".** Segond omits "par l'Esprit" and has "de tout votre coeur". The lesson relies on "pure heart", so b032 adds that the Greek literally says "d'un cœur pur" and that some manuscripts add "par l'Esprit". Please check whether this textual note is too technical for the readers.
7. **1 Peter 1:23, "born again".** Segond has "régénérés". In b032 I added that it means "nés de nouveau". Fill 10's answer is "semence", which is Segond's word, and it also accepts "graine".
8. **1 Peter 1:18, "corruptible things".** Segond has "choses périssables". MC18 follows Segond. SA7 accepts "périssable" and "corruptible".
9. **Romans 3:23, "fall short".** Segond has "sont privés de la gloire de Dieu". MC6 and b014 quote Segond.
10. **Theological terms.** "penalty / power / presence" became "peine / puissance / présence". The three tenses use "j'ai été sauvé / je suis en train d'être sauvé / je serai sauvé". "Cleansing" became "purification" (Fill 8).

## Problems in the English source

1. **Fill 2 / MC4.** The quotation is attributed only to Leviticus 11:44. Peter's exact form is closer to Leviticus 19:2 (and 20:7). This is not an error, but it is narrow. I kept 11:44.
2. **SA1 keyword "flesh".** The lesson never uses "flesh" in this section. In French I replaced it with "excuse/compromi" (b014). I also added "caractère" in place of the duplicate holy/holiness pair.
3. **SA2 keyword "victory".** The lesson says "deliverance", not victory. I accepted "délivr" and "victoire". The groups "sin", "christ" and "remove" would match almost any answer, so I replaced them with justifi, sanctifi and glorifi (b016).
4. **SA10.** "holy" and "holiness" are the same idea. In French I merged them into one group and added "nature" and "pèlerin/crainte".
5. **Self-passing prompts.** The English prompts for SA3, SA4, SA5, SA7, SA9 and others name most of their own keywords. I reworded the French prompts so that none reaches 3 hits (maximum 2), following convention 10. All models reach 8 of 8.
6. **SA models.** I wrote the models from the French lesson and shortened them. They do not depart from the English meaning.
