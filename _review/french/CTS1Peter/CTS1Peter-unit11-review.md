## Questions for Wayne

1. **1 Peter 4:12, "fiery trial".** LSG has "la fournaise qui est au milieu de vous" (the furnace), with no word "trial" or "fiery". The title, headings, lesson, MC1-2, Fill 1, SA1 and SA10 use "fournaise" (title/heading: "la fournaise de l'épreuve"). In b011 I added: "Le texte grec dit littéralement « embrasement », c'est-à-dire une épreuve par le feu." Fill 1 answer "fournaise"; also accepts "feu", "embrasement", "épreuve ardente", "épreuve par le feu".
2. **1 Peter 4:13, "glad with exceeding joy".** LSG: "dans la joie et dans l'allégresse lorsque sa gloire apparaîtra". Used throughout (MC4, SA1, SA8).
3. **1 Peter 4:14, "blessed".** LSG has "heureux" (as in the Beatitudes). Lesson, MC5 and Fill 4 use "heureux", glossed once as "bénie de Dieu"; MC5 option reads "Heureux (bénis de Dieu)". Fill 4 also accepts "bénis", "bienheureux".
4. **1 Peter 4:14b missing in LSG.** "On their part He is blasphemed, but on your part He is glorified" is not in Segond (it follows the critical text). b015 and Fill 6 depend on it. I quoted LSG for the verse, then added one sentence: "Une partie des manuscrits grecs ajoute à la fin du verset : « De leur part, il est blasphémé, mais de votre part, il est glorifié. »" Please confirm this wording is acceptable.
5. **1 Peter 4:14, "reproached".** LSG "outragés"; used for "reproached/reproach" throughout (heading b014: "Heureux d'être outragés pour le nom de Christ").
6. **1 Peter 4:15, "busybody".** LSG: "comme s'ingérant dans les affaires d'autrui" (no noun). Lesson and Fill 7 use "indiscret"; MC7 combines both. Fill 7 accepts "fouineur", "curieux", "importun".
7. **1 Peter 4:16, "glorify God in this matter".** LSG: "à cause de ce nom", which actually suits the lesson's "in that very name" better. Used throughout.
8. **1 Peter 4:18, "where will the ungodly and the sinner appear?"** LSG: "que deviendront l'impie et le pécheur ?" Quoted LSG; in b020 added "Le texte grec du verset 18 dit littéralement : « où paraîtront l'impie et le pécheur ? »" MC14 uses LSG's wording. (The OT source, Prov 11:31, reads differently in LSG; it is not quoted.)
9. **1 Peter 4:19, "commit their souls … in doing good, as to a faithful Creator".** LSG: "remettent leurs âmes au fidèle Créateur, en faisant ce qui est bien". "Commit" = "remettre" throughout.
10. **John 15:20, "servant is not greater than his master" (b013, Fill 3).** LSG Jean 15:20 has "maître" (Jean 13:16 has "seigneur"). Fill 3 answer "maître"; accepts "seigneur" ("patron" removed by checker: not a correct answer to the quoted saying).

## Problems in the English source

1. **SA keywords.** In the English, every SA prompt reaches 3+ of its own keywords (e.g. SA1 "fiery, trial, strange, rejoice"; SA5 "commit, soul, faithful, creator"). Following convention 10, French groups use 8 ideas from the lesson; no French prompt reaches any of its own keywords.
2. **MC14 stem** says verse 18 "quotes the Old Testament" (Prov 11:31 LXX), but the lesson never says so. Translated as written.
3. **MC2 why / lesson b011** say "As we saw earlier in this letter" (1 Peter 1:7). Fine, just noting the cross-reference.
