# CTS1Peter Unit 7 — French draft review

Scripture check (checker): every quotation in the lesson and exams (1 Pierre 3:1-2, 3:3-4, 3:4, 3:5-6, 3:7, including partial quotes in MC/fill/SA) was compared with the offline LSG and matches verbatim.

## Questions for Wayne

1. **1 Peter 3:4, "the hidden person of the heart".** Segond has "la parure intérieure et cachée dans le coeur". I quoted Segond, and in b020 I added that the Greek literally says "l'homme caché du cœur". The heading (b018), MC7, Fill 5 and SA4 use "la parure cachée dans le cœur".
2. **1 Peter 3:4, "incorruptible beauty… very precious".** Segond has "la pureté incorruptible… d'un grand prix devant Dieu". The prose uses "beauté" outside the quotations. MC8 uses "d'un grand prix", and SA4 accepts both "grand prix" and "précieu-".
3. **1 Peter 3:7, "the weaker vessel".** Segond has "comme avec un sexe plus faible". The lesson explains the image of a vessel, so b026 adds "Le texte grec dit littéralement « comme avec un vase plus faible »". MC12 gives both wordings. Fill 7 answer: **sexe**. It also accepts vase, vaisseau and vases. Please confirm that "sexe" is acceptable as the graded answer for Congolese readers.
4. **1 Peter 3:7, "dwell with understanding".** Segond has "montrez… de la sagesse dans vos rapports". The correct option in MC11 is "Avec sagesse et compréhension, en les honorant". SA6 accepts sagesse, compréhension and intelligence.
5. **1 Peter 3:7, "heirs together… that your prayers may not be hindered".** Segond has "comme devant aussi hériter avec vous de la grâce de la vie… afin que rien ne vienne faire obstacle à vos prières". In the prose I use "hériter ensemble" together with the "entravées" wording. MC13 and MC14 follow Segond. Fill 9 ("prières… entravées") follows the lesson prose, not the verse.
6. **1 Peter 3:5, "who trusted in God".** Segond has "qui espéraient en Dieu". The lesson builds on *trust*, so b023 says "elles espéraient en Dieu et se confiaient en lui". I did not add a Greek note here. Tell me if you want one.
7. **1 Peter 3:6, "not afraid with any terror".** Segond has "sans vous laisser troubler par aucune crainte". MC10 and Fill 6 follow Segond.
8. **1 Peter 3:1-2, "chaste conduct accompanied by fear".** Segond has "manière de vivre chaste et réservée". No exam item depends on the words "fear" or "reverence".
9. **Andi Cook's word "subservient" (b013).** I translated it as "servile" ("une épouse devait être servile envers son mari") so the joke still works: a harsh-sounding word that her explanation softened. Please check that this tone is right for a remark about your late wife.
10. **Monica.** I used the usual French form "Monique". Fill 3 also accepts Monica and sainte Monique.

## Problems in the English source

1. **MC19 and SA9 refer to "the grandfather's account".** The lesson (b017) never mentions a grandfather. It only says "a godly woman in one congregation". I translated "le récit du grand-père" faithfully. Either the lesson should say who told the story, or the items should say "the account of the godly woman".
2. **The SA7 model** explains "the weaker vessel" as weaker "in physical strength or outward standing". The lesson (b026) explains it instead as something "precious and finely made, to be handled with care". The two can stand together, but the model brings in an idea the lesson does not teach. I translated it faithfully.
3. **Fill 7 `accept_en` includes "partner"** ("the weaker partner"). That is not the wording of the verse or the lesson. I did not carry it over.
4. **SA prompts.** Several English prompts contain their own keywords: SA1 (wives/submiss/word/won/unbeliev), SA2 (voluntary/nag/argue/faith), SA3 (monica/augustine/witness), SA4 (outward/hidden/heart), SA5 (sarah/holy/women), SA6 (husband), SA7 (heir/grace/together/submiss), SA8 (husband/christ/pattern), SA9 (grandfather/godly/life/argue). I reworded the French prompts so that none passes its own keywords (convention 10).
5. All MC answer keys are correct. Each `why` matches its own question.
