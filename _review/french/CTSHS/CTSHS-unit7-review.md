# CTSHS Unit 7 — French draft review

## Questions for Wayne

1. **"Quench" (b007, MC3, MC4, Fill 1, SA1).** Segond's 1 Thessalonians 5:19 reads "N'éteignez pas l'Esprit". "Éteindre" fits the fire image well, so no extra note was needed. Fill 1 answers **éteignez** and also accepts "éteindre", "éteins" and "étouffez".
2. **1 Thessalonians 5:16 (b009, MC6).** Segond has "Soyez toujours joyeux" ("be always joyful"), not "Rejoice always". The meaning is the same. I quoted Segond, including in the MC6 stem.
3. **1 Thessalonians 5:21 (b009, MC7, Fill 4).** Segond has "examinez toutes choses; retenez ce qui est bon". The verse begins with "Mais", which I left out of the quotation. Fill 4 answers **bon** and accepts only "bien" ("bonne" and "juste" were removed as wrong).
4. **2 Corinthians 4:13 (b013).** Segond writes "le même esprit de foi" with a lowercase "esprit". The lesson only cites the reference and does not quote the verse, so the prose says "l'Esprit de foi". Hebrews 10:29 ("l'Esprit de la grâce") and Romans 1:4 ("l'Esprit de sainteté") match the lesson.
5. **"Backslider" (b013, Fill 7).** French has no exact equivalent. I used "un rétrograde", the word French-speaking evangelicals commonly use, which also appears in another French draft. Fill 7 also accepts "chrétien rétrograde", "égaré" and "chrétien égaré". Please confirm that this term is understood in Kinshasa.
6. **Fill 9, "Satan".** Segond's Matthew 12:24 has "Béelzébul, prince des démons". The answer is **Satan**, as in the lesson. The fill-in also accepts "Béelzébul", "Belzébuth", "diable" and "prince des démons".
7. **Spiritual gifts.** "Do not despise prophecies" is quoted from Segond ("Ne méprisez pas les prophéties"). Like the English, the lesson applies it to preaching. I added nothing about continuationism or cessationism. (Checker: confirmed the French is exactly as balanced as the English here.)
8. **Perseverance (b011, MC11, SA4).** The French keeps the English's neutral wording ("n'est pas de trancher tous les désaccords…"). It does not affirm or deny eternal security.

## Problems in the English source

1. Answer keys: scrambled in English; corrected in pull request #83.
2. **MC14, option 0, says "one unconfessed sin".** The lesson's wording is "one act of disobedience that we will not turn over to God". The meaning is close. I translated it as written.
3. **The SA prompts give away their own keywords in English** (e.g. SA1 quench/spirit, SA3 griev/spirit/love/word, SA4 seal/redempt/griev). Following convention 10, the French uses 8 distinct ideas, and no French prompt passes on its own.
4. **SA4 keywords "remain" and "cast"** come from an older eternal-security wording. The current model answer does not use them. The French groups follow the current, neutral lesson.

## Adaptations

- b007: I added "L'évangéliste américain" before Dwight L. Moody, because Congolese readers may not know him. "So cold you could skate down the aisles" became "si froide qu'on aurait pu glisser sur la glace dans l'allée centrale".
- b007: "Lying in state" became "exposé pour la veillée", using the familiar Congolese practice of the funeral wake. "An empty grate" became "un foyer de cendres froides".
- b019: I kept the railroad-crossing illustration ("passage à niveau"). It is understandable in Kinshasa, but Wayne could replace it with a busy road or a market if he prefers.
