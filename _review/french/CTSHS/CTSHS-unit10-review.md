# CTSHS Unit 10 — French draft review

## Questions for Wayne

1. **The lesson quotes the KJV, not the NKJV.** Examples: "Holy Ghost", "to profit withal", "charity never faileth", "dividing to every man severally". I quoted Segond 1910 everywhere, following the brief. The points below list only the places where the wording matters.
2. **1 Corinthians 13:8, "charity" (b013, MC16, Fill 6, SA6).** Segond also has "charité" ("La charité ne périt jamais"), so the quotation fits the lesson's wording. I added one sentence saying that the Greek word translated "charité" is agapè, that is, love (convention 1). Fill 6 answers **charité** and also accepts "amour", "agapè" and "agape".
3. **1 Corinthiens 12:31, "a more excellent way" (b013, Fill 7).** Segond has "une voie par excellence". Fill 7 answers **excellence** and also accepts "excellente" and "plus excellente".
4. **1 Corinthiens 12:7, "to profit withal" (b009, MC7, SA4).** Segond has "pour l'utilité commune". I used "utilité commune" in the lesson and in MC7. SA4 accepts "utilit" or "profit".
5. **Romans 12:8, "with simplicity / diligence / cheerfulness" (b015, SA7).** Segond has "avec libéralité / avec zèle / avec joie", and "celui qui préside" for "he that ruleth". I quoted Segond and added one sentence: the Greek word behind "libéralité" means literally "simplicity", that is, giving from a heart without calculation.
6. **Barnabas, "son of encouragement" (b015, MC17).** Segond (Actes 4:36) has "fils d'exhortation". I used it, which also links him to the exhortation gift in the lesson. I added the reference Actes 4:36 in b015.
7. **Éphésiens 4:11, the keyword "some" (b021, b022).** French has no single word for this. Segond has "les uns… les autres". I made the key words *les uns… les autres*, as the Spanish edition does with *a unos… a otros*. Segond's "pasteurs et docteurs": in b022, I glossed "docteurs" once as "c'est-à-dire enseignants", since today "docteur" usually means a physician.
8. **Spiritual gifts today (b017, MC19, SA8).** I translated this as neutrally as the English. Sincere believers differ. The churches extend charity and do not divide over "ce dont l'Écriture ne fait pas une condition de communion". The prose does not lean toward either the cessationist or the continuationist view. The 1 Corinthiens 13:8 wording ("les prophéties prendront fin… la connaissance disparaîtra") speaks only of "when we see God face to face", as in the English.
9. **"The third Person of the one Godhead" (b019).** I rendered it as "la troisième Personne de la Divinité unique".
10. **Pulpit heading (b020).** I rendered it as "De la chaire du Dr Cook".
