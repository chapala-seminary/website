# CTSHS Unit 3 — French draft review notes

## Questions for Wayne

1. **Psalm 139:7, "flee from Your presence".** LSG has "loin de ta face". In b011 I quoted LSG and added "La « face » de Dieu, c'est ici sa présence." MC10 uses LSG. Fill 5 answer is "face"; "présence" and "visage" are also accepted.
2. **Hebrews 9:14, "the eternal Spirit".** LSG has "par un esprit éternel" (lowercase, indefinite). The lesson and MC11/Fill 6 depend on this being the Holy Spirit. I quoted LSG and added in b011: "il s'agit ici de l'Esprit de Dieu". MC11 now quotes "un esprit éternel". Please confirm this is acceptable, since the French reader may see LSG's lowercase as weakening the proof.
3. **Psalm 104:30, "You send forth Your Spirit".** LSG has "Tu envoies ton souffle" — no "Esprit". Per convention 1, b013 now adds: "Le texte hébreu dit littéralement « ton Esprit » : le même mot hébreu signifie « souffle » et « Esprit »." MC13 quotes LSG.
4. **Job 33:4.** LSG writes "L'esprit de Dieu" (lowercase). Quoted verbatim. Fill 7 "souffle" matches LSG exactly.
5. **2 Peter 1:21, "moved by the Holy Spirit".** LSG: "c'est poussés par le Saint-Esprit que des hommes ont parlé de la part de Dieu" (no "holy men of God"). I quoted the whole verse in b013 because it also covers "did not speak on their own". MC14 and Fill 8 use "poussés"; Fill 8 also accepts "poussé", "mus", "portés", "conduits", "inspirés".
6. **2 Timothy 3:16.** Per convention 3: "inspirée de Dieu", littéralement « soufflée par Dieu ». No fill-in depends on it in this unit.
7. **Acts 5:3.** LSG's verse continues "…et que tu aies retenu une partie du prix du champ?". I quoted it only up to "au Saint-Esprit", as the English does.
8. **Luke 1:35.** "the Highest" = LSG "Très-Haut" (Fill 4 answer; also accepts "Dieu Très-Haut", "Plus Haut").
9. **"Godhead" vs "deity".** "Godhead" → "Divinité" (capitalized, = the one divine Being); "deity" → "divinité" (lowercase, = being God). Please have the French reader confirm the capital/lowercase distinction is clear enough, or whether "la divinité de l'Esprit" should become "la pleine divinité".
10. **Spiritual gifts.** The unit mentions "gift" only as "not merely a gift or a power" (b009, b019), never spiritual gifts; checked that the French adds nothing on cessationism/continuationism.

## Problems in the English source

1. **MC8, MC9, MC10:** only the correct option has an explanatory gloss ("omniscience — He is all-knowing", etc.), which gives the answer away. Translated as is.
2. **SA keywords:** the English prompts hit their own keywords in SA2 (4), SA3 (4), SA5 (3), SA7 (3), SA8 (4) and SA9 (3), mostly through "god", "spirit", "deity", "prove". Under convention 10, I replaced these with 8 distinct lesson ideas per question. Every French prompt now has at most 1 hit; every French model hits 6–8.
3. **b019** says the Spirit "is worshiped as God dwelling in His temple"; section 3 argues from indwelling, not worship. Minor; translated faithfully.
