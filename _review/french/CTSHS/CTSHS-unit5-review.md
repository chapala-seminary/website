# CTSHS Unit 5 — French draft review

## Questions for Wayne

1. **Luke 1:35, "that Holy One" (b005, MC1).** Segond has "le saint enfant qui naîtra de toi sera appelé Fils de Dieu". The lesson builds on the phrase "that Holy One", so I quoted Segond's "le saint enfant" and added: "Le texte grec parle littéralement de « l'être saint qui naîtra »." MC1's correct option reads "le saint enfant, appelé Fils de Dieu".
2. **Luke 24:49, "the city of Jerusalem" (b015, Fill 6, MC13).** Segond has only "restez dans la ville" (a Greek textual difference), and "the Promise of My Father" becomes "ce que mon Père a promis". I quoted Segond and then wrote "Cette ville, c'était Jérusalem (voir Actes 1:4)" so that Fill 6 can be answered from the lesson. The Fill 6 prompt is a paraphrase that cites Luke 24:49 together with Acts 1:4.
3. **Hebrews 9:14, "through the eternal Spirit" (b009).** Segond has "par un esprit éternel" (lowercase, indefinite). That weakens the lesson's point. I quoted Segond and added one sentence (convention 1): the Greek can also be read "par l'Esprit éternel", that is, the Holy Spirit.
4. **"Helper" (John 14:16, 14:26, 16:7; b003, b010, b011, b013, MC10, MC11, Fill 4, SA4, SA5).** Segond has "consolateur" (lowercase). As in earlier units: quotations use Segond's "consolateur", lesson prose uses "le Consolateur", and b011 adds one sentence explaining "Paraclet" (the one called alongside to help).
5. **Acts 2:13, "new wine" (b019, MC19, SA8).** Segond has "vin doux". Used throughout; SA8 also accepts "du vin"/"de vin".
6. **Fill 8, Passover.** The answer is **Pâque**. Also accepted: "Pâques", "fête de la Pâque", "Pâque juive", "Pessah". Please confirm that accepting "Pâques" (used loosely by many French speakers for both feasts) is acceptable.
7. **Fill 1.** The answer is **Oint**; "Messie" (same meaning) is also accepted. Please confirm.

## Problems in the English source

1. **b017 reference.** "And they were all amazed" is from Acts 2:7 or 2:12, not 2:11. The second quotation is from 2:11. In French I cited (Actes 2:11-12) and quoted Segond 2:12 and 2:11.
2. **SA prompts that pass their own keywords.** The SA9 prompt hits joel, pentecost and fulfil. The SA10 prompt hits promis, spirit and "give" (inside "given"). Following convention 10, the French groups use 8 distinct ideas, and no French prompt hits more than 1 group.
3. **SA9 keyword "all"** is only 3 letters and matches almost any answer. The French uses other groups.
4. **MC20 / b019.** The lesson says "about three thousand were saved". Acts 2:41 says they "received his word", "were baptized" and "were added". The meaning is the same, so I translated it as written.
