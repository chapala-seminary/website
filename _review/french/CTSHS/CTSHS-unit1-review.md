# CTSHS Unit 1 — French draft review notes

## Questions for Wayne

1. **Deuteronomy 6:4** (b009, MC5): NKJV "The LORD our God, the LORD is one"; Segond has "l'Éternel, notre Dieu, est le seul Éternel" ("the only LORD"). The lesson uses the verse for God's oneness. I quoted Segond and added: "Le texte hébreu dit littéralement : « l'Éternel est un »." MC5 quotes Segond.
2. **John 14:16** (b015, MC12, fill 8, SA7): NKJV "another Helper"; Segond "un autre consolateur" (lowercase). The lesson's point is about "another", which Segond keeps, so nothing is lost. When the lesson's own prose uses the title, I wrote "le Consolateur" with a capital, the way the English capitalizes "Helper". Please confirm.
3. **Genesis 1:2** (b009, MC7, fill 5): Segond writes "l'esprit de Dieu" (lowercase) and "se mouvait" where the NKJV has "hovering". The lesson uses this verse to show that the Spirit was present from the beginning. I quoted Segond exactly, lowercase included. Should we add a note that this means the Holy Spirit?
4. **Matthew 3:16–17** (b011): Segond "il vit l'Esprit de Dieu descendre comme une colombe et venir sur lui" and "en qui j'ai mis toute mon affection" ("well pleased"). The sentence around the quote was adjusted so the wording fits.
5. Wording choices: I used "Divinité" for "Godhead", "Divinité trinitaire" for "triune Godhead", and "critère" for "test" (section 7, SA9). The sentence "He is sent by the Father and by the Son" (b015) is translated exactly, with nothing added for or against the *filioque*.
6. b005 lists "Charismatics, cultists, and the older denominations" together. I translated it faithfully ("les charismatiques, les sectes et les Églises plus anciennes"). Congolese readers include many charismatics and Pentecostals, so they may read this list as putting charismatics next to the sects. Should it be softened, for example "les charismatiques, les Églises plus anciennes, et même les sectes"? I left it as written. Nothing else in this unit takes a side between cessationists and continuationists. MC17's distractor "produire avant tout des signes et des prodiges" keeps the English's "above all".

## Problems in the English source

1. **MC8**: the lesson names only "polytheism". The option says "polytheism (tritheism)", but the `why` says "tritheism", a term the lesson never teaches. This is minor. I translated it as is.
2. **MC4**: the stem says "Through much of church history". The lesson only says "For a long time it seemed…" and speaks of recent decades. This slightly overstates the lesson.
3. **MC14 and SA10**: both speak of "the incarnate Son, who was in one place at a time". The lesson puts it conditionally ("Had the Lord remained bodily…"). The SA10 model also adds "took a true human body", which the lesson does not state. This is consistent with the lesson, just not taught in those words.
4. **SA9**: the English keyword "point" does not appear in the English model answer. The French groups were chosen so that the French model hits 7 of the 8 groups.

## French glossary used

- Holy Spirit → Saint-Esprit (l'Esprit)
- Godhead → la Divinité
- Trinity / triune → la Trinité / trinitaire
- Person (of the Trinity) → Personne (capitalized, as in the English)
- attributes / historical events / activities → attributs / événements historiques / activités
- Helper → consolateur (Segond), le Consolateur in lesson prose
- testify of → rendre témoignage de
- glorify → glorifier
- polytheism / tritheism → polythéisme / trithéisme
- masks / roles (modalism) → masques / rôles
- test (of claims) → critère
- yield to Him → se soumettre à lui
- present age → notre époque
