# CTSHS Unit 4 — French draft review

## Questions for Wayne

1. **Lowercase "esprit" in Segond (whole unit).** Segond often writes "l'esprit de l'Éternel" or "l'esprit de Dieu" in lowercase (Genesis 1:2, Judges, 1 Samuel, Psalm 51:11 "ton esprit saint", Ezekiel, Joel). I quoted Segond exactly. In b005 I added one sentence telling the student that Segond often writes "esprit" in lowercase even where the context shows it is the Spirit of God. The lesson's own prose uses "l'Esprit".
2. **Psalm 104:30, Job 26:13, Psalm 33:6 (b005).** Segond has "souffle" (breath), not "Spirit", in Psalm 104:30 ("Tu envoies ton souffle"). For Job 26:13, Segond has "Son souffle donne au ciel la sérénité", where the NKJV has "By His Spirit He adorned the heavens". I quoted Segond and added: the Hebrew word for "souffle" (rouah) is also the word for the Spirit, and in Job 26:13 the Hebrew says literally that by his breath the heavens become beautiful.
3. **"Came upon" verbs (b009, b011, MC6, MC10, Fill 4, Fill 5).** Segond varies the verb. Judges 6:34 has "Gédéon fut revêtu de l'esprit". Judges 14:6 ("came mightily") has "L'esprit de l'Éternel saisit Samson". 1 Samuel 10:10 and 16:13 have "saisit". Judges 3:10 and 11:29 have "fut sur". I quoted Segond each time. The prose keeps the lesson's pattern ("l'Esprit venait sur"). For Othniel, I wrote "Dieu suscita Othniel, et « L'esprit de l'Éternel fut sur lui »" (from Judges 3:9–10), so the quotation fits the sentence. MC6's stem now quotes "saisit".
4. **2 Peter 1:21 (b013, MC13, SA5).** Segond has "c'est poussés par le Saint-Esprit que des hommes ont parlé de la part de Dieu". There is no "holy men" (this is a Greek textual difference). I quoted Segond in the lesson and in MC13.
5. **Ezekiel 36:27, "statutes" (b017, MC17, SA8).** Segond has "je ferai en sorte que vous suiviez mes ordonnances". I quoted it as written. SA8 accepts "ordonnanc", "statut" and "lois".
6. **Isaiah 42:1 and 61:1 (b019).** Segond has "Il annoncera la justice aux nations" (NKJV "Gentiles") and "porter de bonnes nouvelles aux malheureux" (NKJV "poor"). I quoted Segond as written.
7. **Ezekiel 37, "dry bones" (b017, MC18, Fill 9, SA8).** Segond's chapter uses "ossements" ("Ossements desséchés", 37:4). I wrote "la vallée des ossements desséchés". Fill 9 answers **ossements** and also accepts "os" and "ossement".
8. **Joel 2:28 (b017, Fill 8, SA7).** Segond has "je répandrai mon esprit sur toute chair". Fill 8 answers **chair** and accepts "personne" and "humanité" (the sense of the English alternate "people"). The checker removed "gens" and "hommes", which do not fit the singular "toute ____".
9. **Names (Fill 1, 3, 4, 6).** I used Segond's spellings: Betsaleel, Zorobabel, Gédéon, Jephthé, Saül. The fill-ins also accept Bezalel, Zerubbabel, Gideon and Jerubbaal (Segond, Judges 6:32).
10. **Fill 2, "seventy".** The answer is **soixante-dix** (Segond). I also accept "70" and "septante", the form many Congolese speakers use. Keep "septante" as accepted only? (Checker: left as is.)
11. **Spiritual gifts.** This unit does not discuss whether gifts continue or have ceased. No wording on cessationism or continuationism was added. The "prophesy" wording of Joel 2:28 is translated as written.

## Problems in the English source

1. **SA prompts give away their own keywords.** For example, SA8 hits ezekiel, spirit, inward and promis (4 groups, which passes on its own). SA2 hits spirit, equip and servic. SA6 hits spirit, covenant and measur. Most items have "spirit" as a keyword, and every prompt contains that word. Following convention 10, the French groups use 8 distinct ideas, and no French prompt hits any group.
2. **SA9 English model misses its own "christ" keyword.** The model never names Christ (it still passes with 7 hits). The French model ends with "Cette promesse s'est accomplie en Christ", which the lesson teaches (b019).
3. **SA1 keyword "job"** is only 3 letters. In French I used "job 3", "job 2" and "tout puissant".
4. **b017 / SA7.** "Menservants and maidservants" comes from Joel 2:29, not from 2:28, which is the verse cited. I translated it as written.
