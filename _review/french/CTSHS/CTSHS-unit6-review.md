# CTSHS Unit 6 — French draft review

## Questions for Wayne

1. **Titus 3:5, "washing of regeneration" (b007, MC2, Fill 1, SA1).** Segond has "par le baptême de la régénération et le renouvellement du Saint-Esprit". The lesson says this birth "is not the work of water baptism", so Segond's word "baptême" works against the lesson's point. I quoted Segond exactly. Following convention 1, I added one sentence: the Greek word rendered "baptême" literally means "bain" or "lavage", so Paul is speaking of a cleansing. Fill 1 blanks "régénération" and accepts "nouvelle naissance", "renaissance" and "naissance nouvelle".
2. **Ephesians 1:13-14, "guarantee" (b011, MC4, Fill 5, SA3).** Segond has "lequel est un gage de notre héritage". I added one sentence: the Greek word means a down payment given as a guarantee of full payment. Fill 5 answers **gage** and accepts "garantie", "acompte" and "arrhes". Segond's wording for 1:13 ("scellés du Saint-Esprit qui avait été promis") stands in for "the Holy Spirit of promise".
3. **1 Corinthians 12:13, "by one Spirit" (b015, MC8, Fill 8, SA5).** Segond has "baptisés dans un seul Esprit, pour former un seul corps". The lesson stresses that the Spirit is the one who baptizes. I quoted Segond and added: the Greek can be read "in" or "by" one Spirit, and it is the one Spirit who carries out this baptism. Fill 8 still blanks **corps**.
4. **Ephesians 5:18 (b017, MC10, Fill 9).** Segond has "Ne vous enivrez pas de vin: c'est de la débauche." It has no "in which is dissipation" clause, but the meaning is the same. I used it as written.
5. **Hannah / Eli (b017, MC12, SA7).** I used Segond's names, "Anne" and "Eli". The SA7 keyword group accepts "hannah", and also "samuel" for the reference.
6. **"Filling" (b003, b017, MC11, SA6).** "Remplissage" sounds mechanical in French, so the checker replaced it with "le fait d'être rempli de l'Esprit" (from Segond's "remplis de l'Esprit", Eph 5:18). The summary line in b003 keeps "la plénitude de l'Esprit". The SA keywords accept both "rempli" and "plénitude".
7. **Spiritual gifts (b019, MC16-18, SA8).** The paragraph keeps the same balance as the English (confirmed by the checker). The cessationist view and the continuationist view each get one sentence, in the same order and at similar length. For "sign-gifts" I wrote "dons de signes", and for "test of fellowship" I wrote "condition de la communion fraternelle". Nothing was added or softened.
8. **Assurance and sealing (b011, b013).** "Kept by God" is "gardés par Dieu", and "bring us safely home" is "nous conduira sains et saufs jusque chez lui". Both are the same strength as the English. I added nothing on perseverance in either direction.

## Problems in the English source

1. **MC15 and SA2 (indwelling as "permanent", "unlike the OT").** This lesson only says "not for a season but forever" (b005). The contrast with the Old Testament comes from units 4 and 5 (John 14:16). The items are fair for the course, but this unit does not teach them directly. SA2 is still graded on 7 French groups, with "permanen" as only one of them.
2. **MC7 `why`** cites Galatians 4:6, which the lesson does not quote. The citation is correct, so I translated it as written.
3. **SA prompts reuse their own keywords.** For example, "spirit" is in every prompt, and SA6's prompt contains "fill", "spirit" and "baptiz". In line with convention 10, the French groups are 8 distinct ideas. No French prompt hits more than 1 group.
4. **SA8 prompt** says "sign-gifts", which is one of its keywords. The French prompt says "les langues et les dons de miracles" so it does not give away the "signes" group. The meaning is unchanged.

## French glossary used

- new birth / born again → nouvelle naissance / naître de nouveau
- regeneration → régénération
- indwelling → présence intérieure / l'Esprit qui habite en nous
- seal / signet ring → sceau, sceller / anneau à cacheter
- guarantee (of inheritance) → gage (Segond)
- Spirit of adoption → Esprit d'adoption
- bears witness → rend témoignage (Segond)
- baptism with the Spirit → baptême de l'Esprit
- be filled with the Spirit / filling → être rempli de l'Esprit / le fait d'être rempli (plénitude in b003)
- second blessing → seconde bénédiction
- sign-gifts / miraculous gifts → dons de signes / dons miraculeux
- test of fellowship → condition de la communion fraternelle
- tests (of the Spirit's work) → critères

- Scripture: the checker looked up every quotation in Segond 1910 (Jean 3:5-7; Tite 3:5; Romains 8:9, 8:15-16; 1 Corinthiens 6:19, 12:13, 12:30; Éphésiens 1:13-14, 4:30, 5:18). All are verbatim. The only fixes were Segond's spacing in "Abba! Père!" (MC7, SA4, SA10) and "langues?" (MC17).
