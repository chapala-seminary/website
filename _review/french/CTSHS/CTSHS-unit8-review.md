# CTSHS Unit 8 — French draft review

## Questions for Wayne

1. **"Helper" (b005, b024, MC1, Fill 1).** Segond's word for the Paraclete is "consolateur" (John 14–16). As in Units 1–2, I wrote "le Consolateur (en grec, le Paraclet)" at first mention. In other places I wrote "celui qui nous aide". Fill 1 answers **Consolateur** and accepts Paraclet, Aide, Avocat and Défenseur.
2. **Romans 8:26, "groanings" (b007, b011, MC2, Fill 2, SA3, SA10).** Segond has "intercède par des soupirs inexprimables" (sighs). I quoted Segond and added one sentence: the Greek word means deep groanings. Fill 2 answers **soupirs** and accepts "gémissements".
3. **Romans 8:28, "work together" (b013, b015, MC9, MC12, Fill 4, SA4–6).** Segond has "toutes choses concourent au bien … appelés selon son dessein". The lesson's third point leans on the words "work together". I quoted "concourent" and added that the Greek verb literally means "travaillent ensemble". Fill 5 answers **dessein** and accepts "propos", "plan", "projet" and "but".
4. **2 Corinthians 12:7, "thorn" (b009, MC6, Fill 3).** Segond has "écharde". Fill 3 answers **écharde** and accepts "épine" and "aiguillon".
5. **Leviticus 11:44 (b021, b022).** The English quotes KJV wording ("be ye holy"). Segond has the future tense: "vous serez saints, car je suis saint". I quoted Segond and added that in Hebrew this has the force of a command. The heading keeps "Soyez saints" as the lesson's summary of the command (Segond itself uses "Soyez saints" in Leviticus 19:2 and 1 Peter 1:15). Ephesians 5:18 is quoted from Segond ("Soyez, au contraire, remplis de l'Esprit").
6. **b019, "He will never leave the work unfinished … will surely complete".** Because perseverance is an open question at CTS, I translated this at exactly the English strength ("il ne laissera jamais l'ouvrage inachevé … il l'achèvera sûrement"). I did not add or soften anything.
7. **Spiritual gifts.** This unit does not discuss cessationism or continuationism. Nothing was added on either view.

## Problems in the English source

1. Answer keys: scrambled in English; corrected in pull request #83.
2. **MC1.** The stem says the Spirit is "called the Helper" in Romans 8. That title comes from John 14–16. Romans 8:26 only says that the Spirit "helps". The French stem says "présenté comme" instead.
3. **MC15.** The stem says "the conclusion Paul reaches". The lesson (b017) presents this as our conclusion, not Paul's. I translated it as written.
