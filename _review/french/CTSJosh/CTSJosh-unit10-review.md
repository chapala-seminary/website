# CTSJosh Unit 10 ("Choose This Day"): French review notes

All Scripture was checked against the offline LSG 1910 tool by an independent checker. Every quotation matches LSG; one partial quote in b064 was corrected (see Checker's changes).

## Questions for Wayne

1. **Josh 24:15 (b005, b007, b027, b038, SA3).** NKJV has "if it seems evil to you." LSG has "si vous ne trouvez pas bon de servir l'Éternel." Section 5 ("rejectable") still works with this wording. LSG has no "But" before "Moi et ma maison."
2. **Josh 24:14, "Now therefore" (b024, MC6).** LSG has a single word, "Maintenant." In b024 I changed "Two words on which…" to "Un seul mot…". The MC6 stem now reads "Quel mot de transition… (LSG)," and the correct option is "« Maintenant »." The answer index is unchanged.
3. **Josh 24:14, "in sincerity and in truth" (b024, b025, Fill 4).** LSG has "avec intégrité et fidélité." The Fill 4 answer is "fidélité," and it also accepts "vérité" and "loyauté." In b025, "Sincerity is not enough" became "L'intégrité ne suffit pas."
4. **Josh 23:10 (b018, Fill 1).** The NKJV/KJV quote is a future promise ("shall chase… fighteth"). LSG uses past tenses: "Un seul d'entre vous en poursuivait mille; car l'Éternel… combattait pour vous." I changed the lead-in so that Joshua "reminds" them instead of promising. The "promise that runs both directions" framing is now weaker.
5. **Josh 23:14 (b009, b064).** "Ready to go the way of all the earth" became LSG's "je m'en vais… par le chemin de toute la terre."
6. **Josh 21:45 (b050, b070).** LSG has "aucune ne resta sans effet: toutes s'accomplirent" (none remained without effect). It means the same as "There failed not ought."
7. **Oak / terebinth (b021, b039, b073, b076).** For Gen 35:4, LSG has "le térébinthe qui est près de Sichem," so in b021 I wrote "près de Sichem… sous le térébinthe." For Josh 24:26 LSG has "le chêne," so the Shechem stone scenes keep "chêne."
8. **"Wholly followed the LORD" (b071, b074, b078, MC20).** LSG renders it "pleinement suivi la voie de l'Éternel" (Josh 14:14; Num 32:12). I used that wording throughout. Note that LSG says this of Caleb (14:14) and of Caleb and Joshua together (Num 32:12); the lesson applies it to Joshua alone as an epitaph.
9. **Josh 5:13–15 (b059).** LSG has "chef de l'armée de l'Éternel," "mon seigneur" (lowercase), and "Ôte tes souliers." I translated the lesson's "Captain/Commander" as "le Chef" (capitalized) in b064, b074 and b075. Please confirm the capital is right, since LSG keeps it lowercase.
10. **Josh 24:32, "an hundred pieces of silver" (b067, b068).** LSG has "cent kesita." The block quote keeps it, and in b068 I glossed it as "cent pièces d'argent (« cent kesita »)."
11. **Place names in LSG spelling.** I used Sichem (Shechem), Guilgal (Gilgal), Silo (Shiloh), Thimnath-Sérach (Timnath-serah), Kadès-Barnéa, Gueschuriens, Baal-Peor and Acor. For Eleazar's burial, the lesson says "Gibeah," but LSG names the place "Guibeath-Phinées," and I used the LSG name. Fill 3 accepts Shechem, Sychem and Siquem. Fill 9 accepts Gilgal.
12. **"Twelve men to circle Jericho carrying the ark" (b052).** I translated this as written. See Problems 4.
13. **Book titles.** I kept Lockyer's *All the Promises of the Bible* in English and added a French gloss. Chapman's *Present Day Parables* stays in English. Neither book has a French edition that I know of.
14. **SA prompts reworded so the prompt alone does not pass.** In SA7 and SA10 the literal French prompts already hit about 3 keyword groups (tribu/bénédiction/manqu/orient, and sept/foi/motif/doctrin/résum/cours). I rephrased them without changing their meaning.
15. **b074, "the Gibeonite manslayer."** I wrote "le meurtrier involontaire de Gabaon." (Checker: Unit 9 FR uses "meurtrier involontaire" for the manslayer and "petit garçon des rues de Londres" for the waif, so the terms match. Unit 9 does not call the manslayer a Gibeonite; see Problems 12.)

## Problems in the English source

1. **MC12: wrong answer key.** The key is index 2 ("Faith builds altars"). The lesson's first motif is "Faith believes God keeps His promises," which is index 0. I kept the key as it is.
2. **b021 vs b068 (and MC4 option B, the keyed answer).** b021 says Joseph's bones "were waiting in the ground" at Shechem. b068 says Israel carried them for 400 years and only now buried them. MC4's correct option repeats the b021 claim.
3. **b011.** It says "four lines that mirror Moses' three-fold farewell," but only three farewells are listed.
4. **b052.** "Twelve men… circle Jericho carrying the ark" is wrong. In Josh 6 priests carry the ark and seven priests blow trumpets. No twelve men are mentioned.
5. **b009.** It says "a verse later," but the quote is from 23:14. "Carried for forty-five years" is also doubtful.
6. **b076 timeline.** It places Kadesh-barnea 45 years before the Shechem speech. Josh 14:10 makes 45 years the time from Kadesh to Caleb's request at age 85, not to Joshua's farewell. Kadesh to the Jordan is about 38 years, which also clashes with "twenty-five years after the Jordan."
7. **b068.** "Seven years of allotment" has no basis in the text. Jacob's purchase "four hundred years earlier" is also loose.
8. **SA5 model.** It does not match the lesson. Worship is tied to the Captain and holy ground, but the lesson ties it to circumcision at Gilgal. The seventh motif is given as "Ai attacked without asking God," but the lesson says Gibeon. Waiting is linked to "made war a long time," but the lesson uses the three days at the Jordan.
9. **SA10 model.** It maps the motifs onto justification, sanctification, endurance and consecration. The lesson never teaches this.
10. **SA2 model.** It mentions the covenant renewal "at Ebal and Gerizim," which this lesson does not teach.
11. **SA7 model.** "Cut off from the central place of worship" and "carried away" go beyond the lesson.
12. **b074.** It refers to "the London waif" and "the Gibeonite manslayer" without introducing them in this unit. They probably come from Unit 9.

## Checker's changes

- Scripture: all quotations verified against LSG 1910 (Josh 1:3; 5:13-15; 21:45; 23:1, 3, 7, 10, 13, 14; 24:2-18, 32; Gen 35:4; Jas 1:5; Josh 14:14 / Num 32:12 wording). One fix: b064 echo of 23:14 lacked "maintenant" ("Je m'en vais maintenant par le chemin de toute la terre").
- b074 and MC20 option B: "il a pleinement suivi" changed to "il avait pleinement suivi", matching LSG's pluperfect in Josh 14:14; MC20's other options put in the same tense so the key is not given away by grammar. Answer index unchanged.
- SA6: keyword group ["encadr", "cadre"] reduced to "encadr" ("cadre" also matches "dans le cadre de", a common phrase).
- Validator warnings reviewed and left: b050 (English book title kept on purpose), Fill 8 ("pied/pieds" is the only correct form), Fill 10 ("os" matches inside "Josué"; false alarm).
- Everything else (meaning, theology, MC keys, fill alternates, SA keyword hits) checked and left as drafted.
