## Problems in the English source

1. **b030 / sa8 model** — says Israel entered the land "on the precise calendar day they had begun the journey". The 10th of the first month was when the lamb was set apart (Exode 12:3); the departure was on the 15th (Nombres 33:3). Translated faithfully. MC9 and fill 8 are correct (they refer to the Passover instructions).
2. **b008** — "a mile wide and a hundred and fifty feet deep" at flood stage is a very high estimate for the Jordan near Jericho. Translated (converted to metric) but worth checking.
3. **b014** — "the lepers ... started walking back to the priest": Luke 17:14 has them going to show themselves "aux sacrificateurs" (plural), not "back". French follows Luke.
4. **sa10 model** — the prompt asks for a personal "Gilgal" of 3–5 of the student's own memorial stones; the model only retells the Gilgal story and never gives personal stones. Translated faithfully.
5. **MC13** — distractor "A Civil War souvenir" is US-specific (see Adaptations).
