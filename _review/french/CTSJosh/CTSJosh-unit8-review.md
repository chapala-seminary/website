# CTSJosh Unit 8 — French draft review

## Questions for Wayne

1. **Joshua 13:1 (b011, b013, b014, MC 1, fill 1, SA 1).** NKJV/KJV: "there remaineth yet very much land to be possessed". LSG: "le pays qui te reste à soumettre est très grand". I quoted LSG and used it in the Section I heading and the summary. Fill 1 now blanks *pays* and also accepts *terre, territoire, contrée, terres*.
2. **"Wholly followed the LORD" (b012, b013, b028, b050, b051, MC 7, fill 5, SA 4/5).** LSG reads "pleinement suivi la voie de l'Éternel" in Josué 14:8, 9, 14, Nombres 14:24 and Deutéronome 1:36. I used this phrase everywhere, including the Closing heading. Fill 5 answer: *pleinement* (also accepts *entièrement, totalement, complètement, de tout cœur*). In b051 the words the LORD spoke at Kadesh are quoted from Nombres 14:24: "il a pleinement suivi ma voie".
3. **Joshua 14:12, "this mountain" (b006, b007, b010, b026, fill 3).** LSG: "cette montagne", which is feminine. Fill 3 answer: *montagne*.
4. **Joshua 13:14, 33 (b021, b053).** LSG: "tel fut son héritage" (singular, the tribe). NKJV: "their inheritance". I quoted LSG. In b023 the Levite's confession "L'Éternel est mon héritage" is the lesson's own paraphrase and not a quotation (Psaume 16:5 LSG has "mon partage").
5. **Joshua 15:19, "Give me a blessing" (b033, b034, MC 9, SA 6).** LSG: "Fais-moi un présent". The lesson's prayer application ("the springs of God's blessing") still reads well around it. The MC 9 option is "Un présent — des sources d'eau". SA 6 accepts both *bénédiction* and *présent*. "South land" is "terre du midi" (LSG).
6. **Joshua 15:18, "What wouldest thou?"** LSG: "Qu'as-tu?" I quoted it as is.
7. **James 4:2 (b034).** LSG: "vous ne possédez pas, parce que vous ne demandez pas" (NKJV: "you do not have because you do not ask").
8. **Joshua 17:14–18 (b038).** In 17:14 LSG continues "et que l'Éternel nous a bénis jusqu'à présent". I cut it with an ellipsis. In 17:15 LSG has "Phéréziens et Rephaïm" where KJV has "giants". In 17:17–18 LSG splits the sentence differently. I quoted LSG with an ellipsis.
9. **Joshua 18:1 (b041, MC 10, SA 7).** LSG: "la tente d'assignation" (NKJV: "tabernacle of meeting"). In the prose I kept *tabernacle* and glossed it once as "c'est-à-dire la tente d'assignation". SA 7 accepts either term.
10. **Joshua 18:4–9 (b042, MC 16).** LSG says the surveyors "traceront un plan… dans un livre", not "describe it". My prose says "décrire… dans un livre". The MC 16 option is "le décrire par écrit".
11. **Joshua 19:50 (b046–b049, MC 17, fill 10, SA 10).** LSG: "Thimnath-Sérach" and "Il rebâtit la ville" (KJV: "built"). I used *rebâtit*. Fill 10 accepts *Timnath-Sérach, Timnath-Serah, Thimnath-Serah*.
12. **Proper names in LSG form:** Tselophchad (fill 8 also accepts *Zelophehad*), Machla, Noa, Hogla, Milca, Thirtsa, Acsa, Othniel, Kirjath-Sépher, Anakim, Kadès-Barnéa, Guilgal, Gabaon, Silo, Éléazar, Jephunné, the Kenizien, Gueschuriens, Avviens, Héviens, Nephthali. For Polycarp I used *Polycarpe de Smyrne*.
13. **b007 label.** The source labels the verse as NKJV. In French I labeled it "(LSG)" and quoted LSG.

## Problems in the English source

1. **b007, b012, b026 and all lesson quotations.** The text is labeled NKJV but quotes the KJV ("whereof the LORD spake", "Anakims", "thou heardest", "fourscore and five"). The NKJV has "of which the LORD spoke".
2. **b028.** It calls Deuteronomy 1:36 Moses' "blessing". In fact Moses is recounting the LORD's oath there; it is not a blessing.
3. **b032.** It calls Othniel "Caleb's nephew". Joshua 15:17 ("son of Kenaz, the brother of Caleb") is ambiguous: he could be a nephew or a younger brother. I translated the source as written.
4. **b015.** It places "the Hivites under Hermon" in 13:1–7. Those verses mention Baal-Gad at the foot of Hermon, and the Hivites "under Hermon" are in 11:3. This is minor.
5. **b048.** The lesson says Joshua "takes the smallest city". The text never says Timnath-serah was the smallest.
6. **b049.** The meaning "portion of the leftover / extra portion" for Timnath-serah is debatable. Judges 2:9 has "Timnath-heres".
7. **SA 7 model.** It says Shiloh was the central place of worship "for the rest of the conquest era". The lesson (b041) and MC 11 say about 350 years, until Eli and Samuel. The model also mostly answers a different question, about the survey, rather than about the significance of Shiloh. I translated it faithfully.
8. **SA 5 keyword "faith".** The model hits it only through "life of faith". In French that group is *fidèl/fidel/confian*.

## Adaptations

- **MC 9.** The distractor "Forty acres of vineyards" (a US land measure) became "Seize hectares de vignes".
- **"Deed".** It is rendered "titre de propriété" throughout. The idea of a land title is familiar in Kinshasa, so no further change was needed.
