# CTSJosh Unit 7 — French draft review notes

All Scripture quotations were checked against the offline LSG 1910 text (tools/lsg.py) by the drafter and again by an independent checker.

## Questions for Wayne

1. **"I have given" / "I will deliver" (b013, b019, b023, b040, b045).** LSG has "je livre" (present tense) in Josué 6:2, 8:1 and 10:8, and "je les livrerai" in 11:6. The English contrast between the past and the future ("Past tense again, in the future") doesn't work with LSG. I rendered "I have given" as "je livre" throughout. In b019 I wrote "Encore une fois, un futur aussi certain que le passé" and ended it with "Je livre. Je livrerai."
2. **Josué 11:10, "head of all those kingdoms" (b026–b029, MC8, Fill 1, SA3).** LSG has "la principale ville de tous ces royaumes". I quoted LSG and added once in b027 "littéralement, en hébreu, « la tête »", so the lesson's image of the head can stay (heading III, "la tête doit tomber"). The SA3 keywords accept both "tête" and "principale".
3. **Josué 11:18, "made war a long time" (b006, b009, b010, b036–b038, b051, MC9, SA6).** LSG has "La guerre que soutint Josué contre tous ces rois fut de longue durée". The recurring refrain became "une guerre de longue durée". The MC9 option reads "Elle fut de longue durée".
4. **Josué 11:15, "left nothing undone".** LSG has "il ne négligea rien". I used that wording in every place, including MC13, SA4 and SA10.
5. **Josué 11:21, "cut off the Anakim … destroyed them utterly".** LSG has "extermina … les dévoua par interdit". The English wordplay (the giants "cut down" Israel's faith and are then "cut down" themselves) survives only partly, as "abattre".
6. **Josué 11:23, "the land rested from war" (b007, b049–b051, MC14, SA9, Fill 10).** LSG has "le pays fut en repos et sans guerre". The Fill 10 prompt quotes "le pays fut en repos".
7. **Psaumes 20:7 (b023, Fill 4).** LSG says "nous invoquons le nom" where the NKJV says "we will remember the name". The answer ("nom") is unchanged. (The offline LSG text numbers this verse 20:7, like the NKJV.)
8. **Genesis 15 (b045).** "Unto thy seed have I given this land" became LSG Genèse 15:18, "Je donne ce pays à ta postérité".
9. **Hebrews 4:9 (b050).** LSG has "un repos de sabbat réservé au peuple de Dieu". I quoted LSG, which actually helps the lesson's point.
10. **Deuteronomy 17:16 ("multiply horses").** LSG has "qu'il n'ait pas un grand nombre de chevaux". I used it as a paraphrase in b018 and b022.
11. **Numbers 13:33 (b032, Fill 7).** I quoted LSG: "nous étions à nos yeux et aux leurs comme des sauterelles". The Fill 7 prompt follows LSG's wording, and the answer is "sauterelles".
12. **Herem (b028, Fill 5).** I used LSG's term "l'interdit" and kept "herem" in parentheses. The Fill 5 answer is "interdit", and "herem/cherem/kherem/anathème" are also accepted.
13. **"The LORD as Captain" (b037).** I rendered it as "Chef de l'armée", which echoes LSG Josué 5:14 ("chef de l'armée de l'Éternel").
14. **Names follow LSG spelling.** Hatsor (Hazor is accepted in Fill 1), Mérom, Anakim, Adoni-Tsédek, Schimron, Acschaph, Kinnéreth, Sihon, Hesbon, Og, Basan, Rephaïm, Guilgal, Gabaon. In MC17, Achish became LSG "Akisch". In MC18, Joppa became "Japho" (LSG).

## Problems in the English source

1. **b049 and Fill 10 (factual error).** The lesson says the verb in "the land rested" (Josh 11:23) shares the Hebrew root of "Sabbath". It doesn't. The verb there is *shaqat* ("to be quiet, at peace"), not *shabat*. Fill 10 is built on this claim. I translated it faithfully. Please consider rewording, perhaps to link with Hebrews 4:9's "repos de sabbat" instead.
2. **Version label (b007 and quotations throughout).** The text is labelled "NKJV", but most quotations are KJV wording ("said unto Moses", "smote the king thereof", "There remaineth therefore a rest"). Only a few are NKJV ("hamstrung"). This doesn't affect the French, because all of it is LSG.
3. **b034, "at twenty".** Scripture doesn't give Joshua's age at the spy mission. Caleb was 40 then (Josh 14:7), and Joshua was probably of similar age. "At twenty" is unsupported.
4. **b037, Caleb's ages.** At Josh 14:10 Caleb is 85, which is his age at the time he speaks, near the end of the conquest. It isn't his age at "entry". He was 40 at Kadesh, and about 78 at the crossing. The seven-year conclusion is still right. The wording is muddled, and I translated it as written.
5. **b034, Anakim at Hebron.** The lesson says some Anakim had "retreated" to Hebron for Caleb to take. But Josh 11:22 says the only Anakim left were in Gaza, Gath and Ashdod. Caleb's later expulsion (15:14) creates the tension, but the lesson presents its harmonization as fact.
6. **b038, mission history.** Adoniram Judson reached Burma in 1813 at about 25, not 34. William Carey sailed in June 1793 at 31, two months before he turned 32, so "32" is close. He spent about 41 years in India in total, not seven years plus "forty more". Fill 8 (Burma, six years) is correct.
7. **b027.** "Ten times the size of Jericho" is loose: Hazor's roughly 200 acres is more like 20 times Jericho's tell. This is minor.

## Adaptations

- **b027.** 200 acres became "environ 80 hectares", following the Spanish edition.
- **b040.** "It has not been topped up" became "rechargée comme un crédit de téléphone", a familiar image in Kinshasa.
- **b011, b038.** "Headline" became "titre à la une", and "chapter heading" became "titre du chapitre".
- **Short-answer prompts.** I lightly reworded SA1, SA2, SA9 and SA10 so that the prompt alone doesn't reach three keyword hits. Their meaning is unchanged.
- **Interface text.** b060 is "📚 Catalogue", following the other French units, and b061 is "Réinitialiser". I used French punctuation spacing, with non-breaking spaces before `: ; ? !` and inside « ».
