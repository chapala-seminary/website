# CTSJosh Unit 5 (Achan's Sin and the Restoration of Ai): French draft review

## Questions for Wayne

1. **Josh 7:19 (course verse, b007, b030).** NKJV: "I beg you… make confession to Him". LSG: "Mon fils, donne gloire à l'Éternel… et rends-lui hommage" (no "I beg you", no "confession"). I quoted LSG verbatim. The lesson's point about confession now rests on the surrounding prose, not on the verse.
2. **Prov 16:18 (b019, MC5, fill 2, SA4).** LSG: "L'arrogance précède la ruine, Et l'orgueil précède la chute." The word order is reversed: "pride" goes with the fall, and "arrogance" goes with destruction. Fill 2's answer is "ruine" (it also accepts destruction and perte). I kept MC1's option as "L'orgueil précède la chute", which is LSG's second line.
3. **Josh 7:5 (b017).** NKJV: "hearts… melted and became like water". LSG: "Le peuple fut consterné et perdit courage." The lesson's wordplay ("melted the kings… now its own heart melting") is rebuilt around "perdit courage", which LSG also uses in Josh 5:1. I added "(Josué 5:1)" and wrote "quelques chapitres plus tôt" instead of "one chapter earlier".
4. **Josh 8:1 "I have given" (b049, b052, MC18, SA9).** LSG uses the present tense: "je livre entre tes mains". The lesson's point about the past tense ("the same past-tense word from chapter 6") cannot be shown from LSG. I wrote "je livre entre tes mains" and added a sentence: "Dieu en parle comme d'une chose déjà accomplie." The quoted words in MC18 are "Ne crains point, et ne t'effraie point !"
5. **Josh 8:18, 26 "spear" (b050, b051, fill 8).** LSG has "javelot". Fill 8's answer is "javelot" (it also accepts lance, javeline and sagaie).
6. **"Babylonian garment" (b026, b030, MC10, fill 3).** LSG has "un beau manteau de Schinear". In b026 and MC10 I added "(c'est-à-dire de Babylonie)" / "(de Babylonie)". "Wedge of gold" is "lingot d'or" in LSG.
7. **James 5:16 (b033, MC13, SA5).** The English quotes the KJV ("Confess your faults"). LSG has "Confessez donc vos péchés les uns aux autres… afin que vous soyez guéris". I used LSG.
8. **James 4:2 (b021).** The English quotes the KJV ("You have not because you ask not"). I quoted the end of the verse in LSG: "vous ne possédez pas, parce que vous ne demandez pas".
9. **Josh 7:7 (b022).** The English loosely paraphrases it ("cf."). I quoted LSG's exact words, which name the Jordan and the Amorites, and dropped the "cf.".
10. **Captain of the LORD's host.** I used "le chef de l'armée de l'Éternel", which is LSG's wording in Josh 5:14. The capital "His sword" became a lowercase "son épée".
11. **"Sanctified" / "the witness" (b018, SA3).** The C. J. Fowler story uses holiness-movement language (a second-work sanctification and "the witness" of it). I rendered "the witness" as "l'assurance intérieure" and did not frame the doctrine either way. Is that acceptable for a non-denominational course?
12. **Book title in b009.** For *When Bad Things Happen to Good People* I gave a descriptive French rendering ("Quand le malheur frappe les gens de bien"), not the title of a published French edition. Please confirm, or drop the title.

## Problems in the English source

1. **MC17 (and SA7 prompt):** asks for "the literal meaning of Achan's name". The key is "Trouble". The lesson (b035) says the *Valley of Achor* means trouble and shares a root with Achan's name. It does not say that Achan's own name means trouble. 1 Chr 2:7 plays on the name "Achar". I kept the key. I reworded the SA7 prompt slightly so that the prompt alone does not hit the keywords.
2. **b044:** says "The 1998 case Wayne mentions took out a marriage and three young adult lives." The marriage and three children come from the family story in b042, which happened fifteen years ago. They are not from the 1998 Tucker case. I translated it as written.
3. **b054:** says Moses gave the command of Deuteronomy 27 "forty years earlier". Deuteronomy was given at the end of the wilderness years, shortly before the crossing, not forty years before Josh 8.
4. **b056:** says the "foreign Gibeonite hears it". The Gibeonite treaty comes only in Josh 9, so this is speculative.
5. **MC9:** the distractors "Rooftop" and "Watchtower" do not fit "upon his ____". In French, "Feet" became "Debout" and "Knees" became "À genoux" so that every option completes the line. The order and the answer index are unchanged.
6. **SA6:** the English model does not give the lesson's first reason, that the family was complicit (b036). I added one sentence from b036 to the French model.
7. **SA1:** the model answer is mostly the Dave and Debbie story and Achan, and says little about Fischer's song. This is acceptable but loosely matched to the prompt.
