# CTSJosh Unit 2 (Rahab and the Scarlet Cord): French draft review

## Questions for Wayne

1. **"Scarlet cord" (2:18, 2:21).** LSG says "cordon de fil cramoisi" and "cordon de cramoisi", not "écarlate". The lesson's title and its "three scarlet signs" theme need one steady word, so the prose, MC, fill-ins and SA use "cordon écarlate". The verse quotations keep LSG's "cramoisi". SA keywords accept both words. Is that acceptable?
2. **"Behold the blood, and pass over" (b030).** This is not a verse quotation in the English. I replaced it with LSG Exode 12:13, "Je verrai le sang, et je passerai par-dessus vous", in italics with quotation marks. I used the same words in the SA3 model. Please confirm.
3. **"Heard" (2:10–11).** The lesson stresses that Rahab *heard* (b020). LSG says "nous avons appris" (we learned). I used "appris" in b020, MC18 and SA2 so they match the verse. SA2 also accepts "entendu".
4. **"Our hearts melted… no more courage" (2:11).** LSG says "nous avons perdu courage, et tous nos esprits sont abattus". The SA2 model says "tous ont perdu courage" rather than "melted".
5. **"Our lives for yours" (2:14).** LSG says "Nous sommes prêts à mourir pour vous". It also moves from "vous" to "toi" within the verse. I quoted it verbatim. The lesson's point (a sworn covenant) is unaffected.
6. **Hebrews 11:31.** NKJV "those who did not believe… with peace" = LSG "les rebelles… avec bienveillance". I quoted LSG. Fill 7 blanks only "foi", so it still works.
7. **"Kindness" (2:12) vs MC6 "show mercy".** LSG says "bonté", so MC6 uses "faire preuve de bonté".
8. **Harlot / prostitute.** French has one natural word for both, and LSG uses "prostituée" everywhere. MC16's "Harlot / prostitute" became just "Prostituée".
9. **Name forms follow LSG:** Boaz (Booz is also accepted in Fill 9), Isaï (Jesse), Sittim (Acacia Grove), Astarté (Ashtoreth), Bath-Schéba, Anne (Hannah), Éléazar, Ruth la Moabite.
10. **MC5 option "Yahweh is strongest among the gods" = "Yahvé est le plus fort des dieux".** Yahvé is the usual French spelling. This is a wrong option, so nothing depends on it.
11. **"The whoever gospel" = "l'Évangile du « quiconque »".** This follows LSG Jean 3:16 ("quiconque croit"). The Fill 10 answer is "quiconque". Please confirm the phrase sounds natural to your French reader.
12. **b049 "hung His Son on a cross" = "a suspendu son Fils à une croix".** This keeps the parallel with the cord. "Livré son Fils" would be the softer, more traditional wording if your reader prefers it.

## Problems in the English source

1. **SA1–SA10: every English prompt passes its own keywords.** Each prompt already contains 3 to 7 keyword hits (for example, SA3 hits 7 of 8). A student could pass by copying the question. I reworded all ten French prompts with the same meaning but fewer keyword words. Each French prompt now scores 0–1 hits. The English should be fixed the same way.
2. **MC20 credits the photograph story to Max Lucado.** The lesson (b045) never names Lucado. The item is still answerable, but the attribution is not taught.
3. **MC12 "According to Matthew 1:5, Rahab married Salmon".** Matthew 1:5 says "Salmon begot Boaz by Rahab". The marriage is inferred, not stated. This is minor.
4. **b036 "Salmon — quite possibly one of the two spies".** The text marks this as speculation. That is fine, but it should not be examined. It is not examined at present.
