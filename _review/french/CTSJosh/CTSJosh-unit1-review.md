# CTSJosh Unit 1 — French draft review

## Questions for Wayne

1. **Joshua 1:9, "wherever you go".** This is the course verse (b004, b028, b029, b031, b053, MC6, SA2, SA10). LSG has "est avec toi **dans tout ce que tu entreprendras**", which means "in all that you undertake", not "wherever you go". I quoted LSG everywhere. In b031 I made the sentence "there was no place Joshua could not go" into "aucun lieu où Josué ne pouvait aller, aucune entreprise qu'il ne pouvait mener" so it matches the verse. Is this acceptable, or would you like a note that the Hebrew literally says "wherever you go"?
2. **Joshua 1:8, "make your way prosperous… good success".** LSG has "tu auras du succès dans tes entreprises… tu réussiras". The lesson's pairing "prosperity and success" is rendered "le succès et la réussite" (b034, MC16, SA3). SA3 still accepts "prosper" as a keyword.
3. **Joshua 1:8, "meditate in it".** LSG uses the imperative "médite-le". Fill 6 is "« ____-le jour et nuit »" with the answer *médite*. It also accepts méditer, médites, méditez and méditeras.
4. **"Be strong and of good courage"** is LSG "Fortifie-toi et prends courage" (1:6, 9, 18). In 1:7 it is "Fortifie-toi seulement et aie bon courage". I used the LSG phrase as the lesson's refrain throughout. Section III heading: "Fortifie-toi et prends courage".
5. **Joshua 1:1, "Moses' assistant".** LSG has "serviteur de Moïse", and Exodus 24:13 has "Josué qui le servait". MC3 option C is "Serviteur personnel / assistant".
6. **Joshua 1:6, "divide as an inheritance".** LSG has "mettras ce peuple en possession du pays". The lesson does not depend on this.
7. **The name Yahweh (b043–b044, MC1, fill 8, SA4).** I used "Yahvé" and added "(que la Bible Louis Segond rend par « l'Éternel »)", the same choice as in CTSPent. Fill 8's answer is *Yahvé*. It also accepts Yahweh, YHWH, Éternel and Jéhovah.
8. **Joshua 24:15 (b009, MC6).** LSG has "Moi et ma maison, nous servirons l'Éternel". **Joshua 6:20 (MC6)** has a singular wall, "la muraille s'écroula". **Joshua 1:4** "Hittites" is "Héthiens".
9. **SA prompts reworded.** A literal French translation would have given several prompts keyword hits on their own. To avoid that, I rephrased SA2, SA4, SA7, SA8, SA9 and SA10 without changing what they ask. For example, SA7 now asks the student to "trace at least four stages by which God formed Joshua" instead of listing Egypt, Rephidim, Sinai and the others. None of these prompts reaches more than 2 hits now.

## Problems in the English source

1. **b019 / fill 3: "buried… somewhere on Mount Nebo".** Deuteronomy 34:6 says the Lord buried Moses "in the valley in the land of Moab, opposite Beth Peor". Moses died after going up Nebo, but he was not buried on it. I translated as written, so the fill answer stays *Nebo*.
2. **MC10, MC11 (twelve spies, Joshua and Caleb's minority report).** The lesson never teaches this. b028 only hints at "giants the previous generation had refused to face".
3. **MC13.** The stem asks for "Joshua's three central character qualities, identified by the book's introduction", with the key "faith, courage, devotion to the Law". The introduction (b016) gives three central *emphases of the book*: God's faithfulness, the written Law, and God's holiness. Joshua's character qualities are not listed.
4. **MC19 (24 chapters).** The lesson never states this.
5. **SA7 (Egypt, Rephidim, the Sinai ascent, the spy mission, the commissioning by Moses).** The lesson teaches only the forty years as attendant and the Sinai ascent (b035). Rephidim, the spies and the commissioning are not taught.
6. **MC4 / b027: "Four times… God charges Joshua".** The lesson itself counts verse 18, but that charge comes from the people, not from God. Strictly speaking, God gives the charge three times. I kept the "four times" answer and adjusted the sentence to say "Dieu exhorte Josué… quatre fois", as the English does.
7. **SA5 model.** It says Leviticus 18:24-25 "lists" temple prostitution and infant sacrifice. Leviticus 18 mentions child sacrifice to Molech (v. 21) but not temple prostitution. The keywords "justice" and "holiness" are only implied by b016.
8. **b023** is circular: "The God who keeps His word across centuries was about to demonstrate that He keeps His word across centuries". I translated it literally. You may want to reword it.
9. **b013.** Joshua 24:33 records Eleazar's own death, so Eleazar cannot have written the entire closing account. This is a minor point.
10. **b046: "fifteen hundred years later".** The conquest is dated about 1400 BC, so Christ came about 1,400 years later. This is a minor point.

## Adaptations

- **b024.** I added short explanations: who Booker T. Washington was ("un éducateur noir américain né dans l'esclavage"), "Virginie, aux États-Unis", and what the 1863 Emancipation Proclamation was. The dollar amounts are kept.
- **b048.** I explained Scotland Yard ("la police de Londres"), Chicago ("une grande ville américaine") and twenty-five cents ("le quart d'un dollar").
- **b009.** "Master's-level students" is rendered "étudiants de niveau master".
- **b053.** "Be strong, brother" uses *vous* forms throughout ("Fortifiez-vous, mon frère"), so the LSG wording of 1:9 is adapted to the second person plural.
