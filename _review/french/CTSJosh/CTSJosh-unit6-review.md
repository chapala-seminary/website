## Questions for Wayne

1. **Joshua 9:14 "did not ask counsel of the LORD"**: LSG reads "ils ne consultèrent point l'Éternel" (a verb, with no noun "counsel"). The Section III heading, b012, b028, b032 and Fill 4 use the LSG wording. Fill 4's answer is now **consultèrent** (accepts consulté / consultaient / consulter).
2. **"Moldy bread" (Josh 9:5, 12)**: LSG says the bread was "sec et en miettes" (dry and crumbled), not moldy. The lesson's running image stays "pain moisi" outside the quotations. Fill 3 (answer "pain") still works. Is that acceptable?
3. **James 1:5 "liberally"**: LSG has "simplement". In b024 the lesson builds on "He gives liberally", so I added a short gloss: "Il donne simplement, c'est-à-dire généreusement, sans calcul." Fill 2's answer is **simplement**, and it also accepts généreusement / libéralement / abondamment. Please confirm you are happy with the gloss.
4. **"Past tense again. *I have delivered*" (Josh 10:8, b047)** and **"I have given" (Josh 6:2, b048)**: LSG uses the present tense in both places ("je les livre", "je livre entre tes mains"). The English point about the past tense cannot survive, so b047 now says "La victoire est déjà accordée. *Je les livre.*" and b048 quotes "je livre entre tes mains".
5. **Josh 10:25 vs. 1:6–9**: In LSG the wording is not identical. Chapter 1 has "Fortifie-toi et prends courage / Ne t'effraie point et ne t'épouvante point", while 10:25 has "Ne craignez point et ne vous effrayez point, fortifiez-vous et ayez du courage". b056 therefore says "pour l'essentiel, les paroles mêmes" (essentially the very words), and MC19 quotes 10:25.
6. **Nethinim (b040, MC10, Fill 6)**: LSG spells it "Néthiniens". I used that form, and Fill 6 accepts Nethinim / Néthinim too.
7. **Neh 3:7 "the Gibeonites" (b042)**: LSG reads "les hommes de Gabaon". I quoted it that way.
8. **Matt 10:16**: LSG reads "prudents comme les serpents, et simples comme les colombes". b020 uses "prudence"/"simplicité" for "shrewdness"/"innocence".
9. **High priest**: I used "souverain sacrificateur" (LSG and earlier CTS French drafts), not "grand prêtre". Urim/Thummim follow LSG: "l'urim et le thummim", "pectoral".

## Problems in the English source

1. **b036**: It says God told Joshua "at the burning-bush moment" to take off his sandals. The burning bush was Moses (Ex 3). Joshua's moment is the commander of the LORD's army (Josh 5:15). I translated it as "dans un moment semblable à celui du buisson ardent", which softens the claim to "a moment like the burning bush". Consider citing Josh 5:15 instead.
2. **b056**: The text says "four times in four verses" in Josh 1:6–9. The charge "be strong and courageous" occurs three times in those verses (6, 7, 9); the fourth occurrence is the people's words in 1:18. I translated it as written.
3. **b040**: It says the Gibeonites are listed as Nethinim "by the time of the divided monarchy… in Ezra and Nehemiah". Ezra and Nehemiah are post-exilic. Also, Scripture never explicitly identifies the Gibeonites with the Nethinim; that link is a traditional inference.
4. **SA5 model**: It contrasts Rahab, who lied "out of faith", with the Gibeonites, who lied "only from dread". The lesson (b043) says the opposite: the Gibeonites' fear of the LORD was real, and "both came to faith". The keyword "canaanite" is also never used in the lesson (the Gibeonites are Hivites, 9:7). I translated the model faithfully. The keyword group is cananéen / amoréen / hévien.
5. **b028**: The phrase "three damning words" introduces a quotation much longer than three words. I rendered it as "quelques mots accablants".
6. **b060**: The Jaime Guerrero story is credited "(Doug Goins)", yet the lesson calls Guerrero Wayne's "friend". It may have been Goins's friend. Please check.
7. **Version mixing**: b020 quotes Matt 10:16 in NIV wording, b043 quotes Rom 8:28 in NASB wording, and b055–b056 quote Josh 10:19, 25 in KJV wording, although b007 says the course uses the NKJV. This has no effect on the French, which uses LSG throughout.
8. **b027**: The six-day out-and-back walk is speculation that the text does not support. Kept.
