# CTSJosh Unit 9 — French draft review notes

## Questions for Wayne

1. **Hebrews 6:18.** NKJV reads "who have fled for refuge to lay hold upon the hope". LSG reads "nous dont le seul refuge a été de saisir l'espérance qui nous était proposée", with no verb "fled". Section V (b058) reads three details out of the verse: "Have fled", "For refuge", "To lay hold". I quoted LSG. For the first detail I wrote "the Greek word used here literally means 'we who have fled'", and I kept "Refuge" and "Saisir" as the other two. I also reworded the MC 16 stem ("which verse describes believers as people whose only refuge was to lay hold of the hope"). Fill 8 uses the LSG wording, so the answer is "refuge".
2. **Joshua 21:45.** NKJV reads "There failed not ought of any good thing"; LSG reads "De toutes les bonnes paroles… aucune ne resta sans effet". The refrain in b013, b064 and b065, MC 18, Fill 9 and SA 9 all follow LSG. The Fill 9 answer is "effet", with "effets" accepted ("accomplissement" and "suite" removed by the checker: "sans suite"/"sans accomplissement" do not give the verse's meaning).
3. **Joshua 20:2.** NKJV says "Appoint out for you cities of refuge, whereof I spake unto you by the hand of Moses". LSG has "Établissez-vous, comme je vous l'ai ordonné par Moïse, des villes de refuge". Nothing in the lesson depends on the difference. This is noted only because it is the course Scripture.
4. **Joshua 22:22.** NKJV reads "The LORD God of gods" (twice); LSG reads "Dieu, Dieu, l'Éternel, Dieu, Dieu, l'Éternel". I quoted LSG as it stands.
5. **Joshua 21:2.** LSG keeps the old word "banlieues" for the pasture lands, where NKJV has "suburbs". I quoted LSG, and the next sentence explains it as "pâturages".
6. **Wesley's hymn.** I could not check a standard French singing version of "Jesus, Lover of my soul", so I translated the quoted lines literally: "Jésus, ami de mon âme… laisse-moi voler vers ton sein… Je n'ai point d'autre refuge… couvre ma tête sans défense de l'ombre de ton aile". If Congolese churches sing a known French version, it should replace mine, but Fill 5 depends on the word "sein".
7. **"Kinsman-redeemer"** (b016). I used the LSG phrase from Ruth 3:9, "celui qui a droit de rachat", followed by "le parent rédempteur".
8. **The Hiding Place.** I kept the English title and added the gloss « La cachette ». I did not use a French edition title because I could not verify one.

## Problems in the English source

1. **MC 12.** The stem says "John Wesley" wrote "Jesus, Lover of My Soul". The lesson (b036) says Charles Wesley, which is historically correct. A distractor also reads "A sermon by his brother Charles". The answer key stands, but the stem names the wrong Wesley. I translated it faithfully.
2. **SA 6.** The model answer calls "no other refuge" the **fourth** feature. The lesson makes it the **third** (b039); the fourth is the cleared roads.
3. **b013.** The overview lists the features in this order: "closeness, open gates, cleared roads, singular safety, high priest's death". In Section III, "no other place" is no. 3 and the roads are no. 4.
4. **b024 name meanings.** "Hebron = fellowship" and "Golan = captive/exile" are traditional, popular etymologies that scholars dispute. I translated them as written.
5. **Bible versions.** Revelation 3:20 (b035) and Acts 4:12 (b040) are quoted in ESV-style wording, though the course states NKJV. This has no effect on the French, which uses LSG throughout.

## Adaptations

- **Maryland (b029).** I kept "the state of Maryland", called it "a small US state", and added a comparison: "about three times the area of the city-province of Kinshasa".
- **Distance (b034).** "thirty or forty miles" became "cinquante ou soixante kilomètres".
- **Civil War story (b030, b032, MC 13).** I explained "Confederate" and "Union" as soldiers of the South and of the North in the "guerre de Sécession, la guerre civile américaine".
- **Karen's story.** "Restraining order" became "décision de justice interdisant à son mari de l'approcher". "Safe-house" became "maison d'accueil pour femmes en danger".
- **Short-answer prompts.** Four prompts (SA 2, 6, 7 and 8) were lightly reworded so that the prompt alone does not reach the keyword threshold. The task each one asks for is unchanged.
- **Accepted answers.** Fill 3 accepts only "épaule"/"épaules". No other French word is a correct answer there. Fill 8 ("refuge", quoted from LSG) and Fill 9 ("effet") likewise take only the LSG word.
