## Questions for Wayne

1. **Isaiah 7:14, "the virgin" (b017, MC 13, Fill 6, SA 7).** LSG Ésaïe 7:14 says "la jeune fille deviendra enceinte", but LSG Matthieu 1:23 says "la vierge". I quoted each verse in Segond's own wording. In b017 I added one sentence: the Hebrew word means a young unmarried woman, and Matthew uses the Greek word for "virgin". The MC 13 stem says "la jeune fille (Matthieu dit « la vierge »)". In SA 7, either "vierge" or "jeune fille" counts as a keyword.
2. **1 Timothy 3:16, "God was manifest in the flesh" (b024).** This is a manuscript difference, not a choice of wording. LSG follows the reading "celui qui a été manifesté en chair" ("he who"), not "Dieu". As a result, the closing verse no longer says "God" in so many words. I quoted LSG exactly and added no note, because a "le grec dit littéralement…" sentence would be inaccurate here. Do you want to keep this verse, or add a short note?
3. **John 8:58, "I AM" (b008, MC 3, Fill 1, SA 2).** LSG says "avant qu'Abraham fût, je suis", in lowercase. To keep the lesson's point, I added one sentence: the Greek is literally "moi, je suis", like the name revealed to Moses (Exode 3:14). Fill 1 answer: "suis" (it also accepts "je suis" and "moi je suis").
4. **Isaiah 53:5 (b019, Fill 7, SA 8).** "Wounded for our transgressions… crushed for our iniquities" is "blessé pour nos péchés… Brisé pour nos iniquités" in LSG. I used Segond's wording.
5. **The Father's voice (b021, MC 17, SA 9).** At the Jordan I used LSG Matthieu 3:17: "Celui-ci est mon Fils bien-aimé, en qui j'ai mis toute mon affection". The lesson's transfiguration line ("This is My Son; listen to Him") matches no single verse exactly. I used Marc 9:7: "Celui-ci est mon Fils bien-aimé: écoutez-le!"
6. **"Lunatic".** I used "fou" (and "un fou, un dément" once in b010). Fill 2 answer: "Seigneur".
7. **Names.** I used Segond's spellings, "Bethléhem" and "Emmanuel". The fill-ins also accept "Bethléem" and "Immanuel".

## Problems in the English source

1. **b012, MC 8, SA 4: "before the Romans had even invented crucifixion".** The Romans did not invent crucifixion. They adopted it from earlier peoples, such as the Persians and the Carthaginians. The point still holds, because Psalm 22 is earlier than any known crucifixion. I translated it faithfully. A safer wording would be "long before crucifixion was practised".
2. **b015, mosaic illustration.** The story has tiles handed over "over forty years", but they make "a portrait fifteen centuries in the making". This is an internal inconsistency in an illustration labelled as invented. I translated it as written.
3. **SA 2 model** says the "I AM" name was "revealed to Moses". The English lesson never mentions Moses. In French, my added sentence in b008 (see Question 3) now covers it.
4. **SA 5 model** says the calculation "treats each prophecy's probability as independent". The lesson says only that it assigns "a probability to each prophecy individually". This is close but not the same claim. I followed the lesson's wording.
5. All answer keys match the lesson.
