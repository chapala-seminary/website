## Questions for Wayne

1. **2 Timothy 3:16 (b004, b006, b024, MC 16, Fill 1, SA 9).** LSG says "Toute Écriture est inspirée de Dieu" ("given by inspiration of God"). Following convention 3, b024 adds "littéralement « soufflée par Dieu »", and MC 16 uses "Soufflée par Dieu" as the correct option. Fill 1 answer: "inspirée". It also accepts "soufflée", "soufflée par Dieu" and "insufflée".
2. **2 Peter 1:21 (b024).** The NKJV has "holy men of God spoke as they were moved by the Holy Spirit". LSG has no "holy" and no "of old": "c'est poussés par le Saint-Esprit que des hommes ont parlé de la part de Dieu". I quoted LSG and dropped "of old" from the sentence around it. The lesson's point still holds.
3. **Matthew 24:35 (b026, MC 18).** I used LSG: "Le ciel et la terre passeront, mais mes paroles ne passeront point". This has "le ciel" (singular) and no "by no means".
4. **Isaiah 40:8 (b027, Fill 10).** LSG says "subsiste éternellement" for "stands forever". Fill 10 answer: "éternellement". It also accepts "pour toujours", "à jamais", "pour l'éternité" and "toujours".
5. **"Ring of truth" / "criterion of embarrassment".** I rendered the heading as "L'accent de la vérité" and the technical term as "critère de l'embarras". Please confirm both are acceptable.

## Problems in the English source

1. **b008 "the third trail".** Unit 1 (b030) lists the reliability of the documents as the **fourth** trail. I translated "troisième" as written. One of the two units needs correcting.
2. **"Criterion of embarrassment" (MC 13, SA 7)** never appears in the lesson, which only says "ring of truth". In the MC 13 stem I added the lesson's name in parentheses. I reworded the SA 7 prompt to use "l'accent de la vérité".
3. **SA 10 model.** It ends with "a question only the heart, moved by the Spirit, can answer". The lesson does not say this. I translated it anyway.
4. **Possible factual issues. Please check before publishing.**
   - **Greenleaf (b018).** The story that "a skeptic challenged him" and that he expected to expose the evangelists is widely repeated but poorly documented. Greenleaf was already a practising Christian when he wrote *Testimony of the Evangelists* (1846).
   - **Homer (b010).** The *Iliad* survives in well over a thousand manuscripts and papyri, not "a few hundred".
   - **Caesar (b010, MC 3).** The *Gallic Wars* survives in about 250 manuscripts. The commonly cited figure of "about 10" applies to the best early ones. The earliest copy is from the 9th century, which fits the 900-year gap.
   - **900 or 1,000 years.** b012 says "a thousand-year gap", but b010 and MC 3 say "about nine hundred". I translated each place as written.
5. **Answer keys.** All of them match the lesson.
