## Questions for Wayne

1. **"Firmament" (Ps 19:1; b004, b006, MC 1 why, Fill 1).** LSG says "l'étendue manifeste l'oeuvre de ses mains". The lesson does not comment on the word, so I quoted LSG with no added note. Fill 1 answer: "étendue". It also accepts "firmament", "ciel", "cieux" and "voûte céleste".
2. **Ps 14:1, "has said in his heart".** LSG says "L'insensé dit en son coeur". The lesson's point (heart, not head) still works. Fill 2 answer: "cœur" (also accepts "coeur").
3. **Romans 1:20 (b027).** LSG is longer and freer than the NKJV: "les perfections invisibles de Dieu, sa puissance éternelle et sa divinité, se voient comme à l'oeil… quand on les considère dans ses ouvrages". No exam item depends on the wording. I stopped the quotation before "Ils sont donc inexcusables".
4. **"Designer" / "Lawgiver".** I used "Concepteur" (capitalized where the English capitalizes it) and "Législateur". "Créateur" was too broad for the design argument, and "Architecte" was another option. Fill 5 answer: "horloger". Fill 6 answer: "législateur".
5. **"Longing" / "homesickness" (b021).** I used "nostalgie", "aspiration" and "mal du pays pour une patrie que nous n'avons jamais vue". For Augustine I used the standard French form: "Tu nous as faits pour toi, et notre cœur est inquiet jusqu'à ce qu'il repose en toi."
6. **"The African builds an altar to the sun" (b008).** I translated it faithfully. A Congolese reader may find this generalization about Africans odd or slightly patronizing. Do you want to soften it, for example to "Un peuple ancien bâtit un autel au soleil"?

## Problems in the English source

1. **b008 against Acts 17:23 and the SA 9 model.** The lesson says the Greek "raises a statue to an unknown god". Acts 17:23 (and the SA 9 model) say an **altar**. I translated each place as written ("statue" in b008, "autel" in SA 9).
2. **Terms the lesson never uses.** "Teleological" (MC 7, SA 2) and "ontological" (MC 10, MC 12, SA 4 keyword "ontolog") never appear in the lesson, and "moral argument" (MC 8, MC 9, SA 3) appears only as "argument from conscience". In the French stems I added the lesson's name in parentheses, for example "L'argument téléologique (l'argument du dessein)".
3. **SA 6 and MC 2 `why`.** "Clear the ground / remove obstacles" (keyword "ground") is not in the lesson, which says "lets the builder reach the wall".
4. **SA 10 model (and SA 6).** The model says "the last barrier is in the will, reason alone cannot move the will" and "the Spirit opens it from the inside". The lesson does not teach this. It only says that who waits behind the door is the subject of the next unit. Keywords "will" and "spirit" are therefore not taught here. MC 20 `why` ("the living God within must bring him through") goes slightly beyond the lesson too.
5. **SA 7 keyword "precise".** It cuts against the lesson, which stresses that the oxygen threshold is not fixed to a precise number. I kept a "précis" group, and the model uses it in that hedged sense.
6. **MC 17.** Options 2 to 4 do not grammatically complete the stem ("would make wildfires: Easier to breathe"). I reworded the French stem as a question so that every option reads naturally. The meaning and the key are unchanged.
7. All answer keys match the lesson.
