## Questions for Wayne

1. **Judges 21:25 (b014, MC 7, Fill 6).** LSG has "Chacun faisait ce qui lui semblait bon". It does not have "right in his own eyes", and the lesson leans on that image ("not each man's own eyes"). Following convention 1, I quoted LSG and added: « Le texte hébreu dit littéralement : "chacun faisait ce qui était droit à ses propres yeux" ». The later phrase "les propres yeux de chacun" now reads naturally. MC 7 quotes LSG.
2. **John 14:6, "the way".** LSG has "le chemin". I wrote "un seul Chemin" (capitalized) where the English capitalizes "one Way" (b020, MC 20 why). The pronouns for Christ are lowercase ("il", "ses bras"), as the brief requires. Please confirm "Chemin" as a capitalized title.
3. **"Who do you say that He is?" (b020, MC 20).** I wrote "Et vous, qui dites-vous qu'il est ?", which echoes Matthieu 16:15 LSG ("qui dites-vous que je suis?"). Please check that this matches how the course question is worded in the other units.
4. **"Do" / "done" (MC 10, MC 11, SA 5, SA 10).** I wrote « Faites » / « C'est fait ». MC 11 why says "it is finished", which I rendered as "tout est accompli", the LSG wording of John 19:30.
5. **"The gentleness Peter commanded" (b018, MC 16, SA 6–7).** I wrote "douceur" to match 1 Pierre 3:15 LSG ("avec douceur et respect").
6. **"Draws blood" (b008, Fill 2).** I rendered the idiom as "touche au vif", which is natural in French and not violent.

## Problems in the English source

1. **MC 10 and MC 11 (and SA 5, SA 10 models; SA 5 keyword "done").** The "do" versus "done" slogan never appears in the lesson. MC 11 is therefore untaught. MC 10 says the gospel "differs from all other religions", and its why says "every other faith says do". Both go beyond b016, which says the contrast is "a matter of emphasis... not an absolute line" and names grace currents in Pure Land Buddhism, bhakti and Islam. I translated them faithfully.
2. **SA 5 keyword "ladder".** No ladder appears in the lesson.
3. **SA 4 model contradicts the lesson.** The model says that on the atheist's account there is "no real good or evil" and that he "reached into the Christian's house" to borrow the moral law. The lesson explicitly calls atheist moral realism "a serious position, not a bluff". It only "presses a fair question" and says theism offers "a more natural grounding". The "borrowed sword" idea appears only in the b013 heading. This also affects the keyword "borrow" (SA 4, SA 10). The SA 10 model is closer to the lesson.
4. **SA 7 and SA 9 models add points the lesson does not make.** SA 7 says "a teacher who pretends otherwise loses trust" and that leaving the story open "keeps the door of prayer open". SA 9 says the name should be "received rather than merely debated". These are minor.
5. **The SA 6 English prompt passes its own keywords.** It hits "unbelief", "wound" and "argument". I reworded the French prompt so that it hits none, without changing what is asked. I also reworded SA 8 slightly so that it avoids "exclusi".
6. All answer keys match the lesson.
