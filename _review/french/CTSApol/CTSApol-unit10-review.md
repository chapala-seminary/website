## Questions for Wayne

1. **1 Peter 3:15, "with meekness and fear."** LSG has "avec douceur et respect", which I kept everywhere to match Unit 1 (b004, b008, MC3, Fill 1, SA1, SA2). Following convention 1, b008 adds one sentence: "Le texte grec dit littéralement « avec douceur et crainte »." Fill 1's answer is "douceur", and it also accepts "mansuétude", "humilité" and "gentillesse". LSG puts "avec douceur et respect" in the middle of the verse, so b004 quotes it with an opening ellipsis.
2. **1 Peter 3:15, "sanctify the Lord God."** LSG has "sanctifiez dans vos coeurs Christ le Seigneur" (b006). The MC3 distractor "Sanctify the Lord God" became « Sanctifiez… Christ le Seigneur » to match LSG.
3. **1 Peter 3:15, "give a defense / a reason."** LSG has "vous défendre… rendre raison de l'espérance". In b022, the English "Peter does not say 'a reason for the rules…'" became "rendre raison des règles…", which keeps the contrast.
4. **Revelation 3:20, "dine with him."** LSG has "je souperai avec lui". The b024 paraphrase uses LSG's wording. Fill 10's answer is "frappe".
5. **The wager (b018).** The English never names Pascal, and I did not name him either ("un sage penseur d'autrefois"). The keyword "pari" also matches the city name "Paris", so SA5 and SA10 use the forms "le pari / un pari / ce pari / parie / parié" instead.
6. **"Your walk talks louder than your talk talks" (b012, MC7, Fill 5).** The wordplay does not work in French. I wrote "votre conduite parle plus fort que vos paroles". Fill 5's answer is "conduite", and it also accepts "marche" (the biblical sense of "walk"), "vie", "comportement" and "manière de vivre".

## Problems in the English source

1. **Four words or five?** b008 says Peter added "four small words" ("with meekness and fear" is indeed four words). MC3 and the SA1 model say "five words". I translated each place as written ("quatre" in b008, "cinq" in MC3 and SA1).
2. **SA6 prompt** refers to a "'happiest and healthiest' point". The lesson never uses that phrase, and the SA6 keyword "caveat" is also not in the lesson. I reworded the French prompt to describe what b020 actually says and used "réserve/nuance" as the keyword.
3. **SA2, SA8 and SA9 models go beyond the lesson.** SA2 adds "Meekness keeps the truth from becoming a weapon". SA8 adds "invitation never becomes coercion". SA9 adds "decision". These ideas fit the lesson but are not stated in it. I kept them in light form.
4. **SA keywords.** Several English prompts hit their own keywords: SA1 (archer, arrow, peter), SA5 (wager, faith), SA7 (hope, reason, rules), SA8 (knock, door, latch). Following convention 10, every French prompt has at most 2 hits. All French models hit 8 of 8 groups.
5. All answer keys match the lesson.
