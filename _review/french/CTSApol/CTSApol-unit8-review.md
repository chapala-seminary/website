## Questions for Wayne

1. **Col 1:17, "consist" (b004, b006, b024, MC 15, Fill 1, SA 8).** LSG says "toutes choses subsistent en lui". In b024 I quoted Segond and added the sentence required by convention 1: "le texte grec dit littéralement qu'elles « tiennent ensemble » en lui". Fill 1 answer: "subsistent". It also accepts "subsiste", "tiennent ensemble" and "tiennent". I did not accept "consistent", because in French that verb does not carry this sense.
2. **Ps 14:1 (b025).** Following convention 11, I omitted the title ("Au chef des chantres. De David."). The quotation stops at "point de Dieu!", as in the Unit 2 draft.
3. **"Upholds all things by the word of His power" (b024).** This echoes Hebrews 1:3, so I used Segond's phrase: "soutient toutes choses par sa parole puissante". There are no quotation marks, which matches the English.
4. **"A Mind" (b020, b024, MC 13, 14, 16, SA 7, 8, 10).** I chose "une Intelligence", capitalized as in the English. "Esprit" would be confused with the Saint-Esprit and with "esprit" meaning the human mind, which this unit uses constantly. The human "mind" is "esprit".
5. **"Naked ape".** I used "singe nu", which is also the title of the French edition of Desmond Morris's book.
6. **"Naturalism / materialism".** I used "naturalisme" and "matérialisme", with "naturaliste" for the person. Please confirm that "naturaliste" (which can also mean a field biologist) reads correctly here. "Matérialiste" is the alternative.

## Problems in the English source

1. **b014 and MC 7 `why`: cross-reference to Unit 2.** The lesson says the "great dials we spoke of in Unit Two" were gravity, the electron's charge, cosmic expansion, and the properties of water and carbon. Unit 2 covers Earth-level fine-tuning only (distance from the sun, axial tilt, oxygen, ozone, moon, Jupiter). None of these cosmic constants appear in it. I translated the text as written.
2. **MC 2.** The stem says modern science was "largely born among believing men". The lesson hedges carefully ("many of the founders", and the history is "complex and draws on many streams"). The stem overstates the lesson. The key is unchanged.
3. **b022 (mind and brain, Charles Murray, terminal lucidity).** No exam item covers this section.
4. **SA keywords the lesson does not use.** "undercut" (SA 6), "design" (SA 5, SA 10) and "conscience" (SA 7) are not lesson words: the lesson says "feel guilt", and this unit never names a "Designer". Following convention 10, I built 8 French groups from the lesson's own vocabulary and added synonyms, for example "culpabil/conscience" and "concepteur/dessein/horloger".
5. **SA 8.** The keyword "christ" is not in b024, which says only "in Him". It comes from the context of Colossians. I kept it.
6. **SA prompts.** SA 1, 2, 3, 9 and 10 are worded so that no prompt gives away its own keywords (each hits at most 2 groups); the checker brought them closer to the English wording. French models hit 8 of 8 groups, except SA 7, which hits 7 of 8.
7. All answer keys match the lesson.
