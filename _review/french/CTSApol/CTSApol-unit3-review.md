## Questions for Wayne

1. **Ephesians 1:18, "eyes of your understanding" (b024, MC 16, Fill 10, SA 8).** LSG says "qu'il illumine les yeux de votre coeur". The English adds the gloss "the eyes of the heart", which would repeat the same words in French, so I dropped it. LSG already matches the lesson's point, and the Greek word here is "heart". Fill 10 answer: "cœur". It also accepts "coeur", "intelligence", "entendement" and "compréhension" (the NKJV sense).
2. **"Guarantee" / arrhabōn (b020, MC 12, Fill 9, SA 5).** In Ephesians 1:14 LSG has "un gage", and in 2 Corinthians 1:22 it has "les arrhes de l'Esprit". I wrote "Le mot grec traduit par « gage » est arrhabōn : l'acompte, les arrhes, le premier versement…". Fill 9 answer: "acompte". It also accepts "arrhes", "gage", "garantie" and "premier versement". I reworded the Fill 9 prompt so it does not give away "arrhes"; the checker also removed the article before the blank so "les arrhes" / "un gage" read naturally.
3. **Ephesians 1:13, "sealed with the Holy Spirit of promise".** LSG says "scellés du Saint-Esprit qui avait été promis". I quoted LSG as it stands.
4. **Romans 8:16, "bears witness with our spirit".** LSG says "rend témoignage à notre esprit". The Greek verb means "witnesses together with", but the lesson does not rely on the word "with", so I added no note. Fill 8 answer: "témoignage".
5. **Romans 2:15, "accuse or excuse".** LSG says "s'accusant ou se défendant tour à tour". I used that wording in b010 and in Fill 2.
6. **Romans 1:20, "without excuse" (b010, MC 3).** I quoted the LSG clause "Ils sont donc inexcusables" (still verse 20 in LSG). MC 3 answer: "Inexcusables".
7. **John 1:14.** LSG has "la parole" with a lowercase p in verse 14 but "la Parole" in verse 1. I kept both verbatim inside the quotation. Everywhere else I capitalized "Parole" as the English does.
8. **John 14:9 reference (b013).** Philip's request ("Seigneur, montre-nous le Père") is in verse 8, so I changed the citation to "Jean 14:8-9". MC 8 and Fill 4 still cite 14:9.
9. **"Convict, but not convert" (b011, MC 4, Fill 3).** I wrote "convaincre de péché" so that it cannot be read as "persuade". Please confirm.

## Problems in the English source

1. **MC 16.** The stem quotes "The eyes of the heart being enlightened", but that is not the NKJV wording ("eyes of your understanding"). In French it is not a problem, because LSG says "coeur".
2. **SA 2 keyword "scriptur".** The lesson says "the written Word" and never uses the word "Scripture". I kept an "écritur" group, which matches "l'Écriture".
3. **SA 9 keyword "breath".** The lesson never mentions breath or breathing. It only says "the air and the lungs". The SA 9 model uses "the air we breathe".
4. **SA 8 model.** Its last sentence ("the goal of apologetics is… nearness, where God is known and not merely concluded") goes beyond the lesson. I translated it anyway.
5. **SA 4 model.** The phrase "rests his weight on a syllogism within himself" is garbled. I translated the lesson's own wording instead.
6. All 20 MC answer keys and all fill-in answers match the lesson.
