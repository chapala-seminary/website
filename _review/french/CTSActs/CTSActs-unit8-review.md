# CTSActs Unit 8 — French draft review

## Questions for Wayne

1. **Acts 18:26, "took him aside" (b017, b047, MC3, Fill 1, SA1).** Segond: "le prirent avec eux". I quoted Segond and added: the Greek says literally "le prirent auprès d'eux", that is, apart, away from the public. The lesson's own prose and MC3 still say "à part". Fill 1 now quotes Segond with the answer **avec eux** (accept: à part, à l'écart, en privé, auprès d'eux, chez eux).
2. **Acts 20:31, "warn everyone" (b039, b040, b048, MC18, SA9).** Segond: "je n'ai cessé nuit et jour d'**exhorter** avec larmes chacun de vous". The lesson's third "lesson home" depends on "warn". I quoted Segond, added one sentence that the Greek verb also means "avertir", and used "avertir" in the lesson's own prose (b040, b048 heading). MC18's correct option uses Segond's "Exhorté". SA9 accepts both "exhort" and "avert".
3. **Acts 19:16, "all seven" (b027).** Segond (critical text) says the man "se rendit maître de **tous deux**". The lesson says he overpowered all seven. I did not quote the verse and kept the lesson's paraphrase "tous les sept". Should a note be added, or "all seven" softened?
4. **Acts 19:19, "books of curious arts" (b029).** Segond: "ceux qui avaient exercé les arts magiques". I quoted Segond and added that the Greek says literally "les choses curieuses".
5. **Acts 19:13/15 (b027, b028, MC9, Fill 4, SA5).** Segond: "Je vous conjure par Jésus que Paul prêche!" and "Je connais Jésus, et je sais qui est Paul; mais vous, qui êtes-vous?" Used throughout, including the echo at the end of b028.
6. **Acts 19:25–27, "our trade" (b031).** Segond: "notre bien-être dépend de cette industrie… notre industrie ne tombe en discrédit". The lesson's punchline "Our trade first" became "D'abord notre industrie". Demetrius is "orfèvre" (Segond's word for silversmith).
7. **Diana / Artemis (b031, b032, MC12–13, Fill 6).** Segond: "Grande est la Diane des Éphésiens!" Fill 6 answer **Diane** (accept: Artémis, déesse Diane, Diana).
8. **Acts 19:14, "chief priest" Sceva (b027, Fill 4).** Segond: "l'un des principaux sacrificateurs" (per convention 4).
9. **Acts 20:28, "church of God" (b039).** Segond reads "l'Église **du Seigneur**" (a textual variant). The lesson draws no doctrine from the wording, so I quoted Segond without comment. Should a note be added?
10. **Acts 20:29, "savage wolves" (b039, MC17, Fill 8).** Segond: "loups cruels". Fill 8 answer **loups**.
11. **Acts 20:10, 20:12, 21:4, 21:11, 21:14.** Segond: "se pencha sur lui et le prit dans ses bras"; "ce fut le sujet d'une grande consolation"; "poussés par l'Esprit"; "ceinture" (belt); "nous n'insistâmes pas… Que la volonté du Seigneur se fasse!" Quoted as is.
12. **Acts 18:25, "the things of the Lord".** Segond: "ce qui concerne Jésus". Quoted as is. The lesson does not depend on it.
13. **Names.** I used Segond's spellings: Aquilas, Priscille, Scéva, Tyrannus, Démétrius, Eutychus, Agabus, Milet, Trophime, Tychique, Onésime, Manahen. "Mars Hill" became "l'Aréopage". "Third story" is "troisième étage", as in Segond.

## Problems in the English source

1. **b022, Mandaeans.** "About two thousand people, mostly in Iraq and Iran" is far too low. Usual estimates are tens of thousands, and most now live in the diaspora. Their main sacred book is the Ginza Rabba, not a "gospel of John the Baptist", although they do have a *Book of John*. Translated as written.
2. **b027 vs Acts 19:16.** "Jumped on all seven" does not match the critical text ("both"). See Question 3.
3. **b017.** "Read those eight words": the quotation has more than eight words. I dropped the number ("Relisez ces mots"), as the Spanish did.
4. **b040, Moody.** "Many of the people in that congregation lost their lives that night" goes beyond what Moody's own accounts say (he said he never saw some of them again). Translated as written.
5. **b020, Edison.** The "eleven hundred ways" figure is a popular, unsourced version of the quote (it often appears as "10,000"). Translated as written.
6. **b032.** "Prayed in his lodgings" is not in Acts. Acts 19:30–31 says the disciples and the Asiarchs kept Paul away. Translated as written.
7. **MC12.** The correct option calls the goddess "one of the seven wonders". It was her temple. The French says "son temple était l'une des sept merveilles", which is a slight correction.
8. **SA6 model.** It says "decades of wages", but the lesson (b029) says about 150 years. I translated it as "plusieurs dizaines d'années".
9. **SA8 model.** The last clause repeats the earlier point ("preachers… risk falling asleep during a long sermon"). Translated faithfully.
10. **SA9 model.** "A savage wolf, and wolves like it" is garbled. I rendered it as "des loups cruels".
11. **SA3 keywords "narrow"/"broad".** The lesson never mentions the narrow or broad road. These ideas appear only in the model. They are kept as "étroit/resserré" and "large/spacieux".
12. **SA keywords.** Following convention 10, every French SA has 8 distinct groups. In SA2, SA4, SA5, SA6, SA7, SA9 and SA10, the English keywords ("twelve", "tyran/two/year/asia", "jesus/paul/know", "fifty/silver", "demetr/diana", "whole/counsel/tear", "agabus/bound/die") appear in the prompt itself. I reworded the French prompts and replaced the weak groups so that no prompt scores 3 or more on its own.
