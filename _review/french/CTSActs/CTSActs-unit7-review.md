## Questions for Wayne

1. **Acts 16:7, "the Spirit did not permit them".** Segond reads "l'Esprit de Jésus ne le leur permit pas" (the critical text). I quoted Segond in b015. No exam item depends on it.
2. **Acts 16:9, "Come over to Macedonia and help us".** Segond: "Passe en Macédoine, secours-nous!" This is used in b017 and Fill 1 (answer: Macédoine).
3. **Acts 16:25, "at midnight… singing hymns".** Segond: "Vers le milieu de la nuit… chantaient les louanges de Dieu". I quoted Segond in b021 and MC8. The prose and the "three lessons" keep "minuit" ("Chantez à minuit"). SA4 accepts both "minuit" and "milieu de la nuit".
4. **Acts 16:31, "you and your household".** Segond says "toi et ta famille", and it drops "Christ" ("Crois au Seigneur Jésus"). MC10 and Fill 4 use Segond. Fill 4 answer: famille. It also accepts maison, maisonnée, foyer and familles.
5. **Acts 16:30, "Sirs".** Segond has "Seigneurs". I kept this in b026 and MC9.
6. **Acts 17:6, "turned the world upside down".** Segond: "qui ont bouleversé le monde". Following convention 1, I added: "le texte grec dit littéralement qu'ils ont « mis le monde sens dessus dessous »". MC11 uses "bouleversé le monde".
7. **Acts 17:11, "more fair-minded… searched the Scriptures".** Segond: "Ces Juifs avaient des sentiments plus nobles… examinaient chaque jour les Écritures". This is used in b031, MC12, Fill 5 and SA5. SA5 accepts "examin" and "vérifi".
8. **Acts 17:16, "provoked… given over to idols".** Segond: "son esprit s'irriter… cette ville pleine d'idoles". I rendered the lesson's "paroxysmed" as a note that the verb gives us the word "paroxysme".
9. **Acts 17:18, "babbler".** Segond has "discoureur". The lesson's gloss is translated "ramasseur de graines". Fill 6 answer: ramasseur de graines. It also accepts ramasseur de grains, ramasse-graines, ramasseur de semences and picoreur de graines.
10. **Acts 17:23 inscription.** Segond: "À un dieu inconnu" (indefinite, lowercase "dieu"). MC14 shows it in capitals, "À UN DIEU INCONNU". Fill 8 answer: inconnu. The prose still speaks of "le Dieu inconnu" whom Paul proclaims.
11. **Acts 18:10, "I have many people in this city".** Segond: "j'ai un peuple nombreux dans cette ville". This is used in b040, MC19 and Fill 10 (answer: peuple; it also accepts gens and personnes).
12. **Names (convention 5).** I used Segond's forms: Aquilas, Priscille, Lydie, Thyatire, Denys l'aréopagite, Gallion, Bérée, Phoebé, Cenchrées.
13. **"Elect" (b040, SA9 model).** The English says "the elect of Corinth" and, in SA9, people "already marked for salvation". I translated these literally ("les élus", "destinées au salut"). Given CTS's conditional-election position, please confirm that this wording is acceptable or should be softened. The SA9 keywords include "élus/elect", as the English does.

## Problems in the English source

1. **b023–b025, the Perlman story.** This widely circulated account (1995, Avery Fisher Hall, the quoted remark) has never been confirmed by Perlman. It is generally treated as unverified. I translated it as written. Consider adding "as the story is told".
2. **b033, Pliny the Elder.** The line "easier to meet a god than a man" comes from Petronius (*Satyricon* 17), and it is not about Athens. Mars and Bacchus are also the Roman names; the Greek gods would be Ares and Dionysus. I translated it as written.
3. **b038, "thousand cult prostitutes".** This comes from Strabo and refers to the old Greek Corinth destroyed in 146 BC, not to Paul's Roman Corinth. Scholars dispute it.
4. **SA6 model** says Paul quoted "their own philosophers". The lesson (and Acts 17:28) says *poets*. In French I wrote "poètes et philosophes" so the model fits the lesson. Please confirm.
5. **SA4 model** says the audience "realized the music that remained mattered more than the string", and it describes Paul and Silas as "chained". The lesson says neither (they were in stocks). This is minor, and I translated it faithfully.
6. **SA7 model** says "most" gave the polite delay and that some believed "immediately". The lesson does not quantify either. **SA8 model** says Paul "could have insisted on his right to full compensation" (1 Corinthians 9). The lesson does not teach this.
7. **SA prompts.** Several English prompts contain their own keywords (SA1 "closed/door/spirit/forbid"; SA4 "perlman/string/jail/silas"; SA5 "berea"; SA6 "mars/hill/unknown/altar"; SA9 "many/people"). I reworded the French prompts so that none passes its own keywords (convention 10).
8. All MC answer keys are correct, and the source has no `why` fields.
