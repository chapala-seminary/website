## Questions for Wayne

1. **"Boldness" (Acts 4:29, 31).** LSG says "avec une pleine assurance" and "avec assurance". In b023 I quoted LSG and added one sentence saying the Greek word means boldness, the courage to speak plainly. Elsewhere the prose uses "hardiesse". Fill 5 answer: "assurance". It also accepts "hardiesse", "audace", "franchise" and "courage". MC 6 uses LSG's wording.
2. **"Son of Encouragement" (Acts 4:36).** LSG says "fils d'exhortation". In b025 I quoted LSG and noted that the Greek also means encouragement and consolation. Fill 6 and MC 7 use "fils d'exhortation" (MC 7 adds "ou d'encouragement"). For the name, Fill 6 accepts both "Barnabas" (LSG) and "Barnabé", the familiar form.
3. **"Uneducated and untrained" (Acts 4:13).** LSG says "des hommes du peuple sans instruction", and it puts the council's amazement at their "assurance" in the same verse. I used LSG's wording in b021, MC 5 and Fill 4.
4. **"Lest you even be found to fight against God" (Acts 5:39).** LSG says "Ne courez pas le risque d'avoir combattu contre Dieu". I quoted LSG in b032 and b036, and I used "combattre contre Dieu" throughout the prose, MC 14, Fill 9 and SA 6 and 8.
5. **"All the church" (Acts 5:11).** LSG says "toute l'assemblée". I quoted LSG and added a sentence noting that the Greek word is ekklèsia. MC 10 says "toute l'Église".
6. **"Greatly disturbed" (Acts 4:2).** LSG's "mécontents" is weaker. I quoted it and kept the lesson's joke about understatement as "c'est le moins que l'on puisse dire".
7. **"Indignation (literally jealousy)" (Acts 5:17).** LSG already has "remplis de jalousie", so I quoted that and turned the lesson's aside into "c'est bien le sens du mot grec".
8. **"For His name" (Acts 5:41).** LSG says "pour le nom de Jésus". I used this in b032, MC 15 and b045.
9. **"Of good reputation" (Acts 6:3).** LSG says "de qui l'on rende un bon témoignage". I used this in b038 and MC 18. For "in your own control" (Acts 5:4, MC 9) I used LSG's "à ta disposition".
10. **"Office of deacon."** I translated this as "fonction de diacre" rather than "office", which is more formal and less clear. Fill 10 answer: "diacre". It also accepts "diacres", "diaconat" and "diaconie".
11. **Fill 1.** The gate is "la porte… appelée la Belle" in LSG, and "la Belle Porte" in the French prose. Answer: "Belle". It also accepts "Belle Porte", "porte Belle" and "Belle-Porte".

## Problems in the English source

1. **SA 8 model** says Fuchida "was handed a tract by Jacob DeShazer". The lesson (b035) says an unnamed American handed him a tract that DeShazer wrote. I translated the model faithfully. **Updated 12 Oct 2026:** corrected in the English (beta PR) and in the French; see the update section at the top.
2. **b032** says Gamaliel was "by the New Testament's own admission, a wise man". Acts 5:34 calls him a teacher of the law respected by all the people; it does not call him wise. **MC 13** tests "respected by all the people", which the lesson never states. **Updated 12 Oct 2026:** corrected in the English (beta PR) and in the French; see the update section at the top.
3. **SA 6** asks whether Gamaliel became a believer. The lesson does not address this.
4. **MC 8** and **SA 4 model** say Ananias lied "to the Holy Spirit". The lesson quotes only 5:4 ("to God"); 5:3 (the Holy Spirit) is not quoted. The point is minor, and the answer is still correct.
5. **b017**: "three thousand more became five thousand (Acts 4:4)". This is garbled: Acts 4:4 says the number of men came to about five thousand. I translated it as "les trois mille devinrent cinq mille". **Updated 12 Oct 2026:** corrected in the English (beta PR) and in the French; see the update section at the top.
6. **b035** facts worth checking: Fuchida's rank was Commander (chūsa), not "Captain". The claims that he and DeShazer travelled together "for the next thirty years" and that "tens of thousands" were converted are overstated. Fuchida died in 1976 and mostly toured separately, including in the US. **Updated 12 Oct 2026:** corrected in the English (beta PR) and in the French; see the update section at the top.
7. The answer keys look correct, and no `why` fields are present.
