# CTSActs Unit 5 — French draft review

## Questions for Wayne

1. **Acts 10:4, "come up for a memorial" (b021).** Segond reads "sont montées devant Dieu, et il s'en est souvenu". I quoted Segond and added: "Le texte grec dit littéralement qu'elles sont montées « comme un mémorial » devant Dieu."
2. **Acts 10:15 and 10:28, "common or unclean" (b023, b024, MC9, Fill 3).** Segond has "souillé" (10:15) and "souillé et impur" (10:28). I added one sentence to b023: the Greek word rendered "souillé" literally means "commun", that is, profane. Fill 3 answer: **souillé** (accept: souillée, impur, commun, profane). MC9 uses "ne regarder aucun homme comme souillé et impur".
3. **Acts 10:44, "the Holy Spirit fell upon" (b027, SA4).** Segond: "descendit sur". I added: "Le verbe grec veut dire littéralement que l'Esprit « tomba » sur eux." In b028, "the falling of the Spirit" became "l'Esprit descendu sur les païens". SA4 uses "descend" in place of the English keyword "fell".
4. **Acts 11:17, "withstand God" (MC12).** Segond: "pouvais-je, moi, m'opposer à Dieu?" MC12 option B uses that wording.
5. **Acts 11:18, "repentance to life" (b028, b044, Fill 5).** Segond: "la repentance aussi aux païens, afin qu'ils aient la vie". Fill 5 answer: **repentance** (accept: repentir, conversion).
6. **Acts 12:24, "grew and multiplied" (b042, b046, SA10).** Segond: "se répandait de plus en plus, et le nombre des disciples augmentait". I added: "Le texte grec dit littéralement que la parole de Dieu « croissait et se multipliait »." The lesson's own line "kings die and the word grows" became "les rois meurent et la parole grandit".
7. **Acts 10:47, "Can anyone forbid water…?"**: Segond has "Peut-on refuser l'eau du baptême…?" I quoted it as is.
8. **Segond names and terms.** I used Segond's forms throughout: Énée (Aeneas), corroyeur (tanner), Rhode (Rhoda), Jacques (James), "cohorte dite italienne" (Italian Regiment), and Barnabas "fils d'exhortation" (Acts 4:36), where the English has "Son of Encouragement". For MC5, "Augustan Cohort" became "cohorte Auguste" (Acts 27:1). The Aramaic is spelled *Talitha koumi* / *Tabitha koumi*, as in Segond's Mark 5:41. The fill-ins also accept Aeneas and tanneur.
9. **MC3 distractor "Be still"** became "Tais-toi!", from Mark 4:39 in Segond. MC12 and MC10 distractors are quoted from Segond (Luc 10:29, Matt 27:46, 1 Cor 12:29, Actes 2:38, Jean 11:25, Jean 1:29).
10. **"Commissions" (the three-fold pattern: b032–b037, b045, Fill 6, SA5).** "Commissions" is not natural French in this sense, so I used "envois en mission". The result is "des conversions, des envois en mission et des célébrations", so the alliteration is lost. Is that acceptable?

## Problems in the English source

1. **b042**: "Acts is the only book on the planet that records what happened next." The next sentence says Josephus records the same death. Translated as written. Suggest "the only book that records it from heaven's side".
2. **b042**: "had killed an apostle in the morning… consumed by maggots before sundown." James's death (Acts 12:2) came well before Herod's death at Caesarea, not on the same day. Translated as written.
3. **b036**: Florrie (usually spelled "Florrie", not "Flory") Evans spoke at New Quay in **February** 1904, not "the autumn of 1904". I kept the English date. Wayne may want to check both the spelling and the date.
4. **b018**: "*Tabitha cumi* — two syllables off from *Talitha cumi*." The two phrases differ by one letter, not two syllables. I rendered it as "presque le même son".
5. **b027**: "The Andrew Principle" is used without explanation, and this unit never defines it. I translated it as "le principe d'André". Is it taught in an earlier unit?
6. **SA8**: the lesson tells the stories of James's death and Peter's release, but it never teaches the providence/sovereignty contrast that the model answer develops. The question goes beyond the lesson.
7. **SA keywords that repeat the prompt.** In SA1 (simon/tanner/joppa/vision), SA5 (hand/lord/convers/commis/celeb), SA6 (welsh/flory/evans/antioch), SA7 (christ/christian/antio/first/called) and SA10 (herod/word), the English prompt alone passes or nearly passes its own keywords. Under convention 10, the French prompts were reworded and given 8 distinct groups each. No French prompt reaches 3 hits.

## Adaptations

- "The housewife in Chapala" (b044) became "la mère de famille de Kinshasa".
- "Lincoln's followers, Lincolnites" (b039) became "comme on donne aux partisans d'un chef un nom tiré du sien".
- "Cable news" (b046) became "les nouvelles de la radio ou de la télévision".
- "Nothing but a tax return" (b017) became "qu'un acte de décès".
- "Marketing campaign" became "campagne publicitaire". "Physical therapy" became "kinésithérapie".
- Miles were converted to kilometres: a dozen miles → une vingtaine de km; thirty → une cinquantaine; three hundred → près de cinq cents.
