## Questions for Wayne

1. **Acts 1:3, "many infallible proofs".** LSG has only "plusieurs preuves". There is no word for "infallible", and the lesson's point about *tekmeriois* depends on that idea. I quoted LSG and added one sentence: "Le texte grec dit littéralement « plusieurs preuves certaines »". The heading (b018), Fill 3 and MC3 use "plusieurs preuves".
2. **Isaiah 40:31, "those who wait on the Lord".** LSG has "ceux qui se confient en l'Éternel". That loses the word "wait", which b023 depends on. I quoted LSG and added: "Le verbe hébreu traduit ici par « se confient » signifie littéralement « attendre avec espérance »." Please confirm.
3. **Acts 1:4, "the Promise of the Father".** LSG has "d'attendre ce que le Père avait promis". It has no noun "Promise". I changed Fill 5 to "…pour y ____ ce que le Père avait promis". SA2 has a keyword group for "promes/promis" and another for "le Père / du Père".
4. **Acts 1:1, "began both to do and teach".** LSG has "a commencé de faire et d'enseigner dès le commencement". The lesson's point still works. Fill 1 answer: "commencé" (also accepts "entrepris", "commencer", "commençait"). MC1 option: "a commencé de faire et d'enseigner".
5. **Acts 2:42, "the apostles' doctrine".** LSG has "l'enseignement des apôtres", and "fellowship" is "la communion fraternelle". MC14 and the lesson use LSG's wording. SA6 accepts both "doctrin" and "enseign". The SA6 model says "l'enseignement, ou la doctrine".
6. **Acts 2:47, "those who were being saved" (a continuing action).** LSG has "ceux qui étaient sauvés". I quoted LSG without comment. The lesson does not depend on the tense, and I did not want to tilt the text on the perseverance question.
7. **Acts 2:15.** The lesson says "nine in the morning". I added LSG's "la troisième heure du jour" in parentheses so that the reader can find it in the text.
8. **Acts 2:39, "Read those last seven words again".** The French has a different number of words, so I wrote "Relisez ces derniers mots".
9. **Ekklesia** is glossed as "l'assemblée de ceux qui sont appelés à sortir" (b017, MC17). Please confirm with the French reader that this is natural.
10. **"Gentile"** is rendered "non-Juif" (Luke, b037, SA10) rather than "païen". "Païen" can sound pejorative in Congolese church usage. SA10 accepts both forms.
11. **Acts 2:38, "for the remission of sins"** is quoted as LSG has it: "pour le pardon de vos péchés". I added no comment on how baptism relates to forgiveness.

## Problems in the English source

1. **MC9.** The key says the tongues miracle was "primarily a miracle of hearing". The lesson (b029) says the disciples "began to speak… in languages they had never learned", and Acts 2:4 says they spoke. The lesson never teaches that the miracle was mainly one of hearing; that idea appears only in the SA4 model. I translated it as written. Please consider rewording the key or adding a sentence to the lesson.
2. **b020, Frank Morison.** "Frank Morison" was the pen name of Albert Henry Ross, who was born in 1881. In 1930 he was about 49, not "young", and he worked in advertising and journalism; he was not a lawyer. The book came out in 1930, so it is now about 95 years later, not "three quarters of a century". MC18 calls him "the British lawyer". I translated the lesson and MC18 as written, using "juriste".
3. **b029, "sixteen named countries".** Acts 2:9–11 lists about 15 peoples and regions, depending on how you count; a count of 16 includes "Jews and proselytes" or Rome. This is minor.
4. **SA3 model.** It cites Sinai for the fire background. The lesson names the burning bush, the pillar of fire and Carmel, but not Sinai. My French model names both, and the keyword group accepts "carmel" or "sinaï".
5. **English SA keywords.** In SA1, SA2, SA3, SA6, SA8, SA9 and SA10, the English prompt alone reaches 3 or more keyword hits, so a student could pass by copying the question. SA6 reaches all 8. The problem keywords include "neglect", "modern", "marks", "personal", "specific", "apply" and "sign". Following settled convention 10, I replaced those groups in French with ideas taken from the answers. Each French prompt now reaches no more than 2 hits.
6. **b016.** Paul's death "around AD 68" is late compared with the usual dating of 64–67. I kept it as written.
