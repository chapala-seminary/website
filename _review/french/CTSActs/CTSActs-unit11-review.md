## Problems in the English source

1. **b016 / MC3**: The lesson says "every other book of the Bible has a doxology, an amen, a closing benediction, a colophon", and MC3 makes this the correct answer. This is not accurate. Jonah ends with a question, and many Old Testament books, such as Judges, Ruth and Ezekiel, have no formal closing. Consider wording it as "an unusually open ending". **Updated 12 Oct 2026:** corrected in the English (beta PR) and in the French; see the update section at the top.
2. **b032 / MC13**: These cite "the praetorian guard of Caesar (Philippians 4:22)". Philippians 4:22 is "ceux de la maison de César", Caesar's household. The praetorian guard is in Philippians 1:13. Translated as written. **Updated 12 Oct 2026:** corrected in the English (beta PR) and in the French; see the update section at the top.
3. **b028**: "three of his four (the Sanhedrin, Felix, Festus and Agrippa)" lists four hearings while saying three. Also, the Sanhedrin hearing was in Jerusalem, not Caesarea. Translated as written. **Updated 12 Oct 2026:** corrected in the English (beta PR) and in the French; see the update section at the top.
4. **MC5** has 5 options, not 4. I kept all 5 in the same order. Please check that the engine handles this.
5. **MC12**: The lesson never calls the nephew a "teenager". It never names Lysias and never says the boy went to the tribune; Acts 23:16 says he first told Paul. The correct option can still be guessed from the lesson, but the lesson does not teach it. **Updated 12 Oct 2026:** corrected in the English (beta PR) and in the French; see the update section at the top.
6. **b041 / MC10 / MC11 / Fill 6**: Saying Apollos was discipled at a "kitchen table" and Lydia's church met in her "living room" goes beyond the text. Acts 18:26 says only that they "took him aside" (Segond: "le prirent avec eux"), and Acts 16:15, 40 says "her house". The French follows the English ("autour de la table de leur cuisine", "dans son salon"); if you change the English, change MC10/MC11 too. **Updated 12 Oct 2026:** corrected in the English (beta PR) and in the French; see the update section at the top.
7. **b034**: "The silversmiths… lost their pension fund" is anachronistic. In Acts 19:25–27 the silversmiths fear losing their trade; Acts does not report a loss. Translated as "perdirent leurs revenus".
8. **Short-answer prompts SA2, SA4–SA10**: Several English prompts contain their own keywords; for example, SA7 names Agrippa and Felix with "almost" and "convenient time". Following convention 10, I reworded the French prompts so the prompt alone does not reach 3 hits, and I used 8 distinct French keyword ideas per question.
