# Spanish drafts for Dr. Cook — 30 Sept 2026

These are drafts, written to make the Spanish view complete. Each is stored as
an unreviewed translation (`machine` in the lesson files), so they are easy to
find and correct in the editor. Please mark anything to change; a corrected
line becomes a reviewed one. Quoted verses are from the RVG (checked at
bibliaparalela.com/rvg): Nehemías 8:8 and Mateo 28:19.

## What changed on the page

- **Diagrams.** They were already drawn in both languages: nearly every English
  line has its Spanish line under it, and they stay that way. What was missing
  was Spanish for the lines that had none (dates, verse references, a few
  labels). Those lines now switch with the page's language.
- **Diagram captions** (the line under each diagram) were shown in English and
  Spanish together; each now shows only in its own language. 15 diagrams.
- **Systematic Theology, Unit 7** ("The Order of Salvation") had its own Spanish
  copy with eleven lines left in English; they are now Spanish (listed at the end).
- **Headings and buttons** listed below now switch with the language. The ones
  that were typed straight into the page (Ruth & Esther header, Christian
  Education byline, some buttons) are now editable text like the rest.
