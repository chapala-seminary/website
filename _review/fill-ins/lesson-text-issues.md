# Problems found in the lesson text while drafting the fill-ins

While drafting fill-in-the-blank questions (September 2026), the drafters read every lesson in English and Spanish and noted problems in the lessons themselves. None of these was changed: they are lesson content, for Dr. Cook to decide. No fill-in question was built on a passage listed here where the problem would affect the answer.

"u3" means unit 3; "b021" is the block id in `src/content/lessons/<course>/<unit>.json`; "ES" / "EN" is the Spanish / English text.

Separately, ten blocks in three lessons show literal escape codes to students (for example "serm\u00f3n" for "sermón"): CTSDP unit 1 (b020–b023), CTSHS unit 3 and CTSLOC unit 7. That is a technical fix, suggested as its own task.
