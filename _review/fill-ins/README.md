# Fill-in-the-blank questions — review sheets

For Dr. Wayne Cook. Every unit of the 40 courses on the shared exam engine now has ten fill-in-the-blank questions: 451 units, 4,510 questions. Associate, Th.M. and M.Div. students must get 9 of 10 right in each unit; Certificate of Ministry students answer them for review only.

- **1 Peter** was the pilot, sent for review first.
- **The other 39 courses** were drafted on 26 Sept 2026, before the pilot review came back, at Robert Allen's request. Anything the pilot review changes about the style will be applied to them too.

Each sheet lists every question with its answer in English and Spanish. Two more lists:

- [unsure.md](unsure.md) — questions where the drafter thought another answer might also be right.
- [lesson-text-issues.md](lesson-text-issues.md) — typos, mistranslations and contradictions found in the lessons themselves while drafting. Nothing in the lessons was changed.

Corrections can be made in the CMS (Exam questions → a unit → Fill in the blank) or sent to Robert.

The drafts each sheet was generated from are in `drafts/`; the unit files in `src/content/units/` are what the site uses.
