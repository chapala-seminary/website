# Fill-in-the-blank questions — WiseSpeak Preaching

For review by Dr. Wayne Cook, the course author. Generated by `tools/add-fill-ins-page.mjs` from the drafts; do not edit this sheet — send corrections and they go into the drafts.

10 units, 100 questions. Each sentence has one gap (____). Associate, Th.M. and M.Div. students must get 9 of 10 right in each unit; Certificate of Ministry students see them for review only.

**How answers are marked.** The student's answer must be exactly the answer shown (or an "also" alternative), in English or Spanish. Capitals, punctuation, extra spaces and a leading article ("the", "a", "la") are ignored; accents and spelling are not. Each answer is marked the moment the student presses Check, and the right answer is then shown.
