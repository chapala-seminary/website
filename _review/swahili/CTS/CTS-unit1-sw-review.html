I. Why We Read the Old Testament FirstI. Kwa Nini Tunasoma Agano la Kale Kwanza
Imagine handing someone the second half of a great novel and asking them to make sense of it. They might enjoy the prose, follow the dialogue, even guess at the plot — but they would never really know who any of the characters are or why anything matters. That is what it is to read the New Testament without the Old. Three quarters of our Bible is the Old Testament. The world Jesus stepped into was the world of Moses and David and Isaiah, and the first thing He told the disciples on the road to Emmaus, after His resurrection, was that the Scriptures had been about Him all along (Luke 24:27). Those Scriptures were not the Gospels. They were the Old Testament.Fikiria kumpa mtu nusu ya pili ya riwaya kubwa na kumwomba aielewe. Anaweza kufurahia maandishi, kufuata mazungumzo, hata kukisia mwelekeo wa hadithi — lakini hatawahi kuwajua kwa kweli wahusika ni akina nani wala kwa nini jambo lolote lina maana. Ndivyo ilivyo kusoma Agano Jipya bila Agano la Kale. Robo tatu ya Biblia yetu ni Agano la Kale. Ulimwengu ambao Yesu aliingia ulikuwa ulimwengu wa Musa na Daudi na Isaya, na jambo la kwanza alilowaambia wanafunzi njiani kuelekea Emau, baada ya kufufuka kwake, ni kwamba Maandiko yalikuwa yakimhusu Yeye tangu mwanzo (Luka 24:27). Maandiko hayo hayakuwa Injili. Yalikuwa Agano la Kale.
Jesus went further than that. He told the religious leaders of His day, "You search the Scriptures because you think that in them you have eternal life; and it is they that bear witness about me" (John 5:39). The Bible Jesus held in His hands was the Old Testament, and He said it was a book about Him. So when we open Genesis we are not stepping back into ancient history. We are stepping into the same story, only earlier. Every great theme of the New Testament — grace, faith, covenant, sacrifice, redemption, kingdom — appears first in the Old, like a stream that begins as a small spring high in the mountains and grows into a mighty river by the time it reaches the sea.Yesu alikwenda mbali zaidi ya hapo. Aliwaambia viongozi wa dini wa siku zake, "Mwayachunguza maandiko, kwa maana mnadhani kwamba ndani yake mnao uzima wa milele; nayo ndiyo yanayonishuhudia" (Yohana 5:39). Biblia aliyoishika Yesu mikononi mwake ilikuwa Agano la Kale, naye alisema ni kitabu kinachomhusu Yeye. Kwa hiyo tunapofungua kitabu cha Mwanzo hatuurudii historia ya kale tu. Tunaingia katika hadithi ile ile, ila katika hatua ya mapema zaidi. Kila wazo kuu la Agano Jipya — neema, imani, agano, dhabihu, ukombozi, ufalme — linaonekana kwanza katika Agano la Kale, kama mto unaoanza kama chemchemi ndogo juu milimani na kukua kuwa mto mkubwa wenye nguvu unapofika baharini.
The Old Testament also gives us the language of worship. The Psalms shape every Christian's prayer life. The Ten Commandments still stand behind our moral conscience. And every drop of blood ever shed on every altar is a shadow that points to one cross outside Jerusalem.Agano la Kale pia linatupa lugha ya ibada. Zaburi zinaumba maisha ya maombi ya kila Mkristo. Amri Kumi bado zinasimama nyuma ya dhamiri yetu ya kimaadili. Na kila tone la damu lililowahi kumwagwa juu ya kila madhabahu ni kivuli kinachoelekeza kwenye msalaba mmoja nje ya Yerusalemu.
II. The World of the Old Testament: Geography, Language, and CanonII. Ulimwengu wa Agano la Kale: Jiografia, Lugha, na Orodha ya Vitabu Vitakatifu
The land of Israel sits at the crossroads of three continents — Africa, Asia, and Europe. Every great empire of the ancient world — Egypt, Assyria, Babylon, Persia, Greece, Rome — passed through that narrow corridor. That was no accident. God placed His people on the world's busiest highway so that when the message went out, it would travel in every direction. He was thinking of you and me long before we were born.Nchi ya Israeli iko kwenye njia panda ya mabara matatu — Afrika, Asia, na Ulaya. Kila milki kuu ya ulimwengu wa kale — Misri, Ashuru, Babeli, Uajemi, Ugiriki, Roma — ilipita katika njia hiyo nyembamba. Hilo halikuwa ajali. Mungu aliweka watu wake kwenye barabara yenye shughuli nyingi zaidi duniani ili ujumbe utakapotoka usafiri kila upande. Alikuwa akitufikiria mimi na wewe kabla hatujazaliwa.
The Old Testament was written largely in Hebrew, with a few sections in Aramaic. Hebrew is a concrete, picture-rich language. Where Greek excels at abstract ideas, Hebrew paints. The word for "know" — yada — is not a filing-cabinet word. It is a relationship word. When Genesis says Adam knew his wife Eve, the word means more than information; it means intimacy. Knowing God in Scripture is never a matter of passing a quiz. It is friendship.Agano la Kale liliandikwa zaidi kwa lugha ya Kiebrania, na sehemu chache kwa Kiaramu. Kiebrania ni lugha thabiti, iliyojaa picha. Ambapo Kigiriki hung'aa katika mawazo ya kidhahania, Kiebrania huchora picha. Neno la "kujua" — yada — si neno la kabati la nyaraka. Ni neno la uhusiano. Mwanzo inaposema Adamu alimjua mkewe Hawa, neno hilo lina maana zaidi ya taarifa; linamaanisha ukaribu wa ndani. Kumjua Mungu katika Maandiko si kamwe suala la kufaulu mtihani. Ni urafiki.
The Hebrew Bible has three divisions: the Law (Torah), the Prophets (Nevi'im), and the Writings (Ketuvim) — together, the Tanakh. Jesus referred to these three divisions in Luke 24:44 when He said all things written about Him in "the Law of Moses, the Prophets, and the Psalms" must be fulfilled. Our course will follow a similar order — beginning with the five books of Moses, then moving through the historical books, the wisdom literature, and finally the prophets.Biblia ya Kiebrania ina sehemu tatu: Sheria (Torah), Manabii (Nevi'im), na Maandishi (Ketuvim) — kwa pamoja, Tanakh. Yesu alirejea sehemu hizi tatu katika Luka 24:44 aliposema mambo yote yaliyoandikwa juu yake katika "torati ya Musa, na katika manabii, na katika zaburi" lazima yatimie. Kozi yetu itafuata mpangilio unaofanana — tukianza na vitabu vitano vya Musa, kisha vitabu vya historia, fasihi ya hekima, na hatimaye manabii.
III. The Book of Genesis: The Book of BeginningsIII. Kitabu cha Mwanzo: Kitabu cha Mwanzo wa Vitu Vyote
Moses wrote Genesis a thousand years before the Greek historians were born. And yet when you open it, it reads as if it were written this morning, because human nature has not changed since chapter three.Musa aliandika kitabu cha Mwanzo miaka elfu moja kabla wanahistoria wa Kigiriki hawajazaliwa. Hata hivyo unapokifungua, kinasomeka kana kwamba kimeandikwa asubuhi ya leo, kwa sababu asili ya mwanadamu haijabadilika tangu sura ya tatu.
Genesis is the book of beginnings. It opens with the most majestic sentence ever written: "In the beginning, God created the heavens and the earth" (Genesis 1:1). That single line answers three questions every soul eventually asks. Who? God. What? The heavens and the earth — everything that is. When? In the beginning, before time as we know it. From that one verse the rest of Scripture flows.Mwanzo ni kitabu cha mianzo. Kinaanza kwa sentensi tukufu kuliko zote zilizowahi kuandikwa: "Hapo mwanzo Mungu aliumba mbingu na nchi" (Mwanzo 1:1). Mstari huo mmoja unajibu maswali matatu ambayo kila nafsi hujiuliza wakati fulani. Nani? Mungu. Nini? Mbingu na nchi — kila kilichopo. Lini? Hapo mwanzo, kabla ya wakati kama tunavyoujua. Kutoka katika mstari huo mmoja, Maandiko yote yanatiririka.
In Genesis we find the origins of the universe, of humanity, of marriage, of sin, of sacrifice, of the nations, and of the languages of the world. It has been called the "seed plot of the Bible," because every doctrine that grows tall in the rest of Scripture is first planted here. Creation. Fall. Covenant. Faith. Promise. Redemption. They all begin in Genesis.Katika Mwanzo tunapata chimbuko la ulimwengu, la wanadamu, la ndoa, la dhambi, la dhabihu, la mataifa, na la lugha za ulimwengu. Kimeitwa "shamba la mbegu la Biblia," kwa sababu kila fundisho linalokua na kuwa refu katika Maandiko yote lilipandwa kwanza hapa. Uumbaji. Anguko. Agano. Imani. Ahadi. Ukombozi. Vyote vinaanzia katika Mwanzo.
To be made in the image of God (Genesis 1:26–27) means that human beings are uniquely able to relate to God, to exercise responsible dominion over creation, to create, to love, and to reason. That image was marred by the fall, but never erased — which is why human life still has inherent dignity, no matter how broken the life. The whole storyline of redemption, from Genesis to Revelation, is the story of God restoring that image in Christ (Romans 8:29; Colossians 3:10).Kuumbwa kwa sura ya Mungu (Mwanzo 1:26–27) kunamaanisha kwamba wanadamu wana uwezo wa pekee wa kuwa na uhusiano na Mungu, kutawala uumbaji kwa uwajibikaji, kubuni, kupenda, na kufikiri. Sura hiyo iliharibiwa na anguko, lakini haikufutwa kamwe — ndiyo maana uhai wa mwanadamu bado una heshima ya asili, haijalishi maisha hayo yamevunjika kiasi gani. Hadithi nzima ya ukombozi, kuanzia Mwanzo hadi Ufunuo, ni habari ya Mungu kuirejesha sura hiyo ndani ya Kristo (Warumi 8:29; Wakolosai 3:10).
A. The Six Days of Creation: Three Faithful ViewsA. Siku Sita za Uumbaji: Mitazamo Mitatu Iliyo Aminifu
Genesis 1 describes the work of creation in six days, followed by a seventh day of rest. Conservative evangelicals have offered several interpretations of these "days" while standing together on the central truth that God alone is the Creator and that what He made is good. Three views are most common:Mwanzo 1 inaeleza kazi ya uumbaji katika siku sita, ikifuatwa na siku ya saba ya kupumzika. Wainjilisti wahafidhina wametoa tafsiri kadhaa za "siku" hizi huku wakisimama pamoja juu ya kweli kuu kwamba Mungu peke yake ndiye Muumba na kwamba alichokiumba ni chema. Mitazamo mitatu ndiyo ya kawaida zaidi:
The Calendar (Literal) Day View — The Hebrew word yom with a numerical adjective and "evening and morning" indicates ordinary 24-hour days. The Sabbath command (Exodus 20:11) grounds the weekly cycle in creation week itself.Mtazamo wa Siku za Kalenda (Halisi) — Neno la Kiebrania yom likiwa na kivumishi cha namba na maneno "jioni na asubuhi" linaonyesha siku za kawaida za saa 24. Amri ya Sabato (Kutoka 20:11) inaweka mzunguko wa kila wiki katika juma lenyewe la uumbaji.
The Day-Age (Progressive Creation) View — Reads the "days" as long, indefinite periods. Defenders point to Psalm 90:4 and 2 Peter 3:8, where a thousand years and a day are interchangeable from God's vantage point.Mtazamo wa Siku-Enzi (Uumbaji wa Taratibu) — Unazisoma "siku" kama vipindi virefu visivyo na kikomo maalum. Watetezi wanarejea Zaburi 90:4 na 2 Petro 3:8, ambapo miaka elfu na siku moja ni sawa machoni pa Mungu.
The Framework (Revelatory) View — Reads the six days as a literary structure: three days of forming (light, waters, land) parallel three days of filling (lights, fish and birds, animals and man), framing creation as ordered, purposeful, and good.Mtazamo wa Mfumo (Ufunuo) — Unazisoma siku sita kama muundo wa kifasihi: siku tatu za kuumba umbo (nuru, maji, nchi kavu) zinaendana na siku tatu za kujaza (mianga, samaki na ndege, wanyama na mwanadamu), zikionyesha uumbaji kuwa wenye utaratibu, kusudi, na wema.
All three views agree on what matters most: that God is the sovereign Creator, that creation was originally good, and that human beings — uniquely — are made in His image. The doctrine of creation does not finally rest on a particular timeline. It rests on the Creator.Mitazamo yote mitatu inakubaliana juu ya yaliyo muhimu zaidi: kwamba Mungu ni Muumba mwenye enzi, kwamba uumbaji ulikuwa mwema hapo mwanzo, na kwamba wanadamu — kwa namna ya pekee — wameumbwa kwa sura yake. Fundisho la uumbaji hatimaye halitegemei ratiba fulani ya wakati. Linamtegemea Muumba.
B. The Fall: How the Story BrokeB. Anguko: Jinsi Hadithi Ilivyovunjika
By Genesis 3 the story has broken. Adam and Eve, tempted by the serpent, eat the one fruit they were told to leave alone. The first thing that happens after their disobedience is not that they die — that comes later — but that they hide. The fellowship is fractured before the funeral arrives. Shame, blame, and fear walk into the garden where intimacy used to live, and they have lived there ever since.Kufikia Mwanzo 3 hadithi imevunjika. Adamu na Hawa, wakijaribiwa na nyoka, wanakula tunda lile moja walilokatazwa. Jambo la kwanza linalotokea baada ya kutotii kwao si kwamba wanakufa — hilo linakuja baadaye — bali kwamba wanajificha. Ushirika unavunjika kabla mazishi hayajafika. Aibu, lawama, na hofu zinaingia katika bustani ambamo ukaribu ulikuwa ukikaa, na zimekaa humo tangu wakati huo.
And yet, in the very moment of judgment, God speaks a promise. He tells the serpent that "the seed of the woman" will one day crush his head, even as the serpent strikes the heel of that promised seed (Genesis 3:15). Theologians call this verse the Protoevangelium — the "first gospel." Before Adam and Eve have left the garden, God has already promised a Redeemer. The cross was not God's emergency response to a problem He did not see coming.Hata hivyo, katika saa ile ile ya hukumu, Mungu anatamka ahadi. Anamwambia nyoka kwamba "uzao wa mwanamke" siku moja atamponda kichwa, ingawa nyoka atamchoma kisigino uzao huo ulioahidiwa (Mwanzo 3:15). Wanatheolojia huuita mstari huu Protoevangelium — "injili ya kwanza." Kabla Adamu na Hawa hawajaondoka bustanini, Mungu alikuwa ameshaahidi Mkombozi. Msalaba haukuwa jibu la dharura la Mungu kwa tatizo ambalo hakulitarajia.
C. The Flood, Babel, and the Doors We Mark "Emergency Use Only"C. Gharika, Babeli, na Milango Tunayoiandika "Kwa Matumizi ya Dharura Tu"
By Genesis 6, sin has so corrupted the human race that God grieves He ever made them. The flood that follows is one of the great pictures of judgment in Scripture, but it is also a picture of grace. One man — Noah — finds favor in God's eyes, and through him a faithful remnant is preserved. That principle, of God preserving a believing few through judgment, will run through the whole Old Testament. From Noah to the prophets, the remnant is always small, but it is always saved.Kufikia Mwanzo 6, dhambi imeuharibu mwanadamu kiasi kwamba Mungu anahuzunika kwa kuwaumba. Gharika inayofuata ni mojawapo ya picha kuu za hukumu katika Maandiko, lakini pia ni picha ya neema. Mtu mmoja — Nuhu — anapata kibali machoni pa Mungu, na kupitia yeye mabaki waaminifu wanahifadhiwa. Kanuni hiyo, ya Mungu kuhifadhi wachache wanaoamini katika hukumu, itapita katika Agano la Kale lote. Kuanzia Nuhu hadi manabii, mabaki huwa wachache siku zote, lakini huokolewa siku zote.
When the waters recede, God meets Noah with a covenant. He promises that the flood will never again destroy the earth, and He sets the rainbow in the clouds as the sign of that promise (Genesis 9:11–13). Every storm since then carries that bow as a reminder: judgment has limits, but mercy does not.Maji yanapopungua, Mungu anakutana na Nuhu kwa agano. Anaahidi kwamba gharika haitaharibu dunia tena kamwe, naye anaweka upinde wa mvua mawinguni kuwa ishara ya ahadi hiyo (Mwanzo 9:11–13). Kila dhoruba tangu hapo hubeba upinde huo kama ukumbusho: hukumu ina mipaka, lakini rehema haina.
After the flood, humanity quickly regroups in rebellion. At Babel they decide to build a tower up to heaven and "make a name" for themselves (Genesis 11:4). Notice the direction. Babel is man's religion: the human race climbing up to God by its own effort. Bethlehem is God's gospel: the Lord Himself coming down to man.Baada ya gharika, wanadamu wanakusanyika haraka tena katika uasi. Huko Babeli wanaamua kujenga mnara hadi mbinguni na "kujifanyia jina" (Mwanzo 11:4). Angalia mwelekeo. Babeli ni dini ya mwanadamu: jamii ya wanadamu ikipanda kumfikia Mungu kwa juhudi zake yenyewe. Bethlehemu ni injili ya Mungu: Bwana mwenyewe akishuka kwa mwanadamu.
I have often thought that we still build little Babels in our own lives. We treat God the way we treat the door at the end of a hospital corridor — the one with the red sign that says "For Emergency Use Only. Alarm Will Sound." We are perfectly comfortable wandering the halls without Him until something terrifying happens, and then we slap that door open and expect Him to come running. But Genesis is showing us a different God: not an emergency exit, but the very air we are made to breathe. At Babel He scattered the languages so that the tower could not stand. At Pentecost, in Acts 2, He reversed the miracle — the same Spirit gave one message in many tongues, so that the gospel would gather what pride had once divided.Mara nyingi nimefikiri kwamba bado tunajenga Babeli ndogo katika maisha yetu wenyewe. Tunamtendea Mungu kama tunavyoutendea mlango ulio mwishoni mwa korido ya hospitali — ule wenye ubao mwekundu usemao "Kwa Matumizi ya Dharura Tu. Kengele Italia." Tunastarehe kabisa kutembea kwenye korido bila Yeye hadi jambo la kutisha litokee, kisha tunaufungua mlango huo kwa nguvu na kutarajia aje mbio. Lakini Mwanzo inatuonyesha Mungu tofauti: si mlango wa dharura, bali hewa yenyewe tuliyoumbwa kuipumua. Huko Babeli alitawanya lugha ili mnara usisimame. Siku ya Pentekoste, katika Matendo 2, alibatilisha muujiza huo — Roho yule yule alitoa ujumbe mmoja kwa lugha nyingi, ili injili ikusanye kile ambacho kiburi kilikuwa kimekigawanya.
D. The Scarlet Thread BeginsD. Uzi Mwekundu Waanza
Before Genesis ends, four scenes have been painted in red that the rest of the Bible will keep painting. Trace them together and you have what preachers have long called the Scarlet Thread — the unbroken line of innocent blood that runs from Eden to Calvary.Kabla Mwanzo haijaisha, matukio manne yamechorwa kwa rangi nyekundu ambayo Biblia nzima itaendelea kuyachora. Yafuatilie kwa pamoja nawe utapata kile ambacho wahubiri wameita kwa muda mrefu Uzi Mwekundu — mstari usiokatika wa damu isiyo na hatia unaoanzia Edeni hadi Kalvari.
The first scene is in the garden itself. The moment Adam and Eve cover themselves with fig leaves, the Lord God Himself kills an animal and makes them garments of skin (Genesis 3:21). Sin requires a covering, and the covering requires a death. The first funeral is held in Eden, and God presides. Before any altar is built, before any priest is ordained, God has already shown that sin will be answered by substitutionary sacrifice — and that God Himself will be the one who provides it.Tukio la kwanza liko bustanini penyewe. Mara Adamu na Hawa wanapojifunika kwa majani ya mtini, Bwana Mungu mwenyewe anaua mnyama na kuwatengenezea mavazi ya ngozi (Mwanzo 3:21). Dhambi inahitaji kifuniko, na kifuniko hicho kinahitaji kifo. Mazishi ya kwanza yanafanyika Edeni, na Mungu ndiye anayeongoza. Kabla madhabahu yoyote haijajengwa, kabla kuhani yeyote hajawekwa wakfu, Mungu alikuwa ameshaonyesha kwamba dhambi itajibiwa kwa dhabihu mbadala — na kwamba Mungu mwenyewe ndiye atakayeitoa.
The second scene is one generation later. Abel brings the firstborn of his flock; Cain brings produce from the ground. The Lord receives Abel's bloody offering and rejects Cain's bloodless one (Genesis 4:4). Cain murders his brother, and Abel's blood cries from the ground — the first innocent blood in the world. Centuries later the writer of Hebrews will say that the blood of Jesus speaks a better word than the blood of Abel (Hebrews 12:24).Tukio la pili liko kizazi kimoja baadaye. Habili analeta mzaliwa wa kwanza wa kundi lake; Kaini analeta mazao ya ardhi. Bwana anaipokea sadaka ya Habili yenye damu na kuikataa ya Kaini isiyo na damu (Mwanzo 4:4). Kaini anamuua ndugu yake, na damu ya Habili inalia kutoka ardhini — damu ya kwanza isiyo na hatia duniani. Karne nyingi baadaye mwandishi wa Waebrania atasema kwamba damu ya Yesu inanena mambo mema kuliko damu ya Habili (Waebrania 12:24).
The third scene is on the deck of an ark whose door has just opened on a washed world. Noah's first act in the new earth is not a feast or a celebration. He builds an altar and offers burnt offerings to the Lord (Genesis 8:20). The thread is there at every new beginning, because every new beginning requires a death.Tukio la tatu liko juu ya sitaha ya safina ambayo mlango wake umefunguliwa tu kuelekea ulimwengu ulioosha. Tendo la kwanza la Nuhu katika dunia mpya si karamu wala sherehe. Anajenga madhabahu na kumtolea Bwana sadaka za kuteketezwa (Mwanzo 8:20). Uzi huo upo kila mwanzo mpya, kwa sababu kila mwanzo mpya unahitaji kifo.
The fourth scene is on a mountain ridge called Moriah. We will return to that mountain in a moment, but for now: an old man raises a knife over his only son, and the knife is stopped at the last instant by a ram caught in the thicket (Genesis 22:13). Abraham names the mountain Jehovah-Jireh — "the Lord will provide." Two thousand years later, on that same ridge of hills, God will provide a real Lamb. The thread will run on — through the Passover blood on the doorposts, through Rahab's scarlet cord in the window at Jericho, through the temple sacrifices, through the Suffering Servant of Isaiah, through the pierced Messiah of Zechariah. But it begins here, in Genesis, where God's own hand killed the first animal in the garden.Tukio la nne liko juu ya kilima cha mlima uitwao Moria. Tutarudi kwenye mlima huo baada ya muda mfupi, lakini kwa sasa: mzee anainua kisu juu ya mwanawe wa pekee, na kisu kinazuiwa dakika ya mwisho na kondoo dume aliyenaswa vichakani (Mwanzo 22:13). Ibrahimu analiita jina la mlima huo Yehova-Yire — "BWANA atatoa." Miaka elfu mbili baadaye, juu ya safu ile ile ya vilima, Mungu atatoa Mwana-Kondoo halisi. Uzi huo utaendelea — kupitia damu ya Pasaka juu ya miimo ya milango, kupitia kamba nyekundu ya Rahabu dirishani huko Yeriko, kupitia dhabihu za hekalu, kupitia Mtumishi Anayeteswa wa Isaya, kupitia Masihi aliyechomwa wa Zekaria. Lakini unaanzia hapa, katika Mwanzo, ambapo mkono wa Mungu mwenyewe uliua mnyama wa kwanza bustanini.
E. The Patriarchs: Five Men, Five Pictures of the Christian LifeE. Mababu: Watu Watano, Picha Tano za Maisha ya Kikristo
From Genesis 12 to the end of the book, the camera narrows from the whole human race to one family. God calls Abram out of Ur and gives him three great promises: a land, a people, and a blessing that will reach to all the nations of the earth. Abraham believed God, and that faith was credited to him as righteousness (Genesis 15:6) — the verse the apostle Paul will quote four times in the New Testament to explain how anyone, in any century, has ever been made right with God.Kuanzia Mwanzo 12 hadi mwisho wa kitabu, kamera inapunguza mtazamo kutoka jamii nzima ya wanadamu hadi familia moja. Mungu anamwita Abramu atoke Uri na kumpa ahadi tatu kuu: nchi, watu, na baraka itakayofika kwa mataifa yote ya dunia. Ibrahimu alimwamini Mungu, na imani hiyo ikahesabiwa kwake kuwa haki (Mwanzo 15:6) — mstari ambao mtume Paulo ataunukuu mara nne katika Agano Jipya ili kueleza jinsi mtu yeyote, katika karne yoyote, alivyowahi kufanywa mwenye haki mbele za Mungu.
By the close of Genesis we have met five patriarchs — Noah, Abraham, Isaac, Jacob, Joseph — and the rest of the Bible will look like a long conversation about what God was doing in and through each of them. A pattern many preachers have noticed is that those five men together draw an unforgettable picture of the Christian life.Kufikia mwisho wa Mwanzo tumekutana na mababu watano — Nuhu, Ibrahimu, Isaka, Yakobo, Yusufu — na Biblia yote iliyosalia itaonekana kama mazungumzo marefu kuhusu kile Mungu alichokuwa akifanya ndani yao na kupitia kwao. Mfano ambao wahubiri wengi wameuona ni kwamba watu hao watano kwa pamoja huchora picha isiyosahaulika ya maisha ya Kikristo.
Noah is a picture of regeneration. He passes through the waters of judgment and steps out into a new world, alive on the other side of death. That is what the new birth is.Nuhu ni picha ya kuzaliwa upya. Anapita katika maji ya hukumu na kutoka katika ulimwengu mpya, akiwa hai upande wa pili wa kifo. Huo ndio kuzaliwa upya.
Abraham is a picture of justification by faith. He was pagan-born, seventy-five years old, and called out of his comfort zone to live in a tent in a land he had never seen — and he went. He believed God, and God counted it as righteousness. The apostle Paul will quote that one verse four times to explain how anyone, in any century, has ever been made right with God: not by climbing, not by earning, not by trying harder, but by believing.

Abraham waited twenty-five years for the son God had promised him. When the boy finally came, God asked for him back. On Mount Moriah, Abraham raised the knife over his only son Isaac — and the knife was stopped by a ram caught in the thicket. What God wanted was Abraham's heart, not Isaac's life. I once read of a pastor whose wife lay dying, and that night he prayed, "Lord, she and I have been together so many years; I release her into Your hands." Around midnight he heard the Lord answer back: "Who are you to grant Me permission to take your wife? I do not need your permission. Just obey Me. Do not try to take My place." Abraham learned that on a mountain. Sometimes we learn it in a hospital corridor. And in that one act — a father willing to give his only beloved son — Abraham became the only man in Scripture who is a type of God the Father. Two thousand years later, on the same ridge of hills, the Father did not stop the knife. There was no ram in the thicket that day. There was only His Son.		Ibrahimu ni picha ya kuhesabiwa haki kwa imani. Alizaliwa kati ya wapagani, alikuwa na umri wa miaka sabini na mitano, na akaitwa atoke katika eneo lake la starehe aishi hemani katika nchi ambayo hakuwahi kuiona — naye akaenda. Alimwamini Mungu, na Mungu akamhesabia kuwa haki. Mtume Paulo ataunukuu mstari huo mmoja mara nne ili kueleza jinsi mtu yeyote, katika karne yoyote, alivyowahi kufanywa mwenye haki mbele za Mungu: si kwa kupanda, si kwa kujipatia, si kwa kujitahidi zaidi, bali kwa kuamini.

Ibrahimu alimngojea mwana ambaye Mungu alikuwa amemwahidi kwa miaka ishirini na mitano. Mvulana huyo alipokuja hatimaye, Mungu akamdai arudishwe. Juu ya Mlima Moria, Ibrahimu aliinua kisu juu ya mwanawe wa pekee Isaka — kisu kikazuiwa na kondoo dume aliyenaswa vichakani. Alichokitaka Mungu ni moyo wa Ibrahimu, si uhai wa Isaka. Nilisoma habari ya mchungaji ambaye mkewe alikuwa akikaribia kufa, na usiku ule aliomba, "Bwana, mimi na yeye tumekuwa pamoja miaka mingi; ninamkabidhi mikononi mwako." Karibu usiku wa manane alimsikia Bwana akijibu: "Wewe ni nani hata unipe ruhusa ya kumchukua mkeo? Sihitaji ruhusa yako. Nitii tu. Usijaribu kuchukua nafasi yangu." Ibrahimu alijifunza hilo juu ya mlima. Wakati mwingine sisi hujifunza katika korido ya hospitali. Na katika tendo hilo moja — baba aliye tayari kumtoa mwanawe wa pekee mpendwa — Ibrahimu akawa mtu pekee katika Maandiko aliye mfano wa Mungu Baba. Miaka elfu mbili baadaye, juu ya safu ile ile ya vilima, Baba hakulizuia kisu. Hakukuwa na kondoo dume vichakani siku hiyo. Alikuwepo Mwanawe tu.
Isaac is a picture of sonship. He was the son of promise, the apple of his father's eye. The New Testament will say of every believer, "Beloved, we are God's children now" (1 John 3:2). Isaac shows us what that looks like.Isaka ni picha ya uana. Alikuwa mwana wa ahadi, mboni ya jicho la baba yake. Agano Jipya litasema juu ya kila mwamini, "Wapenzi, sasa tu watoto wa Mungu" (1 Yohana 3:2). Isaka anatuonyesha jinsi hilo linavyoonekana.
Jacob is a picture of sanctification. He spent the first half of his life trying to win by his wits, and ended up deceiving his father, his brother, and his father-in-law. On the night he heard that Esau was coming with four hundred armed men, Jacob was finally alone, and he wrestled until daybreak with a Stranger who turned out to be the Lord. The Stranger asked one question: "What is your name?" God was not having a memory lapse. He was making Jacob own who he was. When Jacob finally said the word — "Jacob," supplanter, deceiver — the Lord touched his hip, and Jacob limped for the rest of his life. That is sanctification. We do not become Israel by trying harder. We become Israel by being broken in the right place.Yakobo ni picha ya utakaso. Alitumia nusu ya kwanza ya maisha yake akijaribu kushinda kwa akili zake, na mwishowe akamdanganya baba yake, ndugu yake, na baba mkwe wake. Usiku aliposikia kwamba Esau anakuja na watu mia nne wenye silaha, Yakobo hatimaye alikuwa peke yake, naye akashindana mweleka hadi kukaribia mapambazuko na Mgeni ambaye aligeuka kuwa Bwana. Mgeni huyo aliuliza swali moja: "Jina lako ni nani?" Mungu hakuwa amesahau. Alikuwa akimfanya Yakobo akubali yeye ni nani. Yakobo alipolisema neno hilo hatimaye — "Yakobo," mwenye kunyakua, mdanganyifu — Bwana akamgusa kiuno chake, na Yakobo akachechemea maisha yake yote yaliyosalia. Huo ndio utakaso. Hatuwi Israeli kwa kujitahidi zaidi. Tunakuwa Israeli kwa kuvunjwa mahali panapostahili.
Joseph is a picture of glorification. His life moved through four chapters: a favored son with prophetic dreams; a teenager thrown in a pit and sold by his brothers; a slave so faithful that Potiphar trusted him with everything — and so godly that when Potiphar's wife tried to seduce him, he did not just walk away, he fled and ran (Genesis 39:12). For that he was thrown in prison, where he stayed until his accurate interpretation of Pharaoh's dream lifted him from the dungeon to the second throne of Egypt. When his frightened brothers finally stood before him, the man who had every right to take revenge instead said: "You meant evil against me, but God meant it for good" (Genesis 50:20). That is what God has been doing on every page of Genesis, and on every page of your life.Yusufu ni picha ya kutukuzwa. Maisha yake yalipita katika sura nne: mwana mpendwa mwenye ndoto za kinabii; kijana aliyetupwa shimoni na kuuzwa na ndugu zake; mtumwa mwaminifu sana hata Potifa akamwamini kwa kila kitu — na mcha Mungu hata mke wa Potifa alipojaribu kumshawishi, hakuondoka tu, bali alikimbia mbio (Mwanzo 39:12). Kwa ajili ya hilo alitupwa gerezani, ambako alikaa hadi tafsiri yake sahihi ya ndoto ya Farao ilipomwinua kutoka shimoni la gereza hadi kiti cha pili cha enzi cha Misri. Ndugu zake walipokuja mbele yake kwa hofu hatimaye, mtu yule ambaye alikuwa na kila haki ya kulipiza kisasi badala yake alisema: "Ninyi mlinitakia mabaya, bali Mungu aliyakusudia kuwa mema" (Mwanzo 50:20). Hayo ndiyo Mungu amekuwa akifanya katika kila ukurasa wa Mwanzo, na katika kila ukurasa wa maisha yako.
IV. Key Themes — and Where They Are HeadingIV. Mada Kuu — na Zinakoelekea
Genesis lays down the lines that the rest of the Bible will fill in. Creation: God is the source of everything that exists, and creation is good. Sin: human rebellion has fractured our relationship with God. Covenant: God binds Himself to His people with promises He alone can keep. Grace: He provides a way out of judgment that we could never provide for ourselves. Faith: Abraham believed God, and it was counted to him as righteousness. Promise: the seed of the woman will come.Mwanzo inaweka mistari ambayo Biblia iliyosalia itaijaza. Uumbaji: Mungu ndiye chanzo cha kila kilichopo, na uumbaji ni mwema. Dhambi: uasi wa mwanadamu umevunja uhusiano wetu na Mungu. Agano: Mungu anajifunga na watu wake kwa ahadi ambazo Yeye peke yake anaweza kuzitimiza. Neema: Anatoa njia ya kuepuka hukumu ambayo sisi hatungeweza kuitoa wenyewe. Imani: Ibrahimu alimwamini Mungu, nayo ikahesabiwa kwake kuwa haki. Ahadi: uzao wa mwanamke utakuja.
By the last chapter of Genesis, Israel is a small family of seventy souls living in Egypt. They do not know it yet, but the long centuries of slavery are about to begin. The stage is set for the exodus, the giving of the Law, and the formation of a nation. The Old Testament is not a museum of ancient stories. It is the long, patient, unbreakable love of God for His image-bearers — and every story, every law, every prophecy is leaning forward, waiting for the One who will come and put the broken image of Adam back together in Himself.Kufikia sura ya mwisho ya Mwanzo, Israeli ni familia ndogo ya watu sabini wanaoishi Misri. Hawajui bado, lakini karne ndefu za utumwa zinakaribia kuanza. Jukwaa limeandaliwa kwa Kutoka, kutolewa kwa Sheria, na kuundwa kwa taifa. Agano la Kale si jumba la makumbusho la hadithi za zamani. Ni upendo mrefu, wa subira, usiovunjika wa Mungu kwa wale walio na sura yake — na kila hadithi, kila sheria, kila unabii unaegemea mbele, ukimngojea Yule atakayekuja na kuiunganisha tena ndani yake sura iliyovunjika ya Adamu.

Model answer: Genesis lays down the great lines that the rest of the Bible will fill in. Creation shows that God is the source of everything and that what He made is good. The fall shows that human sin has fractured our relationship with God. Covenant shows that God binds Himself to His people with promises He alone can keep. Faith is seen in Abraham, who believed God and was counted righteous. Promise runs through the whole book in the assurance that the seed of the woman will come. Together these themes of creation, fall, covenant, faith, and promise set the stage for the exodus, the Law, and the long story of redemption that leads to Christ.		10. Unganisha mada kuu za Mwanzo (uumbaji, anguko, agano, imani, ahadi) katika aya moja inayoeleza jinsi kitabu hiki kinavyoweka msingi wa historia yote ya ukombozi.
Key ideas: uumbaji, aliumba, muumba, kuumba, iliumbwa | anguko, dhambi, uasi, kuanguka, kutotii | agano, maagano, patano, ahadi za mungu | imani, aliamini, kuamini, amini, kumwamini | ahadi, uzao wa mwanamke, mkombozi, ahadi ya mkombozi, mwokozi | ukombozi, kukomboa, wokovu, kuokoka, mkombozi, kuokolewa
Model answer: Mwanzo inaweka mistari mikuu ambayo Biblia iliyosalia itaijaza. Uumbaji unaonyesha kwamba Mungu ndiye chanzo cha kila kitu na kwamba alichokiumba ni chema. Anguko linaonyesha kwamba dhambi ya mwanadamu imevunja uhusiano wetu na Mungu. Agano linaonyesha kwamba Mungu anajifunga na watu wake kwa ahadi ambazo Yeye peke yake anaweza kuzitimiza. Imani inaonekana kwa Ibrahimu, aliyemwamini Mungu na akahesabiwa kuwa mwenye haki. Ahadi inapita katika kitabu chote kwa uhakika kwamba uzao wa mwanamke utakuja. Pamoja, mada hizi za uumbaji, anguko, agano, imani, na ahadi zinaandaa jukwaa kwa Kutoka, Sheria, na hadithi ndefu ya ukombozi inayoongoza kwa Kristo.
