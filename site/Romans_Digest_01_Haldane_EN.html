Robert Haldane — An Exposition of Romans

About the Author

Robert Haldane (1764–1842) was a Scottish evangelical preacher, theologian, and Bible expositor whose work helped shape evangelical Christianity throughout Europe. His exposition of Romans became one of the most influential evangelical commentaries ever written on Paul's epistle.

Introduction: The Great Theme of Romans

Haldane regarded Romans as the clearest and most complete explanation of the gospel found anywhere in the Bible. Paul's purpose is to explain how sinful human beings can be reconciled to a holy God. The central theme of Romans is the righteousness of God revealed in the gospel.

Humanity's Universal Sinfulness

Romans 1–3 demonstrates that every person stands guilty before God. Neither pagan morality nor religious privilege can save. The law reveals sin but cannot remove it.

Justification by Faith

God provides a righteousness apart from the law through Jesus Christ. Justification is God's declaration that a sinner is righteous because of Christ. Faith receives this gift and all boasting is excluded.

Abraham: The Example of Faith

Abraham was justified before circumcision, demonstrating that salvation has always been by grace through faith.

The Blessings of Justification

Believers receive peace with God, access to grace, hope of glory, endurance in suffering, and assurance of God's love.

Adam and Christ

Through Adam came sin and death; through Christ come righteousness and life.

Freedom from Sin and Life in the Spirit

Union with Christ breaks sin's dominion. Romans 8 declares that there is no condemnation for those in Christ Jesus. The Holy Spirit empowers believers and guarantees future glory.

Conclusion

Salvation begins with God's grace, is accomplished through Christ, applied by the Spirit, and completed according to God's purpose.

Key Takeaways

  1. All humanity stands guilty before God.
  2. Justification comes through faith alone in Christ.
  3. Christ's death secures forgiveness.
  4. Believers possess peace with God.
  5. The Spirit empowers Christian living.
  6. Nothing can separate believers from God's love.
