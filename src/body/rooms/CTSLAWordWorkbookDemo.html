Observation: λόγος can mean word, message, or discourse. “The Word” depends on John's prologue.

Try comparing the forms

Touch θεόν in line 1b and θεὸς in line 1c. They are forms of the same Greek noun. The first is accusative after πρὸς; the second is nominative, functioning as a predicate. The article ὁ marks λόγος as the subject of the final clause.
