## Source-level observations grounded in the staged reproductions

- The **Issue 6** baptism candidate distinguishes seeking the Spirit from seeking tongues as an end in themselves. Its **WJ.S.** initials are useful attribution evidence but are not a substitute for original facsimile verification.
- The **Issue 5** gifts candidate argues from 1 Corinthians and Ephesians and connects gifts with love and Scripture. Its author remains unconfirmed.
- The **Issue 4** counterfeit candidate argues that spiritual claims should be tested, but the newspaper's descriptions of people and phenomena are **claims of its writers**, not independently verified diagnoses or evidence for contemporary disciplinary sanctions.
- The **Issue 3** stewardship candidate warns against selling possessions or abandoning family obligations under spiritual pressure; its signed ending makes it a promising critical-reading source, subject to page collation.
- The **Issue 7** visitor report is valuable for **distinct narrative voice** and accounts of tongues/interpretation, but it is not automatically Seymour's testimony and cannot be cleared by Seymour's death alone.
