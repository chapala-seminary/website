## Verifiable bibliographic candidate

**Maria Beulah Woodworth-Etter (1844–1924), *Signs and Wonders God Wrought in the Ministry for Forty Years* (Indianapolis: M. B. W. Etter, 1916), 584 pp.** The title page says compiled and written by herself and names the 1916 copyright; verify the particular chapter against the original printed pages before republication. Open Library edition ID `OL28381728M` and its Internet Archive record `signswondersgodw0000wood` identify the edition.

Two **specific, substantive first-person sermons** are unusually well suited to A-C3:

1. **Chapter LXI, “Try the Spirits,” original table-of-contents p. 488 onward.** Source explicitly frames a sermon to ministers and workers. Main themes: discernment of prophetic speech and tongues, Scripture as criterion, pastoral restraint, and the dangers of coercive or sensational claims. Distinguish the historical speaker's views from CTS faculty evaluation. Transcription: https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Signs_and_Wonders_God_Wrought_in_the_Ministry_for_Forty_Years/Chapter_61
2. **Chapter LXII, “Work of the Holy Ghost,” original table-of-contents p. 494 onward.** The page explicitly attributes the sermon to Woodworth-Etter. Main themes: Pentecost, Spirit baptism, tongues, gifts, mission witness, and how the author understood Acts 2 and 1 Corinthians 12–14. The author's claim that Spirit baptism normally entails tongues is a historically situated Pentecostal position, not an established consensus across CTS traditions. Transcription: https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Signs_and_Wonders_God_Wrought_in_the_Ministry_for_Forty_Years/Chapter_62

**Original edition and author witness:** https://openlibrary.org/books/OL28381728M/Signs_and_Wonders_God_Wrought_in_the_Ministry_for_Forty_Years ; table of contents and original title page transcription: https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Signs_and_Wonders_God_Wrought_in_the_Ministry_for_Forty_Years . ## Preliminary Mexico rights screen (not legal clearance)

Mexican Ley Federal del Derecho de Autor, Article 29, protects identified authors' economic rights during life plus 100 years. With Woodworth-Etter's documented death in 1924, her **own 1916 original expression** is a strong public-domain candidate in Mexico from **1 January 2025**, subject to verification of authorial contribution and applicable rules. Consult current official text: https://www.diputados.gob.mx/LeyesBiblio/pdf/LFDA.pdf ; WIPO's accessible statutory Article 29 witness: https://www.wipo.int/wipolex/es/legislation/details/19901 .

**Do not treat the entire 584-page compilation as automatically cleared.** It contains introductions, newspaper reports, third-party testimonies and potentially other contributors; their independent authorial expression needs separate attribution and rights checks. A modern edition, introduction, scan enhancement, or ES/FR/SW translation can have separate rights. Reuse only the original author-attributed sermon text after image collation and rights-owner review; CTS-created EN digests and ES/FR translations need explicit author/translator provenance and permissions. A freely readable online transcription is not a blanket licence for its editorial additions.

## Faculty reading objectives and review guide (non-assessment-bearing)

**EN:** Compare Woodworth-Etter's tests for spiritual discernment with 1 Corinthians 12–14 and 1 John 4:1. Separate her historical testimony, interpretation and reported events. Contrast her understanding of tongues and Spirit baptism with the existing Seymour/Apostolic Faith selections. Do not ask students to affirm miraculous reports as independently proven facts.

**ES:** Comparar los criterios de discernimiento de Woodworth-Etter con 1 Corintios 12–14 y 1 Juan 4:1. Distinguir el testimonio histórico, la interpretación bíblica y los acontecimientos narrados. Contrastar su perspectiva sobre las lenguas y el bautismo en el Espíritu con los textos actuales de Seymour. No presentar los relatos de milagros como hechos verificados de forma independiente.

**FR:** Comparer les critères de discernement de Woodworth-Etter avec 1 Corinthiens 12–14 et 1 Jean 4.1. Distinguer témoignage historique, interprétation biblique et événements rapportés. Comparer sa conception des langues et du baptême dans l'Esprit avec les textes actuellement attribués à Seymour. Ne pas présenter les récits de miracles comme des faits vérifiés indépendamment.

These are **faculty prompts**, not reviewed translations of the original source or approved student readings.
