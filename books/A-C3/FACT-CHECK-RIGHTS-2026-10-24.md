## Additional history fact check (row 98)

1. **A-C4 Woodworth-Etter's 1884 Winebrenner connection: CONFIRMED, more precise wording needed.** Christian Biblical Equality's historical essay says she left the United Brethren association and was **licensed to preach by the 39th Indiana Eldership of the Churches of God in 1884**: https://www.cbeinternational.org/resource/maria-beulah-woodworth-etter-trance-evangelist/ . Independent biography likewise reports 1884 evangelist licensing: https://healingandrevival.com/BioMWEtter.htm . Prefer “received a Churches of God evangelist license in 1884” over unqualified “joined the denomination.”
2. **A-C4 1890 Oakland earthquake prophecy: CONTROVERTED ATTRIBUTION.** Contemporary campaign was followed by a widely circulated prediction of a destructive earthquake/tidal wave for **14 April 1890**, which did not occur. A historical synthesis notes Woodworth-Etter's biographer attributes it to her, while defenders assign it to followers: https://creedalfaith.org/the-healing-movement/ . A separate biography specifically says a **man named Ericson** made the prediction at one meeting: https://healingandrevival.com/BioMWEtter.htm . Without original 1890 newspapers and Woodworth-Etter's own statements, say “a prophecy circulated at her Oakland campaign; attribution to her is disputed.” Do **not** teach it as an uncontested direct prophecy by Woodworth-Etter.
3. **A-C4 1913 Arroyo Seco camp meeting: CONFIRMED.** Two historical summaries identify Maria Woodworth-Etter as conducting or main speaker at the **April 1913** camp meeting: https://www.apostolicarchives.com/articles/article/8795236/172422.htm and https://www.apostolicarchives.com/articles/article/8801925/173376.htm . Both are same archival host; an independent original program is desirable before a detailed narrative.
4. **C-C5 “Mr. Walsh” in Wesley's 1752 Ireland entries: CONFIRMED in context.** Wesley's published journal explicitly calls the Irish-language preacher “**Mr. Thomas Walsh**” on Wednesday **2 September 1752**: https://wesley.nnu.edu/john-wesley/the-journal-of-john-wesley-vol-4/the-ninth-part-section-two/ . Only expand the abbreviated “Mr. Walsh” where context is this preacher; don't assume every Walsh reference is the same person.
5. **C-C1 Sophia Hopkey/William Williamson marriage: 12 March 1737.** An early Wesley biography quotes Wesley's journal entry dated **Saturday 12 March**, four days after her engagement: https://www.uecf.net/books/the-life-of-john-wesley/chapter6.html . An independent historical account likewise gives March 12: https://www.historyhome.co.uk/people/wesley.htm . Distinguish engagement 8 March from wedding 12 March.
6. **C-C1 Georgia slavery ban: exact chronology.** The Trustees consented/legislation changed in **1750**; the legal permission **took effect 1 January 1751** according to Betty Wood, *New Georgia Encyclopedia*: https://www.georgiaencyclopedia.org/articles/history-archaeology/slavery-in-colonial-georgia/ and its January chronology https://www.georgiaencyclopedia.org/articles/history-archaeology/january-in-georgia-history/ . Correct phrasing: “The ban was overturned in 1750, with slavery legalized effective 1 January 1751.” Slavery had existed illegally before legalization; the ban was not equivalent to the absence of enslaved people.

No authenticated issue PDF was retrieved here; **zero Durham raw-text files are claimed**. - **Historical chronology warning:** Christopher J. Richmann, “William H. Durham and Early Pentecostalism: A Multifaceted Reassessment,” *Pneuma* 37 (2015), challenges the often repeated claim of a documented **May 1910 Stone Church 'Finished Work' sermon**, noting lack of contemporaneous confirmation and evidence for public emergence around late 1910/early 1911. Scholarly discussion: https://www.pentecostaltheology.com/william-h-durham-and-early-pentecostalism/ . Teach chronology as disputed, not as a securely documented single-event origin.
