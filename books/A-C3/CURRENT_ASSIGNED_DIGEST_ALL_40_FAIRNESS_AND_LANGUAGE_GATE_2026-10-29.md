## Source and Mexico-rights gate — preliminary, not legal clearance

The assigned digests identify Edward T. Hiscox, *The New Directory for Baptist Churches* (Philadelphia: American Baptist Publication Society, 1894), especially chapters on the church, membership, officers, government and discipline. Internet Archive's Library of Congress copy identifies Hiscox (1814–1901), publication year 1894, Philadelphia publisher, 604 pages, and states that the Library of Congress is unaware of copyright restrictions for that digitized item: https://archive.org/details/newdirectoryforb01hisc.

Mexico's current official *Ley Federal del Derecho de Autor* (latest reform displayed 14 May 2026) states in Article 29 that patrimonial rights endure for the author's life plus 100 years and then pass to the public domain: https://www.diputados.gob.mx/LeyesBiblio/pdf/LFDA.pdf. Hiscox's 1901 death makes the underlying 1894 author text a strong public-domain candidate under that term. This is not a blanket clearance for a particular scan, OCR, modern edition, translation, CTS derivative prose, third-party quotation, illustration or editorial note. Exact page-image collation, imprint verification, contributor attribution and translation provenance remain required.

The assigned digests also contain later CTS context (for example Ignatius and episcopal traditions, the Baptist World Alliance, Mexican religious-association law, and modern abuse-safeguarding commentary). Those passages must be separately sourced, academically reviewed and version-pinned. They should not be represented as Hiscox's own 1894 text.
