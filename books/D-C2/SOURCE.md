## Historical primary source and access

John Calvin (1509–1564), *Institutes of the Christian Religion*, final Latin/French authorial recension 1559, English translation by John Allen (1771–1839), sixth American historical edition, Philadelphia: Presbyterian Board of Publication. The two Project Gutenberg records identify the same historical translation but are separate physical volumes.

- **Volume I**, PG **45001**: https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/45001 ; verified full UTF-8 https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/45001/pg45001.txt . Book III chapters I–XIII occur near the end.
- **Volume II**, PG **64392**: https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/64392 ; verified full UTF-8 https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/64392/pg64392.txt . Opens at Book III chapter XIV, continues through chapter XXV, then Book IV. PG vol. II catalog names Calvin and Allen and labels it public domain in USA.
- These are historical public-domain translations; no contemporary commentary or modern translations are authorized by these links. No complete source text is copied into this handoff; Claude approved verified Gutenberg identifiers + ranges when raw transport is unavailable.

## Five readings — exact book/chapter ranges, all non-overlapping

Extract from **actual chapter bodies**, not contents/arguments or the Gutenberg headers/footers. Book III chapter numbers are continuous **across two PG volumes**, so do not misidentify Book IV chapter I as Book III chapter I. Extractor must report verified start/end strings, word counts, and SHA-256; do not invent them.

1. **The Spirit, faith, and repentance** — **PG 45001, Book III chapters I–III**, beginning Book III chapter I “What Is Declared Concerning Christ Rendered Profitable to Us by the Secret Operation of the Spirit” and ending before chapter IV. Focus: Spirit's work, faith, repentance and regeneration.
2. **Justification defined** — **PG 45001, Book III chapters XI–XIII**, beginning “Justification by Faith. The Name and Thing Defined” and ending at the close of chapter XIII before volume-I footnotes. Focus: forensic justification, mercy, righteousness and divine judgment.
3. **Grace, works and the gospel** — **PG 64392, Book III chapters XIV–XVII**, from the opening body heading “The Commencement and Continual Progress of Justification” to before chapter XVIII. Focus: grace, sanctification, works and the law/gospel relationship.
4. **Election and predestination** — **PG 64392, Book III chapters XXI–XXIII**, starting “Eternal Election, or God's Predestination of Some to Salvation…” and stopping before chapter XXIV. Focus: Calvin's account of election, theological limits and objections; include contextual editorial framing in the *course digest*, not silently in the historical text.
5. **Calling, assurance, and resurrection** — **PG 64392, Book III chapters XXIV–XXV**, from “Election Confirmed by the Divine Call…” through “The Final Resurrection,” ending before Book IV's argument. Focus: calling, hope, assurance, final resurrection.

The readings are **chapter-defined, not yet word-count-verified**. Book III chapters IV–X, XVIII–XX are intentionally not part of the five readings; they may remain further study. ## Six verified further-study destinations (links only)

1. **Calvin, *Institutes*, vol. I, PG 45001** — https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/45001 . Read Book II on sin, freedom and the mediator as doctrinal prerequisites.
2. **Calvin, *Institutes*, vol. II, PG 64392** — https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/64392 . Read Book III chapters XVIII–XX on merit and prayer, omitted from the assigned five readings.
3. **Westminster Assembly, *Westminster Confession of Faith* (1646/47)** — https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Westminster_Confession . Select the original 1647 edition and read chapters on decree, calling and justification; **do not silently substitute the later revised confession**.
4. **Westminster Assembly, *Confession* historical text index (1646/47)** — https://www.ccel.org/creeds/westminster.htm . Compare confessional definitions of grace, justification, perseverance; edition/revision must be checked before quotation.
5. **Guido de Brès, *Belgic Confession* (1561)** — https://www.thegospelcoalition.org/publication-online/belgic-confession/ . Read articles 16–26 on election, Christ and salvation; this webpage is a **modern editorial presentation**, link only unless its exact English translation rights are separately cleared.
6. **Ursinus/Olevianus, *Heidelberg Catechism* (1563)** — https://www.dbnl.org/tekst/ursi003heid01_01/index.php . Historical Dutch source with scans and text, useful for original-language comparison; **do not pass its Dutch text off as a cleared modern English translation**.
