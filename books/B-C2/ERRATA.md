# B-C2 — textual and reference QA notes (2026-10-24)

**Scope:** Targeted independent check of Claude's three flagged passages in `reading-2.md`, `reading-4.md`, and `reading-5.md`. These are **editorial annotations**, not alterations to the documentary source. They do **not** replace a page-by-page collation with the 1857/1871 print edition, which remains outstanding.

## 1. Reading 2 — "undersigned coincidences" [CONFIRMED TRANSCRIPTION ERROR]

In the Creation chapter, the present text says **"The undersigned coincidences which have been discovered..."**. A digitized historical printed edition reads **"The undesigned coincidences..."**. The next sentence explicitly discusses whether the coincidence was "undesigned", confirming the intended word.

**Correction for CTS authored digests, questions, and translations:** use **undesigned**, not undersigned. Preserve the source transcription unchanged until scan-collation is documented, or record any emendation in a separate apparatus.

Evidence: historical-print PDF https://www.reformedontheweb.com/baptists/manual-of-theology-john-dagg.pdf ; current transcription https://www.gracegems.org/31/dagg_manual_of_theology3.htm . Multiple modern online transcriptions copy "undersigned" and are not independent evidence.

## 2. Reading 4 — "Holy Spirit ... sending the Son" [NOT A MISSING-WORD ERROR]

The unusual wording occurs intact in both Grace Gems and independent Founders/Swartzentruber reproductions of Dagg's *Covenant of Grace*. The next paragraph explicitly says **"In all this the Father appears as the representative of the Godhead"**. This is a **historical syntax/antecedent problem**, not evidence that a phrase was lost in the handoff. Do not silently rewrite Dagg's primary text or teach that the Holy Spirit sends the Son. Explain that the paragraph describes the Father's covenantal authority and the Spirit's distinct role; confirm punctuation/line breaks from the print edition before any textual emendation.

Evidence: https://www.gracegems.org/31/dagg_manual_of_theology7.htm ; https://founders.org/library/dagg-bk-7-chapter-ii/ .

## 3. Reading 5 — "Because you are sons ... Abba, Father" [BIBLICAL REFERENCE MISMATCH]

The quoted sentence is from **Galatians 4:6**, whereas the staged transcription labels it **(Rom 8:15)**. Romans 8:15 also contains "Abba, Father" but does **not** begin "Because you are sons, God has sent forth the Spirit of his Son..." (Galatians does). The transcription also says **"into yours hearts"**, which is a likely typo for **"into your hearts"**.

**For CTS notes/tests:** cite **Galatians 4:6** for the exact quotation, optionally compare **Romans 8:15** as a parallel; do not use the erroneous attribution in answer keys. Mark the source citation as a historical-text error pending original-edition verification.

Evidence: https://www.gracegems.org/31/dagg_manual_of_theology7.htm ; https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Galatians%204%3A6%3BRomans%208%3A15&version=KJV .
