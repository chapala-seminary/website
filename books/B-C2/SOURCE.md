## Textbook

John Leadley Dagg (1794–1884), *Manual of Theology: A Treatise on Christian Doctrine*, first published 1857. Source transcription by Grace Gems identifies 1857; independently verifiable historical scan: 1871 American Baptist Publication Society edition, Library of Congress (https://www.loc.gov/item/39007012/). The 1857 and 1871 texts have **not** been compared word-for-word. The reader selects Books I, IV, and VI (29,556 words including provenance and headings):

## Academic cautions

- Dagg writes from a nineteenth-century Baptist theological perspective; compare doctrinal interpretations fairly with other Christian traditions.
- He discusses sin, judgment, hell, divine sovereignty and the historical language of human depravity. Contextualize these teachings; do not present a single historic author's positions as CTS-wide consensus.
- Scripture citations in Grace Gems may have been normalized or reformatted; verify against historical scan. Keep the original text separate from Claude's EN/ES/FR digests and exams.
