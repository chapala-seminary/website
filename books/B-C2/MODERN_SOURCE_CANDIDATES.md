# B-C2 — modern source candidates and permissions screen

**Status (2026-10-24): research only. No modern work below is cleared for translation, adaptation, question-bank extraction, or incorporation into CTS lessons.** Keep all candidates link-only unless the stated use is both unaltered and within the source's published terms, or CTS receives written permission. This is a permissions screen, not legal advice.

## Candidate 1 — Third Millennium Ministries, *We Believe in God*

- Type: contemporary multi-lesson systematic-theology course (individual lesson pages observed with 2015 dates).
- Course/source: https://thirdmill.org/seminary/course.asp/vs/GOD
- Terms: https://thirdmill.org/seminary/reproduction_policy.asp
- Verified permission: Thirdmill permits royalty-free reproduction and free distribution to churches, Bible schools, theological study centers, seminaries, missionaries, and indigenous individuals, provided copies are not sold above reproduction cost and the original production is not edited or altered.
- CTS use now: link to the official course, or distribute an unaltered Thirdmill production within those terms with full attribution.
- Hold: CTS digests, excerpts rearranged as a new textbook, EN→ES/FR translation, assessment-bank generation, or any other altered/adapted use require written clarification or permission from Thirdmill. This is Reformed/evangelical, not specifically Baptist, so it is a comparative supplement rather than a denominational authority.

## Candidate 2 — Third Millennium Ministries, *What Is Man?*

- Course/source: https://thirdmill.org/seminary/course.asp/vs/MAN
- Terms: https://thirdmill.org/seminary/reproduction_policy.asp

- Hold: no translation, editing, digesting, or private/public assessment extraction without written clarification. It supplements Dagg on humanity and sin but is not a Baptist-polity text.

## Candidate 3 — *Baptist Foundations: Church Government for an Anti-Institutional Age* (2015)

- Editors: Mark Dever and Jonathan Leeman; multi-author volume.
- Publisher page: https://bhacademic.bhpublishinggroup.com/product/baptist-foundations-2/
- Fit: modern Baptist treatment of congregationalism, ordinances, membership, discipline, elders, and deacons; a strong complement to Dagg/Hiscox.
- License status: commercial copyrighted book; no open license or reproduction/translation permission was found on the official product page.
- CTS use now: bibliographic link only.
- Hold: any reproduction, translation, digest, or question bank based substantially on the book requires written permission from B&H Academic/Lifeway and may involve fees. No assumption that permission will be granted.

**To:** resources@thirdmill.org  
**Subject:** Permission clarification for tuition-free CTS theological courses

Dear Third Millennium Ministries,

Chapala Theological Seminary is a tuition-free, non-commercial online ministry serving adult pastors and church leaders internationally. We would like to use selected Thirdmill courses, beginning with *We Believe in God* and *What Is Man?*, as modern supplementary readings in a Baptist-theology course.

We understand your reproduction policy permits free, unaltered distribution to churches, Bible schools, theological study centers, seminaries, missionaries, and indigenous individuals, provided copies are not sold above reproduction cost. Before doing anything beyond linking to your official pages, we request written clarification on whether CTS may:

1. host or distribute unaltered English, Spanish, and French versions that Thirdmill has already produced;
2. prepare clearly labeled CTS study digests that summarize the courses without replacing them;
3. translate portions where Thirdmill does not already provide a target-language version; and
4. create private assessment questions keyed to the material without reproducing substantial passages or exposing answers publicly.

CTS charges no tuition, examination, certificate, or reproduction fee. We would preserve attribution, copyright notices, source links, and any additional conditions you specify. We will not publish adapted material unless you give written permission.

With appreciation,
