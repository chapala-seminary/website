## Preliminary Mexico rights and edition check (not clearance)

John Leadley Dagg lived **1794–1884**. The Library of Congress catalogs an **1871 Philadelphia** `Manual of Theology: A Treatise on Christian Doctrine` and identifies its own digitized copy as public domain in the United States: https://www.loc.gov/item/39007012/ . This is not evidence that the 1871 printing was the 1857 edition cited by CTS; exact original printed pages, title/copyright history, scan transcription and embedded contributors still require collation. Under Mexico's Ley Federal del Derecho de Autor, article 29 (ordinary author life plus 100 years), Dagg's original expression is a strong preliminary public-domain candidate; official law: https://www.diputados.gob.mx/LeyesBiblio/pdf/LFDA.pdf . This does **not** clear third-party modern editorial notes, OCR/database contractual restrictions, Spanish/French/Swahili translations, CTS-authored digests, or faculty approval.
