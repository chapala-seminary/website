## Five reading windows

These cover all 33 chapters **exactly once**, in original order, with no chapter overlap. Simple whitespace counts below include file provenance/header and reading heading; counts are provisional and should be independently recomputed after any edits.

1. `reading-1.md` — Chapters **1–5**, Scripture, Trinity, decree, creation, providence; **2481 words**.
2. `reading-2.md` — Chapters **6–12**, fall, covenant, Christ, free will, calling, justification, adoption; **2389 words**.
3. `reading-3.md` — Chapters **13–18**, sanctification, faith, repentance, works, perseverance, assurance; **1909 words**.
4. `reading-4.md` — Chapters **19–26**, law, liberty, worship, civil magistrate, church; **3436 words**.
5. `reading-5.md` — Chapters **27–33**, sacraments, government, resurrection, judgment; **2244 words**.

**Editorial risk:** the original chapter 23 explicitly calls for suppression of blasphemy and heresy by the civil magistrate, and chapter 31 assigns civil authorities a role in synods. These reflect **historical seventeenth-century state–church relations**; never imply they are current CTS policy or endorse religious coercion. A historical context digest and Wayne's theological review are required. Reformed denominations have also adopted differing later revisions; do not silently substitute American revisions for the 1647 original.

## Further-study shelf (six distinct historical works)

1. Westminster Assembly, *Westminster Larger Catechism* (1647) — https://nonlinearfruit.github.io/Creeds.json/westminster_larger_catechism.html . Read Q1 onward and sections on covenant/justification; deeper exposition of confessional teaching. Historical original; verify exact edition against scan before republishing.
2. Westminster Assembly, *Westminster Shorter Catechism* (1647) — https://nonlinearfruit.github.io/Creeds.json/westminster_shorter_catechism.html . Read Q1–38, then Q39 onward for doctrine/duty comparison; concise catechetical complement.
3. Ursinus/Olevianus, *Heidelberg Catechism* (1563) — https://www.dbnl.org/tekst/ursi003heid01_01/index.php . Read the sections on misery, deliverance and gratitude in historical Dutch; do not copy unverified modern translations.
4. Guido de Brès, *Belgic Confession* (1561) — https://www.thegospelcoalition.org/publication-online/belgic-confession/ . Read articles 1–26 and compare confession structure; modern website presentation **link only**, translation rights unverified.
5. John Calvin, *Institutes of the Christian Religion*, vol. I, John Allen translation (historical edition), PG 45001 — https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/45001 . Read Book II on sin, covenant and Christ; broader theology behind confessional categories.
6. John Calvin, *Institutes*, vol. II, John Allen translation, PG 64392 — https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/64392 . Read Book III on grace and Book IV on the church, sacraments and polity; doctrinal comparison.
