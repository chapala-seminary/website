3. **Structural assessment fairness:** raw staged reading-1..5 are near-verbatim excerpts of the staged textbook. Replacing current CTS digests with those chapters without independent-reading redesign would create a *textbook/reading duplication*, even where questions have direct support. Replacing them with five different authors without rewriting and independently checking the reading bank would create a *source mismatch*.
4. **Faculty-voice protection:** describe Hiscox's 1894 Baptist convictions accurately as his own. Other Baptist successionist and Landmark positions may be presented faithfully and attributed; do not treat Hiscox's disagreement as a mandatory CTS verdict. Contextualize nineteenth-century discipline, women's ministry, pastoral authority and ecclesiastical polity without flattening authentic denominational voices.
