## Why a correction requires faculty/owner review

Mexico's LFDA **Article 29** generally ties economic rights for identifiable authors to their life plus 100 years; a work's publication before 1931 is not, by itself, a sufficient clearance test. **Article 78** treats translations, adaptations, and other derivatives separately. Primary statutory text: https://www.diputados.gob.mx/LeyesBiblio/pdf/LFDA.pdf (Articles 29 and 78; check the latest consolidated version at review time). This is a *risk screen*, not a conclusion about any specific excerpt or exception.

The room itself names multiple contributors and secondary reproductions. In particular, Reading 4's footer says signed Florence Crawford material (d. 1936) appears in the course textbook. That death date alone demonstrates why “all before 1931” is not a defensible universal shortcut under the ordinary Mexican term. Do not equate short quotation, citation, age of a newspaper, or an unsigned masthead with automatic permission to reproduce or translate every contribution. Record quotation amount, attribution, author, source issue/page, legal basis/permission, translation origin, and faculty disposition **per retained passage**.

## Proposed reusable replacement text for faculty and legal review

**EN — contributor footer (retain reading-specific contributor details separately):**

> **Source and contributor review.** CTS summarizes historical material for study and commentary. Each retained quotation, reproduced passage, and translation must be checked against its original author, issue/page, edition, and applicable rights or permission. Publication date alone does not establish the status of every contribution. The source and translation review remains in progress.

**ES:**

> **Revisión de fuentes y colaboradores.** El STC resume materiales históricos para su estudio y comentario. Cada cita conservada, pasaje reproducido y traducción debe verificarse según su autor original, número y página, edición y derechos o permisos aplicables. La fecha de publicación, por sí sola, no determina la situación de todos los aportes. La revisión de fuentes y traducciones sigue pendiente.

**FR (machine-assisted proposal; human review required):**

> **Examen des sources et des contributions.** CTS résume des documents historiques à des fins d'étude et de commentaire. Chaque citation conservée, passage reproduit et traduction doit être vérifié au regard de son auteur, du numéro et de la page d'origine, de l'édition et des droits ou autorisations applicables. La date de publication ne suffit pas à établir le statut de chaque contribution. L'examen des sources et des traductions reste en cours.
