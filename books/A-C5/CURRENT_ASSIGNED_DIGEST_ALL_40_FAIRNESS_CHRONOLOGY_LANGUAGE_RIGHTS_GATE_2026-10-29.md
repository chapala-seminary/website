### Specific faculty review flags
1. **Q6 / R1 §4, Acts 16:6–7:** the CTS digest says the Spirit forbade Paul and Silas to preach in Bithynia. In NIV Acts 16:6 the Spirit prevents preaching in the province of **Asia**, while 16:7 says the Spirit of Jesus prevents their entering **Bithynia**. The question's wording blends the two restrictions. **Faculty should inspect the 1792 Carey wording and decide whether a non-punitive clarification is needed.** Do not alter the live exam or answer bank without approval; preserve valid earned credit.
2. **Temporal provenance:** Q16 (1814), Q22 (Carey's later Indian translation), Q23 (1987), Q24 (1905), Q29–32 (1800–1805 and later), Q37 (society founded October 1792, after the book), Q38 (Fuller died 1815), Q40 (1873–1912) cannot be attributed as statements *inside the already published 1792 Enquiry*. They are currently supported by the CTS editorial digests. A future 1792-only replacement would strand these questions unless editorial context is retained and clearly assigned.
3. **Q14 and Q34:** exact historical dates/labels should be collated to a reliable secondary or archival source rather than silently treated as direct Carey quotations. Q19's Greek lexical gloss is CTS explanation.
4. **Q37 historical cross-check:** the William Carey Center identifies the society's founding at Kettering on 2 October 1792; the pamphlet itself precedes that event. Keep source-event chronology explicit.

### Rights and source version — preliminary Mexico screening, NOT legal clearance

Under Mexico LFDA Article 29's general life-plus-100-year rule, the **original Carey-authored 1792 text** is a strong public-domain candidate in Mexico.
