## Source and edition

- **Periodical:** *The Apostolic Faith*, Los Angeles original 1906–1908 issues, associated with William J. Seymour (1870–1922) and the Azusa Street mission.
- **Official historical archive:** https://www.apostolicfaith.org/library/azusa-papers . This archive links both original issue facsimiles and modern readable reproductions.
- **Source transport:** For each issue, extracted text from the archive's modern **reproduction PDF** at `https://objectstorage.us-phoenix-1.oraclecloud.com/n/ax22fojcamx7/b/PDFs/o/Historical%2FAzusa-Paper-NN.pdf`, where NN is the two-digit issue number. The original facsimile for the same number is separately linked on the official archive and must be used for collation.
- **Rights warning:** Seymour died in 1922, but the newspapers contain many signed and unsigned reports, letters, testimony and contributions from OTHER authors. The death dates/permissions of these authors are **not verified**; therefore the issue text is **NOT cleared for reproduction in a student textbook or translation in Mexico**. Modern reproduction layout, modern annotations, photos and contemporary edits may have separate rights. Before release, select only passages with proven author/rights, or use source links and original CTS commentary instead.
- **Accuracy warning:** The readable reproduction text is not a certified diplomatic transcript of the original 1906–1908 paper. Original-facsimile collation is required. The issues record contemporary religious claims and events; do not represent every report as independently verified.
