## Source and Mexican-rights gate — NOT cleared

John Wesley (1703–1791) and historical editor Thomas Jackson (1783–1874) are both more than 100 years deceased, making the underlying historical text a plausible public-domain candidate under Mexico's *Ley Federal del Derecho de Autor*, Article 29 (life plus 100 years). This is a **preliminary author/edition screen**, not permission for a particular PDF reproduction, modern editorial notes, third-party contributions, translation, or new CTS derivative content. The linked Wesley House Digital Library / Jackson Vol. II scan still requires original title-page/imprint confirmation and page-image collation. The provisional OCR source has not been corrected against facsimiles. Embedded contributors (especially Bolzius and the separately quoted Moravian communication), bibliographic apparatus, translations and modern annotations require separate verification. Do not treat five excerpts from Wesley's **one journal** as five independent scholarly works. The journal's British/Irish focus does not by itself substantiate a full **global missions** syllabus.
