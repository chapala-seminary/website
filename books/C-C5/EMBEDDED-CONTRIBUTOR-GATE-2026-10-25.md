## Independent external evidence (not publication approval)

1. **Bolzius/Boltzius identification:** Georgia Historical Society historical marker, `https://www.georgiahistory.com/ghmi_marker_updated/the-rev-john-martin-bolzius-the-rev-israel-christian-gronau/`, records his death **19 Nov 1765**. This supports a *preliminary* Mexico life-plus-100 screen for his own 1749 letter, but not modern transcription/translation rights.
2. **Textual transmission:** Wesley Works Editorial Project, *John Wesley's In-Correspondence (1746–50)*, `https://wesleyworks.ecdsdev.org/editorial-docs/jw-in-correspondence-1746-50.pdf`, identifies Bolzius's correspondence and notes a **Wesley journal abridgment** of the 1749 letter. Verify its exact edition and relation to the staged Jackson text before editing.
3. **Missing social context:** *New Georgia Encyclopedia*, `https://www.georgiaencyclopedia.org/articles/history-archaeology/johann-martin-boltzius-1703-1765/`, reports Bolzius's changing position on enslavement and eventual ownership of enslaved Black laborers. His letter's self-description of settlement and labor is **not** a complete account of Georgia's Indigenous, Black, or enslaved populations. A critical contextual note is needed; do not rewrite his historical letter to imply it says what it does not.
4. **Mexico rule:** WIPO Lex, *Ley Federal del Derecho de Autor* (amended through **14 May 2026**), `https://www.wipo.int/wipolex/es/legislation/details/23966`; Article 29 generally provides **author's life plus 100 years**. The rule alone does not establish authorship, provenance, protection of a particular edition/translation, or any right to republish unidentified embedded documents.

- For R2, keep **Bolzius letter** and **Moravian Daily Post communication** as *two distinct contributor records*. Both currently remain **editorial holds**; the former has stronger preliminary death-date evidence, the latter is unresolved.
- Do not silently delete disputed historical testimony. When an extract cannot be cleared, substitute an **original CTS summary with accurate citation** or a lawful external link, and keep the exclusion visible in the internal manifest.
- Do not create student assessments that assume the editor's comments are Wesley's, that treat source claims as independently established facts, or that test text omitted from the approved reading. Store all future questions/answers privately.
