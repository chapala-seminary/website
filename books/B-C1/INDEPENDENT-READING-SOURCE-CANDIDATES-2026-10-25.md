## Five independent historical source leads

1. **Thomas Crosby, _The History of the English Baptists_, vol. 1 (1738)** — English Reformation, dissent and early Baptist congregations. Original-edition bibliographic witnesses: Folger Shakespeare Library https://catalog.folger.edu/record/750149 and Wellcome https://wellcomecollection.org/works/h2hcm7u5 . **Metadata caution:** catalogs disagree on Crosby's birth/death years (Folger and Wellcome list 1665–1768; other biographies differ). Either reported death predates Mexico's 100-year term by 2026, but verify identity/attribution and contributors rather than asserting a particular year.
2. **Isaac Backus, _A History of New England, with Particular Reference to ... Baptists_, vol. 1 (1777)** — colonial dissent, religious liberty, conscience and Baptist testimony. Original 1777 digitized volume, Princeton Theological Seminary https://commons.ptsem.edu/id/historyofnewengl01back (catalog lists Backus 1724–1806). Prefer original 1777 text; a later 1871 edition adds David Weston notes and is NOT automatically the same rights/edition witness.
3. **David Benedict, _A General History of the Baptist Denomination in America, and Other Parts of the World_, vol. 2 (1813)** — early American/global denominational networks and regional coverage. Original-edition facsimile https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:A_general_history_of_the_Baptist_denomination_in_America,_and_other_parts_of_the_world_(IA_generalhistoryof02beneiala).pdf ; Huntington rare-books catalog https://www.huntington.org/collections/lib-76171 (Benedict 1779–1874). Do NOT substitute the 1971 reprint as the rights witness.
4. **Dudley C. Haynes, _The Baptist Denomination: Its History, Doctrines, and Ordinances_ (1857)** — nineteenth-century institutions, religious liberty, mission activity and polity as historically understood. Library of Congress original digitized 1857 edition https://www.loc.gov/item/33008574/ (Haynes 1809–1888; LOC states its digitized historical volume is public domain in the United States). This overlaps thematically with B-C3 and B-C5: select historical narrative, not polity or missionary excerpts already assigned elsewhere.
5. **William Cathcart (ed.), _The Baptist Encyclopaedia_, vol. 2 (1881)** — geographically broader biographical and mission history, including Asian Baptist figures. Princeton Theological Seminary 1881 facsimile https://commons.ptsem.edu/id/baptistencyclopa02cath (Cathcart 1826–1908). **Special rights gate:** this is a multi-contributor edited encyclopedia. Cathcart's death does NOT clear signed entries by other authors. Select only entries whose individual author/anonymous status and Mexico rights can be established; otherwise replace this candidate.
