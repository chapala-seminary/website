# Revelation Intensive unit 3 — Spanish “riñones” clarification (student-reported)
**27 October 2026 | Nonproduction course-quality handoff; not an approved lesson edit**

## Verified facts
- A student asked whether Revelation 2:23 in the Thyatira lesson should say **mente** instead of **riñones**.
- Live-source repository witness: `src/content/lessons/CTSRev/3.json` on `main`, blob `c43610d2f9bcd3c7d6c605dc6cb2f162cf44cf7e`; block `b019` Spanish text includes “yo soy el que escudriña los riñones y los corazones” (Revelation 2:23). The block's Spanish translation metadata is marked human-reviewed.
- The 1909 Reina-Valera has the literal “riñones y los corazones”; the 1960 Reina-Valera uses “la mente y el corazón.” Greek `νεφρούς` literally denotes kidneys, figuratively the inward thoughts or affections. The student's concern is a **comprehension/translation-era issue**, not evidence of a Greek-text mistake.
- Witnesses checked: https://bible.4disciples.com/es/Revelation/2/23/ (1909 and Greek lexical alignment); https://www.bibliatodo.com/ceb/bibliya/Reina-Valera-1909/apocalipsis-2-23 (1909/1960 comparison).

## Recommended minimal editorial repair
