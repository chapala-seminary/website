## Historical and academic safeguards

Broadus wrote in the nineteenth-century U.S. South and served as a Confederate chaplain. His work must be taught as a historically situated primary source. Course lessons should explicitly reject racism and critically identify any racial, social, or political assumptions inherited from the source context. Dargan's 1898 revision must be attributed separately from Broadus's original 1870 work.
