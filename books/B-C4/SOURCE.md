- Internet Archive item: https://archive.org/details/treatiseonprepar00broa
- OCR text used: https://archive.org/download/treatiseonprepar00broa/treatiseonprepar00broa_djvu.txt
- Page-image source for collation: https://archive.org/details/treatiseonprepar00broa
- Original copyright notice: 1870, John A. Broadus
- Author: John A. Broadus (1827–1895)
- Replacement-volume scan includes later preservation notices; those notices and other added front matter are not part of the selected excerpts.

The underlying 1870 text is public domain in the United States. Broadus died in 1895, so the 1870 text is also beyond Mexico's life-plus-100 term. This is a source-status determination for curriculum preparation, not legal advice. Recheck any jurisdiction-specific rule before public release.

## Why this replaces the 1898 package

The previously staged 1898 edition was revised by Edwin C. Dargan (1852–1930). Dargan's contributions may remain within Mexico's life-plus-100 term through 2030. Therefore the 1898 full-text files were replaced in place with selections from Broadus's 1870 first edition. Do not restore or publish the 1898 Dargan revision without separate rights review.

## Historical and academic safeguards

Broadus enslaved people and served as a Confederate chaplain. His work must be taught as a historically situated primary source. Course lessons should explicitly reject racism and critically identify racial, social, or political assumptions inherited from the source context.
