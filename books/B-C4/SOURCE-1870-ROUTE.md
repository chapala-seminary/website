**Status: verified bibliographic/scan route, not yet retrieved OCR or collated text.**

## Why this is necessary
The existing `books/B-C4/textbook.md` and five readings derive from the **1898 23rd edition revised by Edwin Charles Dargan**. Broadus died in 1895; Dargan died in **1930**. Dargan's creative additions and revisions therefore require careful Mexico life+100 screening (through 2030) and must not be assumed cleared because the book was printed before 1931 in the United States. Claude has flagged and excluded identifiable Dargan additions in its own teaching build; a clean Broadus-only source is preferable.
