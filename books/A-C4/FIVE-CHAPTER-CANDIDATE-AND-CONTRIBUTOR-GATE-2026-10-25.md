## Independently discovered contributor-rights stop condition

Part 06, offset **78076**, opens **Chapter LXXXV, “Further Details of the Dallas Meeting,”** and explicitly credits **Carrie Judd Montgomery, editor of *Triumphs of Faith***. This is **not** Woodworth-Etter-authored material merely because it is bound into her book. The original chapter heading itself identifies a different author. The institutional Home of Peace biography gives Montgomery's death as **26 July 1946**: https://homeofpeace.com/history/mrs-carrie-frances-judd-montgomery/ ; Christian History Institute independently confirms **1946**: https://christianhistoryinstitute.org/dailystory/permalink/carrie-judd-healing .

The **2026** Mexican Federal Copyright Act (LFDA), Article 29(I), continues to state **life plus 100 years** for economic rights: https://www.wipo.int/wipolex/es/legislation/details/23966 . Thus Montgomery's own work is **not presumptively free for Mexican republication in 2026**; obtain permission or exclude it. The exact expiration date, authorship/assignment and possible exceptions are **legal-review questions**, not approved here. Chapter LXXXVI includes a hymn and similarly requires lyric/composer screening. This proves the per-contribution gate is essential across the entire volume, including the five proposed windows.
