**Do not mislabel issue 11 as simply October 1907** or issue 13 as Vol. I. The issue 11 masthead explicitly spans October–January 1908. These are original **Los Angeles** issues, not later Portland editions.

**Contributor-level Mexico rights:** issues 10–13 contain individually attributed reports and letters, including H. L. Blake and Alice Taylor (No. 10); W. J. Tomlinson, Mary A. Martin and Andrew G. Johnson (No. 11); Wm. H. Durham and others (No. 12); and Lucy M. Leatherman and other contributors (No. 13). The death years and rights for these authors have **not** been established. Therefore use **links to historical issues** as optional reading leads, but do not copy the complete issues into CTS materials or translate signed contributions based on a blanket 1906–1908 public-domain assertion. Distinguish source attribution and the legal status of each passage.

**Academic caution:** the periodicals record claims of miraculous healing, languages, and other spiritual experiences; describe them as claims of historical witnesses rather than independently verified medical or linguistic facts. Do not endorse statements that people should abandon medicines.
