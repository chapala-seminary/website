## Preliminary Mexican rights assessment — NOT a clearance

- Current Mexican *Ley Federal del Derecho de Autor*, Article 29, generally provides life plus 100 years for identified authors and a separate 100-year-after-disclosure category. The current law entry is at WIPO Lex (amended through 14 May 2026): https://www.wipo.int/wipolex/es/legislation/details/23966 . This is an initial screen, not a legal opinion.
- The 1906–1907 newspaper issue text is a strong **historical public-domain candidate**, but do **not** attribute every unsigned article to Seymour or apply Seymour's life dates indiscriminately. Identify bylined contributors and editorial/testimonial authors, dates, coauthors, and the relevant applicable term for each passage. The 1991 reprint and modern edited transcriptions may contain separately protected original additions.
- Separate rights in **original periodical expression**, **CTS-created English digests and editorial medical guidance**, **Spanish/French/Swahili translations**, facsimiles, modern transcription/edition corrections, and external quotations. Original scans and page-line citations are not yet collated; modern derivative rights and translation permissions are not confirmed.
- A-C1's medical warning is appropriate as an educational safety distinction, but faculty should review the language of Q32 across four languages to ensure the question tests the assigned CTS guidance, not an implied endorsement of avoiding medical care.
