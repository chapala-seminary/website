## Cross-artifact gates that remain open

1. **Contributor/derivative gate.** Existing source review explicitly holds the named Pendleton and Rosa material (Q7–Q8), the unidentified Methodist layman (Q12), and the unidentified first-time visitor (Q28). Their continued presence in the public bank is a provenance-consistency failure, not a legal conclusion.
2. **Facsimile gate.** The staged readings derive from HTML transcriptions; original issue/page/column boundaries and every prompt-bearing passage still require scan collation and an emendation ledger.
3. **Rights gate.** Mexico contributor-level review remains open. Pre-1931 United States status does not itself resolve each contributor, translation, or publication question in Mexico.
4. **Academic-independence gate.** R1 is 98.679% body-contained in the textbook; R2–R5 are distinct later-issue primary passages but still represent one periodical, not five independent scholarly voices.
5. **Assessment gate.** The private bank, accepted variants, and durable EN/ES/FR/SW grading were not available to this audit. Public FR/SW prompts do not prove that French or Swahili responses can pass.
6. **Student-fairness gate.** The live requirement is student-facing now. Do not silently disable it, revoke prior credit, substitute questions, or alter requirements without Wayne/faculty approval and a verified migration plan.
