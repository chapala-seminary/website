# C-C2 — Preliminary source and Mexico rights evidence (25 October 2026)

**Decision status:** research note only; not a legal clearance or authorization to publish translated/edited editions.

1. **Identity and edition.** Project Gutenberg catalog [ebook 59743](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/59743) identifies *The works of the Rev. John Wesley, Vol. 01 (of 32)*, John Wesley (1703–1791), public domain in the USA, released 12 June 2019. It credits Richard Hulse and the Online Distributed Proofreading Team, using Internet Archive scans. The staged `SOURCE.md` identifies the Bristol William Pine 1771 printing; scan-to-text collation is not complete.
2. **Author's death.** Historical *Dictionary of National Biography* entry [Wesley, John](https://www.ccel.org/ccel/ccel/eee/files/wesleyj.htm) records death **2 March 1791**. Project Gutenberg's catalog independently lists 1703–1791. These establish that Wesley's original eighteenth-century sermons exceed the usual Mexico life-plus-100 period.
3. **Mexican statute.** WIPO Lex's consolidated [Ley Federal del Derecho de Autor](https://www.wipo.int/wipolex/es/legislation/details/20225) reproduces Article 29(I): the author's lifetime plus 100 years. This supports a **preliminary** public-domain assessment for Wesley's original 1771 words in Mexico. WIPO's available consolidated version is not itself a current legal opinion; confirm the governing version before academic publication.
4. **Scope exclusions.** The above does **not** clear a contemporary edition's editorial notes, illustrations, independent introductions, modern translations, website presentation, trademark usage, or material added by transcribers. A modern Spanish or French translation must have its own rights basis. Our new CTS digests are original summaries; full translations have **not** been made.
5. **Textual integrity.** Pine 1771 and Jackson 1872 sermon numbering/variants must be reconciled. Preserve exact chapter/sermon references and mark transcriber-added footnotes before creating examination questions. The five staged readings are five two-sermon portions of **one textbook**, not five separate works; this is a curricular approval question for Wayne.
6. **Release holds.** Human theological review, ES/FR language review, scan collation, rights review of additions, academic decision on independent readings, full source translations, and PRIVATE answer-key banks remain open. No public exam or production deployment is authorized.

**Source audit boundary:** Three named external sources above were checked on 25 October 2026. The complete original scans were not collated, and no legal conclusion was made.
