## Textbook: primary historical edition

John Wesley (1703–1791), *The Works of the Rev. John Wesley*, vol. I (Bristol: William Pine, **1771**), **Sermons I–X** (ten complete sermons). Digitized as Project Gutenberg ebook **59743**, release June 12, 2019, transcription from original book scans. This is the **1771 Pine edition**, not the 1872 Thomas Jackson edition; variant wording and sermon numbering should be checked before mixing with Jackson-based course material.

## Rights screen (preliminary, not a legal opinion)

- Wesley died **1791**. The **1771** original text is historical public domain in the U.S. (pre-1931 publication) and meets the usual Mexico life-plus-100 term for Wesley's original authorship.
- Gutenberg transcription and modern editorial apparatus: strip its trademark/license boilerplate and modern transcriber notes from any CTS republication; verify individual modern additions before reuse. The current staging retains any inline editorial markers; check them at final collation.
- Do **not** import modern Spanish/French translations from external websites without their own rights basis. Commission new EN-based translations or use separately cleared historical translations. Do not treat a source link as permission to republish its modern site-specific presentation.
