## Rights and integrity screening — preliminary, not final legal approval
Carey died in 1834; his 1792 original work is beyond both the U.S. pre-1931 publication cutoff and Mexico's author life+100 threshold. The original work can be screened as public domain. ## Academic cautions
Carey's religious/geographical terms and world-population tables reflect 1792 assumptions and colonial-era categories, not present-day CTS terminology or reliable modern demographic data. Teach the text as a primary historical document, contrast it with modern missiology and ethical reflection, and avoid equating eighteenth-century descriptions with current communities. Section III's historical numerical tables warrant especially careful collation.
