NO.4 of Pentecostal Testimony reached my wife today, and since I took it up to read, I passed just about

and this last one has done likewise. The truth always
refreshes and vitalizes the souls that are open to it, and
with the many hundreds of God's saints throughout the
earth, we praise God for what He has been doing' in
Chicago.
While still in America, God showed me the truth
about this Pentecostal Outpouring and in May, 1907.
at Columbia, S. c., I began to seek my personal
baptism. At the same time He showed me the Scriptural distinctiol1 between the "gift of tongues" and the

baptized in Him. The latter I saw clearly was the infallible sign of being baptized into the Holy Spirit.
These points of truth were clear to me when I began
to tarry.
.
God, in His wisdom, permitted me to leave for India
in the fall of 1907, and set me down in the midst of
some missionaries in India, who had heard, but had not
yet seen the Spirit poured out upon a believer. During
Christmas week of that year we decided to have some
waiting meetings and on December 26, 1907, the Lord
Jesus Christ wrought what He showed me He would
do in the previous Spring. The Holy Spirit came
mightily upon me, taking possession of my entire being,
and declared His presence by speaking for some hours
in other tongues. During this same time in which the
Spirit was speaking, God was pleased to lay bare His
heart of love to my redeemed soul by causing me to see
Christ on the Cross, bearing away the sin of the world.
At that time Romans 5:5 became a glowing reality to
my soul. Hallelujah for such a salvation!
In connection with two others, God led out to publish abroad what He had done and was so willing, yea
waiting and wanting to do for all His people. We had
the joy of seeing many souls likewise enter into the
cloud. Part of the time I have been privileged to labor
with Brother A.H. Post, of Los Angeles, who has
often spoken. of his experience with you in Chicago.
We are expecting him to rejoin us again in the work
in Bombay in the very near future.
The Lord's leadings were of such a character last
Spring that I found it necessary to separate from my
mission and launch out, after the manner of the Pentecostal missionaries. Since I clearly understood it as
His will, I followed with joy, and at present we are
working in connection with a Pentecostal Home in
Bombay, conducted by Miss Orlebar; The intense
opposition' toward this present outpouring has been
manifested to an unusual degree in India, but God has
enabled us to some extent to recognize this as an opportunity for faith and a call to press the battle to the
gates. On the whole, many have entered into the
Baptism. Many friends gathered during this past
Christmas for two weeks' conference in Fyzabad, No.
India. The Lord rejoiced us with His presence and
manifested that His hand was upon us by baptizing
several more of His saints. Other incidents of His
working could be recorded, but at present I will refrain.

There are three distinct kinds of manifestations. The
first is produced by the direct operation of the Spirit
of God. It is this one, and only this that we wish to
defend. The other extreme is- any manifestation which
is produced by the direct operation of demon power.
No one with any knowledge of Scripture can deny that
the devil has power and that he gets control of people
whenever possibl~. Between these two is what I
would call manifestations of the flesh. There is much
that \ould not be said to be of God, and yet I would
not dare to say it was of the devil. My opinion is that
many people are entirely too liberal with the devil.
That is, they are too quick to ascribe to him any manifestation or work which they do not understand. Such
people, as soon as they see a physical manifestation,
are ready to cry out that it is the devil; simply because
they do not understand it. If asked for proof that it "
is Satan they simply say: "0, God would never work
in that way," as though they were authority on what
God will or will not do.
As some who in a general way believe in the Pentecostal movement are afraid of physical manifestations,
we will deal briefly with these three kinds of manifestations mentioned, in the hope that many will be made
to see the truth. While it would be wrong to say that
every physical manifestation is of God, it is entirely
wrorig to say that any great per cent of them are from
the devil. N or is it correct to say that that which i5
physical is of the flesh, as so many do say. God is putting His power not only upon our spirits, but upon our
bodies as well. Paul says, "The body is the temple of
the Holy Spirit." The Spirit of God comes upon and
into our bodies, and physical manifestations are the
inevitable result. So that a physical manifestation
which is produced by the direct operation of the Holy
Spirit is just as real and acceptable as a shout or similar Spiritual manifestation. To my mind, by far the
greater part of physical manifestations which I have
seen, such as shaking, falling under the power, etc.,
were produced by the direct operation of the Spirit
of God upon the physical beings of His children. I
have seen several cases, howeve'r, where I believe it
was the flesh working. In other cases when God really
works, folks become self-conscious and desire to be
seen, and thus it becomes evident that while God is
working the flesh is working also. In many genuine
experiences the flesh is allowed to get in in some little
measure, and God is hindered and the person does not
have as beautiful an experience as he would, had he
kept self entirely out of it. I would have to say that
in a few cases, perhaps a half dozen or more, I have
seen what I was compelled to believe was the power of
Satan working, but it has generally been in persons
,vho have at some time dabbled with Spiritualism, or in
the case' of divorced and remarried women.
Some one will say, "If there have been cases where
the enemy got hold of people, is it really safe for us
to seek the experience?" Our answer is, that it is
perfectly safe for any honest person to seek to be
filled with the Holy Spirit, as the Blood of Christ in
which they trust makes it impossible for Satan to
touch them. They are in no more danger than an
honest sinner is in when he is seeking God for salvation. In other words, we have too much confidence in
God to believe He would allow an honest soul while
seeking His face, and desiring to be filled with the

anything. The words of Christ on this lirie have great

stone?" etc. There is no safer place under Heaven for

He has promised to His children.

Blood of Christ alone, and ask Him for the gift of the
Holy Spirit-not that you may be great and powerful,
but that you may be brought into a right relationship
to God, and possess your promised inheritance, and
that Jesus Christ may be glorified through the Holy
Spirit's dwelling in you. To my mind, it is a sad mistake to seek God for His Spirit's power with a desire
prominent in your mind to do or be something. A
person seeking power with a desire to do some great
thing, who is not perfectly honest in his desire to he
right with God, or who does not first seek to be fully

side of the question, I make this attempt to *answer
some of the statements which he makes. I shall have
only time to touch on a very few points, and space
compels me to deal briefly with those I shall be able to
touch on at all. I wish to' state that I always had a
very high regard for Mr. Dixon, as I have believed
him to be faithfully preaching Christ as the Savior,
and standing firmly for the Bible as the Word of God.
1 have no desire to say or do any thing against him,

and to which I believe my brother has failed to do
justice.
In the first place Mr. Dixon admits of a doubt
as to whether the Apostles and others on the day

the people simply heard in their own language. The

10 :47, Peter says of the people at the house of Cornelius, "They have received the Holy Ghost as well as
we." In Acts I I :15, Peter says, "The 'Holy Spirit

he received on the day of Pentecost, entirely removes

HOW THE WORK IS PROGRESSING..
In the December, 1909, number of PENTECOSTAL
TESTIMONY we published a brief history of the work
in Chicago, or, rather, that part of it with which we
are connected.' We. found that, though our article
was lengthy, it was only an abridged account of the
work, as it would .be impossible to report all the Lord
has done in anyone article.
Since the publication of this article, the Lord has
continued to work in a beautiful and wonderful way.
Many have been baptized in the Holy Spirit, and many
have been healed, and in many ways results have been
obtained that cannot be reported in detail.
In the last report it was stated that the Lord was
beginning to work in the Humboldt Park Swedish
Baptist Church, and that its three deacons had received
the Holy Ghost. A little later the Lord met and baptized the pastor, and a few days ago one of the deacons
told me that more than one hundred people have received the Boly Ghosf up to date, including all the officers (so far as I know) and a large percentage of the
members. Best of all, the Lord continues to work,
and this church is literally transformed into a Pentecostal Assembly. So far as I know they are taking
the s-ame firm stand for the truths of Pentecost that
we take. Glory to God for this ?Teat work which He
has wrought in the power of the Holy Ghost!
We also reported that a number of Persian brethren
had been baptized in water and in the- Holy Ghost here.
Since then numbers have continued to receive, and the
power of God has increased upon them. Recently
they have been expelled from Moody's Church, and,
after waiting on the Lord, felt He would have them
open a mission on the North Side of Chicago. So,
two weeks ago, they opened a beautiful little place at
821 North Clark street, near Chicago avenue.
The
place was full at the first service, and the power and
glory of God \vas upon the service. There were many
beautiful manifestations of the Spirit. The singing in
the Spirit was beautiful indeed; then there was speaking in tongues, interpretation, etc. The people from
different missions participated in this service. Elders
Sinclair and Bell, Millen, Reese, the writer and some
others were present. Brother Andrew Urshan is the
pastor of this mission.
The It~lian brethren have bought and ehlarged and
beautified their mission on GLand avenue, between
Center and May streets. The seating capacity has
been increased at least one-third. Last week I was
there and preached for them one night, and the place
was full, and was also full of the powert'!Pand. glory
of God. The people were simply shaking and trembling under the mighty power of God, and there was
a constant volume of praise ascending to God that
Was beautiful to hear. Oh, how willing God is to work
if only He is given a chance! Brother Ottoline, wpo
has spent two years in St. Louis in the work of the
Lord, is soon to leave for Italy with his whole family,
where he will preach the blessed Pentecostal Gospel to
all whom the Lord gives him access.
There is a prayer band which meets in Oak Park.
I have not the number of their meeting place. There
is also a company which meets in Berwyn three or
four nights in the week, in the different homes.
Brother Fred Peterson is the leader of this band.
.The home work is going forward, with the blessing
and power of God upon it. In the Sunday services
the mission is crowded to the doors, and the attend-

. The work at 1414 Barry avenue still goes on among
the Swedish people, and there is a mission on Willow
street, near Halsted, where the services are in the

In our work we observe the ordinance of the Lord's
supper at 6 :45 p. m. every Sunday, and much blessing
comes to us in doing so. We baptize by immersion
only, and I humbly believe there have been one thousand people immersed in our work ih three years.
We recognize only two ordi1)ances in the Gospelthe Lord's supper and baptism. The ordinances of the
Gospel symbolize grace. Weare buried by baptism
into His death. We commune of the body and blood
of the Lord. One ordinance symbolizes our getting
. into Christ, the other our' partaking of Christ, or of
His coming into us. The Gospel is briefly summed up
in these few words: the believer in Christ, and Christ
in the believer. We believe in the rites and ceremonies, customs, etc., in their proper places, but we
do not believe. anointing' with oil or laying on of
hands, or foot-washing to be ordinances, but we recognize them to be Scriptural, and believe in giving
them their proper places.
