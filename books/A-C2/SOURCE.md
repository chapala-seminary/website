## Primary candidate and blocker

- William J. Seymour (1870–1922), *The Doctrines and Discipline of the Azusa Street Apostolic Faith Mission of Los Angeles, California, with Scripture Readings* (Los Angeles, 1915). Original publication independently cataloged at https://books.google.com/books/about/The_Doctrines_and_Discipline_of_the_Azus.html?id=12iVtAEACAAJ .
- The accessible 2000 Christian Life Books / Larry E. Martin edition is a **modern rearranged and edited reprint**, with added contemporary forewords, photographs and editorial matter. It is **not** a rights-cleared source for full copying or translation in Mexico. Catalog: https://openlibrary.org/works/OL8802376W/The_Doctrines_and_Discipline_of_the_Azusa_Street_Apostolic_Faith_Mission_of_Los_Angeles .
- Gastón Espinosa's 2014 Duke University Press documentary edition also contains the 1915 text with modern selection, transcription and commentary; it is a modern scholarly publication, **link only**, not an extraction source. https://www.degruyterbrill.com/document/doi/10.1515/9780822376873-015/html
- The historical Azusa site https://www.azusastreet.org/William%20Seymour%20Doctrine.htm provides selected excerpts and a description, but not a verified complete original 1915 edition.
- **Blocker:** No complete, independently scanned 1915 original found in this source pass. Do not copy the 2000/2014 modern editions and claim them to be 1915 public domain.
