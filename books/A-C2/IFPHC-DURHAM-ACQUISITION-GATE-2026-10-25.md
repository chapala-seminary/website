## Smallest safe acquisition procedure

1. Use a free iFPHC account to open the official *Pentecostal Testimony* collection.
2. Download only the archive-provided historical issue scans; do not buy materials or use modern edited reprints.
3. Preserve each file unchanged in a private or nonproduction source workspace. Record the official item URL, displayed issue date/number, filename, file size, page count and SHA-256.
4. Inspect the printed masthead/title page. Reconcile the displayed catalog date with the scan itself; flag uncertainty instead of normalizing it silently.
5. Build an article-level inventory with printed page range, exact title, visible byline/signature, opening/closing text, and whether the item continues across pages or issues.
6. Classify authorship separately for every article, letter, testimony, hymn and embedded quotation. Durham's death in 1912 does not clear an anonymous or separately signed contributor.
7. Only Durham-authored, page-collated passages may enter the initial A-C2 candidate pool. Keep world-news items and third-party testimony out unless separately verified.
8. Select five nonoverlapping substantive readings only after the issue inventory is complete. Prefer passages that together document the Finished Work position, conversion/sanctification, Spirit baptism and gifts, atonement/healing claims, and ministry/ecclesial practice—**but do not assign these themes until exact Durham articles support them**.
9. Create corrected English derivatives from the scans, preserving raw images/OCR separately. Record printed pages, exact word counts, source hashes and all editorial corrections.
10. After rights and academic review, prepare ES/FR translations and original digests. Assessment banks and keys belong only in the private assessment repository.

- article-inventory.csv: issue, pages, title, byline/signature, first/last text, continuation, preliminary author status, rights status.
- durham-candidate-ledger.md: candidate passage, exact pages, rationale, exclusions, nonoverlap, word count and unresolved questions.
- Corrected EN text only after page collation; ES/FR and digests only after that.
- No answer keys, student records, grades, production files, merge or deployment.

## Stop conditions

Stop and report rather than proceed if login demands payment; the displayed issue and scan disagree; a scan is incomplete; an article lacks reliable authorship; a reading boundary splits a sentence or continuation; a modern editor's transcription is the only accessible text; or the requested material would expose answer keys/student data.
