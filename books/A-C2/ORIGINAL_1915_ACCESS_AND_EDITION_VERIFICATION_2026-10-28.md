# A-C2 original 1915 Seymour manual — access and edition verification

**Date:** 2026-10-28  
**Scope:** source-access research for A-C2 on `handoff/book-sources` only.  
**Status:** the original 1915 edition is bibliographically verified and two physical archival holdings are now identified; no complete open-access original scan was located in this pass. No text, translation, assessment, rights, or production release is authorized.

## Verified findings

1. **Original 1915 bibliographic record.** Google Books catalogs William J. Seymour's *The Doctrines and Discipline of the Azusa Street Apostolic Faith Mission of Los Angeles, California: With Scripture Readings* as published in 1915. The record does not expose a downloadable complete scan in the inspected interface:
   - https://books.google.com/books/about/The_Doctrines_and_Discipline_of_the_Azus.html?id=12iVtAEACAAJ

2. **Flower Pentecostal Heritage Center holding.** The FPHC archive search returns a distinct book record titled *The doctrines and discipline of the Azusa Street Apostolic Faith Mission of Los Angeles, Cal.: with scripture readings*, author William J. Seymour, separate from the Seymour/Martin modern reprint record:
   - https://archives.ifphc.org/index.cfm?ResourceTypes=Books%2CPhotographs%2CAudio%2FVisual%2CRecord+Group%2CArtifacts%2CDocuments%2CPeriodicals&fuseaction=search.moreArchivesBySubject&search_Subject=Azusa+Street+Mission+%28Los+Angeles%2C+CA%29--Doctrines.&search_referrer=search.moreArchivesBySubject

3. **Dixon Pentecostal Research Center holding.** Lee University's Azusa Street research guide lists the original/undated early edition at call number **Pent. BX8762.5.Z5 S4 1915**, separately from the 2000 Christian Life Books reprint:
   - https://leeuniversity.libguides.com/c.php?g=458417&p=3133463

4. **Modern-edition distinction remains material.** The 2000 Christian Life Books edition is cataloged with Larry Martin as editor/reprint arranger and modern front matter; it is not a substitute for an original-image source:
   - https://openlibrary.org/books/OL8527001M/The_Doctrines_and_Discipline_of_the_Azusa_Street_Apostolic_Faith_Mission_of_Los_Angeles
   - https://books.google.com/books/about/The_Doctrines_and_Discipline_of_the_Azus.html?hl=en&id=Q7sgAAAACAAJ&output=html_text

5. **Modern documentary editions are corroborating references only.** The 2014 Duke/JSTOR documentary edition reproduces Seymour's manual in a modern scholarly publication. It may help identify structure and provenance, but it does not remove the need for original-image collation or permission analysis for modern editorial matter:
   - https://www.jstor.org/stable/j.ctv11cvzs4.17
   - https://api.pageplace.de/preview/DT0400.9780822376873_A35684097/preview-9780822376873_A35684097.pdf
