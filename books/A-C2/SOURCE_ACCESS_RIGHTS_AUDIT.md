## Verified distinction: 1915 original vs. 2000 edition

1. **1915 bibliographic record:** Google Books catalogs William J. Seymour, *The Doctrines and Discipline of the Azusa Street Apostolic Faith Mission of Los Angeles, California: With Scripture Readings* (1915). This confirms an edition record, **not access to a complete 1915 facsimile or checked transcription**. https://books.google.com/books/about/The_Doctrines_and_Discipline_of_the_Azus.html?id=12iVtAEACAAJ
2. **Current azusastreet.org source is partial:** The historical article says the 1915 book is rare and links to **excerpts** (doctrinal overview, amended articles, constitution). It also offers a modern reprint for sale. The site's own description does **not** establish that its linked pages reproduce the full original. https://www.azusastreet.org/William%20Seymour%20Doctrine.htm
3. **2000 edition is not the 1915 source:** Open Library records Larry E. Martin's *The Doctrines and Discipline ...* (Christian Life Books, 2000; ISBN 9780964628991), including new prefatory material and an edited presentation. The publisher's description explicitly identifies a reorganization and modern spelling/grammar corrections. The 2000 edition must **not** be represented as an unaltered scan of the 1915 book, and modern forewords, layout, photography, editorial additions and other original editorial content must not be copied without permission. https://openlibrary.org/books/OL8527001M/The_Doctrines_and_Discipline_of_the_Azusa_Street_Apostolic_Faith_Mission_of_Los_Angeles ; https://www.drlarrymartin.org/bookstore_azusa7.htm
4. **Compilation provenance:** Both historical descriptions acknowledge that the 1915 manual incorporates material from other church disciplines. Seymour's death in 1922 alone does **not** prove the provenance or rights status of every incorporated passage. Establish original authors/editions and any modern modifications before a Mexico life-plus-100 assessment. A historical Methodist discipline may provide independently verifiable primary passages, but should not be silently labeled Seymour's original writing.
