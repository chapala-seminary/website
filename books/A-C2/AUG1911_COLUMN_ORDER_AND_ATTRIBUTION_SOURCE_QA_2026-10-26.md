## Confirmed source defects and boundaries
1. **Printed p.4 is not one article.** The first-person Azusa Street account begins under its heading on p.3, continues into p.4, and closes before a separate `TESTIMONY.` heading on p.4. The new testimony continues onto p.5 and ends with the byline **R. J. Scott**. A whole-p.4 extraction would merge two speakers, and a p.4–5 extract attributed entirely to Durham would wrongly include Scott's separate testimony. The heading `TESTIMONY` is not proof that the preceding Azusa narrative and following signed Scott testimony have the same author. Verify Azusa byline/editorial attribution on the facsimile before final attribution.
2. **Printed p.5 has another boundary.** After the R. J. Scott signature, the heading `THE TWO GREAT EXPERIENCES OR GIFTS` begins a different theological article. That article continues on p.6 and into p.7. A complete p.5 transcription is not a self-contained reading.
3. **Printed p.7 contains an article transition.** After the preceding theological discussion, `THE SECOND WORK OF GRACE PEOPLE ANSWERED` begins on p.7; the OCR renders the first word as `TIdE`. The text continues on p.8. Headings should be checked against the image rather than matched by exact OCR spelling alone.
4. **Printed p.8 has demonstrably interleaved columns.** In the OCR, a left-column clause about separation of soul/spirit/body is immediately followed by a right-column clause about words spoken to disciples, and subsequent left/right clauses alternate within sentences. The page is **not** a faithful continuous reading order; this is more serious than a typo and cannot be fixed by ordinary proofreading.
5. **Printed p.12 has another severe column-order collision.** The heading for `AN OPEN LETTER TO MY BROTHER MINISTERS IN AND OUT OF THE PENTECOSTAL MOVEMENT` is inserted mid-sentence into an unrelated discussion about tongues/evidence. Its words are broken across OCR lines and columns; the p.12 source text then interleaves both articles. The open letter continues onto p.13. Searching for its complete title as a contiguous exact string will fail, and extracting p.12–13 as plain OCR risks importing another author's material.
6. **Cross-issue caution remains in force.** The separate eight-issue audit established extensive May/August 1912 memorial reprinting and a repeated Feb 1911/Jan 1912 article. Do not count reprints as independent evidence or five distinct readings.
