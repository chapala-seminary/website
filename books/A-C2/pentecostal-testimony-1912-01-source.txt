The year of Our Lord 1911, which has just closed,
has been one of unusual activity along all lines.
During the entire year the smoke of battle has never
once cleared away. It opened with a battle, and
ended with a battle. The beginning of the year
found us in the city of Chicago, proclaiming boldly
the "The Finished Work of Calvary." Early in January we went to Minneapolis, Minn., and conducted
a ten days' meeting. Here the Lord gave blessed
victory and about twenty-two were baptized in the
Spirit. From there we returned to Chicago, where
Brother Van Loon was pushing the battle and
together we continued the battle there, till the ninth
of February, when we left for California. During
the month of January we had thirty-two baptisms in
Chicago. .
About the first of February the number seven of
the Pentecostal Testimony came out, and as soon as
it went into circulation the intensity of the heat of
the battle increased. God had been burning the
truth of the Finished Work of Calvary into my heart
for two or three years, and as the light came I had
been preaching it and standing for it in the paper,
when I referred to the subject at all. The article
"IDENTIFICATION WITH CHRIST" in the
number six had stirred up considerable opposition,
but it was the number seven that brought on the
battle proper.
The truth had taken such a finn grip on my heart
and soul, that I could do nothing less than stand for
it, and proclaim it with all my power. I saw clearly
