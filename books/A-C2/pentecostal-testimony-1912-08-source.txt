IN MEMORIAM
On the seventh day of July, nineteen hundred and
twelve, at ten minutes after eight in the morning,
our beloved brother Durham entered into rest. His
end was peaceful, and he gladly obeyed the divine
summons to enter the Master's presence. The
following facts concerning his life will be gratefully
received by all who knew him:
Wm. H. Durham was born in the state of Kentucky, in the year eighteen hundred and seventythree. He joined a baptist church at the age of
eighteen, but was not converted, and not until he
had lost confidence in religion and wandered into
sin, was he arrested by the Holy Spirit and put
under conviction. Day by day this conviction
deepened, until finally he resolved to yield and call
upon God for mercy. Instantly the Spirit revealed
to him Jesus hanging on the cross and said" Christ
died for your sins." He believed it with all his
heart, and instantly the Spirit witnessed that he
was born again. This was in Minnesota in the year
eighteen hundred and ninety-eight. He was soon
after called of the Lord into active service, and
wherever he went crowds came to the altar and were
saved and healed. This continued until the light
came on the baptism of the Holy Spirit, which he
finally accepted, and travelled from Chicago to Los
Angeles that he might wait on God unmolested by
his pastoral duties. He was baptized in the Spirit on
March .2nd, 1907, and returned to Chicago. And
then began that wonderful minitltrywhich astonished the world. Ten missions preaching the full
gospel sprang up under his' ministry in Chicago.
Ministers from far and near came to his mission and
accepted the truth and went away filled with the
Spirit.
He published Pentecostal Testimony which went
all over the earth, and carried conviction wherever
it went. Scores were converted through reading its
pages, and many hundreds were convicted to seek
for the baptism.
About I::ighteen months ago God led him to fearlessly proclaim the great truth, the finished work of
Calvary. It met with strong opposition, but God
confirmed the truth in such a way that men were
forced to listen. In February 1910, he came to Los
Angeles, the great Pentecostal centre, to deliver his
message, and amidst persecution and opposition of
every kind, God baptized hundreds in the Spirit
in a few months, and established an assembly of
over six hundred people who stand for the truths
of the finished work of Calvary, and the baptism
in the Holy Spirit.
Last February he went to Chicago much weakened
by continual labors to hold a two weeks' convention,
and God gave him the greatest revival of moc:er-~l
times. God thus signifying to the world his approvtl
of his servant up to the last. This wonderful

where we laid his body in the grave, with a certain
