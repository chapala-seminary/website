# A-C2 William H. Durham source route

Status: source located; raw-text acquisition pending.

This note records only sources that were independently verified in public archive indexes. It does not treat search snippets, modern transcriptions, or secondary quotations as authoritative course text.

## Rights screen

- William H. Durham died in 1912. Durham-authored text is therefore the preferred A-C2 source candidate under the course's Mexico public-domain screen.
- Each periodical issue may also contain contributions by other authors. Do not import an entire issue without byline-level review.
- The posthumous compilation *Articles Written by Pastor W. H. Durham Taken from Pentecostal Testimony* is described in scholarship as a 48-page archive compilation, but its compiler/editor and publication history remain insufficiently documented. Use the original Durham-bylined periodical pages instead.
- No usable scan of Seymour's 1915 *Doctrines and Discipline* was found in this search. Do not substitute a modern edition.
