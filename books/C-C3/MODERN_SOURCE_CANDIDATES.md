# C-C3 — modern source candidates and permissions screen

**Status (2026-10-24): research only.** One source below permits unchanged non-commercial sharing under CC BY-NC-ND 4.0; that license forbids derivative works, including translation or adaptation. The other modern books are not cleared for CTS republication or translation in Mexico. Keep them link-only until the precise use is licensed or written permission is received. This is a permissions screen, not legal advice.

## Candidate 1 — Olga Druzhinina, *Introduction to Christian Theology* (2025)

- Publisher: European Nazarene College e.V.; sponsored by European Nazarene College and Global Nazarene Publications.
- Official record/download: https://www.whdl.org/en/browse/resources/22338
- Verified license: Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial-NoDerivatives 4.0 International (CC BY-NC-ND 4.0).
- License link: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-nd/4.0/deed.en
- CTS use now: link to the official record; unchanged non-commercial sharing with attribution and the license notice is potentially within the license.
- Hold: no CTS translation, digest, excerpt adaptation, edited reader, or derivative assessment text should be produced under ND. Seek written permission for any adapted or translated use.

## Candidate 2 — W. T. Purkiser, *Beliefs That Matter Most* (1959)

- Publisher shown by WHDL: Beacon Hill Press.
- Official record/download: https://pointloma.whdl.org/en/browse/resources/20943
- WHDL status: the record states that due diligence determined the item is public domain in the United States because of registration/term/formality issues.
- Mexico caution: U.S. public-domain status does not by itself establish Mexican public-domain status. Purkiser's modern authorship places Mexican life-plus-100 analysis and any publisher rights outside this screening.
- CTS use now: link to WHDL for individual access.
- Hold: obtain written permission before CTS republishes, translates, adapts, or builds a course reader/question bank from the text for Mexican and worldwide distribution.

## Candidate 3 — William M. Greathouse and H. Ray Dunning, *An Introduction to Wesleyan Theology* (1982)

- Publisher: Beacon Hill Press of Kansas City.
- Official WHDL record: https://www.whdl.org/en/browse/resources/20991
- Verified restriction: WHDL permits viewing and one personal printed copy only; it expressly forbids multiple copies and directs users to contact the publisher for translation or publishing use, including digital media.
- CTS use now: bibliographic/link-only shelf item.
- Hold: all republication, course integration, translation, digests, and assessment-bank work require written publisher permission.
