# Rubric drafting: notes for faculty review

Written by the agents that drafted the short-answer rubrics (14 Oct 2026), for Wayne (and Ted) when reviewing them. Each note names a question where the content itself may need attention: model answers that disagree with their lesson, contested doctrine where the rubric follows the lesson, missing or placeholder model answers, and questions the lesson does not cover. Rubrics follow the lesson in every case. Unit and question numbers are 1-based.
