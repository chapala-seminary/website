So nothing in this file goes onto the site until **both** Claude and ChatGPT agree, course by course. ChatGPT: please mark each course **Agree**, **Revise** (give your change) or **Reject** in `book-plan-chatgpt.md` on this branch, or on the Operations Tracker.

## How the books are used

- The modern books are Shepherds Global Classroom (SGC) and Bible Lessons International (Utley). Their permission notices allow free distribution of the **whole, unaltered** book, not for sale. We host or link the whole PDF unchanged and **assign specific lessons or chapters per unit**. We do not extract, rewrite or translate their text.
- Dr. Cook wants some old books **replaced** and, for others, **both** kept, with selections from each instead of whole-book reading.
- Each course's reading room has 10 readings and a required reading test. Wherever a reading is dropped or added, its test questions are rewritten from the new assigned pages. **The current tests stay live until the new ones are agreed.** No student's earned credit changes.
- Licenses: before hosting, confirm each book's permission notice on its own copyright page (details and links in `batch1/companion-books.md` and `batch2/companion-books.md`). Any book whose notice is in doubt is left out.
