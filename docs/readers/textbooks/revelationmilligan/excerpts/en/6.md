# Three Enemies of the Church

William Milligan, *The Expositor’s Bible: The Book of Revelation* (1889), chapter XI, on Revelation 12–14. Assigned excerpt (the opening of the chapter). The interpretations are the author’s; Milligan is one historical interpretive voice.

The twelfth and thirteenth chapters of this book were designed to set before us a picture of the three great enemies of the Church of Christ. We have been told of the dragon, the principle and root of all the evil, whether inward or outward, from which that Church suffers. He is the first enemy. We have been further told of the first beast, of that power or prince of the world to whom the dragon has committed his authority. He is the second enemy.
