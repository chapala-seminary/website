# The Millennium and Interpretive Difficulty

William Milligan, *The Expositor’s Bible: The Book of Revelation* (1889), chapter XVI, on Revelation 20. Assigned excerpt (the opening of the chapter). The interpretations are the author’s; Milligan is one historical interpretive voice.

In now approaching chap. xx., with its yet unsolved difficulties of interpretation, it is of essential importance to observe, in the first place, the relation of the chapter to what immediately precedes. The Seer is not entering upon an entirely new subject. He distinctly continues, on the contrary, the prosecution of a theme he had before begun.
