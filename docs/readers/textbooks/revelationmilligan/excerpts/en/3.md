# The Main Action and the Church’s Victory

William Milligan, *The Expositor’s Bible: The Book of Revelation* (1889), chapter IV, on Revelation 6–18. Assigned excerpt (the opening of the chapter). The interpretations are the author’s; Milligan is one historical interpretive voice.

With the sixth chapter of the Apocalypse the main action of the book may be said properly to begin. Three sections of the seven into which it is divided have already passed under our notice. The fourth section, extending from chap. vi. 1 to chap. xviii. 24, is intended to bring before us the struggle of the Church, the judgment of God upon her enemies, and her final victory. No detail of historical events in which these things are fulfilled need be looked for.
