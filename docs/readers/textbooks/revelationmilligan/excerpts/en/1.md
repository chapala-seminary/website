# The Prologue: Revelation, Not Mystery

William Milligan, *The Expositor’s Bible: The Book of Revelation* (1889), chapter I, on Revelation 1. Assigned excerpt (the opening of the chapter). The interpretations are the author’s; Milligan is one historical interpretive voice.

The first chapter of Revelation introduces us to the whole book, and supplies in great measure the key by which we are to interpret it. The book is not intended to be a mystery in the sense in which we commonly understand that word. It deals indeed with the future, the details of which must always be dark to us; and it does this by means of figures and symbols and modes of speech far removed from the ordinary simplicity of language which marks the New Testament writers. But it is not on that account designed to be unintelligible.
