# Psalm 22: Lament Turns toward Praise

Alexander Maclaren, *The Expositor’s Bible: The Psalms* (1892–94), on Psalm 22. Assigned selection: verses of the Psalm in Maclaren’s own English rendering. The interpretations are the author’s.

My God, my God, why hast Thou forsaken me? Yet Thou art Holy, throned upon the praises of Israel. In Thee our fathers trusted; they trusted and Thou deliveredst them. To Thee they cried and were delivered; in Thee they trusted and were not put to shame. But I am a worm, and not a man; a reproach of men and despised of people. All who see me mock at me; they draw open the lips, they nod the head. “Roll thy cares on Jehovah—let Him deliver him; let Him rescue him, for He delights in him.” I will declare Thy name to my brethren; in the midst of the congregation will I praise Thee. For He has not despised nor abhorred the affliction of the afflicted one, and has not hid His face from him; and when he cried He hearkened to him. All the ends of the earth shall remember and turn to Jehovah; and all the families of the nations shall bow down before Thee. For the kingdom is Jehovah's.
