The **modern volumes are worthwhile introductions**, but both and Hurst reflect historical vantage points. The combined SGC course ends at 2000; neither volume provides 2001–2026 chronology or a sufficiently broad Africa/Asia/Latin America perspective. The **CTS Unit 10 lesson** remains the authority for more recent events until an original, sourced supplement is reviewed.

## Academic notes and release judgment
**Recommend YES** to McElwain volumes alongside *retained* Hurst, without requiring 246 pages cover-to-cover. Ancient dates sometimes presented as exact are approximate or contested (e.g. apostolic martyrdom accounts, canon milestones, 1054's complexity). Give the Orthodox, Catholic, non-Western and Protestant histories a fair hearing. CH2's strong holiness/revival emphasis is useful context, not CTS doctrinal orthodoxy. **A separate original 2001–2026 update is academically desirable but is not grounds to postpone approval of the main reading selection.** CTS may initially teach its existing Unit 10; any new update must be historically sourced, not invented.
