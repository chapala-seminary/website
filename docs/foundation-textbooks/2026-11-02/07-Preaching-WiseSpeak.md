# CTS Foundation 07 — Preaching/WiseSpeak: keep the author's distinctive textbook; add short communication readings
**Prepared 2 November 2026. Ten live teaching units distinguished from the thirteen sections of the original WiseSpeak book.**
## Books: definite recommendation
**Primary text must remain** Wayne W. Cook (with Andi Cook where credited), *WiseSpeak: Simple Skills for Preaching* (1993), CTS-owned complete author source: https://drive.google.com/file/d/114mbF6oxcs4ItXoaXs36JXvmMtSnNPVX/view . **Do not replace WiseSpeak** with a generic homiletics book or remove Andi Cook's authorship contribution. The current website has **10 one-page units**, not the same unit count/order as the **13 chapters of the original authored WiseSpeak book**. The guide below follows the current public course, not a fabricated thirteen-unit website sequence.
**Modern complementary book:** Danny McCain, R. G. Hutchison and Randall D. McElwain, *Principles of Communication*, 3rd ed. (Shepherds Global Classroom, 2023), ISBN 978-1-960285-09-6, 151 PDF pages; exact whole original in CTS faculty archive https://drive.google.com/file/d/1BSU_N735S6P7CeVYllojL4r0HvchpYfE/view . Original publisher PDF https://shepherdsglobal.org/files/english/POC-3E-en.pdf .
**Existing standard graduate homiletics reading:** keep John A. Broadus, *On the Preparation and Delivery of Sermons*, as a further classic when valuable; it must not crowd out short CTS examples or require the full book. Mention its particular historical context and rhetoric.
The published POC contents confirm lessons: L1 Theology of Communication p7; L2 Principles p19; L3 Introduction to Preaching p33; L4 Mechanics p53; L5 Preparing Expository Sermons p67; L6 Written Communication p83; L7 Teaching p93; L8 Human Relations p111; L9 Cross-Cultural p123; L10 Spirit-Anointed p133.
## Proposed reading guide for the 10 currently hosted Preaching units
Read only the relevant teaching prose, not the SGC exercises or test questions. Existing original WiseSpeak teaching and hands-on sermon preparation remain PRIMARY in every unit. Modern short readings in English PDF printed pages are:
| Website unit (current exact heading) | Optional/additional SGC pages | Distinctive CTS emphasis / classic | Original ungraded exercise |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Prepare the Foundation | L3 *Introduction to Preaching*, **33–40** | WiseSpeak text selection; no forced sermonic formula | Choose a text and explain its central claim in one sentence. |
| 2. Develop an Idea | L5 *Preparing Expository Sermons*, **67–74** | Keep CTS keyword and guiding-question method | Give an organizing idea and show where the passage supports it. |
| 3. Construct Your Outline | L4 *Mechanics of Preaching*, **53–60** | Deductive movement and clarity | Write three coherent points that serve the controlling idea. |
| 4. Build Outlines Inductively | L5 *Preparing Expository Sermons*, **75–82** | Inductive journey must remain distinct from deductive structure | Arrange two observations so that listeners discover the claim. |
| 5. Amplify the Outline | L4 *Mechanics of Preaching*, **61–66** | CTS Explanation–Application–Illustration (EAI); true stories preferred | Add one explanation, one application, one honest illustration. |
| 6. Beautify and Refine | L2 *Principles of Communication*, **19–26** | Respect the hearer's attention; plain prose rather than ornament | Replace a technical paragraph with two accessible spoken sentences. |
| 7. Preaching from the Inside Out | L1 *A Theology of Communication*, **7–14**, loosely related only | **Distinct CTS unit; do not imply SGC is equivalent** | Describe how conviction affects tone without manipulating emotion. |
| 8. Preparation and Delivery | L2 *Principles of Communication*, **27–32** | CTS delivery and preparation skills first | Rehearse a 90-second introduction, then note what improved. |
| 9. Four Great Centuries of Preaching | **No SGC counterpart** | Read the CTS historical unit; optional original Broadus historical examples | What can we learn from a historic preacher without importing his era's habits? |
| 10. The Four Purposes of Preaching | L7 *Teaching*, **93–100**, as one purpose's illustration | CTS's own four purposes determine categories | Classify a sermon purpose and explain how the outline serves it. |
**Modern total: 68 pages over nine units (seven 8-page spans and two 6-page spans); SGC reading is a modest supplement, not a substitute for the WiseSpeak book.** Unit 9 does not need an irrelevant modern chapter. Source ranges are wholly inside their listed publisher chapter boundaries. Students may keep the *whole* approved original PDF offline and select the specified sections.
