# CTS Foundation 05 — Pastoral Ministries: two-book selection and 12-unit reading guide
**Prepared 2 November 2026; faculty review. No live lesson, student or examination changes.**
## Definitive book decision proposed
**Preserve as the primary pastoral companion:** Hezekiah Harvey, *The Pastor: His Qualifications and Duties* (classic), complete CTS reference: https://drive.google.com/file/d/1YArfaIdeDvpem4wQ3JtuXGNqncso8BNH/view . Though historical, this covers calling, worship, pastoral visitation, funerals and pastoral character more directly than a management textbook.
**Add, do not replace with:** Stephen K. Gibson, *Ministry Leadership*, 3rd ed. (Shepherds Global Classroom, 2024), ISBN 978-1-960285-28-7, 175 printed PDF pages, verified unaltered CTS archive https://drive.google.com/file/d/1Ujp3BV2UphwYoxAGuXmI1K4bISiVEKHO/view . Original source https://shepherdsglobal.org/files/english/ML-3E-en.pdf .
**Do not force a third book for this cycle.** CTS's own pastoral lessons must cover present-day ethics, safeguarding, hospital privacy, grief care and professional referral. This is a real content gap in *both* volumes; an additional book should be chosen only if it demonstrably fills it and rights are established.

For Harvey, assign **only the named chapter(s) related to the unit**, not the full book. Because the surviving Harvey editions differ, these are chapter-based assignments, not fabricated print page numbers. The CTS unit page should place the Harvey chapter heading in the guide for easy navigation and print use.
## Required faculty cautions
Harvey's 19th-century church assumptions, gender/polity prescriptions and health counsel are historical perspectives, not binding CTS requirements. Modern leadership text also represents its author’s tradition; assess any potentially coercive advice. For hospital, child, abuse and mental-health situations, never treat an old pastoral text as authority over relevant law and professional care. If applied to a different country, student must follow local protection requirements.
