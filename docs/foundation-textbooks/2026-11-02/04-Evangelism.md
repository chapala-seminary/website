**Academic center:** CTS's existing Scripture-based 13-unit Evangelism teaching and CWT material remain primary. SGC brings discipleship continuity absent from some older manuals; Torrey brings useful direct counsel. Neither one's denominational assumptions automatically become CTS doctrine. This selection passes the readable/practical test; a second modern textbook is unnecessary.
