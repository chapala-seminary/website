# Editorial standards for recovered teaching material

Applies to recovery from Wayne Cook's historical Gemini export and other submitted sermons, notes, textbook drafts and teaching outlines. Tracks [issue #261](https://github.com/chapala-seminary/website/issues/261). **Not** a change to CTS's Statement of Faith, degree requirements, student records, or release governance.

## Provenance: quote the author, not the chatbot

1. **Words personally submitted by Wayne** can be quoted in routine editorial changes without repeated individual editorial approval. Verify the original prompt, preserve the argument and distinctive voice, and label translations or condensed versions as adaptations. His *having submitted* words does not itself establish their authorship: inspect the context for pasted third-party material.
2. **Co-written or borrowed source documents** need proper attribution and rights checks before substantial reuse. The historical WiseSpeak manuscript names another contributor; do not assume sole ownership from a Gemini export.
3. **Gemini-generated responses** are only working suggestions. Never attribute their claims, invented illustrations, historical facts, citations or inferred theological positions to Wayne. Do not promote them to source status without independent confirmation.
4. **Keep confidential source files private.** Do not commit the raw export, personal conversation links, unpublished mixed-rights manuscript bundles, test banks, or any student records to public GitHub. Public editorial notes may identify non-sensitive source IDs for traceability, but not publish private conversations.

## Theology

- CTS teaches from the authority of Scripture and the evangelical center of the Gospel, not as a denominational polemic. Core courses should not require students to choose a single side of contested matters such as Calvinism/Arminianism, cessationism/continuationism, millennial interpretation, or a particular model of church government.
- Present the strongest defensible biblical arguments from the major evangelical traditions; do not misdescribe Calvinists as believing every person is maximally evil, or Arminians as earning salvation by human initiative. Distinguish what Scripture explicitly says from a scholar's or tradition's theological inference.
- A tradition-specific elective may teach its own theological commitments directly, **with clear labeling**, while representing alternatives faithfully.
- Read texts in literary and historical context. Verify references, quotations, translations, and original-language claims. Do not force an end-times scheme onto a passage beyond what the text warrants.
- Maintain CTS's existing critical boundaries concerning prosperity-gospel exploitation, church–state conflation/Christian nationalism, religious legalism, and treating earthly nationalism as loyalty higher than Christ. Avoid partisan slogans and unsupported accusations.
- Personal illustrations explain doctrine but do not prove it. Name the limits of an analogy when a reader could infer an unwarranted theological conclusion.

## Teaching and student access

- Retain readable, direct, narrative-rich, practical prose in Wayne's voice. Good doctrinal depth does not require technical vocabulary without explanation.
- Serve resource-limited, often mobile-only students: prioritize downloadable/offline-friendly content, low bandwidth, accessible language, and clear, useful Scripture references. Avoid requiring purchase of unavailable books or relying on unstable outside links.
- Preserve the current Certificate, Associate, M.Div. and Th.M. tracks and their actual exam policies. A recovered older syllabus is historical data, **not authority** to change graduation, accreditation, testing, credit, honor or translation rules.
- Keep master's Part II integrated with the existing foundation courses and five reading rooms rather than building a disconnected parallel program. Add original illustrations or deeper analysis where they improve the material without retreading Part I.
- For biblical counseling, include safety and referral limits; do not imply pastors can replace licensed clinical or emergency care. For third-party book excerpts, confirm the relevant rights or link instead of reproducing them.
- Review the **English and Spanish** texts together; do not overwrite previously human-edited translations through an automatic sync. Reconcile French and Swahili presentation only with tested translation workflow and preserve human edits.
- Preserve URLs, progress keys, test integrity and student records. Never expose answer keys in student-visible files.

## Editorial decision procedure

1. Compare each proposed addition with the **current beta source**, not a superseded lesson or course title alone. Map older units by **subject**, not just numerical unit.
2. In review notes, record source ID and provenance, target lesson/Part II reading, duplicated content, theological/citation/rights questions, and action taken.
3. Reject duplication, insecure data or stale policy. Add a discrete, context-appropriate passage in both teaching languages. Check Scripture/theology and historical provenance; if uncertain, paraphrase as an illustrative recollection without invented details.
4. Stage edits on a focused branch, run repository checks and confirm the exact beta deployment. No direct production changes by the AI account. Production promotion follows the existing Wayne/Robert GitHub approval policy; routine editorial approval is **not** permission to bypass technical release gates.
