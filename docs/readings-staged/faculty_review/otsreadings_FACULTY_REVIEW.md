# Old Testament Survey — draft bilingual reading examination

DRAFT ONLY — NOT FACULTY APPROVED, NOT INSTALLED, NOT REQUIRED

Baseline: CTSOTS five bilingual digests, captured from chapala-seminary/website on 10 October 2026.

**40 fill-in questions**: 8 per reading, draw 4 each, 20 total, pass 18.

**Evidence**: `Answer_Evidence_Ledger.json` ties each primary answer to a paragraph in each CTS language version. Word occurrence in a paragraph is an automated structural check; faculty must confirm the paragraph actually supports the complete question in context.

## Review priorities

- Confirm source questions are faithful to the CTS reading in BOTH languages, not just biblically plausible.
- Supply 3–5 accepted alternatives when meaningful; do not add incorrect synonyms.
- In particular, review names and differing Bible transliterations, singular/plural, and phrasing like “Day of the LORD.”
- The preview uses `requiredFrom: null`: no Master’s completion requirement should be imposed yet.
- Do not copy `otsreadings.preview.NOT_FOR_SRC_CONTENT.json` into `src/content/readings`; it has not passed faculty review, and would require repo code, page, catalog and tests.
- Protect all past awards and completions. Final deployment is Robert’s decision after the full Worker/D1/browser test suite.

## Draft questions

### 1. Reading 1
EN: In the opening chapters of Genesis, human sin brings judgment, yet the reading says ____ always follows. — **grace**
ES: En los primeros capítulos de Génesis, el pecado humano trae juicio, pero la lectura afirma que siempre sigue la ____. — **gracia**

### 2. Reading 1
EN: In Genesis 12, God calls ____ and promises him land, descendants, and blessing to the nations. — **Abraham**
ES: En Génesis 12, Dios llama a ____ y le promete tierra, descendencia y bendición para las naciones. — **Abraham**

### 3. Reading 1
EN: God changes Jacob’s name to ____. — **Israel**
ES: Dios cambia el nombre de Jacob a ____. — **Israel**

### 4. Reading 1
EN: God raises up ____ to lead His enslaved people out of Egypt. — **Moses**
ES: Dios levanta a ____ para conducir a su pueblo esclavizado fuera de Egipto. — **Moisés**

### 5. Reading 1
EN: At Mount ____, Israel receives the Ten Commandments and the Law. — **Sinai**
ES: En el monte ____, Israel recibe los Diez Mandamientos y la Ley. — **Sinaí**

### 6. Reading 1
EN: Under Joshua, Israel crosses the river ____ to enter the promised land. — **Jordan**
ES: Bajo Josué, Israel cruza el río ____ para entrar en la tierra prometida. — **Jordán**

### 7. Reading 1
EN: God promises King ____ an enduring royal throne. — **David**
ES: Dios promete al rey ____ un trono real perdurable. — **David**

### 8. Reading 1

### 9. Reading 2

ES: El Antiguo Testamento contiene ____ libros en la Biblia protestante descrita por el estudio del STC. — **treinta y nueve**

### 12. Reading 2

### 13. Reading 2

### 14. Reading 2

### 15. Reading 2
