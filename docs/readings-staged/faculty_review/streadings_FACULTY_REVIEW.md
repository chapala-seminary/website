40 bilingual fill-in questions, eight on each of five CTS reading digests. Twenty drawn per attempt (four per digest), 18 correct to pass. Answers are in both-language source readings but need faculty review of accuracy, reasonable synonyms, grammar and difficulty.

## Academic cautions

- The sin-and-salvation reading covers the Reformed–Arminian disagreement. These questions do not force the student to endorse either view.
- The last-things reading presents premillennial, amillennial and postmillennial descriptions. The examination tests definitions, not allegiance to a school.
- Primary answers were searched in the actual reading text; that does not establish that each question is pedagogically sound.
- This test is on CTS **digests**, not a separately assigned full-length systematic theology textbook.
- Confirm question 14 terminology (“substitution”/“sustitución”) and the Spirit question #27 in the relevant biblical translations.
- `requiredFrom` is **null**. Activate only following academic review, integration/Worker testing, and advance announcement. Preserve earned completions.
