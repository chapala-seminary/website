## English

Chapala Theological Seminary is not accredited. The Certificate of Ministry,
Associate of Divinity, Master of Theology (Th.M.), and Master of Divinity
(M.Div.) are internal ministry recognitions of this seminary. They are not
official academic degrees. In Mexico, an official degree requires recognition
by the Secretaría de Educación Pública (SEP), usually through an RVOE
(Reconocimiento de Validez Oficial de Estudios). CTS is a ministry of Common
Ground Fellowship at Little Chapel and is pursuing formal recognition in
Mexico; it does not now grant official Mexican degrees.
